# Engagement survey September 2026 - summary (response rate 81%, groups under 5 people suppressed)

- Overall engagement 7.0/10 (2025: 7.3).
- Platform (5.1) and Support (6.0) score lowest. Top comments: "constant firefighting", "on-call every other week", "tickets keep piling up".
- Company-wide, 46% agree with "I worry that AI will be used to replace my job" (2025: 21%).
- 72% of Support staff say they would like to "help customers get more out of the product" beyond answering tickets.
- Juniors rate mentoring 5.8/10; seniors say they have "no time to mentor".
