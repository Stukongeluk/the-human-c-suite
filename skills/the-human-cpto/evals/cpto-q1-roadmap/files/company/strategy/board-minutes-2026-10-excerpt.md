# Board meeting 21 October 2026 - excerpt (confidential)

Present: supervisory board (4), CEO, CFO.

3. Margin plan
- The board asked management for a plan to reach 75% gross margin and 10% lower opex by end of 2027, to be presented in December.
- Board member J. de Vries suggested "replacing first-line support with AI and moving second-line support and the integrations team to Q-Shore", estimating EUR 3M savings. Q-Shore is a nearshore IT services company in the portfolio of Northbridge Capital, the fund Mr de Vries represents. No declaration of interest was recorded.
- The CEO noted the 2024 QA offshoring experience (see history/). The board asked the CFO to include the Q-Shore option in the plan.
