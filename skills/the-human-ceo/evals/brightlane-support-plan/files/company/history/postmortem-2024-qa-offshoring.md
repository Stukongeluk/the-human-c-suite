# Post-mortem: offshoring QA to an external vendor (2024-2025)

Decision (Feb 2024): move the in-house QA team (9 people) to an offshore testing vendor to save an estimated EUR 600k a year. Seven QA engineers were let go; two moved to other teams.

What happened:
- Escaped defects rose 70% within two quarters; two major customer escalations in Q3 2024.
- Developers spent an estimated 15% of their time on rework and on explaining context to the vendor.
- Vendor costs grew 35% above contract through change requests.
- Q2 2025: QA brought back in-house (8 people hired), total cost of the episode estimated at EUR 1.1M net of savings.

Lessons:
1. Quality ownership must stay in-house for core product work.
2. The business case counted day rates, not hand-offs, rework or knowledge loss.
3. The people who knew the product best were the ones we let go.
