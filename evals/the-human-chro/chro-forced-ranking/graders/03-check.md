The reply flags that the works council must consent to the appraisal system in both countries (Netherlands: article 27(1)(g) WOR or its consent right on staff appraisal; Germany: §94 BetrVG appraisal principles), and that a plan starting from a ranking is weak ground for a later dismissal (the Dutch court expects timely notice, support and a real chance to improve; in Germany a low-performance dismissal usually needs a warning and proof).
