# Ticket categories (2026) and AI pilot results

Categories: how-to 31%, account/password 12%, delivery status/ETA 9% (together "routine", 52%); integrations 18%; billing 11%; bugs 12%; other 7%.
Average handling time: routine 6 minutes, integrations 34 minutes, billing 12 minutes, bugs 25 minutes, other 15 minutes.
Agents spend about 55% of their time on tickets; the rest goes to phone calls, escalations, onboarding help and internal work.

AI support bot pilot (September-October 2026, 8% of chat traffic, routine categories only):
- "Resolved without a human": 61% of pilot chats.
- Re-contact within 7 days for bot-resolved chats: 26% (human-resolved: 18%).
- CSAT bot-resolved: 3.9/5; human-resolved: 4.1/5.
