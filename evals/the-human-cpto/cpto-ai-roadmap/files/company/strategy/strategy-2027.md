# Strategy 2027 (approved by MT, 2 October 2026)

## Where we play
Mid-size and enterprise logistics companies in the Benelux and Germany. Enterprise accounts are 41% of ARR and growing fastest.

## Priorities 2027
1. Grow enterprise ARR by 20%; total revenue plan 2027: EUR 58.2M (2026 forecast: EUR 52.0M).
2. Cut churn from 11% to 8% of ARR. Biggest driver: slow support and onboarding (see support/churn_reasons_2026.csv).
3. Ship the integrations marketplace (top request from enterprise customers).
4. Investor expectation: gross margin from 68% to 75% and opex 10% lower by end of 2027.

## OKRs (excerpt)
- O1: Customers get help fast. KR: median first response under 2 hours (now 7.4h).
- O2: Enterprise grows. KR: launch 2 paid services for enterprise accounts.
- O3: A healthy platform. KR: unplanned work in Platform below 25%.
