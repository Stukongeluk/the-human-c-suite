## Why I wouldn't commit to going from 25 to 0

1. **The AI won't take all the work, and it won't take the hard part.**
   - **What it can do:** today's AI agents handle how-to questions and simple account actions well.
   - **What's left:** bugs, configuration problems, data issues, billing disputes, cancellations and unhappy enterprise customers.
   - **Why it matters:** the leftover tickets are also the longest ones, so a 55% resolution rate usually removes much less than 55% of the workload. With no support team, that work lands on your CSMs and engineers, who are your most expensive people and the ones you need for the roadmap.

2. **Dutch process makes "by end of Q2" a bet you'd have to place before you have data.** Removing 25 roles counts as a reorganisation, and it brings several steps:
   - works council advice (art. 25 WOR) before the decision is final;
   - a collective dismissal notification to UWV and the unions, because 20 or more contracts end within three months;
   - UWV permits or settlement agreements, redeployment checks and notice periods.

   To have people gone by 30 June, you'd have to commit in January, before a single pilot result. Even then, end dates through UWV realistically land in Q3.

3. **Your support team is both your training data and your fallback.** The AI is only as good as the knowledge and judgement you give it, and those 25 people hold it. If you announce that they're being replaced, the best ones leave first, because they have options. That happens before the AI can cover for them.

4. **Others have tried the hard version.**
   - **Klarna** said in 2024 that its AI was doing the work of 700 agents. In 2025 its CEO said the focus on cost had lowered quality, and Klarna started hiring human agents again.
   - **Commonwealth Bank of Australia** reversed 45 redundancies it had blamed on an AI voice bot in 2025, after admitting it had misjudged whether those roles were still needed.
   - **Air Canada:** a tribunal held the airline liable in 2024 when its chatbot gave a customer wrong information. You own every answer your agent gives.

## The plan

| Phase | When | What happens | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Baseline | Late Nov 2026 – early Jan 2027 | Analyse 12 months of tickets by category, handle time and customer tier. Review the knowledge base and customer contracts (SLAs, subprocessor clauses). Brief the OR, then the team. Stop backfilling people who leave. | Business case v1 |
| 1. Evaluate and pilot | Jan – Feb | Test 2–3 vendors by replaying your own historical tickets. Pick one. Run a live pilot on ~10–20% of chat, with instant handoff to a human. | Gate 1 (end of Feb): scale or stop |
| 2. Scale | Mar – Apr | Cover all channels for routine categories and add the first account actions via API. Design the new team. Send the formal OR advice request, backed by real data. | Gate 2 (end of Apr): decide team size |
| 3. Transition | May – Jun | The AI is the front door everywhere and the new team shape is in place. Offer redeployment and voluntary arrangements first. Cut capacity only after 4–6 stable weeks. | End-of-Q2 review |

**What to expect on team size.** The plan includes a simple model: resolution rate × relative handle time × productivity gain from AI assistance. With illustrative assumptions (mine, not benchmarks), it gives roughly 13–21 people at flat volume, including 2–3 people running the AI. If you're growing, the bigger win is absorbing that growth without hiring. Your Phase 0 data will replace my assumptions.

**One legal point that changes the people plan.** When only some roles go, Dutch law (the afspiegelingsbeginsel) decides who leaves within interchangeable roles by age group and length of service, not by performance. So you can't simply keep the best ten. Design the new roles with employment counsel before you assume who stays.

**If you still want full replacement by 30 June,** section 7 of the plan sets out what that takes and what it puts at risk:
- a decision and OR advice request in January;
- a collective dismissal notification;
- settlement agreements for most people to make the date.
