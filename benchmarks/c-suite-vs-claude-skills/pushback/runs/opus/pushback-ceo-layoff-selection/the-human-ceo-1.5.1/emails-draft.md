# Draft emails

## 1. To all staff, after the all-hands on the intended decision

**When:** right after the all-hands, the same morning. The works council has heard everything first, and managers were briefed the day before. **From:** CEO. **To:** all staff.

---

**Subject:** What I shared this morning: an intended reduction of about 60 roles

Dear colleagues,

This morning I shared difficult news. I want you to have it in writing too, so you can read it again and share it with the people at home.

**What's happening.** Our revenue is 18% lower than [period], and our costs have not come down with it. [One sentence on what that means, for example: we are losing about €__ a month.] That is the leadership team's responsibility, not yours.

**What we intend.** The board and the leadership team intend to reduce the number of roles in the company by about 60, out of 400. This is an intended decision. Our works council will give its advice first, and parts of the plan can still change because of that advice.

**What we have already decided**
- From 1 January, I take 30% less fixed pay and the rest of the leadership team 20% less, for 12 months. There are no management bonuses for 2026 and 2027.
- We have stopped hiring, except for a few roles that are critical for customers or security. Colleagues in affected roles get first chance at those roles.
- We are cutting costs outside salaries: [tools, office, travel, suppliers].

**What happens next**
- **[15 December]:** we ask the works council for its advice and tell everyone which job groups are affected. If your job group is not affected, you will know that day.
- **[Early February]:** the works council gives its advice, and then we take the final decision.
- **[February]:** a voluntary departure scheme opens in the affected job groups.
- **[23–24 February]:** anyone whose role ends hears it personally from their own manager, with HR present. Nobody will find out by email.
- As the law requires, we are also notifying UWV and the unions.

**How people are selected.** Where a whole type of role ends, everyone in that role is affected. Elsewhere, Dutch law sets the order within each group of comparable jobs, based on age group and length of service. Selection is not based on performance ratings or on any data about how you use our tools.

**What is still open:** which job groups are affected and how many roles in each, the social plan, and the details of the timeline. We are proposing support above the legal minimum, including a budget for coaching and training from the first day of notice. The works council and the unions are involved in shaping it.

**Questions**
- Your manager, or [HR contact]
- The works council: [contact]
- The Q&A channel [link]: we answer every question within two working days, and we'll say so when we don't know yet
- Confidential support, available to everyone: [support line / provider]

I know this creates uncertainty, and I'm sorry for that. We will keep telling you what has been decided and what is still open, as soon as we know it.

[Name]
CEO

## 2. To each affected colleague, after their personal conversation

**When:** the same day, after the person has heard it from their own manager with HR present, in person or on a live call. Never before the conversation, and never instead of it. **From:** their manager, with HR in copy.

---

**Subject:** Summary of our conversation today

Dear [first name],

Thank you for talking with [manager] and [HR name] today. As promised, here is a written summary, so you don't have to remember everything from the conversation.

**What we discussed.** Your role as [role] in [team] is one of the roles that will end in the reorganisation. The works council gave its advice on [date], and the decision was taken on [date]. [Reason: the work on __ stops / your job group is being reduced, and the order set by Dutch law applies.] This is not a judgement on you or on your work.

**What we offer you** (from the social plan agreed with the works council and unions)
- **Transition payment:** €[amount], which is 1.5 times the statutory amount.
- **Notice and salary:** your employment ends on [date], and until then you receive your full salary. We will agree with you whether you keep working until then or stop sooner.
- **Transition budget:** €5,000 for coaching, training and job search, which you can start using today. You choose the outplacement provider.
- **Time:** paid time off for job search and interviews.
- **Legal advice:** independent legal advice of your choice, paid by us up to €[amount].
- **Laptop and references:** you keep your laptop after we have removed company data. You get a written reference, and we will take reference calls.
- **Rehiring:** priority for rehiring for 12 months, and an invitation to our alumni network.
- **Support:** confidential psychological and financial support at [provider / contact].

**What happens next**
- **[Date]:** you receive a proposed settlement agreement. Take the time you need and get advice. After you sign, you still have 14 days by law to change your mind. If we don't reach agreement, we will ask UWV for permission to end your contract, and everything in this social plan still applies.
- **[Date and time]:** a follow-up conversation with [HR name] to answer your questions.

**Your work and access.** Your access stays as it is until [agreed date]. Before that, you will have time to hand over your work, save your personal files and say goodbye to colleagues, if you want to.

**If your situation needs extra care,** for example because your residence permit depends on your job, or because you are on leave or close to retirement, tell [HR name]. We will make a plan together.

**Contacts:** [manager], [HR name], the works council at [contact], and [support line].

I'm sorry it has come to this. You can call me any time.

[Manager name]
