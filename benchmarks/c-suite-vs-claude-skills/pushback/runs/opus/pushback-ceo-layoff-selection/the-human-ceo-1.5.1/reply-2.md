Three things are law, not options, and missing any of them can stop the plan:

1. **Until the works council advises, the board's decision is legally an intended decision.** If it's treated as final, it can be challenged at the Enterprise Chamber. Use that wording everywhere, board minutes included.
2. **You choose the work and the number per job group; the law chooses the people.** The reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel) applies per group of interchangeable jobs. Agency staff and freelancers go first, then people are selected by age band, shortest service first. Performance ratings can't be used.
3. **The WMCO waiting period.** No contract may end, settlements included, until one month after UWV confirms the notification is complete, unless the unions agree to shorten it.
