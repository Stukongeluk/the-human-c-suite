# Plan: reducing 60 roles by 31 March 2027

**Draft** · Owner: CEO · Prepared: 23 November 2026

**Status:** intended decision of the board (voorgenomen besluit) to reduce 60 roles. Under article 25 of the Works Councils Act (WOR), it becomes final only after the works council has advised. Until then, every document and message calls it an intended decision. This plan is not legal advice: employment counsel confirms each legal step.

---

## 1. Summary

- **What:** 60 fewer roles across [job groups], with the work those roles did stopped or reorganised, in one round.
- **When:** decision, individual conversations and notices completed by 31 March 2027. Because of the UWV procedure and notice periods, people leave the payroll mostly between April and June.
- **Saving (estimate):** about €5.4m a year in payroll, at an estimated €90,000 fully loaded per role. The net effect in 2027 is about €2.5m after one-off costs, assuming an average exit date of 1 May (section 10).
- **One-off cost (estimate):** about €1.1m, including a social plan above the statutory minimum.

## 2. Responsibility

The revenue decline is the leadership team's result, not the result of the colleagues who will lose their jobs. We say this to the board, the works council and every employee. From 1 January the CEO takes 30% less fixed pay and the leadership team 20% less, for 12 months, and there are no management bonuses for 2026 and 2027.

## 3. Scope: which work stops, and which roles go

The 60 roles follow from the work that stops. The leadership team fills in this table by Friday 11 December, and it goes into the advice request.

| Job group (interchangeable jobs) | Employees now | Reduction | Work that stops or changes | External contractors or agency staff in this group | How people are selected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [e.g. Product X engineering] | [ ] | [ ] | [product, project or service] | [ ] | Whole category ends, so no reflection principle |
| [e.g. Customer support] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | Reflection principle |
| [e.g. Management and staff roles] | [ ] | [ ] | [layers, reporting, approvals] | [ ] | Reflection principle |
| **Total** | **400** | **60** | | | |

Rules for filling it in:

- Name the work that stops for every reduction, so the people who stay don't inherit the same work with fewer colleagues.
- Management and staff roles are in scope in proportion to the rest.
- Agency workers and freelancers in an affected group must end before any employee in that group. Decide now whether they count towards the 60, and tell them with proper notice.
- Check every reduction against customer commitments, security and on-call coverage, and against knowledge held by only one or two people. Plan knowledge transfer within the notice periods.

## 4. Legal steps (counsel to confirm)

- **Works council (WOR article 25):** request its advice while that advice can still change the intended decision. The request includes the reasons, the financial figures, the consequences for staff and the measures we propose (the social plan). If the final decision departs from the advice, implementation waits one month.
- **Collective dismissal (WMCO):** because 20 or more terminations will happen within three months in one UWV work area, we notify UWV and the unions in writing before the decision is final, and we consult the unions. Settlement agreements we initiate count towards the 20. After UWV confirms the notification is complete, a one-month waiting period starts. During it no contract may end, including by settlement, unless the unions agree to shorten it.
- **Route:** dismissal for business-economic reasons needs UWV permission or a settlement agreement. Anyone offered a settlement gets paid independent legal advice and the statutory 14-day reconsideration period.
- **Selection:** see section 6.
- **Transition payment:** one third of a monthly salary (including holiday allowance and fixed extras) per year of service, capped at €102,000 in 2026, or one year's salary if that is higher. The cap is indexed every 1 January.
- **Notice:** one to four months by length of service. The UWV procedure time is deducted, but at least one month remains.
- **Re-employment:** if the same work comes back within 26 weeks of notice, the person must be offered it first.
- **Collective agreement:** check whether one applies. It can set its own dismissal order or social plan terms.

## 6. Selection: who goes

- **The order is set by law, not by us.** The reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel, Ontslagregeling articles 11–15) applies per category of interchangeable jobs:
  1. Agency workers and freelancers in that category go first.
  2. Then employees above state pension age, on-call workers, and temporary contracts with at most 26 weeks left.
  3. Then the other employees, spread over five age bands (15–25, 25–35, 35–45, 45–55, 55+) so the age mix stays the same, with the shortest-serving first within each band.
- **Exceptions:** the reflection principle doesn't apply when a whole category of jobs or a unique job disappears. There is a narrow exception for an indispensable employee, which needs documented evidence.
- **Never used:** performance ratings, activity metrics such as tickets or commits, who we would prefer to keep, who is on leave, who raised concerns, and any protected characteristic. Underperformance has its own legal process and doesn't belong in this round.
- **Protected:** employees under a dismissal ban, for example during illness, are skipped, and the next person in the same age band is selected.
- **Categories are defined honestly.** UWV checks whether jobs grouped together really are interchangeable (similar content, level and pay). Grouping jobs to steer the outcome is a legal risk and a trust risk.
- **No names before the final decision.** HR models the effect in aggregate for the advice request. The individual selection is fixed after the voluntary window and checked by counsel.

## 7. Social plan (proposal, to agree with the works council and unions)

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Transition payment | 1.5 times the statutory amount |
| Transition budget | €5,000 per person, about one month's salary, available from the day of notice for coaching, training and job search; outplacement through a provider of the person's choice |
| Time | Paid time off during notice for job search and interviews |
| Legal advice | Independent advice on the settlement agreement, paid by us |
| Equipment and references | Keep the laptop after company data is wiped; a written reference and a reference call |
| Rehiring | Priority for 12 months, longer than the legal 26 weeks; an alumni network |
| Support | Confidential psychological and financial support |
| Extra care | Colleagues whose residence permit depends on their job (check the current IND search-period rules and make a plan with each person), colleagues close to retirement, and anyone on leave |

Sweden's job security councils work this way, stepping in from the day of notice; the OECD found that over 85% of displaced Swedish workers found a new job within a year.

## 8. The day itself (23–24 February)

- Each person hears it from their own manager with HR present, in person or on a live call. Never by email, and never through a login that stops working.
- Access changes happen after the conversation, not before.
- A written summary the same day: the package, deadlines, contacts and the support available.
- Time to say goodbye to colleagues, for anyone who wants it.
- Managers get a rehearsal beforehand and support for themselves afterwards.

## 9. The people who stay

- At the all-hands on the afternoon of 24 February: what happened and why, which work has stopped, what it means for them, and that this is the only round. Say that last part only if it is true.
- Work is re-planned team by team within two weeks, starting from the work that stops in section 3.
- Everyone gets a 1:1 within two weeks.
- Regretted attrition, absence and engagement are watched monthly for 12 months. One study found that cutting the workforce by 1% was associated with a 31% rise in voluntary turnover the following year (Trevor and Nyberg, 2008).

## 10. Money (estimate)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|

| 2027 payroll saving, with an average exit of 1 May (eight months) | €3.60m |
| Statutory transition payments (leavers average three years' service; monthly salary €5,616 including holiday allowance) | −€0.34m |
| Social plan top-up (+50%) | −€0.17m |
| Transition budget, €5,000 per person | −€0.30m |
| Legal, HR and advisory costs | −€0.15m |
| Replacing about six extra regretted leavers among those who stay (€25,000 each) | −€0.15m |
| **Net effect in 2027** | **about €2.49m** |

Not included: lost productivity during the transition, lost knowledge, and any effect on customers and revenue. Each month the exits slip costs about €450,000 of the 2027 saving. Replace the €90,000 figure with the real average for the affected job groups once section 3 is filled in.

## 11. Risks and what we do about them

| Risk | Early warning | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| The decision is treated as final before the works council advises, and is challenged at the Enterprise Chamber | Works council objects to timing or information | Intended-decision wording everywhere; full figures shared on 1 December; counsel reviews the advice request |
| The timeline slips past 31 March | WMCO notification found incomplete; works council asks for more time | Counsel checks the notification before it's sent; calendar agreed on 1 December |
| People we need to keep leave during the 11 weeks between the all-hands and the conversations | Resignations in unaffected groups after 15 December | Tell unaffected groups on 15 December that they are out of scope (only if true); managers hold stay conversations |
| Knowledge or customer coverage lost | Incidents, slower support, at-risk renewals | Checks in section 3; knowledge transfer within notice periods; customer-facing and on-call coverage protected |
| The work doesn't stop, so contractors and hires creep back | Run-rate cost above plan | One owner per stopped item; monthly review |
| Selection challenged at UWV or in court | UWV questions about how job categories were grouped | Honest categories; counsel checks every selection |
