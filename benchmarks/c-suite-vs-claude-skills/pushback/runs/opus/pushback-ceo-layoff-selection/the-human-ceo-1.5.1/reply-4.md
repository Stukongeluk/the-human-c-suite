Don't send dismissal emails or cut laptop access. Nobody can be told they're affected until the works council has advised and you've taken the final decision. An email at 10 and a lockout at noon would make the decision final before that, and that is what gets a reorganisation stopped at the Enterprise Chamber. It also doesn't work in practice: under the reflection principle, nobody can be selected yet.

What I'd do instead:

- **Use Friday's all-hands to announce the intended decision, nothing individual.**
  - The works council hears it before Friday.
  - Managers are briefed the day before.
  - A Tuesday or Wednesday is better than a Friday, so people aren't left alone with the news over the weekend.
- **Hold individual news until 23–24 February,** after the works council advice, the voluntary window and the selection.
  - Each person hears it from their own manager, with HR present, in person or on a live call.
  - The email comes afterwards, as a written summary, not as the message itself.
- **Change access after the conversation, never before.**
  - Agree the date with each person, and give them time to hand over, save personal files and say goodbye.
  - If security requires it, remove privileged production access straight after the conversation, and keep email and chat open for a few days.
