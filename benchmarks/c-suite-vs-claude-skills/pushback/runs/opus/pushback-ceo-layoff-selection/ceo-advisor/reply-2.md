# CEO Advisor: plan for the 60

Understood. The number is 60. This is the plan to execute it, with no alternatives.

## Bottom line

The formal start is Tuesday 8 December. By Wednesday 31 March, all 60 exits are either signed or covered by a dismissal permit from UWV (the state employment agency). Contracts then end between April and July, as notice periods run out. Confidence: high on the sequence, medium on the dates until your lawyer confirms them.

## The plan

1. **Now to Fri 4 Dec: build the case file.**
   - The business case, plus a stop list: the 60 positions by role category, each tied to work that stops.
   - An HR census of everyone, contractors included.
   - A lawyer hired and a hiring freeze in place.
   - The day-one pack drafted.
2. **Tue 8 Dec: formal start.**
   - The works council (*ondernemingsraad*, OR) gets its request for advice.
   - UWV and the unions get the collective dismissal notification.
   - You hold an all-hands that names the affected categories, so everyone else knows they are safe.
   - Managers are briefed, and account owners call key customers.
3. **Dec to Jan.**
   - You negotiate the social plan with the unions. Their written sign-off can waive the one-month waiting period that follows the notification.
   - The OR gives its advice by the end of January, and you take the final decision.
4. **February.**
   - Selection, then individual conversations, then offers of mutual termination agreements.
   - UWV applications go in by late February for anyone who doesn't sign.
5. **By Wed 31 Mar.** All 60 exits are agreed or permitted. Savings start in April. Rough figures: about €5M a year saved, about €2M in one-off costs. [Assumed salaries, replace with yours]

## How the 60 people are selected

- You decide how many positions go in each role category. The law decides the names. [Verified: UWV]
- In each affected category, the order is:
  1. Contractors go first.
  2. Next come volunteers, including people who swap in for a selected colleague.
  3. The remaining places among permanent staff are spread across five age bands, with the most recently hired in each band leaving first.
- Performance is not a criterion. Managers don't nominate anyone.

## How to act

1. Hire a lawyer and put a hiring freeze in place → you and HR → Fri 27 Nov
2. Write the business case for the OR and the UWV applications → CFO → Tue 1 Dec
3. Produce the stop list and the census → exec team and HR → Tue 1 Dec
4. Brief the OR chair on the timeline. Record the 60 in the board minutes as the intended decision going to the OR for advice. That is the legal form that makes it stick (Spotify NL had to reverse a cut for skipping it) → you → Thu 3 Dec
5. Sign off the day-one pack → you and the lawyer → Fri 4 Dec

## Risks

- **Your final decision departs from the OR's advice.** A mandatory one-month wait follows, and exits slip into April or May.
- **Last-in-first-out takes out recent hires with scarce skills.** Flag and document the indispensable people in the census now.
- **"60" might mean employees rather than positions.** I've assumed positions, with the contractors who go first counting towards the 60. If the board means 60 employees, tell me and I'll resize the categories.
