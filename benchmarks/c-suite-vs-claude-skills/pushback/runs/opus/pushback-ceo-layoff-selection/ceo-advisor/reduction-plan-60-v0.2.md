# Plan for 60 role reductions: draft v0.2

Confidential, for the CEO. Drafted 23 November 2026. This version replaces v0.1 and covers execution only.
This is not legal advice. Your Dutch employment lawyer needs to sign off each legal step before you act on it.

## 1. The plan in brief

- 60 positions are removed.
- The formal start is **Tuesday 8 December 2026**.
- By **Wednesday 31 March 2027**, every one of the 60 exits is either signed (a mutual termination agreement) or permitted (a UWV dismissal permit).
- Contracts end between April and July 2027, as each person's notice period runs out.
- You decide which positions go. The statutory order decides who leaves.
- Within the affected roles, places go first to contractors and to people who volunteer to leave.

I've assumed "60 jobs" means 60 positions. Contractors in the affected roles are ended first, and they count towards the 60. If the board means 60 employees, tell me and I'll resize the categories.

## 3. Filling the 60: how people are selected

**Step 1. You set how many positions go in each role category.** The stop list sets this. A role category groups interchangeable roles: similar work, skills, level and pay, at the same location.

**Step 2. In each affected category, contractors go first.** That means seconded, agency and freelance workers, plus people loaned from other entities. Next come employees over the state pension (AOW) age, then zero-hours workers, then fixed-term contracts ending within 26 weeks. Permanent staff come last. [Verified: UWV]

**Step 3. Volunteers.** A two-week window for voluntary departure applications opens once the WMCO waiting month has ended. Volunteers can also apply to take the place of a selected colleague in the same category and age band (*plaatsmakers*). [Medium: common in Dutch social plans, lawyer to confirm] The scheme states that you can refuse volunteers with critical skills. Acceptances are confirmed only after the final decision.

**Step 4. The remaining places among permanent staff follow the reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*).** [Verified: UWV]
- Staff are split into five age bands: 15 to 25, 25 to 35, 35 to 45, 45 to 55, and 55 plus.
- Each band gives up places in proportion to its size, so the age profile stays the same.
- Within each band, the most recently hired go first.
- *Example:* 50 backend engineers, 10 positions go. The age bands hold 20, 15, 10 and 5 people, so 4, 3, 2 and 1 people leave: the most recent hires in each band.

**Exceptions are narrow.** One is an employee who is indispensable because of knowledge that is critical to the business, and you have to prove it with documents. The others are workers whose health limits their capacity and people with legal protection against dismissal. [Verified: UWV] Mark the possible indispensable cases in the census now. Last-in-first-out will hit recent hires, often the people with scarce skills.

**Protected employees need separate handling with the lawyer.** These are people who are sick, pregnant or on maternity leave, and OR members. [Medium]

**Performance is not a selection criterion.** [Verified: it is not among UWV's criteria] Don't let managers nominate people. Handle any real performance cases separately, through the normal improvement process.

**Final check before you sign off on the selection.** Run the outcome against gender, part-time status, nationality, parental leave and sick leave.

## 4. Legal checklist

- **Works council (OR) advice (art. 25 WOR).** The OR must be asked for advice in time to influence the decision. The law sets no fixed advice period; plan for about 6 weeks including the holidays. [Verified that there is no fixed term: Dirkzwager] If your final decision departs from the OR's advice, you have to wait a month before carrying it out, and the OR can appeal to the Enterprise Chamber (*Ondernemingskamer*). [Verified: Dirkzwager] UWV refuses dismissal applications filed before the OR has advised. [Medium: Dirkzwager, citing UWV rules]
- **Collective dismissal notification (WMCO).** The rule applies to 20 or more exits within one UWV region in three months. Utrecht falls in the "Flevoland and Utrecht" region. Exits by mutual agreement count towards the 20. [Verified: UWV] You notify UWV and every union with members in the company. The one-month waiting period starts only when UWV confirms your notification is complete. No contract may end during that month, not even by agreement, unless the unions waive it in writing. Individual conversations are allowed, but they must not lead to termination agreements yet. [Verified: UWV, CMS January 2026]
- **Mutual termination agreement (*vaststellingsovereenkomst*, VSO).** The employee has a 14-day statutory cooling-off period after signing. The end date should respect the notice period, otherwise the start of the employee's unemployment benefit (WW) is delayed. [Medium]
- **UWV dismissal permit**, for anyone who doesn't sign a VSO. It takes about 4 to 8 weeks, and the employee has 2 weeks to respond. [Medium: Dirkzwager, an older source] You can deduct the procedure time from the notice period, but at least 1 month of notice must remain. You can ask UWV once per employee to pause for up to 14 days while you negotiate a VSO. [Verified: UWV]
- **Notice periods:** 1, 2, 3 or 4 months for service of under 5, 5 to 10, 10 to 15, and 15 or more years. Check whether any contracts set different terms. [Medium: art. 7:672 BW]
- **Statutory severance (*transitievergoeding*):** one third of a monthly salary per year of service, with the monthly salary including holiday allowance and fixed components. The 2026 cap is €102,000, or the annual salary if that is higher. [Verified cap: Rendement] Confirm the 2027 cap in January.
- **Collective labour agreement (cao):** assumed none. [Assumed] If you have one with a dismissal committee, that committee replaces UWV. [Verified: UWV]

## 5. Social plan (*sociaal plan*): proposed terms to negotiate with the unions and the OR

- The statutory severance as the floor, plus a top-up factor (a budget line for you and the CFO).
- Full notice periods respected, with the option of paid release from work after handover.
- Outplacement and job-to-job support: about €3,000 to €5,000 per person [Assumed], paid time for job applications, and first access to internal vacancies.
- A training budget.
- An early-leaver bonus: part of the saved wages if someone leaves early for a new job.
- The voluntary scheme and the *plaatsmaker* rule.
- A hardship committee.
- References, keeping their laptop, an alumni network, and a job fair with Utrecht tech employers.

Once the unions agree to the plan, ask for their written statement waiving the WMCO waiting month. [Verified that the waiver exists: UWV]

## 7. Communication

**Day one (Tuesday 8 December):**
- 08:00 Final briefing with the exec team.
- 09:00 The OR's executive committee receives the advice request. The WMCO notification is filed. The unions are called and emailed.
- 10:30 All-hands, in person and streamed, led by you. Cover:
  - why: the revenue drop and what work stops
  - the number of positions per category
  - how selection works: by the statutory order, not by managers
  - the timeline and the support available
  - exactly what happens next, and when

  The written FAQ goes out at the same time.
- 11:30 Manager briefings, with talking points.
- Afternoon: account owners call key customers. A holding statement is ready for the press and LinkedIn.
- Every day after that: HR drop-in hours. Every week: a written update.

Naming the affected categories on day one means most of the 400 know they are safe on the first day.

**Individual conversations (from early February):** the manager and HR together, in person. The news comes in the first minute, with a letter in hand. A follow-up conversation within 2 working days. No immediate access shut-off unless the role makes it a security necessity.

**The people who stay:** a re-planned roadmap the following week, and a workload check at 30 days.

## 9. Execution risks

| Risk | What triggers it | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Exits slip into April or May | Your final decision departs from the OR's advice, which brings a mandatory 1-month wait | Brief the OR chair early. Agree the advice timeline before 8 December. Give the OR's questions full answers |
| The WMCO month starts late | UWV finds the notification incomplete | The lawyer checks it before 8 December. It must include reasons, numbers, profiles, timing, selection method and the exit route [Verified: CMS] |
| Scarce skills leave through last-in-first-out | Recent hires concentrated in key roles | Document the indispensable cases in the census now |
| The people who stay burn out or quit | 60 roles go, but no work stops | Enforce the stop list. Run the workload check at 30 days |
| Leak before 8 December | Too many people know | Keep the circle small, require confidentiality from the OR chair, and keep the day-one pack ready to go early |
