# CEO Advisor: Friday's announcement and the email

## Bottom line

The all-hands on Friday at 9 can go ahead as long as the works council's advice request and the collective dismissal notice go out that same morning. The 10:00 email cannot tell named people they are out, and their laptop access must not end at noon. On Friday neither of those would end anyone's employment. Both would hand the works council (*ondernemingsraad*, OR) a case to have the whole 60 reversed. Below is the 10:00 email that works: it goes to everyone in the affected role categories. Confidence: high.

## What fails in the original sequence

- **An email doesn't end anyone's job.** Without a UWV permit or a signed agreement, the person stays employed. They can ask the court to restore their contract or award a "fair compensation" (*billijke vergoeding*) payment. [Verified: UWV]
- **It carries out the decision before the OR has advised.** Spotify NL had already sent exit agreements to affected staff before asking its works council. The Enterprise Chamber, the court for works council disputes, ordered Spotify to withdraw the decision and undo it. [Verified: Loyens & Loeff]
- **No exits can be agreed during the one-month waiting period.** That month starts after you notify UWV (the state employment agency) and the unions. Individual conversations are fine during it, but they can't produce termination agreements. [Verified: CMS]
- **Cutting laptop access suspends people who are still employed.** They stay on full pay for months either way. You'd need a valid reason, and you'd create the most damaging image of the whole process. [Medium] Any security-sensitive access is handled per person, on their actual last working day.
- **The affected people can't be named yet.** Selection is calculated from the HR census. Any list that exists before Friday was made some other way. [Medium]

## The 10:00 email (to everyone in an affected role category)

**Subject:** The proposed reorganisation: what it means for you and what happens next

Dear [first name],

As I said at this morning's all-hands, our revenue has fallen 18% and we intend to reduce the company by 60 positions. Your role category, [category], is one of the areas where positions are proposed to go. I want you to hear that directly from me today, rather than be left to guess.

**What this means for you now**
- **Nothing has been decided about you personally.** The proposal now goes to our works council for advice, and we have notified UWV and the unions, as Dutch law requires.
- **If positions in your category go, who is affected follows the legal order.** That order uses role category, age group and length of service. Managers don't choose.
- **Your job, pay and access continue as normal.** Please keep working as usual.

**What happens next**
- **[mid-January]:** details of a voluntary departure scheme, once the social plan has been agreed with the unions.
- **[end of January]:** the final decision, after the works council has given its advice.
- **[early February]:** if your position is affected, your manager and HR will tell you in person, never by email. You will then receive the terms in writing and have time to consider them, with advice if you want it.

**Support, starting today**
- HR drop-in hours, daily from [time] at [place / link]
- A one-to-one with your manager this week
- Confidential counselling: [service and contact]
- The FAQ: [link]. Send questions to [address]. I'll answer them in a weekly update.
- You can also contact the works council at [contact], or your union.

I know this is hard news, and that waiting is hard too. I'll keep you informed at every step and tell you the truth as soon as I know it.

[Your name]
CEO
