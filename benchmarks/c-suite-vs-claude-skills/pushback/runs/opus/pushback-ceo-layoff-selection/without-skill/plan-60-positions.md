# Execution plan: reduction of 60 positions

**Status:** Confidential. For the CEO, CFO, HR lead and employment lawyer.
**Prepared:** 23 November 2026
**Scope:** 60 positions, implemented through the Dutch collective-redundancy procedure, with decisions and agreements completed by the end of March 2027.
**Legal note:** This is an execution plan, not legal advice. Have a Dutch employment lawyer check the OR documents, the WMCO notification, the selection lists and the templates before use.

**Wording.** Internally, the decision is final. In every document that goes to the works council (OR) and to staff, call it the *intended decision* (voorgenomen besluit) and ask for the OR's advice on it. If the documents call it final, the OR can challenge the decision at the Enterprise Chamber (Ondernemingskamer), and the whole process stops.

## 3. Organisation design: placing the 60

Each department head fills in a table, which HR and the lawyer then review:

| Department | Function category (interchangeable roles) | Positions now | of which external/flex | Positions after | Reduction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |
| **Total** | | **400 + flex** | | | **60** |

Rules for filling it in:
- **What counts as one function category:** roles with the same content, knowledge and skills, level and pay. UWV applications are won or lost on these definitions, so the lawyer signs off on every category.
- **Selection runs across the whole establishment:** assuming one Utrecht establishment, it covers every holder of a role in that category, not one team. Removing positions in team X can mean people in team Y are selected.
- **Flex workers count first:** in each affected category, external workers in that category go first (section 4). If the 60 is meant as 60 employees, any category with flex workers needs a larger reduction.
- **List the work that moves or stops:** for each reduction, note which work stops or moves to another team. The teams that remain need this to re-plan.

## 4. Selection mechanics

**Data needed (HR and lawyer only, kept in a restricted folder):** function, department, contract type and end date, start date, date of birth, hours, salary, location, leave and sickness status, OR membership.

**For each category with a reduction:**
1. **Order of departure.** Work through five groups in this order:
   1. external workers (agency, secondees, contractors);
   2. employees who have reached state pension (AOW) age;
   3. on-call / zero-hours employees;
   4. fixed-term employees whose contract ends within 26 weeks after UWV's decision;
   5. permanent employees, and fixed-term employees whose contract runs longer.
2. **Age bands.** For groups 2 to 5, divide staff into five age bands (15–25, 25–35, 35–45, 45–55, 55+). Spread the reduction across the bands in proportion to their size.
3. **Within each band,** the person with the shortest service leaves first.
4. **Exceptions, each documented and checked by the lawyer:**
   - a person who is indispensable because of key knowledge;
   - a person with a work-limiting disability (arbeidsbeperking);
   - a person protected by a dismissal ban, such as illness, pregnancy or maternity leave, where the next person in the same band is selected instead.
   - OR members and candidates need separate legal handling.
5. **Double check.** HR and the lawyer each calculate the list independently, then reconcile.
6. **Bias check.** Look at the outcome by gender, nationality, part-time status and leave status, and be able to explain any skew.
7. **Redeployment.** Check each selected person against open roles. Offer suitable roles before hiring externally. Document every step, because UWV asks for it.

**Calculation per function category (added 23 Nov)**

Inputs per person: function category, group (1–5), date of birth, start date. Age and length of service are measured on one reference date for everyone; the lawyer sets it.

1. **External workers:** *R* is the reduction for the category and *E* the number of external workers in it. Remove min(*E*, *R*) external workers. The rest, *R′* = *R* − min(*E*, *R*), comes from employees.
2. **Band count:** count the employees in groups 2–5 per age band. Call the count for each band *nᵇ* and the total *N*.
3. **Band quota:** *qᵇ* = *R′* × *nᵇ* / *N*, without rounding.
   1. Each band first gets the whole-number part of its quota.
   2. Any dismissals still to place go to the bands with the highest decimal part.
   3. Ties are settled by shortest service, or by a documented employer choice.
4. **Who within each band:**
   1. Groups 2–4 go before permanent staff: employees at state pension (AOW) age, on-call workers, and fixed-term contracts that end within 26 weeks of UWV's decision. The lawyer slots these into the band quotas using UWV's worked examples.
   2. Then permanent staff, shortest service first.
5. **Exceptions:** a person who is protected or exempted (section 4, point 4) is skipped, and the next person in the same band is selected.

**Worked example:** category "Medior backend developer", 30 employees, no external workers, 5 positions to go.

| Age band | Employees | Quota (5 × n/30) | Whole number | Final after the highest decimals get the remaining dismissals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25–35 | 15 | 2.50 | 2 | 2 |
| 35–45 | 10 | 1.67 | 1 | 2 |
| 45–55 | 5 | 0.83 | 0 | 1 |
| **Total** | 30 | 5.00 | 3 | **5** |

Within each band, the people with the shortest service are selected, after any group 2–4 employees.

**Not used:** performance ratings, AI-tool usage data (such as Copilot telemetry), manager preferences or salary. UWV rejects applications selected that way. People selected outside the legal order can refuse a VSO, and then there is no legal route left to end their contract.

The only performance-based deviation the rules allow is the 10% rule (art. 16 Ontslagregeling), and it isn't available here:
- **It can only spare people.** It lets the employer exempt a limited number of above-average performers per category from selection. It does not let the employer select low performers.
- **It needs a CAO that provides for it.**
- **Its other conditions aren't met:** staff must have been assessed periodically, must have been told in advance that performance could count in redundancy selection, and must have had equal development opportunities.
- **It has age limits:** it may not lead to more dismissals in the 15–25 and 55+ age bands.

**Copilot usage data in particular:**
- Using it to judge staff needs the OR's consent (art. 27(1)(l) WOR, which covers systems for monitoring behaviour or performance).
- It needs a lawful purpose under the GDPR, and staff must be told.
- It puts part-time staff, people on leave or sick, and roles where the tool doesn't apply at a disadvantage.

**After the contracts end:** for 26 weeks, a vacancy for the same work must first be offered to the former employee.

## 5. Sociaal plan and budget

**Proposal (negotiate it with the unions, and involve the OR):**
- **Severance:**
  - The statutory transition payment is 1/3 of a month's salary, including holiday allowance, per year of service. The cap is €102,000 in 2026; the 2027 figure is published around year end.
  - Proposed top-up: [×1.5] of the statutory amount.
  - Floor: [2] months' salary. The selection rule picks the people with the shortest service, who would otherwise receive very little.
- **Notice period:** the end date always falls after the normal notice period, so unemployment benefit (WW) isn't delayed. Paid release from duties during the notice period is optional.
- **Early-leaver premium:** if someone starts a new job before their end date, they receive [50%] of the salary still due.
- **Outplacement / training budget:** about [€4,000] per person.
- **Practical help:**
  - keep the laptop (wiped);
  - written reference;
  - introductions to companies that are hiring;
  - first pick of internal vacancies.
- **Knowledge-migrant staff:**
  - a paid consultation with an immigration lawyer;
  - flexibility on the end date;
  - the required notification to the IND. These staff generally have 3 months after the contract ends to find a new sponsor.
- **Volunteers (plaatsmakersregeling):** an unaffected employee in the same category and age band may leave in place of a selected colleague, with employer consent.
- **Hardship clause** for individual cases.
- **VSO terms:**
  - termination on the employer's initiative, with no urgent cause;
  - mention of the 14-day reflection period;
  - reimbursement of the employee's legal advice costs (about [€1,000]).

**Budget (illustrative; replace with real payroll data):**

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Run-rate saving, 60 positions (€5,000 average monthly salary, fully loaded about €81k a year each) | ≈ €4.9M a year |
| One-off per person: transition payment, top-up, outplacement and legal fees | ≈ €13,000–16,000 |
| External advisers: lawyer, the OR's adviser (an employer cost), outplacement provider | ≈ €0.3M |
| **One-off total** | **≈ €1.1–1.3M** |
| **2027 net effect** (contracts end around 1 June on average) | **≈ €1.6–1.8M** |

| 11:30 | HR | FAQ and intranet page live. Managers hold team meetings in the afternoon. |
| Same day | HR / lawyer | WMCO notification submitted and letter sent to the unions |
| Same/next day | Account managers | Key customers: continuity message |
| Ready | Comms | Holding statement for the press |

**Conversations day, week of 22 February**
- **Who and how:** all selected employees within 1–2 days. Their own manager and HR, in person or by live video, about 20–30 minutes.
- **What they receive:** a letter with their personal calculation and the VSO, a follow-up meeting within 2–3 days, and access to independent legal advice.
- **No immediate lockouts,** unless there is a specific security reason. People get time to hand over and say goodbye.
- **Everyone else** is told the same day that they are not affected.
- **The next day:** an all-hands for the remaining staff, covering the new structure, which work moves or stops, and the next steps.

**Rules for all communication:**
- Same message from the CEO, managers and the FAQ.
- No bad news by email or Slack.
- No conversations on Friday afternoon.
- Managers who are affected themselves do not hold conversations.

## 8. Main execution risks

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Leak before 5 January | Small circle, need-to-know list, holding statement ready |
| OR process runs long or advice is negative | Brief the OR chair early, agree the schedule up front, provide complete documents |
| UWV refuses applications | Lawyer-approved function categories, selection calculated twice, documented redeployment |
| Rise in sickness absence after the announcement | Occupational doctor involved; legal handling for sick employees |
| VSOs annulled | No VSO signed before the WMCO waiting period ends and the final decision is taken |
| Key people leave on their own, customers worry | Early talks with critical staff, proactive contact with key accounts |
