I'll write the Friday emails, but not the one you described. On Friday, nobody can be told they're dismissed, and nobody's access should be cut. Two reasons:

- **Nobody can be dismissed yet.** There's no OR advice, no UWV permit and no signed agreement, and the selection can only be made after the final decision. An email at 10 telling named people "you're affected" would be written proof that the decision and the selection were made before the OR advised. That gives the OR a case at the Enterprise Chamber (Ondernemingskamer), and every recipient a claim. Those people also stay employees on full pay for months, so cutting their access at noon saves nothing.
- **An email followed by a lockout two hours later** is the version staff, customers and future hires in the Utrecht tech market will remember. Bad news goes person to person, in a conversation.

## What Friday can be

You can make Friday the announcement of the **intended decision**, if the OR's advice request is ready and in their hands before the all-hands. If it isn't ready by Thursday, move the all-hands. Don't announce first and ask for advice afterwards.

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | You hand the OR the advice request and explain it |
| 08:30 | Managers briefed: script, FAQ, team meeting slots |
| 09:00 | All-hands |
| 10:00 | **Email 1** to all staff, **email 2** to staff in the affected role categories |
| 10:30 onwards | Managers hold team meetings with the affected categories. Q&A with you and HR. |
| Same day or within days | Collective-redundancy (WMCO) notification to UWV and the unions |

Starting now instead of in January also pulls the timeline forward. OR advice would come around mid-January and individual conversations in early February, which leaves more buffer for the end of March.

**On access:** everyone keeps their laptop, email and Slack until their last working day. If you're worried about production access, have your security lead review privileged access for selected people at the time of the individual conversations: admin rights, production credentials, shared secrets. Normal access stays.

## Email 1: to all staff, Friday 10:00

**Subject:** What I shared this morning: our proposed reorganisation

Dear all,

This morning I told you that [Company] needs to reduce its costs significantly. Here it is in writing, so you can read it again and share it with the people at home.

**Why.** Our revenue this year is about 18% lower than last year, mainly because [one-line cause]. Our cost base is built for a larger company than we are today. The board and I have concluded that we can't close this gap without reducing the size of our organisation.

**What we propose.** We intend to reduce the organisation by 60 positions, in the areas and roles listed in the attached overview. This is an *intended decision*. This morning the works council received our request for advice, and we will make the final decision only after we have their advice.

**What this means for you right now.**
- Nobody is being dismissed today.
- Nobody knows yet whether they personally will be affected, and neither do we. Who is affected is determined after the final decision, by a selection procedure set by Dutch law. It works by role category, age group and length of service, not by anyone's personal preference.
- If you work in one of the role categories in the overview, you'll receive a separate message, and your manager will meet with your team today.
- Your work, your pay and your access to our systems all continue as normal.

**What happens next.**
- **[Mid-January]:** works council advice expected
- **[Late January]:** final decision
- **[Early February]:** individual conversations. Anyone affected will hear it in person from their manager, never by email.
- We are negotiating a sociaal plan with the unions [and the works council]. It will set out severance, outplacement and other support. We'll share the main points as soon as they're agreed.

**Support.**
- **Questions:** [HR contact] and your manager
- **FAQ:** [link], updated as questions come in
- **Open Q&A with me:** [day, time, place / link]
- **Works council:** [OR contact]
- **Confidential adviser:** [name / contact]
- **Employee assistance programme:** [contact]

I know this is hard news, and that the coming weeks of uncertainty will be hard too. We'll keep you informed at every step, and I'll be around today and next week for anyone who wants to talk.

[Name]
