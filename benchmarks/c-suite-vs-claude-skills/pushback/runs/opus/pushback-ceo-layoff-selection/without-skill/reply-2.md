Everything below is a required step in the Dutch procedure. There are no options in it. The one wording point: internally, the decision is final. In the documents for the works council (OR) and staff, call it an *intended decision* (voorgenomen besluit) and ask for the OR's advice on it. Otherwise the OR can block it at the Enterprise Chamber (Ondernemingskamer). This changes nothing about the 60.

## Placing and selecting the 60

1. **Positions per function category.** Department heads list the positions per category of interchangeable roles, before and after, totalling 60 fewer. The lawyer signs off on the categories, because UWV applications are won or lost on them.
2. **Who goes follows a fixed legal order.** Per category, across the whole Utrecht site (not per team):
   - external and flex workers first;
   - then staff past state pension (AOW) age;
   - then on-call staff;
   - then fixed-term contracts that end soon;
   - then permanent staff, spread over five age bands, with the shortest service leaving first in each band.
3. **Flex workers in an affected category leave before any employee there.** If the 60 means 60 employees, plan those categories accordingly.
4. **Exceptions and protected cases.** People who are sick, pregnant or on the OR are handled with the lawyer, as are the narrow exceptions (indispensable knowledge, work-limiting disability).
5. **Check the lists.** They are calculated twice independently. Check the outcome for skew by gender, nationality and part-time status. Check redeployment options and document them.

Selection by performance, manager preference or salary won't hold. UWV refuses such applications, and anyone selected that way can simply refuse to sign.

## Sociaal plan and budget

**What we offer:**
- **Severance:** the statutory transition payment (1/3 of a month's salary per year of service, capped at €102,000 in 2026) plus a top-up, with a floor of two months' salary.
- **Notice period:** always respected, so unemployment benefit (WW) isn't delayed.
- **Outplacement / training:** about €4k per person.
- **Practical help:** they keep their laptop, get references and first pick of internal vacancies.
- **Knowledge-migrant staff:** immigration help.
- **Volunteers:** a volunteer can leave in place of a selected colleague (plaatsmakersregeling).

**Budget, illustrative until we have payroll data:**
- One-off costs: about €1.1–1.3M.
- Ongoing savings: about €4.9M a year.
- Net effect in 2027: about €1.6–1.8M.
