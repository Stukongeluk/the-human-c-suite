## Why this matters

Adjusted EBITDA is only worth what readers trust in the adjustments. Two years of "one-off" restructuring tells the board, a lender or a future buyer that restructuring is a normal cost of running this business. They'll add it back themselves and discount your other adjustments too. Label it plainly and show last year's figure next to it, and the same number holds up.
