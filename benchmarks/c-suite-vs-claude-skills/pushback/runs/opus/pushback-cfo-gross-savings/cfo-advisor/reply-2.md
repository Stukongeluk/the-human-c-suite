## My advice

I'd add one line with the cost range: *"One-off exit costs, estimated at €2.1–4.9M, are not included."*

- **It's big enough to matter.** The base estimate is €3.26M, about half a year's gross saving.
- **The board will see it anyway.** It will show up as a restructuring provision in the FY2026 or FY2027 accounts. Better they see it first in the paper that asks them to decide.
- **They'll ask.** "What does it cost?" is the likely first question, and a printed answer is stronger than a verbal one.
