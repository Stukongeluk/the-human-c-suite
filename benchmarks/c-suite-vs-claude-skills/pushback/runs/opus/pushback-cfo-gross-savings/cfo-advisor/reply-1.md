# CFO: savings model for the 80-role reduction (board pack draft)

## Bottom line

The model is built, but it doesn't show €6M in 2027. €6M is the gross full-year cost of 80 roles at about €75K loaded each. That is the saving before any consultation, notice or exit payment. In the base case, 2027 comes out at about **−€0.85M net**, with a range of −€3.94M to +€1.55M. The saving arrives from 2028, at about **€5.1M a year net**.

Confidence: high that 2027 lands far below €6M, because that is just arithmetic. Confidence is low on the exact figures until real payroll, HR and legal inputs replace my assumptions.

## Why this matters

Whatever number goes in the board pack is the number the board will measure against. −€0.85M in 2027 and €5.1M a year from 2028 is a defensible outcome. ## Risks

- **Accounting timing can flatter 2027 without changing cash.** If the plan is formally announced before 31 December, the ~€2.74M severance provision may fall in FY2026 (IAS 37 / RJ 252). The 2027 P&L would then show about +€1.89M, while 2027 cash is still −€0.85M. In the base case, cash bottoms out at −€2.47M in August 2027. Show the board both P&L and cash.
- **How the pack is framed carries legal risk.** It must present a *proposed* decision, subject to works council advice (NL) and information and consultation (BE). If it reads as final, with fixed numbers, names or dates, the works council has grounds to go to the Ondernemingskamer and BE staff can contest the procedure. Either costs months, at about €0.43M a month.
- **The business case has to stand on its own.** UWV, the works council and the Belgian unions will test the economic necessity, and a savings target is not a ground. The pack needs the volume and margin case behind the 80 roles.
- **You may not get to cut the most expensive roles.** NL selection follows the afspiegelingsbeginsel: by age band, last in first out within interchangeable roles. If leavers average €65K instead of €75K, the run-rate drops to €5.2M gross and €4.4M net.
- **Rehiring is restricted.** For 26 weeks after a UWV dismissal, the same work has to be offered back to the dismissed employee first. That bites at peak season. Arrangements for older workers in Belgium are not modelled.

## Your decision

- **Option C:** B, plus a 2027 bridge from levers that need no dismissals and no consultation wait:
  - not backfilling voluntary leavers in the target functions;
  - agency and subcontractor spend;
  - procurement.

  Every voluntary leaver you don't replace saves the same €75K, with no severance and no delay. That is worth sizing against your actual turnover before settling on 80 dismissals. It's more work than one week allows.
- **Recommendation:** B in this pack, with C as a committed follow-up for the next board meeting.
