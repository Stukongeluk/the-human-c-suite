I kept two labels on the page, because without them it would state something that isn't true:

1. **It says "gross annual run-rate", not "2027 savings".** Even on a gross basis, 2027 only gets about €3.3m. Nobody can leave before the NL works council consultation and the BE Renault procedure are done, so exits land in May or June at the earliest. If the page says "€6m in 2027", the board will hold you to €6m in the 2027 results.
2. **The subtitle says the figures are "before one-off restructuring costs and before any cost that returns through agency staff, overtime or rehiring".** It's one line with no numbers. It just says what "gross" means.

I'd also recommend one footnote with the cost, but that's your call. For example: *"One-off restructuring costs of approx. €3.6m (severance, notice, outplacement, advisers) are presented separately."* My reasons:

- **The board is approving the spend as well as the saving.** The restructuring provision hits the P&L once the plan is announced. It's better the board hears that number from you now than finds it in the results.
- **This is the second round in two years.** The board will compare this case with what last year's delivered. If a gross-only case later turns out to be worth much less net, that's what damages credibility.

If you'd rather put the costs in an appendix or a separate paper, the **Board Summary** tab already has them laid out for that.
