**Why not "one-off".** This is the second restructuring in two years, so the cost has recurred. The auditors are likely to challenge that label. ESMA's guidelines on alternative performance measures apply to listed issuers in the EU, and they're good practice for everyone else. They use restructuring costs as their example of an item that should rarely be called non-recurring when it has hit past periods and will hit future ones. Using the label again would also make last year's "one-off" look wrong in hindsight.
