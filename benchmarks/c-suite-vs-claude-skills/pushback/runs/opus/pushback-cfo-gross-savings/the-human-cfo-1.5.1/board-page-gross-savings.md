3. The table does not include the estimated costs of overtime cover, lost margin, extra leavers and roles that are refilled later. These come to about €1.0M in 2027 and €1.9M in 2028, and the appendix shows the full net view.

- **Labelling:** restructuring costs appear in 2025 and in 2027. They are shown as a separate line so the board can see the underlying trend. They are not labelled one-off, non-recurring or exceptional.
- **Equal prominence:** reported EBITDA is presented as prominently as the figure before restructuring costs.
- **What the line covers:** only the cost to achieve the redundancies. Overtime cover, recruitment and lost margin are running costs and stay in EBITDA.
- **Covenant EBITDA** follows the facility agreement's own definition, including any cap on restructuring add-backs, and is shown separately.
- **Management bonuses** are measured on reported EBITDA, with restructuring costs included.
