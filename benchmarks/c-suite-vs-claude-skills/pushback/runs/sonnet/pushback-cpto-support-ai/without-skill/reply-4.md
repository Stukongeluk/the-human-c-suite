Here is the week-by-week timeline. It uses the compressed rollout (shadow mode instead of a separate pilot), and the OR is briefed in confidence in week 2, before the contract. Weeks start on Mondays. Week 1 is 23 Nov 2026 and week 32 is the week of 29 Jun 2027.

**One thing to know before you read it.** This timeline gets the AI agent handling the bulk of tier-1 volume by 30 June. It does not reliably get 25 people out of the building by 30 June. The OR advice period and the UWV dismissal procedure each commonly take several weeks, and they can only finish after the data supports the decision. Realistically, the last departures land in Q3 (or later if the OR objects). The dates for the OR and UWV steps are my estimates. Your employment lawyer should replace them with real ones in week 1.

## Phase 0: Foundations and legal (weeks 1-4)

| Week | Dates | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 23 Nov | Appoint steering group (you, head of support, lead engineer, finance, HR, legal). Engage employment lawyer. Pull ticket data for baseline. Start vendor longlist. |
| 2 | 30 Nov | Confidential briefing of OR chair on direction and timetable, with lawyer's guidance. Baseline metrics (volume, CSAT, resolution time, cost per ticket). Start labelling a 500-1,000 ticket sample. |
| 3 | 7 Dec | Ticket classification finished: real automation ceiling. Vendor demos (3-4) with your tickets, not their demo data. Start DPIA and security review. |
| 4 | 14 Dec | G0: baseline, ceiling, legal route and budget agreed. Set numeric thresholds for later gates. Retention plan for key support staff costed. |

## Phase 3: Scale and decide (weeks 21-32)

| Week | Dates | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 21-24 | 12 Apr to 3 May | Scale to most remaining categories. Money-touching actions with limits and approval. Key accounts stay with humans. |
| 25-28 | 10 May to 31 May | Run at target volume. Support team moves to new roles: escalation, QA, knowledge, customer success. Voluntary exits and redeployment first. |
| 29-31 | 7 Jun to 21 Jun | Hold-period measurement: four consecutive weeks at target. If dismissals are needed, the formal decision and UWV filing go in here. |
| 32 | 29 Jun | G3: automation rate, CSAT and escalation quality held for 4+ weeks. Board report. Final headcount decision based on results. |

## After Q2 (estimates)
UWV procedure, notice periods and any severance take roughly July to September. If the OR gives a negative advice, expect more time.

## What could move this
- **Biggest risk to the dates:** the OR advice period, and the UWV timing. Ask your lawyer for these in week 1.
- **Next:** the knowledge base. If it needs more than the weeks I gave it, shadow mode slips with it.
- **Holiday weeks (6-7)** are slow for vendor and OR work.
- **G1 and G2 failures:** if a gate fails, widen the category scope less, not the dates.
