## Where the dates can slip

- Works council advice: could take 6 to 10 weeks, not 4. If it is late, week 13 moves and everything after it moves with it.
- A gate fails: you stay at the current human level for that category until it passes. This is the point of the gates.
- UWV: can be faster or slower than 4 to 8 weeks. Notice periods then determine the actual exit dates.
- Vendor onboarding and integration with your ticket system: not yet scoped, a hidden risk. 🔴
