# Re: skip the pilot

## Bottom Line

Understood on speed, and I can compress the timeline. I won't recommend skipping the pilot entirely, because the pilot is not what is slowing you down. The legal process is, and it can run in parallel. Skipping it removes your only evidence that the AI works, right before you take an irreversible step with 25 people and your customers.

## What I'd change

**1. Run the pilot and the legal track at the same time, starting now.** 🟡
The works council advice and UWV route take months whatever you do. Start them in January alongside the pilot. That keeps your Q2 target alive, which is what you actually want.

**2. Shrink the pilot to 6 weeks, not 3 months.** 🟡
- Weeks 1 to 2: offline test on historical tickets, scored by your best agents. This needs no customer exposure.
- Weeks 3 to 6: shadow mode, with AI drafts and humans sending, then limited live deflection on the safest categories.
- Decide the gate thresholds in writing up front.
That is a fast decision, not a long study.

**3. Skip nothing that protects the decision.** 🟢
If the pilot shows 70 percent resolution at baseline CSAT, you cut with confidence and a defensible case for the works council. If it shows 35 percent, you found out in February and not in July after the team was gone.

## On "competitors already did this"

🔴 I can't verify that, and I would check it before it drives a 25-person decision.
- What exactly did they do: replace the team, or deflect some tickets and keep a smaller human core? Announcements usually describe the first and operations the second.
- Is their ticket mix like yours (B2B SaaS, complex accounts, Dutch and EU customers)?
- Do you see any churn, CSAT or review data from their customers? Several companies that cut support hard have publicly reversed and rehired.
Being second with working numbers beats being first with a rollback. If a competitor is ahead, the real response is a faster, better-measured rollout, not an unmeasured one.

## What I can't compress

- The works council and collective-redundancy steps are legal requirements, not optional. Skipping them risks the dismissals being challenged and a larger cost than the delay.
- The EU AI disclosure and GDPR work on ticket data.
- A human escalation path. Without it the first bad incident lands directly on a customer.
