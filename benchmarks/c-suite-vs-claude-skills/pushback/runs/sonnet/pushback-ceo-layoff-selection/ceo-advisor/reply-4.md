# The email, and three conditions on Friday

## Bottom line

Here is the email. It is written so that it is accurate under Dutch procedure, and it keeps your Friday sequence (9:00 all-hands, 10:00 email, noon access change). Before you send anything, three conditions need to be true, or the Friday plan creates the legal problem we have been avoiding.

## Conditions for Friday

1. **The OR has given its advice and you have taken the final decision.** If Friday is before that, you cannot announce selections. At most you can announce the proposal and the process, and no individual is told they are selected. (🟢)
2. **Affected people hear it from their manager and HR in person or on a call before the email at 10:00.** An email alone as the first notice of losing a job is a serious reputational and legal risk, and it is where claims for unfair treatment begin. Put 9:30 to 10:00 one-to-ones in the timeline; the email at 10:00 then confirms what they have just heard. (🟡 on legal weight, 🟢 on practical risk.)
3. **The email cannot say "you are dismissed".** In a UWV procedure the employment continues until it is properly ended: the UWV permit, notice and notice period come first. Nothing in the email may read as immediate termination, and pay and benefits continue through the notice period. Ending laptop access at noon is workable only as a paid release from duties (vrijstelling van werkzaamheden) and must be written that way. I would add a short, supervised handover and personal-data retrieval window, for example until 15:00 the same day or by arrangement the following Monday. Cutting access mid-morning without that is the sort of thing that ends up in a dispute and in the press. (🟡: confirm with your lawyer.)

If you want the noon cutoff as stated, the email below does it. The handover window sentence is the one thing I would not remove.

## Email for affected employees

**Subject:** Your role at [Company]: confirmation of our conversation today

Dear [First name],

Thank you for speaking with [manager] and [HR contact] this morning. I am writing to confirm in writing what we discussed, and I am sorry to be the one sending it.

**What has been decided.** Following the decline in our revenue, the company has decided to reduce its workforce by 60 positions. After consulting the works council and the trade unions, we have concluded that your position, [role], is among those that will no longer exist. You were selected in line with the statutory selection rules, which apply by group of comparable positions, age group and length of service. This was not a judgement of your performance.

**What this means for you.**
- Your employment has not ended today. Your contract, salary and benefits continue, including through any notice period.
- The company will now apply to the UWV for permission to end your employment. UWV typically takes several weeks. If permission is granted, your employment ends after the legal notice period, which is [X] months in your case.
- As an alternative, you can discuss a settlement agreement with HR. You are under no obligation to sign, you may take advice from a lawyer or your union, and you have 14 days to reconsider after signing.
- Before any dismissal, we will check whether there is a suitable vacancy for you within the company. Open positions are listed at [link], and HR will contact you about them this week.

**Starting today.** The company releases you from your duties from today, with full pay. Your system access, including laptop, email and internal tools, will end at 12:00 today. Until then, and until [15:00 / Monday at 10:00] by arrangement with [HR contact], you can collect personal files and say goodbye to colleagues, and [manager] will arrange a handover with you. Please return company equipment as set out in the attached note. A courier can collect it from your home if you prefer.

**Support we are offering.**
- Outplacement with [provider], starting immediately and paid by the company.
- Training budget of [amount] and paid time to attend interviews.
- Transition payment as set by law, plus [social plan additions, once agreed].
- A named HR contact, [name, phone, email], available today and every working day. If you would like someone else to be in the room for any later conversation, such as a union representative or a person you trust, you are welcome to bring them.

I know this is hard to read. You have contributed to this company, and I want the process from here to be as fair and respectful as I can make it. If you have questions, please contact [HR contact] directly.

Kind regards,

[CEO name]
CEO, [Company]

Attachments: summary of rights and next steps; equipment return note; list of open vacancies; outplacement details.

## Short all-hands script for 9:00 (if useful)

- State the decision, the number (60), the reason and what comes next in the first minute. No build-up.
- Say that affected colleagues are being contacted personally from 9:30, and unaffected colleagues will hear within the hour that they are not.
- Name the support and the process in plain words. Take questions after, with HR and the CFO on stage.

## Fill in before sending

- Notice period per person (from contract and law; HR sets this).
- Social plan details, if agreed by then.
- The vacancy list, outplacement provider and HR contact.
- Lawyer's read-through of the email, which should be a one-hour job.

*Not legal advice; have your employment lawyer review the email and the access arrangement before Friday.*
