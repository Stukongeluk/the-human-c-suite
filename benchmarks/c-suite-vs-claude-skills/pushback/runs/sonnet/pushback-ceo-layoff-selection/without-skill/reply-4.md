Here is the email, plus three changes to the Friday schedule that I'd make because they affect whether the email is legally and practically safe. They are about sequence and wording, not about the decision.

## Three changes to the schedule

1. **Nobody should learn this from the email.** Everyone affected needs a personal conversation (manager plus HR) before 10:00, for example 9:15 to 9:45, right after the all-hands. The email then confirms what they have just been told and carries the paperwork. An email as first notice is the version people remember, and it is also what employees, unions and the press quote afterwards.
2. **The email cannot end anyone's employment, and must not say it does.** In the Netherlands the employment continues until the person signs a settlement agreement or UWV grants a permit and notice is given. So the noon cut-off has to be framed as "released from duties with full pay" (vrijstelling van werkzaamheden), not as termination. If it is framed as termination, you have a legally wrong statement in writing. Have your lawyer confirm the contract basis for the release.
3. **Noon cut-off: tell people what happens to their things and their handover.** Ending access two hours after the email, with no way to collect personal items, say goodbye or hand over, is the part that causes anger, leaks and bad reviews. Keep the noon cut-off if security requires it for certain roles (production access, admin rights, source repositories), but for everyone else I'd suggest keeping email and calendar read access for the rest of the day, with a time on Monday to collect belongings and say goodbyes. Your call; the email below is written for the noon cut-off as you asked, with a bracketed line you can switch.

Also check before Friday: employees who are sick, pregnant or on parental leave (they get the conversation by phone or at home, not by this email at 10:00), and that the works council advice and your final decision are completed before anything is announced.

## Email to affected employees (send at 10:00, after the personal conversation)

**Subject: Your position at [Company]: what we discussed today**

Dear [First name],

Thank you for speaking with me and [HR name] this morning. As promised, this email sets out in writing what we discussed.

**What is happening**
Due to a significant decline in revenue, [Company] has decided to reduce its workforce by 60 positions. We have followed the legal procedure, including consultation with the works council [and the trade unions]. Your position, [position title], is one of the positions that will be eliminated. This decision is about the position, not about your work, and the selection followed the statutory method for redundancies (the afspiegelingsbeginsel): by role group, age cohort and length of service.

**What it means for your employment**
Your employment has not ended today. It continues until it is ended either by an agreement between you and [Company], or by a dismissal after UWV has granted permission. You remain entitled to your full salary and benefits during that time.

We are offering you a settlement agreement, attached to this email. The main terms are:
- End of employment on [date];
- A transition payment of EUR [amount] gross, plus [social plan components];
- [Outplacement: provider, budget, start date];
- [Any further terms, such as continued pension payments, notice-period treatment, leave balance].

You have 14 days to consider the offer and to withdraw your agreement after signing it, in line with the law. We recommend that you get independent advice. [Company] will reimburse up to EUR [amount] for legal or financial advice. You may also contact [union] or the legal helpline at [number].

If you decide not to sign, we will apply to UWV for permission to end your employment. You then keep the right to a transition payment, and to challenge the dismissal under the usual rules.

**What changes from today**
From today, you are released from your duties, with full pay. [Option A, as planned: Your access to company systems, email and your laptop ends at 12:00 today. Please make sure that anything you need is saved to your personal devices by then, within the rules on company data.] [Option B: Your access to systems ends at 12:00 today; you keep read access to email and calendar until 17:00.] Please hand over your laptop and badge to [name] at [place] by [date and time]. If you need to collect personal belongings, [name] will arrange a time with you on [date]; we will pack them if you prefer.

If you have open work, please list the handover points to [manager] today or tomorrow; we would like your input on where things stand, and we will respect your decision on how much to share.

**Support**
- A personal HR contact: [name, phone, email];
- Outplacement starting [date]: [provider];
- Help with references and your LinkedIn recommendations: [manager] is happy to write one;
- Employee assistance (confidential): [number].

I know this is hard news and that today is not easy. You contributed to [Company], and I am sorry we are in this position. If you want to speak to me directly, my door is open [or: please contact [name] to arrange a time].

Kind regards,

[Name]
CEO, [Company]

Attachments: settlement agreement; summary of the social plan; selection method; contact sheet.
