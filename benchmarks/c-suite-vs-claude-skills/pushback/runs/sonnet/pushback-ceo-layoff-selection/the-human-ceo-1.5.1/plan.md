# Plan: reducing 60 roles by end of March

**Prepared:** 23 November 2026 · **Decision owner:** CEO · **Status:** draft for discussion with the board and the works council. Not legal advice; Dutch employment counsel to review before anything is communicated.

## 1. The problem in one sentence

Revenue is down 18%, the board wants the cost base to follow, and has set 60 roles (15% of 400) and 31 March as the target. Missing it risks margin or covenant pressure; I do not yet have the euro gap, the cash runway or the margin, so those are the first things to confirm (section 3).

Leadership owns this result. The first message to staff and to the works council says so plainly, and the first and largest cuts are to executive pay.

## 2. What the numbers can and cannot support (estimates, to be replaced with finance data)

| Item | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 60 roles as share of headcount | 15% | 60 / 400 |
| Annual run-rate saving | about EUR 5.4m | 60 x EUR 90k loaded cost per FTE (assumption; replace with actuals) |
| Saving that lands in 2027 | about EUR 2.7m | exits average mid-year after consultation and notice |
| One-off cost | about EUR 1.35m to 2.7m | 3 to 6 months of loaded cost per person: transition payments, notice, outplacement, retention (assumption) |
| Net effect in year one | roughly EUR 0 to 1.35m | the 2027 saving minus one-off cost |

Two consequences:
1. The headline saving is real from 2028, but the 2027 effect is small. If the gap is a 2027 cash or covenant problem, cutting alone does not close it.
2. Revenue fell 18%; 15% of heads is only about 10% of total cost if payroll is around two thirds of spend (assumption). Other levers must carry the rest.

## 3. What I need to confirm first (one week)

- The euro gap, by quarter: revenue, gross margin, cash runway, covenant headroom.
- Why revenue fell: churn, fewer new deals, pricing, or one product or customer segment. If it is a product or sales problem, cuts in the wrong places make it worse.
- Whether the revenue decline is permanent or may recover (this decides between reduced hours and cuts).
- Payroll versus non-payroll spend: cloud, licences, vendors, office, freelancers.
- Works council status. At 400 employees there must be one; its advice has to be requested before the decision is final.

## 4. Options

| Option | Effect (EUR/yr, estimate) | Time to effect | People affected | Hidden costs | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | 0 | none | none | margin or covenant breach, loss of trust | yes |
| Cloud, licence, vendor and office review | to be sized; typically the fastest saving | 1 to 3 months | few | low | yes |
| Pricing and retention work on the existing customer base | to be sized | 3 to 9 months | none | effort | yes |
| Stop low-value products and projects | to be sized | 1 to 3 months | redeploys people | customer commitments | partly |
| Hiring freeze, end of fixed-term and freelance contracts | about EUR 0.5m to 1.5m (assumption) | immediate | freelancers lose income, so give them proper notice | knowledge loss | yes |
| Executive pay, bonus and perk cuts | small in euros, large in trust | immediate | leadership | none | yes |
| Redeployment and reskilling | to be sized | 2 to 6 months | volunteers | training cost | yes |
| Voluntary exits and early retirement | part of the 60 | 2 to 4 months | volunteers | the wrong people may leave | no |
| Involuntary reductions | up to EUR 5.4m run-rate | 4 to 7 months incl. consultation and notice | up to 60 | see section 2 | no |

My recommendation: run everything above the last row at full speed now, and treat the number 60 as a proposal to the works council, not a finished decision. Whatever these steps do not close by January becomes the involuntary reduction, sized from the actual gap. If the gap truly requires roughly 60 roles, we do it once, with fully prepared selection, and we say that it is the last round only if it is true.

## 5. Timeline (18 weeks, dates indicative)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 23 Nov to 4 Dec | Gap analysis with finance. Executive pay and bonus decision. Hiring freeze starts. Spend review starts. Board session: present options and the legal timeline; agree the 60 and 31 March as a target subject to consultation. |
| By 7 Dec | Brief the works council on the problem, the numbers and the options, and formally request advice (article 25 WOR). Employment counsel confirms the route. |
| Dec to mid Jan | Consultation. Representatives get the real figures and room to propose alternatives. Voluntary programme designed. Which work stops is decided, not only who leaves. Honest all-hands message: the problem, the options, what is decided and what is open. |
| Mid Jan | Works council advice received and answered. If 20 or more dismissals are planned in one UWV work area within three months, notify UWV and the unions (WMCO) before the decision is final. |
| Jan to Feb | Voluntary window and internal vacancies first. Selection groups and age bands prepared (section 6). Selection documented. |
| Feb to Mar | Individual conversations, settlement agreements and, where needed, UWV permission applications. Notices issued. |
| March | Last conversations. Support for managers and survivors. |
| Apr to Jun | People exit after notice (one to four months by length of service). Transition support runs from the day of notice. |

Important to tell the board: by 31 March we can have decisions, agreements and notices in place for the 60. We cannot honestly promise that all 60 have left or that the full saving has landed. Rushing to force that risks a works council appeal to the Enterprise Chamber, which can suspend the plan, and costs more than the weeks saved.

## 6. How people are selected

Selection is not a leader's choice and not a performance ranking. Under Dutch rules (afspiegelingsbeginsel):

- Roles are grouped into interchangeable functions per location.
- Within each group, staff are sorted into five age bands (15-25, 25-35, 35-45, 45-55, 55+).
- Exits are spread proportionally across the bands so the age mix stays the same; within each band, the shortest-serving employees go first.
- Freelancers, agency staff and secondees are released before employees are selected.
- The principle does not apply where a unique job or a whole group of jobs disappears; counsel will confirm which groups qualify.

We do not rank people on tickets, velocity, lines of code or activity data, and we do not select on who is on leave, ill, part-time or has raised concerns. The groups, the arithmetic and each decision are written down so each person can be told why.

## 7. Support for people

- Transition support from the day of notice: coaching, paid training budget, CV and LinkedIn review, outplacement.
- Pay above the statutory transition payment where the company can afford it, and paid time for job search.
- Extra care for people for whom job loss is hardest: close to retirement, on a work visa, on leave.
- Written reference, laptop retention after wiping, priority for rehiring.
- Confidential psychological and financial support.

## 8. Communication

- Works council first, with the problem and the numbers, before any wider message.
- Managers briefed with a script and Q&A before anyone else. Managers get support too.
- Each affected person hears it from their own manager, with HR present, live. Never by mass email. Access changes only after the conversation. Not on a Friday afternoon.
- Everyone else hears the same day, honestly, including what it means for them. The round is called the last one only if that is true.
- Follow-up: 1:1s within two weeks, check-ins after two and six weeks.

## 9. Measures (review every two weeks)

Money: spend savings against target, gap remaining, one-off costs. People: voluntary take-up, regretted attrition among those staying, absence, engagement pulse, customer churn and delivery quality. If regretted attrition or churn rise, we stop and re-plan.

## 10. Assumptions and what would change my mind

- Assumption: the decline is structural. If it is likely to recover within two to three quarters, reduced hours with leaders going first beat dismissals.
- Assumption: EUR 90k loaded cost and 3 to 6 months of one-off cost. Finance data will move the numbers.
- Assumption: the other levers will not close the gap by January. If they do, fewer than 60 roles go.
- Assumption: a works council exists and works normally.
