**The selection rule, since it is the part that goes wrong:** performance, tickets, velocity and "who we'd rather keep" cannot be used. Nor can leave, illness or raised concerns. The only discretion is limited and goes through counsel: a unique job or a whole group disappearing sits outside the principle, as does genuine, documented necessity.

**Dates the board should hear in advance:** 31 March gets you decisions and notices. Exits and the full saving come after. Forcing the sequence by skipping consultation risks an Enterprise Chamber appeal that could suspend the plan.

**Package (set the figures with finance):** pay above the transition payment, paid job-search time, outplacement and training budget from the day of notice, references, laptop retention and extra care for people near retirement, on a visa or on leave.

**Measures every two weeks:** voluntary take-up, groups where the numbers don't reconcile, regretted attrition among those staying, customer churn.
