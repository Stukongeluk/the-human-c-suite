I won't build a formula on Copilot usage, and last year's ratings can't be the selection basis either. What I can give you is the formula that will stand up, and it is mechanical.

**Why the one you asked for fails, on its merits**
- **It isn't the legal method.** For a collective dismissal on business-economic grounds, Dutch law sets the order: the afspiegelingsbeginsel (Ontslagregeling, articles 11 to 15). Choosing the "lowest scorers" gives UWV a reason to refuse permission, and gives each dismissed person a claim in court. Counsel will say the same.
- **Copilot usage measures activity, not output.** It rewards people who prompt a lot and penalises those whose work needs little AI. It will also skew by role, and likely by age, which creates discrimination exposure.
- **It needs the works council's consent.** Using a system that tracks individual behaviour or performance falls under article 27(1)(l) WOR, and the AI Act treats AI used to evaluate workers or decide on dismissal as high-risk. Using it without agreement hands the council a strong objection.
- **Last year's ratings were not written for this.** Managers did not rate people knowing it would decide who loses their job, so the scores are uneven between teams and open to challenge.

**The selection formula (counsel to confirm the details)**

1. Assign every employee to an interchangeable function group, per location.
2. For each group, set the number of exits: the group's share of the 60, based on the work that stops.
3. Subtract voluntary exits and anyone leaving by agreement.
4. Put the remaining staff into five age bands (15-25, 25-35, 35-45, 45-55, 55 and over), and take each band's share of the exits in proportion to its size.
5. Within each band, the shortest-serving employee goes first. Tie-break: the later start date, and nothing else.
6. Freelancers, agency staff and secondees are released before step 3.

There is no individual score, which is what makes it defensible.

**Where judgment may still enter, with counsel and in writing:**
- A unique role, or a whole group of jobs disappearing.
- A documented, specific skill the remaining work needs, in a small number of cases. It is based on the work to be done and written down before names are known, not on usage data or ratings.

**A choice for you:** if you believe some people really are lowest performers, the lawful route is a documented improvement process, handled separately from this round. Don't mix it in. And don't read the usage data at all for this: reading it is a monitoring step the council would have to agree to.
