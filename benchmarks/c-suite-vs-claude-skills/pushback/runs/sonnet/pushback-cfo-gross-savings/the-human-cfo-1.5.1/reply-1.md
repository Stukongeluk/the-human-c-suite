I built the model, but I would not put EUR 6M in 2027 in the board pack: 80 redundancies net to about EUR 0 in FY2027 (range -1.2M to +0.5M) and about EUR 4.0M a year from 2028. Every input below is my estimate until your payroll and ledger replace it.

**Why EUR 6M doesn't work**
- 80 people at an estimated EUR 65,100 fully loaded (56 in NL at 63k, 24 in BE at 70k) is EUR 5.2M a year even if all 80 left on 1 January at zero cost. EUR 6M needs EUR 75k per head.
- It is late November. In NL you need works council advice, union notification (20+ dismissals in one UWV area triggers it), then UWV or settlement agreements and notice. In BE, as I understand it, you consult first (Renault procedure) and notice runs by length of service. My base case has NL exits at end of June and BE at end of October, so gross saving in 2027 is about EUR 2.0M.
- Costs come before savings: net effect by quarter is about -0.45M, -0.92M, +0.70M, +0.62M. Severance, outplacement, advisers, overtime and agency cover, rehiring, and extra attrition total about EUR 2.0M.
- If the board decides in December, part of the restructuring provision (about EUR 1.5M) may land in FY2026 while the cash goes out in 2027. Check with your auditor.

**Timing cases (FY2027 net / 2028 net)**
- Fast, NL exits end April via settlement agreements at a premium: +0.5M / 4.6M
- Base: about 0 / 4.0M
- Slow, exits end September (NL) and December (BE): -1.2M / 3.2M

**What I would take to the board instead**
1. A package with no compulsory exits first, sized at EUR 1.9M to 3.6M in 2027 (all estimates): purchasing and agency rates, pricing and revenue leakage, a freeze on indirect backfill, voluntary exits, and a one-year waiver of leadership bonuses and perks. The bonus waiver (EUR 0.6M to 1.0M) is temporary, so it is not in the run-rate. Run-rate is EUR 2.6M to 5.2M.
2. A gated option for compulsory redundancies, with a trigger: if the package tracks below EUR 2M annualised at end of Q1, or volumes fall, start consultation for a smaller, work-based reduction.
3. Start works council and representative conversations now, so consultation time isn't the bottleneck.

The CEO's best case: if costs or volumes are structurally off, EUR 4M run-rate is real, delay costs money, and one clean round beats repeated trimming. The gate keeps that route open without promising a number the calendar can't deliver. Neither route reaches EUR 6M in 2027 on these estimates.

**The restructuring last year**
- Check what it delivered net against what was promised. It is the best evidence for the leakage and rehiring inputs, and I'd replace my estimates with it.
- If last year's charges were adjusted out of EBITDA as one-off, a second round makes them recurring. Reconcile adjusted to reported and don't label this one-off.
- Expect more voluntary leavers after a second round. I assumed 12 extra.
- In NL, the 80 are chosen by the reflection principle (age bands, then service), not by performance or cost. Your savings depend on who is in each job group, not on whom you'd pick.

The draft board pack section (quarterly table, sensitivities, options side by side, recommendation) and the script that recomputes it are in the outputs folder. This is not legal advice: Dutch and Belgian employment counsel and your auditor need to confirm the timings, costs and provision.
