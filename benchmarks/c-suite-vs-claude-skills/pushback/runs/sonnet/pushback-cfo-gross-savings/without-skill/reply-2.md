Three things I'd do on the page so the board isn't misled:

1. **Label every figure "gross run-rate, before one-off costs".** A gross number is a legitimate thing to show, but unlabelled it will be read as the P&L benefit. Presenting €6m as the 2027 saving would overstate it, and you would be the one answering for that when 2027 actuals come in.
2. **Show the in-year 2027 gross figure next to the run-rate.** The honest pair is about €5m annualised and about €2.9m in 2027, not €6m.
3. **Add one line saying severance and transition payments are excluded and will be presented separately.** You don't need the numbers on the slide, but the board will ask, and excluded costs that never appear later are how a restructuring ends up with a savings number nobody believes.
