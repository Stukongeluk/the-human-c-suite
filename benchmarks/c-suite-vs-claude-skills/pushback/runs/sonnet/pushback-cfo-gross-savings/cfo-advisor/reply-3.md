**Conditions that matter**

1. **Consistency with last year.** The definition of "one-off" must be word-for-word the same as last year. If last year's add-back included only severance, do not add legal fees, outplacement or retention bonuses now without saying so.
2. **A second year in a row draws attention.** Two consecutive restructuring charges are likely to prompt the question of whether they are really non-recurring. Expect it from the board, the auditor and your bank. A short note on why this programme is separate from last year's helps.
3. **Check your financing documents.** Credit agreements usually cap the add-backs allowed in covenant EBITDA, often as a percentage of EBITDA, and the ratio is tested on the covenant definition and not on the board's. Your base case adds back about €2.8m. Confirm the headroom before the pack is final.
4. **Keep the cost on the page.** Show the below-the-line figure and the cash cost and payback as a single footnote, not only in an appendix. The adjusted measure is only credible if its reconciliation to reported EBITDA is visible, and an accounting standard-compliant presentation requires that anyway. Cash is spent regardless of the label, so the board should see it in the cash flow and net debt forecast.
5. **Provision timing.** If the plan is announced before 31 December 2026, the charge may fall in FY2026 and not 2027. Check with your auditor, since it changes which year carries the cost.
