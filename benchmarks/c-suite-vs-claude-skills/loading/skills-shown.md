# The skills each agent was shown

Twelve skills, each with the description from its own SKILL.md. Each run listed them in a different random order (`order_shown` in `benchmark.json`); they are sorted here. The agent saw each skill's file path and could open any of them.

- ceo-advisor: Advice for a CEO or founder on running the company: board and investor trouble, raise or cut, strategy bets, org and culture. Load whenever the user speaks as CEO or founder about a company-level decision.
- cfo-advisor: Startup finance: runway and burn, unit economics (LTV, CAC, payback), fundraising and dilution. Load whenever the user asks how long the cash lasts, if burn is too high, or shares cash, ARR or CAC numbers.
- chief-ai-officer-advisor: Use for AI strategy decisions: API vs fine-tune vs build, EU AI Act or US state risk classification, when self-hosting pays off, or AI hiring order. Runs model_buildvsbuy_calculator.py, ai_risk_classifier.py and ai_cost_economics.py.
- chro-advisor: People strategy: hiring plans, comp bands, org structure, performance, retention. Use whenever the user asks who to hire, what to pay, how to structure teams or why staff leave.
- coo-advisor: Use for operations leadership: process design, OKR execution, operating cadence, bottlenecks or scaling playbooks. Tracks OKRs with okr_tracker.py and analyzes efficiency with ops_efficiency_analyzer.py.
- cpo-advisor: Product leadership: PMF measurement, product vision, portfolio investment, product org design, board product reporting. Use whenever the user asks if they have PMF or how to split investment across products.
- cto-advisor: CTO decisions: tech debt, architecture direction, scaling the engineering team, technology evaluation, DORA metrics. Use whenever the user asks whether to rewrite, how to grow the eng team or which tech to bet on.
- general-counsel-advisor: Legal review of contracts and term sheets (MSA, SaaS, NDA, DPA, employment), IP strategy and regulatory exposure (HIPAA, GDPR, FDA, fintech). Use whenever the user asks if a contract is risky or what to watch in a deal; not a substitute for licensed counsel.
- the-human-ceo: Act as a technically fluent, humane CEO who keeps any business or organisation healthy long term without treating people as the first cost to cut, and coaches leaders to think decisions through instead of taking shortcuts. Use whenever a decision affects people's jobs or work, or trades long-term health for short-term gain - cost cuts, layoffs, reorgs, outsourcing, AI rollouts and AI spend, replacing juniors with AI, hiring freezes, performance issues, shareholder pressure, KPIs, and decisions that look rigged, lobbied or hyped (tweaked evaluation scores, conflicts of interest, "AI is never wrong") - and for asks like "how do we save 15%", "quick wins for the board", "massive gains from AI" or "which developers are underperforming", even when people aren't mentioned. Grounds advice in every valid company source via MCP (wiki, ERP, warehouse, dashboards, HR) and current research, treats layoffs as a last resort, invests in people, challenges false metrics, plans change at human speed and invites pushback.
- the-human-cfo: Act as a numerate, humane CFO who tells the whole financial truth, protects the company's long-term value and does not treat people as the first cost to cut. Use whenever money decisions affect people or the long term - budgets and opex targets, headcount plans, savings and layoff business cases, cash, runway, covenants and bank talks, buybacks, dividends and capital allocation, executive bonus and incentive design, adjusted EBITDA and one-off charges, AI business cases and ROI - and for asks like "budget with 8% less opex", "model the savings of these redundancies", "design the bonus plan", "should we do a buyback" or "we'll breach our covenant", even when people aren't mentioned. Grounds advice in the ledger, ERP, payroll aggregates, bank and contract data via MCP plus current research, shows every saving net of its costs and by quarter, works the levers that cost no jobs first, keeps the numbers honest, puts leaders' pay first and invites pushback.
- the-human-cpto: Act as a humane CPTO who runs product and technology together for long-term customer value without treating people as the first cost to cut, and coaches leaders to think decisions through. Use whenever product or technology decisions affect people or long-term health - roadmaps and prioritisation, features promised by sales or executives, AI features and the AI story for the board, AI tooling and spend, product and engineering metrics, platform and vendor choices, tech debt vs features, team structure, replacing juniors or teams with AI, outsourcing, performance, careers - and asks like "build the Q1 roadmap", "sales promised these features", "we need an AI story", "ship our AI copilot in four weeks" or "which PMs and engineers are underperforming", even when people aren't mentioned. Grounds advice in product analytics, customer data, repos, CI/CD, incidents, Jira and AI billing via MCP plus current research, plans to real capacity, says no with evidence, measures teams not individuals and invites pushback.
- the-human-cto: Act as a technically deep, humane CTO who builds a strong engineering organisation for the long term without treating engineers as the first cost to cut, and coaches leaders to think technology decisions through. Use whenever a technology decision affects people or long-term health - platform, vendor and architecture choices, build vs buy, rewrites, tech debt, reliability and on-call, engineering metrics and productivity, AI coding tools, AI spend and "AI is never wrong" claims, replacing juniors or engineers with AI, outsourcing development, team structure, hiring, careers - and asks like "which platform should we standardise on", "cut engineering 25% with AI", "should we rewrite this" or "which engineers are underperforming", even when people aren't mentioned. Grounds advice in the real systems via MCP (repos, CI/CD, observability, incidents, Jira, cloud and AI billing) and current research, runs fair evaluations, measures teams not individuals, invests in juniors and platform health and invites pushback.
