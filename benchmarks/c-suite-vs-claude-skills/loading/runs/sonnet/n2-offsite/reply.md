# Leadership Offsite, Lisbon: Two-Day Agenda

I had no details on team size, goals or venue, so this is a flexible template. Swap in your own priorities where marked. Times assume a 09:00 start and an early-evening finish on day two.

## Before you go
- Pick 2-3 outcomes the offsite must deliver (for example: agreed priorities for next year, a clearer way of working together, and 3-5 owned decisions).
- Send a short pre-read and ask everyone for one thing that is working and one that is not.
- Book a room with daylight and wall space, and leave breaks unscheduled where possible.

## Day 1: Align and Decide What Matters

| Time | Session | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00-09:30 | Welcome, goals, ground rules | Set outcomes for the two days, agree how decisions will be made, phones away |
| 09:30-10:30 | Where we stand | Honest review of the past year: results, customers, team health, what we learned (data-led, with the hard parts included) |
| 10:30-10:45 | Break | |
| 10:45-12:15 | Strategy: where to focus | Review the market and our strengths. Choose the few bets for next year and name what we will stop doing |
| 12:15-13:30 | Lunch (off-site, local) | Unstructured time |
| 13:30-15:00 | Deep dive 1: priority bets | Small groups per bet: goal, owner, capacity needed, risks. Plans must fit real team capacity |
| 15:00-15:15 | Break | |
| 15:15-16:30 | Deep dive 2: tradeoffs | Share group proposals, challenge each other, resolve conflicts over people, budget and sequencing |
| 16:30-17:15 | Day 1 wrap-up | Capture decisions, open questions and parking-lot items |
| 19:30 | Group dinner | Relaxed, no work agenda. Pick a restaurant in Alfama or Bairro Alto |

## Day 2: Commit and Build How We Work

| Time | Session | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00-09:30 | Reflection | What stood out from Day 1? What do we need to revisit? |
| 09:30-11:00 | How we lead together | Team effectiveness: trust, decision rights, meeting rhythm, handling disagreement. Use a short exercise, not a lecture |
| 11:00-11:15 | Break | |
| 11:15-12:30 | People and culture | Hiring, development, workload and retention. What do our teams need from us? |
| 12:30-13:45 | Lunch and walk | Time outdoors, for example along the river at Belem |
| 13:45-15:15 | Roadmap and ownership | Turn decisions into a 90-day plan: owners, milestones, success measures, first steps |
| 15:15-15:30 | Break | |
| 15:30-16:30 | Communicating back | Agree what we tell the wider company, who tells it and when |
| 16:30-17:15 | Commitments and close | Each leader states their commitments. Quick feedback on the offsite. Schedule the follow-up check-in (30 days) |

## Practical notes
- **Facilitation:** Consider an outside facilitator, or rotate session leads so the most senior person is not always steering.
- **Decision log:** Assign a note-taker to record decisions, owners and dates. Circulate it within 48 hours.
- **Pace:** Keep evenings light. Lisbon is a great setting for the informal bonding that makes the work sessions more productive.
- **Logistics:** Book the venue and dinner early, and check for dietary needs and travel times.
