**If a gap remains,** in this order:
1. Redeploy and reskill people toward the work that stays.
2. If the dip is temporary, reduced hours or a short-time work scheme (where one exists) keeps skills for the recovery.
3. Voluntary exits.
4. Involuntary reductions, once, decently, with a fair package and support from the day of notice.
