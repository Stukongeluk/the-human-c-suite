**Sensitivities (year 1 net):** severance at 20% / 30% / 50% of loaded cost gives about €1.2m / €0.8m / €0.2m. Backfill at 20% instead of 10% takes roughly €0.25m off. Severance at 50% with 20% backfill turns year 1 negative (-€0.1m).

**Break-even:** at an assumed 40% gross margin, about €2.1m of lost revenue wipes out year 1, and about €6.4m wipes out year 2. For a team that touches customers, that is a small number of accounts.

## What the board pack should also show

- **The problem this fixes.** If it is a covenant, margin or runway issue, the pack needs the date it must be fixed by. Nothing lands before Q3, and cash is lower than today through Q2.
- **Alternatives costed on the same basis**, including doing nothing. Work through them in this order: cash and working capital, vendor and cloud spend, pricing and revenue leakage, financing talks, stopping low-value work, a hiring freeze and natural attrition, redeployment, then voluntary exits. Leaders' bonuses and pay go before frontline jobs. I cannot say how much these close without ledger data, so I would not let the pack count any of them as closing the gap until each has a sized, labelled estimate.
- **Involuntary exits as the last option**, with that reasoning written down and one round only. Repeated small rounds cost more in attrition than they save.
- **Honest labelling.** If restructuring recurs, it is not a "one-off". Reconcile any adjusted figure to the reported one.
- **People measures** next to the money: regretted attrition, time to fill, customer retention, reviewed quarterly.

## Things that change the numbers (please check with counsel)

- **Law and timing.** Consultation, notice and who may be selected differ by country. In the Netherlands, 20 or more dismissals within three months in one UWV area triggers collective-dismissal notification, the works council must be asked for advice early, selection follows the age-band reflection rule, and dismissal needs UWV permission or a settlement agreement. Each of these can push savings later than my three-month assumption. In the UK, 20 or more at one establishment triggers collective consultation of 30 days or more. Not legal advice.
- **Selection.** Which roles go may not be your choice, so the real average cost may differ from €85k.

## The trap, and two questions

"Model 40 redundancies" starts from the solution. A headcount number is not a plan, and a saving that comes back as rehiring, lost customers or more leavers was never a saving.

1. What is the target (cash, margin, covenant) and by what date? That decides whether this timing works at all.
2. Which country and entity are the 40 in, and are they spread across teams or concentrated in one? That sets consultation length, severance and who is selected.
