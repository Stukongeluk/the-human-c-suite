# Leadership Offsite, Lisbon: Two-Day Agenda (Draft)

**Assumptions** (change any that don't fit): 8–12 leaders, all in person, one facilitator. The offsite has three goals: agree where the business stands, choose the priorities for the next 12 months, and fix how the leadership team works together. Attendees arrive the evening before and leave after Day 2.

## What you should leave with

1. A shared, honest picture of where the business stands
2. 3–5 company priorities, each with one owner and a definition of success
3. Decisions on the 2–3 open strategic questions you bring
4. 2–3 working agreements for the leadership team
5. A plan for what you tell the rest of the organisation, and when

## Pre-work (send two weeks ahead)

- **Each leader:** a one-page "state of my area" covering what went well, what didn't, the biggest risk, and one thing they need from their peers.
- **Short anonymous team-health survey:** trust, decision-making, meeting quality, clarity of roles.
- **Host/CEO:** at most 3 decisions to make at the offsite, each with a one-page pre-read (the options, what we know, what we'd have to give up).
- **Facilitator:** combines everything into one pre-read pack and sends it 5 days before.

## Day 0: Arrival evening (optional)

Informal welcome dinner with no agenda and no presentations. Book for 20:00. Lisbon eats late, and this gives late arrivals time to get there.

## Day 1: Look back, look out, choose direction

| Time | Session | Format / notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 | Breakfast and coffee | At the venue |
| 09:00 | **Opening** | Why we're here, the outcomes above, ground rules (laptops closed, disagree in the room, what's said here stays here), walk through the agenda. Led by the host. |
| 09:30 | **Check-in** | Each leader gets 3 minutes: their high point and low point of the year, and what they need from these two days. |
| 10:15 | **State of the business** | One shared fact base instead of a slide deck per function: financials, customers, people, delivery. 30 min input, 30 min questions. |
| 11:15 | Break | |
| 11:30 | **Retrospective** | Silent writing first, then cluster: what worked, what didn't, what we should stop. Name 3 lessons to take forward. |
| 13:00 | Lunch | 13:00 is a normal lunch time locally. Take 75 minutes and get outside. |
| 14:15 | **Looking out** | 10-minute inputs from 2–3 leaders or an outside guest (a customer, an industry expert) on market, customers, competitors, technology and regulation. Then discuss: what does this mean for us? |
| 15:15 | Break | |
| 15:30 | **Strategic choices** | Work through decisions 1 and 2 from the pre-read: lay out the options, risks and trade-offs, and what we'd stop doing. Either decide, or agree exactly what's needed to decide and by when. |
| 17:00 | **Day 1 wrap-up** | What we decided, the parking lot, what's still open for tomorrow. Quick energy check. |
| 17:30 | Free time | |
| 19:30 | **Sunset and team dinner** | Drinks at a miradouro (viewpoint), such as Santa Luzia or Senhora do Monte, then dinner in Alfama. Optionally book a fado house with a set menu or a private room. |

## Day 2: Priorities, ownership, how we work together

| Time | Session | Format / notes |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 | Breakfast | |
| 09:00 | **Recap and reflections** | Quick round: one thing that's been on your mind since yesterday. Close decision 3 if it's still open. |
| 09:30 | **Choosing priorities** | Go from the long list to 3–5 priorities: dot-vote, then argue about the cut line. For each one, agree what success looks like in 12 months and who owns it. Write down what you are explicitly *not* doing. |
| 11:00 | Break | |
| 11:15 | **Capacity and trade-offs** | Do the priorities fit the people, budget and time you actually have? Where do teams' goals conflict? Co-led by the host and the finance lead. Adjust the list if it doesn't fit. |
| 12:45 | Lunch | If the venue is near Belém, walk along the river and stop at Pastéis de Belém. |
| 14:00 | **How we work as a leadership team** | Go through the survey results. Agree 2–3 working agreements, e.g. decision rights, meeting cadence, how we escalate, how we disagree and then commit. |
| 15:00 | Break | |
| 15:15 | **Commitments and communication** | Each owner presents the first 90 days for their priority. Agree the message to the organisation: what we say, who says it, by when. |
| 16:15 | **Close** | Round of two sentences each: one commitment I'm making, and one thing I appreciated in a colleague. Two-minute written feedback on the offsite. |
| 16:45 | End | Lisbon airport is about 20–30 minutes from the centre by taxi or metro, so evening flights work. |

## After the offsite

- **Within 48 hours:** the facilitator sends a summary of decisions, priorities, owners and working agreements.
- **Within 1 week:** each leader cascades the agreed message to their teams.
- **At 30 and 90 days:** a check-in on priorities and working agreements in your regular leadership meeting.

## Lisbon logistics

- **Time zone:** Lisbon is one hour behind Central European Time, so a 09:00 start feels like 10:00 for anyone flying in from Amsterdam, Berlin or Paris.
- **Meal times:** lunch is around 13:00 and dinner from 20:00. Book group dinners well ahead, especially in Alfama and Bairro Alto.
- **Getting around:** the city is hilly and cobbled. Tell people to bring comfortable shoes. Use taxis or ride-hailing for the group. Tram 28 is charming but crowded.
- **Venue:** you need daylight, room for the whole group plus a breakout space, walls or flip charts, and reliable wifi. For fewer distractions, consider Cascais or Sintra (about 30–45 minutes out). The trade-off is a longer trip to dinner in the city.
- **Season:** summer can go above 30°C, and winter is mild but often wet. Have an indoor backup for anything planned outside.
- **Facilitation:** if the CEO wants to take part rather than run the room, use a neutral facilitator.

## Easy swaps

- **New or recently changed team:** add a 45-minute "how I work best" exercise on Day 1 morning and shorten the retrospective.
- **Tension in the team:** move the team-health session to Day 1 afternoon, before the strategic choices.
- **More customer focus:** replace the Day 1 "looking out" inputs with a customer panel or a customer visit.
- **Lighter on content:** turn the Day 2 lunch into a longer team activity, e.g. a walk through Belém or a food tour.
