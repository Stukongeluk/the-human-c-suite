# Support transition: people plan (draft for leadership and employee representatives)

Scope: first-line support, Stockholm (10 people) and Helsinki (8). Today 18 people; target 6 within 12 months, a reduction of 12.
Status: draft for discussion. Nothing in it is decided or announced. Not legal advice; Swedish and Finnish counsel and HR must review before anyone relies on it (rules checked November 2026).

## 1. Purpose and the principle behind the plan

AI now resolves most first-line tickets. The aim is a support organisation that keeps customers well served at lower cost. We treat "6 people" as a hypothesis the data must confirm, not a promise made in advance. Every step down is released by a quality gate (section 3), and a step that fails its gate waits. If the gates are met, we reach 6 by the end of month 12. If they are not, we stop where customers are still well served and say so openly.

## 2. What we need to know first (months 0-2)

We do not yet have the evidence to fix the number. Before anything is announced:

| Question | Measure | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| How much does the AI really resolve? | Share of tickets closed with no reopen within 7 days and a customer-confirmed outcome (not "deflected") | Head of Support with Product |
| Is quality equal or better? | CSAT, reopen rate and complaint rate, AI-resolved vs human-resolved, by language and ticket type | Support analytics |
| What stays with humans? | Volume and handling time of escalations, by language (Swedish, Finnish, English), by hour of day | Head of Support |
| What do 6 people have to cover? | Contacts per year x handling time / productive hours per person, plus coverage hours, absence and holiday cover, and language cover | Head of Support, Finance |
| Where is the volume heading? | Ticket trend and the share of second-line work as AI absorbs the easy cases | Product analytics |
| What does the AI cost per resolved ticket? | Model, platform and oversight cost, in full | Finance |
| Who are our people? | Skills, languages, interests, constraints, tenure. Collected in conversation, used for placement, never for ranking | Line managers, HR |

If the capacity calculation gives a number above 6, the plan target changes to match. A coverage model that only works with overtime is not accepted.

## 3. Phases and gates

| Phase | Months | Headcount (up to) | What happens | Gate to move on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Understand | 0-2 | 18 | Measurements above; open the conversation with employee representatives and unions with the real numbers; legal review in both countries | Representatives have seen the problem and the options; capacity model agreed |
| 1. Absorb | 2-5 | about 16 to 14 | Hiring freeze on support; natural attrition not replaced; voluntary options opened; redeployment pilots begin; leaders' own cuts agreed | AI resolution quality holds for 2 consecutive months: CSAT and reopen rate not worse than before AI |
| 2. Redeploy | 5-8 | about 12 to 10 | People move into new roles (section 4); voluntary exits with a package; remaining team works the new model alongside the AI | Escalation workload per remaining person within the agreed limit; pulse and absence not worse |
| 3. Settle | 8-12 | up to 6 | Final step to the target size, only if gates are green. Involuntary reductions are considered only under section 6 | Quality, workload and people indicators green for 2 months |

The headcounts are ceilings, not targets for each phase. Attrition, redeployment and voluntary exits decide the actual pace. Moving to a smaller team before the AI's quality is proven is the failure to avoid: Klarna said in 2024 that its AI did the work of 700 agents, then in 2025 said quality had suffered and began hiring human agents again.

## 4. What the remaining and new work looks like

Not everyone needs to leave support, and not all 12 reductions need to leave the company. Roles that exist or will exist as the AI takes over first-line work:

- Second-line and complex-case specialists (the work AI escalates, usually harder and more valuable)
- AI quality and resolution designers: review AI answers, find failures, write and fix the knowledge base, maintain the test set
- Customer success, onboarding and proactive outreach: the work customers want and nobody had capacity for
- Product feedback: turning ticket patterns into product fixes with the product team
- Roles elsewhere in the company, ranked by skills fit: people are offered internal vacancies first

IKEA's Ingka followed the same pattern: its AI assistant handled 47% of customer queries over two years, and it retrained about 8,500 call-centre staff as remote interior design advisers instead of cutting them.

Each person gets a career conversation in phase 0 or 1, a named placement owner and paid learning time during working hours, with mentoring and shadowing. Learning is not done in evenings or weekends.

## 5. The order we work in

Involuntary reductions are the last step. In order:

1. Stop paying for what does not help: unused tools, seats, overlapping AI vendors
2. Stop or simplify low-value work in support
3. Hiring freeze and natural attrition. We will check the real attrition rate before counting on it
4. Redeploy and reskill, with paid time
5. Leaders go first: the leaders who own this plan take the first and largest cut in pay or bonus, as a signal of ownership
6. Voluntary exits, with a package better than the statutory minimum
7. Only then, involuntary reductions, once, under section 6

## 6. If involuntary reductions are still needed

Before proposing it, we document: the size of the gap and its deadline; each earlier step, what it saved and why it is not enough; that leadership cuts came first; that voluntary options were offered; that internal vacancies were offered first to affected people; and that the work itself is being cut, not only the people doing it.

If it goes ahead, it happens once, not in rounds, and by the country rules below. Selection follows the law, not a manager's ranking, and never uses performance scores from activity metrics.

### Sweden (Stockholm, 10 people)

- Negotiate with the unions under the Co-determination Act (MBL) before the decision, at the problem stage.
- Selection under the Employment Protection Act (LAS) is by length of service, one order per operational unit and collective agreement area, unless a collective agreement or an agreed list says otherwise. Each employer may exempt three employees from the order. Managers cannot just pick who stays, so a team that keeps skills and languages needs an early view on how the exemptions and any agreed lists apply.
- Notification to the Public Employment Service (Arbetsförmedlingen) in advance: at least two months ahead for 5 to 25 people.
- Notice periods run one to six months by length of service. People dismissed for lack of work have priority for re-employment for nine months (if employed more than 12 months in the last three years).
- Job security council support (for example TRR for white-collar private-sector staff, if our collective agreement covers it) from the day of notice. If it does not, the company funds an equivalent.

### Finland (Helsinki, 8 people)

- Co-operation negotiations under the Co-operation Act. The full act applies from 50 employees; below that, negotiations are required only if measures affect at least 20 employees within 90 days. HR confirms which applies, including the group's total Finnish headcount.
- Change security: employers with 30 or more employees must offer training or coaching, worth at least one month's salary, to people dismissed for economic reasons after five years' service.
- Re-hiring duty: employers with 50 or more employees must offer similar work to people they dismissed for four months (six after 12 years' service).

### Across both countries

Where rules differ, we apply the stricter process to both locations. We do not play the two sites against each other: the split between Stockholm and Helsinki follows the capacity model, language needs and each country's legal order, not which site is cheaper to cut. The first-year saving will be well below the annual figure, because consultation, notice periods and packages come before the savings do.

## 7. Package for anyone whose role ends

- Severance above the statutory amount and paid notice, so job hunting does not cost income
- Coaching, job search and paid training from the day of notice, with a budget per person (at least one month's salary equivalent in both countries)
- CV and LinkedIn review, written references and a promise of a positive reference call
- Priority for rehiring and an alumni network
- Confidential psychological and financial support
- Keep the laptop or phone where possible, after wiping company data
- Extra care for people close to retirement, on leave, or with a work permit tied to the job

## 8. Communication

- Employee representatives and unions hear first and with real numbers, in phase 0, while they can still change the plan. Their objections count as free risk analysis; the plan changes when they find a better route.
- Every person hears from their own manager in person, with HR where appropriate, before it becomes rumour. Never by mass email, never by a login that stops working.
- Team meeting in each city explaining: the problem, the numbers, what is decided and what is open, what we are doing for people, and the dates of the next steps.
- A named owner and a Q&A place for each group. Monthly update to everyone with the gate indicators, including when they are not green.
- Anyone can say "this plan is wrong" without it being held against them.

## 9. Customers and compliance

- Customers are told when they are talking to an AI (EU AI Act, Article 50, applicable since August 2026), and a route to a human stays available. Offering the human route is good practice even where the law does not require it.
- The company remains liable for what its AI tells customers (Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024).
- AI tools that monitor or score individual employees (for example automatic QA scoring of agents) need employee representatives involved before selection. We report on teams and outcomes, not on individuals. AI used for task allocation or worker evaluation is high-risk under the AI Act; the duty to inform workers before using it applies from December 2027, and national information and consultation duties apply now.
- Data protection review of the support data used to train or tune the AI.

## 10. Indicators and review

Reviewed monthly by leadership and representatives, with the numbers shared with the whole team:

- Customers: CSAT, reopen rate, complaint rate, first-response and resolution time, human-route usage; AI vs human split
- Delivery: escalation backlog, tickets per remaining person, overtime hours (target: none)
- People: pulse survey, absence, regretted exits, redeployment placements and satisfaction
- Money: full cost of the AI plus the team, against the baseline

A gate is only passed when the customer indicators are at least as good as before the change.

## 11. Key assumptions and what would change the plan

- The AI's measured resolution quality holds across Swedish, Finnish and English and across ticket types. If it fails in one language, that team stays larger.
- Escalated work will not grow so much that 6 people cannot cover it with decent hours and leave cover.
- Attrition and voluntary exits cover a meaningful part of the 12. If they cover very little, the timeline stretches or the target rises.
- Local rules and collective agreements are as summarised here. They need checking.

Open questions: Which collective agreements apply in each country? How many people does the company employ in Sweden and in Finland in total (it changes which consultation and rehiring rules apply)? What is the real attrition rate?
