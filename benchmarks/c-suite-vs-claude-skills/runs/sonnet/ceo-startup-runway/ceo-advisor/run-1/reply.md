## What I think is true

Redundancy order is by seniority within each collective-bargaining unit, so you cannot simply keep your best people. You must also negotiate with the union where one is bound, and notify the employment office. Check this with Swedish employment counsel before you tell anyone a date.

## How to act

1. This week: build a 13-week cash forecast and a 12-month scenario model (base, growth stalls at 2%, growth holds at 4%). You need net burn, cash, revenue and its concentration, and gross margin. Do not take a number into the board room that you cannot defend line by line.
2. This week: call both investors separately before the board does. Say you take the runway seriously and will bring a concrete plan within two weeks. Ask them directly: would you put in a bridge (convertible or extension) to fund the gap? Investors who want a 30% cut are often more open to funding a smaller one.
3. Within two weeks, bring the board a plan with:
   - a hiring freeze and a review of all non-people spend;
   - voluntary levers: founder and executive pay cuts, optional unpaid leave or reduced hours, voluntary severance offers;
   - non-dilutive funding: Vinnova, the Swedish Energy Agency, Almi, EIB-backed or venture debt;
   - the commercial push: pull forward annual contracts, offer prepay discounts, and make sure sales capacity is protected.
4. In parallel, draft a targeted contingency plan covering 10 to 20% of the team, built around what the company needs for the next 12 months, within what Swedish rules allow. Have counsel review it and keep it confidential. Do not announce it.
5. Set the trigger date now, in writing, with the board. Because of notice periods, it needs to be early: roughly end of January to mid February. Example trigger: no signed bridge and no credible Series B lead conversations by that date, or growth below 2% for two straight months, then you execute.
6. Start Series B conversations now, not in spring. Soft-circle existing investors, and talk to two or three new leads. The 4% monthly growth, if it holds, is your best asset: about 60% a year.
7. Talk to your team before rumours do. A short, honest all-hands: the situation, the levers, and the date you will decide. People take uncertainty better than surprise.
