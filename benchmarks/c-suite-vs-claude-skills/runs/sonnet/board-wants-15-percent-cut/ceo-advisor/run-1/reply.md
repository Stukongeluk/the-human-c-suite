## THE PLAN FOR 60

**Selection (what you can and cannot do in the Netherlands)**
- You select roles first, not people: which activities stop, shrink or merge. Choose them by what you are no longer going to do, not by who you rate.
- Within each group of interchangeable roles, the afspiegelingsbeginsel (mirror principle) applies. Dismissals are spread proportionally across age cohorts (15-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+), and within a cohort the shortest-serving go first. You cannot choose "underperformers" in a collective redundancy.
- Cost per head will therefore not match the €73.8k average. Older and longer-serving people cost more in salary and in transition payment. Get tenure and age data from HR before you promise the board a savings number.
- Check contractors and ZZP/freelance spend first, and open vacancies. Both are cheaper to cut than employees and sit outside the OR and UWV process.

**Process and timeline** (indicative; employment counsel must confirm)
1. **Monday, board:** approve the target definition, the envelope and the process. Do not ask them to approve names.
2. **Week of 1 December:** brief the OR in confidence and file the formal request for advice. If 20 or more people are affected within three months, notify the unions and UWV as required for a collective dismissal. Appoint employment counsel before this step.
3. **December to January:** define the roles at risk, build the interchangeable-function groups and negotiate a social plan with the unions. Start non-personnel actions in December.
4. **February:** OR advice received, final decision made. Offer settlement agreements (vaststellingsovereenkomst) first, and use UWV applications for those who don't sign. Tell everyone affected on the same day.
5. **March to June:** notice periods run, exits complete by about 30 June, 2027. The 15% run-rate is in place at the start of Q3.

**Non-personnel actions, starting in December**
- AWS (€7.5M): rightsizing, savings plans or reserved capacity, and a review of the top 10 services by spend. Check contract and commitment dates first.
- SaaS (€3.2M, about €7.6k per employee): licence audit and consolidation. Do this in parallel with the layoffs, since fewer people means fewer seats.
- Marketing (€2.1M), office (€2.8M), other (€1.4M): cut programmes with the weakest pipeline attribution, check lease break dates and sublet options, and freeze discretionary spend.

## YOUR DECISION: three options for Monday

- **Option A, as asked:** 60 redundancies plus about €2.6M non-personnel. Reaches about 14.7% to 16%. Highest one-time cash cost (up to €2.4M) and highest loss of capability and customer-facing capacity.
- **Option B, my recommendation:** 60 fewer people by 30 June, made up of about 35 to 40 redundancies and 20 to 25 non-backfilled leavers. This assumes roughly 10% natural attrition, which gives about 30 leavers by Q3 [A, check your actual rate]. Add a hiring freeze, the same non-personnel programme and a retention pool of €0.3M to €0.5M for 15 to 20 critical people. One-time cost falls to roughly €1.0M to €1.6M, and so does the legal and morale exposure. The cost is that attrition is not selective, so you lose some people you wanted to keep.
- **Option C, break-even only:** about €4.5M of cuts (about 25 to 30 redundancies plus non-personnel). Does not meet the board's 15% but gets to break-even. Use it as your fallback position if the board pushes back on cash.

## WEAKEST POINTS AND RISKS

- **Biggest vulnerability:** the plan assumes the OR and UWV process runs on schedule and that the average-cost figure holds. If the OR challenges the business case, or the cohort mix skews expensive, you could land at 11% to 13% by Q3 and need a second round. A second round is far more damaging than a slightly larger first one. If you cut, size it to hit the target with margin.
- **Regretted attrition:** after a cut, your best people get calls from recruiters in the Utrecht and Amsterdam market. Start retention conversations before the announcement, not after.
- **Customer impact:** if redundancies touch support, customer success or onboarding, expect churn. Ask your customer lead which roles are safe to cut before you finalise the role list.
- **Runway:** 22 months is comfortable today. If you do nothing and revenue stays flat, you cross the 12-month alarm line in about 10 months, around September 2027. Combine the cut with a plan for what happens at that point, including whether to raise.
