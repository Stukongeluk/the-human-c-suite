# Board paper: reaching break-even and a 15% lower cost base by Q3 2027

**Prepared:** 23 November 2026 · **Decision owner:** CEO · **Decision needed from the board:** approve the plan and the gate in section 6, not a headcount number

Leadership owns this result. We are EBITDA-negative, and that is a management outcome, not something the people in the company caused. The first and largest cuts therefore start with the executive team (section 5).

## 1. The problem in one sentence

We lose about €4M a year with 22 months of runway (cash lasts to roughly September 2028). The board asks for opex down 15% (€7.2M of €48M) by Q3 2027. Break-even needs €4.0M (8.3%); the remaining €3.2M is margin and buffer.

The subtotal reaches break-even in the middle of its range and covers 50-83% of the 15% target. The remaining gap is €1.2-3.6M, equal to roughly 16-48 roles at average cost, not 60. The headcount figure is the output of this table, not an input.

## 4. Recommendation

1. **Now to week 8:** execute the non-people levers above, start the hiring freeze, make the leadership pay cut, and size pricing and stopped work.
2. **Week 8 gate (late January 2027):** compare delivered and contracted savings with the target. Only the remaining gap, in euros, can justify involuntary exits.
3. **If a gap remains,** open a voluntary scheme first, then run a single, well-prepared reduction through the legal process in section 7. One round, not several.

What this looks like to each seat: Finance gets break-even without waiting for a restructuring cycle. Customers see no service dip. Engineering keeps the people who run the platform. People avoid a reduction that may not be needed.

## 5. Leaders go first

Executive fixed pay -20% and bonuses suspended until EBITDA is break-even, announced before any other measure. Board fees reviewed in the same way. This is a signal of ownership, not a savings plan.

## 6. Timing and cash, the honest version

- If exits happened mid-2027, 2027 in-year saving would be about half the run-rate (about €2.2M) against a €1.5-2.6M one-off cost, so 2027 cash is about neutral and the benefit arrives in 2028.
- Payback after exits: about 4-7 months. During the process the burn continues; cash of about €7.3M falls toward €4-6M before it recovers.
- Statutory timing alone puts exits after Q2 2027 (works council advice, UWV notification, notice periods). To reach a run-rate by Q3 the process would have to start in December, before the board has seen whether cheaper savings work. That is why the gate in section 4 is set at week 8 and the works council is informed at the same time (section 7).

## 7. If involuntary reductions are needed: how we would do it

This is not legal advice. Employment counsel and HR must confirm every step before anything is decided.

**Process (Netherlands)**
- The works council has a right to advise on a reorganisation (art. 25 WOR). Inform it now, with these numbers and these options, while it can still change the plan. Ask for its advice before any decision is final.
- Dismissing 20 or more people within three months in one UWV work area requires notifying UWV and the unions and consulting them (WMCO). Settlement agreements we initiate count towards the 20.
- Dismissal for business reasons generally needs UWV permission or a settlement agreement, and people are generally entitled to a transition payment.
- We do not choose individuals. Under the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel), within each group of interchangeable roles at a location, people are sorted into five age bands and the shortest-serving in each band go first. Agency staff, contractors and freelancers go before it applies; it does not apply where a unique role or a whole group of roles disappears. So the plan is about which work stops, and the legal rule then determines who. No ranking by velocity, tickets or activity.

**Package, above the statutory minimum**
- Paid notice and severance above the transition payment (budget in section 3), with a budget per person for coaching, outplacement and paid training from the first day of notice, not after the last day.
- Keep laptop and phone after wiping, continued references, rehire priority, access to confidential psychological and financial support.
- Extra care for people for whom job loss costs more: those on a work permit, close to retirement, or on leave.

**The day, and after**
- Each person hears it from their own manager, with HR, in person or live; written summary the same day; access changes only after the conversation; never a mass email, never a Friday afternoon or the days before a holiday.
- Everyone else is told the same day, including that the round is final, but only if that is true.
- Stop the work that goes with the roles, so survivors do not carry the same load with fewer people. 1:1s within two weeks; track absence, regretted exits and engagement for 6-12 months.

## 8. Human impact

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who is affected? | Up to 60 of 420 (14%), composition set by the legal selection rule; likely to fall more on recent hires and younger staff within each role group |
| What do they lose? | Income, health continuity, in some cases work permit status, community |
| What do survivors experience? | More work, uncertainty, loss of trust. Research (Trevor and Nyberg, 2008) links each 1% of downsizing to a 31% rise in voluntary turnover the following year; do not extrapolate it linearly to 14%, but plan for the best people leaving |
| Customers? | Support, onboarding and platform reliability are the first places a cut shows |
| How will we know it is going badly? | Regretted attrition above baseline, absence, incidents, customer churn; agreed thresholds trigger a review |

## 9. Assumptions, and what would change this recommendation

- Assumption: revenue stays flat at about €44M. If revenue falls, the gap grows and the gate may open earlier.
- Assumption: the savings percentages in section 3 are achievable. If the week-8 check shows less than €2.5M delivered or contracted, the gap rises toward 48 roles.
- Assumption: cash is about €7.3M and there is no covenant or investor condition that sets a date. If a covenant or an investor milestone sits earlier than Q3, the schedule moves forward and I would say so to the board.
- Would change my mind: evidence that the product is losing customers for reasons cost cuts would worsen, or a funding term that rules out waiting for the gate.
