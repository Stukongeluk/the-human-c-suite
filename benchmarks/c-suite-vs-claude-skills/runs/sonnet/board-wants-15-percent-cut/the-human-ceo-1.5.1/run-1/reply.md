I would not put "60 people" in front of the board on Monday. I'd put a plan that starts with €3.6-6.0M of savings outside involuntary exits and sets a decision point in late January for any remaining gap. The draft board paper is in `board-paper.md` (same folder).

**Why 60 is the wrong number to lead with**

- **60 people is not 15%.** At your average of about €74k fully loaded (€31M / 420), 60 people is about €4.4M a year. That is 9.2% of opex and 61% of the €7.2M target. You would still be short.
- **Break-even needs €4.0M, not €7.2M.** That is 8.3% of opex. The extra €3.2M is buffer and margin, and the board should say whether that is what it wants.
- **Layoffs are slow and expensive in the Netherlands.** My estimate of the one-off cost is €1.5-2.6M. Works council advice, UWV and notice periods push exits to about Q2 2027, so 2027 cash is roughly neutral and the benefit lands in 2028. With about €7.3M of cash (my estimate from your runway), that matters.
- **Survivors leave too.** One study links each 1% of downsizing to a 31% rise in voluntary turnover the next year. Don't extrapolate it to 14%, but plan for losing people you wanted to keep.

**What I'd propose instead (all estimates, to be validated in 8 weeks)**

| Lever | Run-rate saving |
|---|---|
| AWS (15-25%) | €1.1-1.9M |
| SaaS licences (15-25%) | €0.5-0.8M |
| Office, marketing, other (10-20%) | €0.6-1.2M |
| Hiring freeze, no backfill on 15-25 leavers | €1.1-1.8M |
| Executive pay -20%, no bonuses | about €0.3M |
| **Total** | **€3.6-6.0M** |

This reaches break-even in the middle of that range. The remaining gap to 15% is €1.2-3.6M, which is roughly 16-48 roles, not 60. The headcount is the result of the table, not the starting point. Pricing (about €0.4M per 1%) and stopping low-value work are not yet sized.

**For Monday**

1. Leadership owns the result and goes first: executive fixed pay down 20% and bonuses suspended, announced before anything else.
2. Approve the non-people levers and the hiring freeze now.
3. Set a gate in late January. Only the euro gap left then can justify involuntary exits. If it does, open a voluntary scheme first, then run one reduction.
4. Bring the works council in now, with these numbers. Its advice (art. 25 WOR) must come before the decision is final, and 20 or more dismissals in a UWV work area also trigger the WMCO notification. If you wait for the gate, you cannot make Q3 by this route. Say that to the board plainly.

**If cuts go ahead:** the paper covers the package, the day itself, support for the people who stay, and the legal selection rule. Under the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel) you cannot pick names. You decide which work stops, and the rule decides who. That means no ranking by velocity or activity, and the cut tends to fall on recent hires within each role group. Have employment counsel confirm every step. This is not legal advice.
