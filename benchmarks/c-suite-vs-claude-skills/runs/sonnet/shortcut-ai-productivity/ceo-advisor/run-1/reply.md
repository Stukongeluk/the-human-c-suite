**Bottom Line:** Don't chase "40%" firm-wide by Q2. Chase 20-40% on 2-3 specific workflows, measured, and let it spread. 🟡

**The three moves**

- **1. Automate the paperwork-heavy workflows first (weeks 1-6).** 🟡
  - Target: submission/application intake and data entry, quote comparison and proposal drafting, renewal prep, certificates and endorsements, routine client emails.
  - Use AI to draft and extract; a licensed person reviews before anything goes to a client or carrier.
  - Pick the 2-3 workflows with the highest hours and volume. Don't start with a firm-wide rollout.

- **2. Baseline and measure now, before you buy anything (week 1).** 🟢
  - Capture today's numbers: turnaround time per submission, accounts or policies per producer or account manager, renewal retention, hours per task.
  - Without this you can't tell whether you hit 40% or just feel busier. It also tells you whether your competitors' claim is real.
  - Ask how they measured it. "40%" often means time saved on one task, not output per person.

- **3. Decide in advance where the freed time goes, and put owners on it (weeks 4-12).** 🟡
  - Redeploy time into more accounts per person, faster quotes, and cross-sell. Don't announce headcount cuts; that kills adoption.
  - Name one owner per workflow and a champion on each team. Track weekly usage and review quality.
  - Set guardrails first: client-data handling, E&O exposure, carrier and regulator rules. Get your compliance sign-off in week 1, not week 10.

**Honest expectations**
- 🟢 Real gains in targeted workflows by Q2 are plausible.
- 🔴 40% across all 400 people by Q2 is unlikely. Treat it as a competitor's marketing number until you see their method.

**Your decision:** Approve the three workflows and one accountable owner this week, and have the baseline in hand before spending on tools. I'd stake the plan on that, not on the 40% target.
