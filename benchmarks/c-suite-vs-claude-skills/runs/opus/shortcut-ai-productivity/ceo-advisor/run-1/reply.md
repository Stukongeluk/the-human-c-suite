**Bottom line:** By the end of Q2 you can realistically get 30-40% faster on three high-volume workflows. You won't get 40% across the whole firm by then, so don't promise that. *(medium confidence)*

**Why it matters:** If you promise 40% firm-wide and can't measure it, you either miss the target or it turns into a headcount target. Both kill adoption.

**Reality check on the 40%**
- Your competitors' "40%" is most likely one workflow or a self-report, not output across the whole firm. Ask them what they measured. Self-reports can be badly off: in METR's 2025 study, developers felt 20% faster but were measured 19% slower. *(competitor claim unverified; METR study verified, though it covered software work with early-2025 tools)*
- In the best field study (5,179 support agents), AI raised productivity 14% on average. Novices gained 34%; experienced staff gained almost nothing. *(verified)*
- In a study of BCG consultants, those using AI were 25% faster on tasks AI is good at. On tasks it's bad at, they were 19 points less likely to get the right answer. *(verified)*
- A realistic Q2 result is 30-40% time saved on 3 targeted workflows. That frees roughly 5-15% of capacity in those teams. *(my estimate; it depends on your baseline)*

**The three moves**
1. **Pick 3 workflows and measure them now.** Choose the highest-volume, most paper-heavy ones, e.g. renewal prep and quote comparisons, submissions and data entry into your broker system, checking issued policies against the binder, certificates and endorsement requests. Record hours, turnaround time and error rate per item. *Owner: COO. By Fri 18 Dec.*
2. **Buy tools rather than building them, and use them only on those 3 workflows.** That means an enterprise AI assistant whose contract keeps your client data out of model training, plus any AI add-ons in your broker system you may already pay for. Start with one pilot team per workflow and a named champion in each. A person signs off on anything that goes to a client or carrier. *Owner: Head of IT + Compliance. Pilots live by Fri 29 Jan; all teams on those workflows by 31 Mar.*
3. **Redesign the work, and say what the freed time is for.** Rewrite each process around the tool: who checks what, and what you stop doing. Put the freed hours into renewals, cross-sell and new business. Say plainly that this program won't cut jobs and that normal attrition absorbs any extra capacity, because people won't report time savings that cost them their jobs. *Owner: you + division heads + HR. Monthly scorecard against the baseline; Q2 review 30 Jun.*

**Risks**
- **Cutting heads before the gains are proven:** staff hide their savings, service backlogs grow and clients leave.
- **Unchecked AI wording in a proposal or coverage summary:** that becomes an E&O claim (errors and omissions, your professional liability cover). Talk to your E&O insurer before go-live.
- **Client data pasted into free consumer AI tools:** that's a data-protection breach. Block those tools and give staff the enterprise one.

**Your decision**
- **A:** Announce "40% firm-wide by Q2." It's a strong headline, but you'll likely miss it and teams will game the number.
- **B:** Commit to "40% faster on 3 named workflows by Q2" and report the firm-wide number as measured. It's a smaller headline, but credible, and it gives you a real number to compare with your competitors' claims.
- **My recommendation: B.**
