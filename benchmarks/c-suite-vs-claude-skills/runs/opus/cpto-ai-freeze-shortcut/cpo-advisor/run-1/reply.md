📊 CPO — Responding to the competitor's AI assistant

**BOTTOM LINE**
Not as framed. Build it, but start with 2 of the 8 teams, keep a tech-debt floor instead of a freeze, and add teams when customer evidence says so. Confidence: 🟡 medium on the direction, 🔴 low on the exact numbers. I haven't seen your retention, churn or the competitor's product.

**WHAT**
- A competitor's launch shows their roadmap, not your customers' needs. Nobody has checked yet what job your users would hire an assistant for. 🔴 assumed
- An assistant is a feature, not a moat. Competitors can copy it. Yours only wins if it's built on what they can't copy: your customers' data and workflows. 🟡
- Eight teams on one new product turns coordination into the main job, and it leaves your core product idle for six months. More teams won't make it ship sooner. 🟡
- An assistant leans hard on data access, APIs, auth and monitoring. If you freeze that debt, you ship onto a platform that's getting weaker. That debt sits on the assistant's critical path. 🟡

**WHY THIS MATTERS**
Waiting means losing share, so move now. But going all-in with a freeze only looks fast. It puts the core product and the assistant's reliability at risk together.

**HOW TO ACT**
1. Customer interviews (10–15 recent buyers and churners) plus a teardown of the competitor's assistant → Head of Product → 2 weeks
2. Two teams on the assistant: one specialist AI team, plus one product team that owns the user-facing experience → CPO + CTO → week 3
3. The other six stay on core, with ~20% of capacity on tech debt. Debt in the assistant's path goes first → CTO → starting now
4. Ship a narrow v1 to 5–10 pilot customers. At the end of Q1, compare usage and retention for adopters vs non-adopters, and add teams for Q2 if it's working → CPO → end of Q1

⚠️ **RISKS**
- If interviews show customers already leaving for the competitor's assistant, two teams is too slow. Add a third right away instead of waiting for the Q1 checkpoint.
- The team split and the 20% floor are my assumptions and haven't been checked with your CTO. Confirm them before you commit.

🔑 **YOUR DECISION**
- **Option A:** All eight teams, debt frozen. Strongest internal signal, but the most coordination drag, a stalled core and growing platform risk.
- **Option B:** Two teams now, 20% debt floor, scale up at the Q1 checkpoint. The start looks slower, but the bet matches the evidence and the core stays healthy.
- **Recommendation:** B. You get an assistant customers actually use, without betting the whole roadmap on a competitor's launch.
