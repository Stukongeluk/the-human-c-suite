**Bottom line:** Don't buy anything for this yet. A dashboard of each engineer's usage and acceptance rate, used by managers to chase low adopters, measures the wrong thing. In Berlin and Amsterdam you also can't switch it on without your works councils agreeing first. Instead, track adoption per team with the analytics your AI assistant already includes, and ask the low-adoption teams why they aren't using it.

**Why not a per-engineer dashboard**
- **Your works councils have to agree first. Your rollout probably already needs that.** In Germany, the Betriebsrat must agree to any system that is objectively *capable* of monitoring behaviour or performance. What you intend to use it for doesn't matter ([BAG 1 ABR 16/23](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/1-ABR-16-23.pdf)). In the Netherlands, the OR must consent to systems "aimed at or suitable for" monitoring, or it can have the decision declared void ([art. 27(1)(l) WOR](https://www.praxikon.com/nl/posts/or-instemmingsrecht-ai-op-de-werkvloer)). Copilot's metrics API, for example, already reports data per user ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/copilot-usage-metrics/copilot-metrics)), so the assistant itself triggers this. Systematic employee monitoring also needs a DPIA (a formal data-protection impact assessment) ([AP list](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-64418.pdf); the [2026 draft exemptions](https://www.salarisvanmorgen.nl/2026/07/06/consultatie-lijst-dpia-uitzonderingen-verwerking-persoonsgevens-werknemers/) keep this requirement). *[verified; assumed: you have an OR in Amsterdam and a Betriebsrat in Berlin]*
- **Acceptance rate says little about a person.** It mostly counts accepted inline suggestions. Engineers who work through agents or chat, or who reject weak suggestions, score low. Once managers chase the number, people will accept suggestions and then delete them. DX "strongly caution[s] against" using these metrics to judge individual performance ([DX](https://getdx.com/research/measuring-ai-code-assistants-and-agents/)). *[medium]*
- **Using AI more doesn't mean delivering more.** DORA 2024 linked rising AI adoption to an estimated 1.5% drop in delivery throughput and a 7.2% drop in stability ([DORA](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/devops-sre/announcing-the-2024-dora-report)). METR measured experienced developers as 19% slower with AI, while they believed they were 20% faster. Its 2026 follow-up says they're probably faster now, but calls its own evidence "very weak" ([METR](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/), [update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/)). Someone who uses AI less isn't necessarily a weaker engineer. *[verified]*

**What to do instead**
1. **Agree the rules with the OR and the Betriebsrat, and run the DPIA.** Report only at team level, with at least about 5 people per group. Keep the data out of performance reviews and set a retention limit. Only admins see unused seats, for reclaiming licences. Owner: you, HR and the DPO. Do this before the general rollout.
2. **Use the analytics you already pay for, per team.** Track the share of people actively using it, unused seats, and how they use it (inline completion vs. agents). Owner: the platform/DevEx lead. Start in week 1.
3. **At day 30, run a 5-question anonymous survey:** where does it help, where does it fail, what's blocking you? Help the low-adoption teams through internal champions, pairing and time to learn, not through manager follow-ups. Owner: the DevEx lead.
4. **At day 90, compare each team's lead time, change failure rate and PR cycle time with their numbers from before the rollout.** Owner: the engineering managers, with you reviewing.

**Your decision**
- **A: the assistant's built-in analytics plus the survey.** No extra spend and quick to start, but someone has to combine the exports by hand.
- **B: an engineering-insights platform such as Swarmia or DX.** It shows AI adoption (Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code) next to delivery metrics. It costs more and needs the same works-council agreement. Only buy one if you can keep managers at team level, because Swarmia, for example, has a per-member view ([Swarmia](https://help.swarmia.com/features/ai-tools/ai-adoption)).
- **My recommendation:** start with A. If combining the exports by hand gets painful, trial B for 2–4 weeks once the agreement is in place.

This isn't legal advice. Have an employment lawyer in each country check the agreement wording.
