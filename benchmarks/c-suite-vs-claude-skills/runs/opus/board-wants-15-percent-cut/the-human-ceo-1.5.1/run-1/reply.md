My recommendation: don't take a 60-person layoff plan to the board on Monday. Take them a plan that reaches the full €7.2M by Q3 2027 and decides forced redundancies last, once, on verified numbers. On current estimates that's about 16 roles (somewhere between 0 and 38), not 60. On their own, the 60 save about €4.4M a year, only 61% of the target.

**The numbers behind that**

- **60 redundancies:** 60 × your €73.8k average cost per person is €4.4M at run-rate. That leaves €2.8M to find elsewhere, or about 38 more people. After Dutch procedure (works council advice, the collective dismissal notification, one to four months' notice) and severance, 2027 sees only about €1.5M of it.
- **Cloud and tools:** AWS is €7.5M, roughly 17% of your implied €44M revenue. In [Flexera's 2026 survey](https://www.flexera.com/about-us/press-center/flexera-finds-cloud-value-is-rising-while-ai-waste-grows) (March 2026), organisations estimated 29% of their cloud spend is wasted. [Zylo's 2026 index](https://zylo.com/news/2026-saas-management-index) (January 2026) found 36% of SaaS licences unused. Even at 15–25%, AWS and tools give €1.6–2.7M without touching a job.

The 60 is 15% of your headcount rather than the result of a diagnosis. A target without a diagnosis usually ends with cutting what's easiest to count, and here it doesn't even reach the number.

**Before Monday**

- **Leadership goes first.** The gap is the leadership team's responsibility, yours first, and the paper says so in your voice. It proposes −30% for you, −20% for the executive team and no leadership bonuses in 2027. That's only €0.2–0.4M, a signal that earns trust rather than a saving. Change the figures to ones you'll stand behind, but keep the sentence.
- **The legal floor sets the timeline.** At 420 people you have a works council. Its advice (article 25 WOR) must be asked while it can still change the decision, so on Monday any job measure is a proposed decision. Twenty or more exits within three months triggers the collective dismissal procedure (WMCO), and voluntary settlements count towards the 20. And you don't choose who goes: within each group of interchangeable jobs, the [afspiegelingsbeginsel](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/ontslag/afspiegelingsbeginsel) sorts people by age band and length of service. This isn't legal advice, so have employment counsel read the paper before Monday.
- **Headcount still falls, by about 50 by Q3** in the planning case, mostly through roles not refilled and voluntary exits. In the worst case of my estimates it's about 64, with 38 forced. The paper shows that openly.
- **The board's case:** they're right that costs outrun revenue, and that one decisive move beats several small ones. Where I differ is the order: size the irreversible part last, on real numbers, so it only happens once.
