**CPO: Malmö support, selection approach for 30 to 21**

**BOTTOM LINE**
Don't rank people on tickets resolved and CSAT. In Sweden, people are selected for redundancy by length of service unless you agree a different list with the union. Those two metrics also measure the job as it was before the AI. Use the approach below. Confidence: high on the legal frame, which Swedish employment counsel still needs to confirm; medium on the rest.

**WHAT**
- **The default rule (high).** In a redundancy (*arbetsbrist*), the Employment Protection Act (LAS 22 §) ranks people by total length of service. The ranking is done separately in each selection pool (*turordningskrets*). There is one pool per operating unit and collective-agreement area. If two people have equal service, the older one stays. You can pass over a longer-serving person only if they lack sufficient qualifications for the remaining work, allowing a reasonable time to learn it.
- **You can exempt three people (high).** Before ranking, you may exempt up to three employees who are especially important to the business. After that, you can't make another exemption for three months. This choice is rarely open to challenge unless it is discriminatory.
- **Ranking on merit needs union agreement (high).** You can rank on competence only through a list agreed with the union (*avtalsturlista*). That list must not be discriminatory or arbitrary.
- **HR's metrics are the wrong yardstick (medium).** The AI now handles the easy tickets, so pre-AI counts reward the old job, and the people who took the hard cases look worse. Raw counts also penalise part-timers and anyone on parental or sick leave. A dismissal linked to part-time status or parental leave can be declared invalid.
- **Agree the process before naming anyone (high).** In order:
  1. Negotiate with the union under MBL 11 § before you decide.
  2. Give notice (*varsel*) to Arbetsförmedlingen, the public employment service. This applies when 5 or more people in one county may lose their jobs, and it must be filed at least 2 months before the first person's employment ends.
  3. Check in writing for redeployment options across the whole company.
  4. Give those who leave re-employment priority for their notice period plus 9 months, if they worked for you more than 12 months in the last 3 years.

**WHY THIS MATTERS**
Choosing by HR's metrics without union agreement breaches the selection rules. Each affected person can claim damages, and where the choice links to leave or part-time work the dismissal can be overturned. You would also lose the people who handle what the AI can't.

**HOW TO ACT**
1. Before calling the union (Head of Support + CPO): prove that 21 is the right number with 8 to 12 weeks of AI data (escalation and reopen rates, CSAT on AI-handled tickets, the human workload that remains). Define the 21 roles honestly: complex cases, AI quality review, knowledge base.
2. In the same window (HR + Swedish employment counsel): map the selection pools, your collective agreement (it can change these rules) and redeployment options.
3. Before the negotiation (HR): offer voluntary departures on good terms. Each volunteer is one fewer forced exit.
4. At the start (HR): open the MBL negotiation and file the varsel at the same time, with a copy to the unions.
5. After agreement (HR + team leads): give written notice with the correct notice periods, offer transition support (TRR/TSL), and explain re-employment priority in person.

**RISKS**
- If the AI can't keep handling those tickets, you will rehire at a higher cost. Klarna started rehiring human agents in 2025 after its CEO said AI support gave "lower quality".
- If names are shortlisted before the negotiation, the union will treat the outcome as decided in advance.

**YOUR DECISION**
- **Option A:** Length-of-service order plus up to three exemptions. Lowest legal risk and predictable, but you have the least say over who stays.
- **Option B:** Negotiate an agreed list based on criteria for the *future* role, backed by documented evidence that accounts for hours worked and case difficulty. You get more control, but it needs union agreement and takes longer.
- **Recommendation:** Option A. Spend the three exemptions on skills the new role depends on. Move to Option B only if the union agrees and the criteria describe the job that remains, not past ticket counts.

*Assumptions (not verified): one Malmö operating unit, a collective agreement in place, and all 30 on permanent contracts. Have Swedish employment counsel check this before anything reaches staff.*
