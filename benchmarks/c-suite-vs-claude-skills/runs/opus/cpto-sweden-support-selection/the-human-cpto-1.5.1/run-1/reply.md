Don't rank on tickets resolved and CSAT. In Sweden the Employment Protection Act (LAS) sets the order by length of service, you negotiate with the union (MBL) before deciding, and you can exempt at most three people. So: reduce the 9 first, then select by an agreed, written order.

- **Check the AI first:** full resolution (not deflection), repeat contacts and CSAT on AI tickets over 8–12 weeks. Klarna went back to hiring human agents in 2025 after quality slipped.
- **Reduce the 9:** hiring freeze, end agency or temp cover, natural leavers, paid voluntary exits, redeployment into escalations or AI quality work (LAS requires the redeployment check).
- **Union in now,** with the real numbers, while they can still change the plan.
- **Selection:** total length of service per operating unit and agreement area (possibly all of Malmö, not just support); people keep priority only for work they're qualified for.
- **HR's real goal, skills for harder tickets:** use the qualifications test, the three exemptions or a union-agreed list with skill criteria fixed before scoring.
- **Not tickets or CSAT:** once AI takes the easy tickets, counts reflect ticket mix, part-time and leave, not skill, and invite discrimination claims.
- **Timing:** notify the Public Employment Service two months before anyone leaves; notice runs one to six months; nine months' rehiring priority.
- **Support from day of notice:** the job security council (TRR, under a collective agreement) plus company-paid coaching.
