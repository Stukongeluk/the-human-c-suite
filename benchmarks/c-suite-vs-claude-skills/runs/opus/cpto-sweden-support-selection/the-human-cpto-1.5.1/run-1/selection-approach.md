# Malmö Customer Support: selection approach, 30 to 21 roles

*Draft for HR, the Malmö leadership team and union representatives, November 2026. Not legal advice: to be checked by Swedish employment counsel before use. Items in [brackets] need confirming.*

## Summary

AI now handles much of the first-line work, and we expect Malmö support to need 21 roles instead of 30. Before anyone is selected we confirm the need, reduce involuntary exits and negotiate with the union. Any remaining selection follows the Employment Protection Act (LAS) order, or a list agreed with the union with criteria fixed in advance. Individuals are not ranked on tickets resolved or CSAT.

## 1. Confirm the need

- Over 8–12 weeks, compare AI-handled with human-handled contacts: resolution with no human follow-up, repeat contacts within 7 days, CSAT and complaints.
- Size the remaining human work in hours (escalations, complex cases, checking AI answers), including summer holiday and sick cover, without regular overtime. If quality or hours don't support 21, change the number before negotiating.

## 2. Reduce involuntary exits

Each step lowers the 9:

1. Hiring freeze in support and related teams.
2. End agency, consultant and fixed-term cover for work our staff can do.
3. Expected leavers before the effective date [last 12 months' leavers?].
4. Voluntary exits with a package, open to all 30 [terms agreed with the union].
5. Written offers of vacant or new roles across the legal entity, with reasonable training: escalations, AI quality, knowledge base, onboarding, customer success (LAS §7). Ingka (IKEA) retrained about 8,500 call-centre staff as remote design advisers after its AI assistant took 47% of queries.
6. Reduced hours for anyone who wants them.

## 3. Negotiate before deciding

- Primary negotiation with [union] under the Co-determination Act (MBL) before any decision. Share the reasons, the AI data, headcount, numbers affected, timing, the draft order list, the redeployment investigation and a work-environment risk assessment for leavers and stayers.
- Tell the team the problem and options at the same time as the union, not after the decision.

## 4. Selection, only for roles still redundant after step 2

- **Default order (LAS §22):** longer total employment keeps priority; on equal service, the older employee does. One list per operating unit and collective-agreement area, regardless of job title, so it may include Malmö staff outside support [confirm].
- **Qualifications:** priority applies only to remaining work a person is qualified for. Write down what the 21 roles require before drawing up the list.
- **Exemptions:** up to three people of particular importance to the business, named at the MBL negotiation with a written reason each.
- **Agreed list (avtalsturlista), if both sides prefer:** criteria and weights fixed before anyone is assessed, two independent assessors, documented scores, and a check for disadvantage to any protected group before use.
- **Not used:** tickets resolved, CSAT, handle time or other individual activity counts. With AI taking the simpler tickets, they mostly reflect ticket mix, part-time hours and leave, which risks indirect discrimination (Discrimination Act 2008:567; Parental Leave Act 1995:584 §16), and the data was collected to run support, not to decide dismissals (GDPR Art. 5(1)(b)).
- Leave, sickness, union activity and raising concerns are never factors; people on leave are listed on the same terms.

## 5. Notice and timeline

- Written notice to the Public Employment Service (Arbetsförmedlingen) at least two months before the first affected person's employment ends (5–25 people).
- Notice periods of one to six months by length of service (LAS §11); savings start once notice ends.
- Notice in person and in writing, stating rehiring priority: during notice and nine months after, for anyone with more than 12 months' employment in the last three years (LAS §25).

## 6. Support for people who leave

- From the day of notice: the job security council (TRR for white-collar staff under a collective agreement), coaching and paid time to look for work.
- Company package [to agree]: severance above the minimum, references, keeping laptop or phone after a wipe.
- Information on CSN's transition study support (omställningsstudiestöd): about 80% of salary up to a ceiling, for up to about a year of study.

## 7. The 21 who stay

- Decide which work stops or moves to the AI, so 21 people aren't doing the work of 30. Explain the same day; one-to-ones within two weeks.
- Review at three and six months, team level only: CSAT and repeat contacts (AI and human), backlog, overtime, sick leave, voluntary leavers. If quality drops, revisit staffing: Klarna went back to hiring human agents in 2025 after quality suffered.
