These three moves get you a real, provable gain by Q2. Expect roughly 10–20% faster on the workflows you pick, not 40% firm-wide (my estimate).

- **Pick three workflows and baseline them in the next four weeks:** submission intake, renewal and quote comparisons, client emails. Measure team (not individual) hours per file and error rates now, or Q2 proves nothing.
- **Equip the people who do that work, with paid learning time:** a secure enterprise tool your DPO approves, a few hours' training a week, and let them build the prompts and checks. A human still signs off all client advice; bring your works council in before you choose the tool.
- **Decide now where the freed hours go:** client reviews, cross-sell, retention, new business. Say "nobody loses their job over this; roles will change", and people will show you where the time really goes.

The trap: a competitor's 40% with no definition usually turns into buying licences and cutting what's easiest to count.
