Here is the week-by-week timeline, starting the week you say go. Week 1 is early December on your calendar, and end of Q2 lands around week 30. Dates assume the build team confirms its capacity.

| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Confidential briefing for the works council chair and representatives. Name owners for support, the agent and finance. Vendor shortlist and contract terms: pilot-scale, termination right on failed gates, roll-out conditional on the works council process. Support leads begin labelling contacts. |
| 2 | Formal advice request to the works council. Team lead tells the 25 live. Labelling of 500 to 1,000 contacts done. Test set built. Baselines pulled: CSAT, first-contact resolution, cost per contact. |
| 3 | Contract signed (after the council has the request in hand). Agent connected to the knowledge base. Shadow mode starts on all contact types. Staff rate drafts. |
| 4 | Fix knowledge-base gaps found in rating. Data protection assessment and processor agreement finished. First read of automatable share. |
| 5 | Gate 1: accuracy on test set at target, no serious failures. Works council advice due. Disclosure text and one-step human route built. |
| 6 | Go live on the two or three highest-volume routine types. Daily sampled review. Kill switch tested. |
| 7 to 8 | Gate 2 on those types: CSAT and resolution at or above human baseline. First figure for the board: measured share and cost per resolved contact. |
| 9 to 10 | Add contact type 4. |
| 11 to 12 | Add contact type 5. Mid-programme review with council and pulse survey. |
| 13 to 20 | One new type every two weeks, each through its own gate. Fix product issues that cause contacts. Start moving staff to onboarding, customer success and complex cases as capacity frees. |
| 21 to 26 | Remaining types that pass gates. Monthly review of CSAT, cost, pulse and attrition. |
| 27 to 30 | End-of-Q2 report: types live, share resolved by the agent, quality against baseline, cost per contact, team feedback. Capacity decision is prepared, to be taken after a full quarter of live data. |
