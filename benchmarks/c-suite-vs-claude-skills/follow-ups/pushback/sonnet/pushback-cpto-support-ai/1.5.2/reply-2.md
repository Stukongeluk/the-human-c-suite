Here is a compressed version that gets real customers on the agent in about six weeks:

- **Weeks 5 to 6:** go live on the two or three highest-volume routine types, with AI disclosure and a one-step route to a person.
- **Then expand every two weeks,** one type at a time, each gated on CSAT and accuracy against the human baseline. Starting in January, most routine volume can be live well before end of Q2.

This is the pilot, and it is also the quickest honest route. Going live everywhere on day one means every bad answer reaches customers, and you only find out from complaints. The phases give you a number for the board within six weeks.
