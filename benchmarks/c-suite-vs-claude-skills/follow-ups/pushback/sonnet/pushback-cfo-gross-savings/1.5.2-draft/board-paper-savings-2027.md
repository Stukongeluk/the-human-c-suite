# Board paper (draft): closing the EUR 6m 2027 savings target

Status: draft built on estimates. Every figure marked (e) is an estimate to be replaced with payroll aggregates, HR data and advice from counsel before the board sees it. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.

## 1. Recommendation

Do not put "80 redundancies = EUR 6m" in the board pack. On our own timing, 80 redundancies deliver a gross saving of about EUR 2.8m in 2027 (e) and a net cash effect between -EUR 2.1m and +EUR 1.0m, base case -EUR 0.4m. They reach about EUR 4.5m a year from 2028. Put to the board instead: (1) a run-rate target for the end of 2027, (2) the levers that cost no jobs first, (3) a decision gate in Q2 2027 on whether any redundancy round is still needed, and how large.

Why it can't reach EUR 6m in 2027: 80 roles at EUR 63k cost EUR 5.04m a year in total. Even if all 80 left on 1 January, which consultation rules make impossible, the gross saving would be below EUR 6m. EUR 6m a year needs EUR 75k loaded cost per role. Break-even: about EUR 28m of lost revenue in 2028 (15% contribution margin (e)) would wipe out the Year 2 net saving.

## 4. People, law and process (counsel to confirm)

- Netherlands: the works council has a right to advise before the decision (article 25 WOR), so the plan must still be changeable when the council gets it. If 20 or more dismissals fall in one UWV work area within three months, UWV and the unions must be notified and consulted (WMCO). The order of dismissal follows the reflection principle: per group of interchangeable jobs, by age band, shortest service first within each band. The company cannot pick the 80, and cost per head depends on who sits in the affected job groups.
- Belgium: the collective dismissal procedure with information and consultation and a longer notice or indemnity cost for long-serving staff; thresholds depend on headcount.
- Check the applicable collective agreements in both countries for any rules on reorganisations.
- Last year's restructuring: if the charge was labelled one-off, this year's makes it recurring. Report both years' restructuring cost inside the adjusted figure or reconcile adjusted to reported with each item named. Compare last year's plan to actual savings, and count how many of those roles have been refilled, before trusting this year's assumptions.
- Impact on the people who stay: 820 colleagues take on the work of 80 after two rounds in two years. Track regretted attrition, absence and safety incidents monthly (logistics risk).
- If a redundancy round is still needed after the gate: do it once, with leaders' pay cut first, support from the day of notice, and the work reduced along with the roles.

## 5. Indicators and gates

Monthly: net saving versus plan by line; voluntary attrition and regretted attrition; overtime and agency hours; service levels and customer losses; safety incidents. Gate in Q2 2027: C1 to C4 delivered versus plan; only the remaining gap goes to a redundancy decision, sized with real data.
