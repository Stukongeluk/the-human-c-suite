**Why 80 roles don't give €6M in 2027**
- €6M over 80 roles needs €75,000 loaded cost per head, with everyone gone on 1 January and no costs. My estimate is €60k in NL and €68k in BE, which gives €5.0M a year at full run-rate, and only from late 2027.
- Timing: the Dutch works council advice, the UWV notification and permit or settlement route, and the Belgian collective-dismissal consultation all come before notice starts. I put most NL exits at April to June and BE at August to October. That gives about €2.2M gross in 2027.
- Costs arrive first: severance, top-ups and outplacement (€1.5M), advisers (€0.25M), overtime and agency cover because volumes don't shrink with headcount (€0.44M), extra leavers among those who stay (€0.24M) and some rehiring (€0.07M).
- Cumulative net turns positive around February 2028. Even at €75k a head the 2027 net is about zero.

No option reaches €6M in 2027. That's the real finding for the board. A saving of €6M is only reachable as a run-rate by the end of 2027 (B's high case gets close), or as something other than 2027 P&L, such as financing or a covenant reset.

**My recommendation:** start B now. It's reversible and starts in January. Put a gate at the end of Q1 and run a smaller, single round (C) only if B is behind. The steelman for the CEO: if volumes are falling for good, a structural cut is right, and A's €5M run-rate from 2028 is real. But it should be sized to the demand forecast, not to €6M, and the 2027 gap still has to be covered some other way.

**Two questions that would change the numbers**
