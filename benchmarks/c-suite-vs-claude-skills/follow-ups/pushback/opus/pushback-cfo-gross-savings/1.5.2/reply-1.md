**Last year's restructuring changes three things**
- **Honest numbers.** A second restructuring charge in two years isn't one-off. ESMA's guidelines say restructuring costs that affect past and future periods will rarely count as non-recurring. Show any adjusted EBITDA next to the reported figure, and check how your facility agreement treats restructuring add-backs. Don't time the announcement to pull the provision into 2026: that would make 2027 look €3.0M better without changing a euro of cash.
- **Evidence.** Last year's promised versus delivered savings, the roles that came back and turnover among the people who stayed should replace my hidden-cost estimates.
- **People.** A second round tells the people who stay that a third may follow. If any involuntary round happens, do it once, and only say it's the last if that's true.

The trap: a headcount number chosen before the diagnosis usually cuts what's easiest to count and saves less than promised.
