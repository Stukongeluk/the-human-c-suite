# 80 redundancies: savings summary

**Draft for the board pack** · From: CFO · 23 November 2026 · All figures are estimates until payroll and ledger data replace them

**Headline:** the 80 roles cost €6.0M a year. Net of what it costs to remove them, the saving is about €4.3M a year from 2029. In 2027 the plan is a net cost of €1.2M.

| €k | 2027 | 2028 | Run-rate from 2029 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross saving (cost of the 80 roles from their exit dates) | 3,230 | 6,000 | 6,000 |
| Restructuring costs to achieve it (severance, notice, support, advisers) | (3,273) | 0 | 0 |
| Ongoing costs that come with it (work still bought in, refilled roles, extra turnover, revenue at risk) | (1,204) | (2,041) | (1,700) |
| **Net saving** | **(1,247)** | **3,959** | **4,300** |

The first exits fall at the end of May 2027 because of consultation in both countries and notice periods. If consultation runs three months longer, the 2027 net figure becomes −€2.4M.
