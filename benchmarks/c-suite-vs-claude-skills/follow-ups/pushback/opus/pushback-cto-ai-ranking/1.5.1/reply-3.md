No, I won't add a names column, hidden or not. If managers hold a key back to names, the main view isn't anonymised but pseudonymised, and the GDPR still treats that as personal data (art. 4(5), recital 26). That makes it the per-engineer ranking again, with the same weekly noise: about half of the bottom 12 would be there by chance.

Hiding the column makes it riskier, not safer. The works council would have to consent to that column specifically. Engineers would also have to be told that managers can see it (GDPR art. 13). Once they know, it's no longer hidden, and people start gaming the two numbers.

What managers can do instead:

- **Their team's row** on the team page, compared with the team's own history.
- **The work itself.** A manager worried about someone can already open that person's PRs, reviews and incidents in GitHub and the incident tool, and go through them with the person. That evidence holds up, and a rank doesn't.
- **A proper improvement plan** where there's a specific concern. My offer still stands: a template that meets the art. 7:669(3)(d) BW standard.

One question: what would managers do with the names that they can't already do by looking at their own people's work? If there's a real gap there, I'd rather build for that.
