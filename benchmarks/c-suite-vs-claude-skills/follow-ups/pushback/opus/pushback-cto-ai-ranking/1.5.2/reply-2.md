If the goal is to act on real underperformance, this is what works. It's new in this reply: `improvement-plan-template.md`, one page for managers and HR.

- **Start from a named concern:** a specific gap against the role's expectations, with dated examples from the work and feedback from 2–3 peers.
- **Rule out the system first:** on-call load, unclear expectations, being alone on a legacy system, and health (which goes through HR and the company doctor).
- **Write the plan:** 3–5 observable goals from the job profile, named support (a mentor, pairing, training), two to four months, and check-ins every two weeks that the employee can respond to in writing.
- **Close it out:** close the plan, extend it once, or look for another role before anything further.

That gives each case a fair chance and the paper trail it needs.
