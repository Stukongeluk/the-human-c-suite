Don’t argue against layoffs on sentiment—beat the board on math, Dutch labor reality, and 2028 execution risk.

In the Netherlands, cutting ~38 people triggers the WMCO collective dismissal process: Works Council (OR) advice, UWV and union procedures, social plan negotiations, and *transitievergoeding*. You’ll burn cash on severance and legal fees, stall productivity for 6+ months, and jeopardize the two 2028 titles that actually determine the studio’s survival.

Walk into Thursday with a **Runway & Discipline Plan** that gives the board real cost control without gutting delivery:

1. **Take the first hit at the top.** Waive executive bonuses (and consider a 10–15% temporary C-suite pay cut), and freeze company-wide bonuses and non-critical raises for 12 months. That signals discipline immediately.
2. **Freeze hiring and use natural attrition.** Halt non-essential hiring and require CEO/CFO sign-off on backfills. Normal studio attrition (8–12%) will lower headcount organically without severance shocks or morale collapse.
3. **Cut non-payroll burn now.** Wind down the flopped title to minimal maintenance immediately—stop defending a sunk cost. Cut external contractors, discretionary opex, events, and tooling. Bring a line-by-line target (e.g., €4–6M annualized savings) that stretches your €40M comfortably past 2028.
4. **Reallocate ruthlessly and install 90-day stage-gates.** Move strong staff from the flopped project onto the two 2028 games only where it accelerates delivery. Commit to quarterly playable reviews with explicit scope-cut or kill criteria.
5. **Offset burn with non-dilutive cash.** Maximize WBSO R&D tax credits and pursue milestone-based publishing or platform advances (e.g., Game Pass, PS+) to de-risk 2027–2028 cash flow.
6. **Offer a hard trigger.** Boards reject open-ended promises. Propose a two-quarter covenant: if monthly burn exceeds €X or either 2028 title misses its Q2 2027 milestone, leadership will initiate a pre-scoped restructuring.

Frame it simply: **layoffs are theatrical discipline that delays 2028; this plan is real cash discipline backed by a hard safety net.**
