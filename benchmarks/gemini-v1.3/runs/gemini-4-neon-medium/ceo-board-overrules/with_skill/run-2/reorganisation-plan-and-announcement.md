# Reorganisation Plan and Staff Announcements (22 Roles, Amsterdam)

> **Important:** This document is a management plan and communication draft, not legal advice. Dutch employment and collective dismissal law is strict and procedural errors can delay or invalidate dismissals. Have Dutch employment counsel and HR review every step, timeline, and draft before filing notifications or speaking to staff.

## Part 1: Reorganisation Plan

### 1. Context and Scope

- **Company size:** 180 employees (Amsterdam).
- **Proposed reduction:** 22 roles (12.2% of headcount), primarily in Operations and Support.
- **Post-reorganisation headcount:** 158 employees.
- **Status:** **Intended decision (*voorgenomen besluit*)** until statutory consultation with the Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad*, OR) and trade unions is complete.
- **Business rationale:** `[Insert the specific financial gap, cash/margin target, and deadline the board set, plus what happens if it is not closed.]`
- **Cash-flow reality check:** Because of Dutch consultation timelines, statutory notice periods (1–4 months by tenure), and severance/transition costs, first-year net cash savings will be substantially lower than the annualised salary run-rate while upfront costs land first. Model the monthly cash curve with Finance before locking dates.

### 2. Prerequisites Before Any Announcement

Complete these four items before filing or announcing:

1. **Leadership goes first.** Document the reductions to executive pay, bonuses, and perks taking effect immediately, and confirm who on the leadership team is accountable for the misses that brought the company here.
2. **Cut the work, not just the people.** Define explicitly which Operations and Support queues, manual processes, SLAs, products, or reporting lines are stopping. As a regulated fintech, verify with Risk/Compliance that KYC/AML, safeguarding, and incident-response coverage remain intact at 158 people.
3. **Map internal vacancies (*herplaatsingsplicht*).** Freeze external hiring for any role an affected employee could fill (including with reasonable paid reskilling) and prepare the full list of open and upcoming internal positions.
4. **Confirm Dutch legal posture with counsel:**
   - **Works Council (OR):** Mandatory from 50 employees under the Works Councils Act (*Wet op de ondernemingsraden*, WOR). Under Article 25 WOR, the OR has a right to advise on reorganisations *before* a final decision is taken. (If no OR currently exists, counsel must advise immediately on how to proceed and mitigate Enterprise Chamber risk.)
   - **Collective Dismissal (WMCO):** Because 22 roles exceed the threshold of **20 or more employees within three months in one UWV work area**, the Collective Dismissal Notification Act (*Wet melding collectief ontslag*, WMCO) applies — including if exits are handled via employer-initiated settlement agreements (*vaststellingsovereenkomsten*, VSOs). You must notify UWV and the relevant trade unions and consult the unions before the decision is final.

### 4. Fair Selection and Redeployment

- **Objective, lawful criteria:** Group affected roles into interchangeable positions (*uitwisselbare functies*) and apply the statutory **reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*)** across age brackets so the age distribution before and after remains balanced.
- **No shortcuts:** Never select based on individual performance rankings, ticket counts, activity metrics, protected characteristics, parental/sick leave, or having raised concerns.
- **Voluntary exits & redeployment first:** Explore with the OR and unions whether a structured voluntary-exit window (subject to business-criticality rules) and priority internal redeployment can reduce the number of involuntary dismissals.

### 5. Transition Package (Social Plan Framework)

Use Dutch statutory minimums as the floor, not the ceiling, wherever the balance sheet allows:

- **Severance:** Statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding*) plus a negotiated top-up multiplier in the Social Plan/VSO.
- **Notice & paid job-search time:** Full statutory notice period (1–4 months by tenure), with paid exemption from work (garden leave) where feasible so departing colleagues can focus on finding their next role without losing income.
- **Legal review budget:** Fixed contribution (e.g., €`[750–1,500]` excl. VAT) for independent Dutch employment law advice on any settlement agreement.
- **Day-one transition support:** Modelled on Sweden's job security councils (where over 85% of displaced workers find new employment within a year; OECD, *Back to Work: Sweden*, 2015), start outplacement, 1-on-1 career coaching, CV/LinkedIn review, and a **paid training/reskilling budget per person** on the first day of notice, not after the final day.
- **Practical support:**
  - Allow departing colleagues to keep their work laptop or phone where possible (after company data wipe and fintech security sign-off).
  - Written reference letter from leadership and a commitment to positive reference calls.
  - Priority for rehiring (in addition to the statutory 26-week re-employment condition, *wederindiensttredingsvoorwaarde*) and an opt-in alumni network.
  - Confidential psychological and financial counselling.
- **Extra care for high-impact situations:**
  - **Highly skilled migrant (*kennismigrant*) visa holders:** Provide dedicated Dutch immigration-law support and structure notice/garden leave as generously as possible, as visa holders generally have a 3-month search period once employment ends.
  - **Employees close to retirement or on leave:** Review individually with HR and counsel to ensure full statutory protection and tailored transition support.

### 6. Notification Day Protocol

- **Live, human delivery:** Every affected person hears the news from their own manager or a senior leader, with HR present, in person or on a live video call. Never by mass email, Slack, or a disabled login.
- **Security with dignity:** Fintech access controls matter, but disabling accounts before the conversation is humiliating and unnecessary. Coordinate with Security so access transitions happen *after* the live conversation (or during a supervised handover), with a clear way to retrieve personal files and say goodbye to teammates.
- **Same-day written summary:** Every affected employee leaves the conversation with a clear written document covering timeline, financial package, redeployment options, transition support, reflection period, and named contacts.
- **Support for managers:** Delivering this news is hard on managers too. Brief them thoroughly, pair every manager with HR, and give them time and support afterwards.

### 7. Protecting the 158 People Who Stay

- **Same-day honesty:** Tell remaining staff the same day what happened, why, which work is stopping, and whether this round is over (only state that it is over if you can stand behind it).
- **Re-plan workloads together:** Within two weeks, hold 1:1s with every remaining employee in Operations and Support and reset team priorities and SLAs together.
- **Measure people health alongside finances:** Track engagement pulse scores, sickness absence, and regretted exits monthly for 6–12 months alongside the financial plan.

We also explored alternatives with the board last week, including `[briefly name 2–3 non-headcount measures already being taken, e.g., vendor/cloud cuts, hiring freeze, stopping X projects]`. Those steps are necessary and are going ahead, but they are not enough to close the `[€X]` gap in the time we have.

**Nothing is final today.** Under Dutch law, this is an intended decision:
- We have formally asked our Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad*) for their advice and shared our full financial numbers with them.
- Because the proposal affects more than 20 roles, we have notified UWV and the relevant trade unions and invited the unions to consult with us on the plan and on a Social Plan.
- Nobody is being told their role is ending today, and no individual will ever hear news about their job by email.

**How we will handle this if the proposal goes ahead:**
- **Fair, lawful selection:** Where roles are affected, selection will follow Dutch statutory rules (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*) and objective role groupings — never arbitrary rankings.
- **Internal roles first:** We are freezing external hiring for any role that affected colleagues could move into, and we will actively look for internal redeployment first.
- **A decent transition package:** We are proposing a Social Plan above the statutory minimum, including enhanced severance, paid notice time to search for a next role, a budget for independent legal advice, and day-one transition support (career coaching, outplacement, and a paid training budget), plus dedicated immigration support for any colleague on a work visa.
- **Cutting work, not just roles:** We will not ask 158 people to carry the workload of 180. Alongside this proposal, we are setting out which Operations and Support workflows and SLAs will stop or change, and we will work through those with the teams.

**What happens next:**
- **Today at `[Time]` CET:** We will hold an all-hands Q&A `[in the Amsterdam office / link]` where I and the leadership team will answer your questions as openly as we can.
- **`[Dates / Weeks 2–6]`:** Consultation with the Works Council and trade unions. We will share updates at `[weekly cadence / key milestones]`.
- **`[Target Date]`:** Once consultation is complete and a final decision is taken, any colleague whose role is affected will be told first in a live, private 1-on-1 conversation with their manager and HR, followed by a written summary the same day.

I know how unsettling it is to read this, especially for our Operations and Support teams. Please bring your questions to `[Time]` today, or reach out directly to me, your manager, or `[HR Contact]` at any time. Confidential support is also available through `[EAP / confidential counsellor details]`.

`[Your Name]`
