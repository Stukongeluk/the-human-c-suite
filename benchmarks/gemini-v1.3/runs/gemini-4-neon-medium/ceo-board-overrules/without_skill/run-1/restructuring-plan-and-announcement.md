# Restructuring Plan & Staff Announcement
**Scope:** Proposed reduction of 22 roles (out of 180 FTE), primarily in Operations and Support  
**Jurisdiction:** Netherlands (Amsterdam)  
**Status:** Draft for leadership & Dutch employment counsel review  

---

## 1. Critical Dutch Legal & Regulatory Guardrails (Read Before Acting)

Because you are based in Amsterdam with **180 employees** and proposing **22 redundancies**, two mandatory Dutch statutory frameworks apply immediately. Getting the sequencing or wording wrong creates serious legal and reputational risk:

1. **WMCO (Wet melding collectief ontslag / Collective Redundancy Notification Act)**
   - Triggered whenever **20 or more employees** are dismissed for economic reasons within a 3-month window—**even if resolved via mutual settlement agreements (VSOs)**.
   - Requires formal notification to the **UWV** and the relevant **trade unions** (*vakbonden*).
   - Triggers a mandatory **1-month waiting period** (*wachttijd*) before terminations can take effect (unless unions confirm in writing that they have been consulted and waive the waiting period).
2. **WOR (Wet op de ondernemingsraden / Works Councils Act)**
   - Applies to companies with 50+ employees. A 12% restructuring requires a formal **request for advice (*adviesaanvraag*)** to your **Works Council (OR)** under Article 25 WOR *before* a final decision is taken.
   - **Crucial wording rule:** Until the OR has issued its advice and the board has taken a final decision, you must communicate this as a **proposed decision (*voorgenomen besluit*)**—never as a done deal. *(Note: If you do not yet have an OR in place, consult Dutch employment counsel immediately before announcing.)*
3. **Selection & Redeployment (*Afspiegelingsbeginsel* & *Herplaatsingsplicht*)**
   - For interchangeable roles (*uitwisselbare functies*)—very likely in Operations and Support—you cannot cherry-pick individuals. Selection must follow the **reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*)** across age brackets (after first terminating flex/agency/AOW-age workers where required).
   - You have a statutory **redeployment obligation (*herplaatsingsplicht*)** to assess whether impacted staff can be placed into suitable open roles.
4. **International Staff (IND / Highly Skilled Migrants & 30% Ruling)**
   - Affected staff on a Highly Skilled Migrant (*kennismigrant*) visa have a **3-month search period (*zoekperiode*)** after employment ends to find a new IND-recognized sponsor (and a >3-month gap also impacts the 30% ruling). Structuring notice periods/garden leave thoughtfully in VSOs makes a major practical difference.
5. **Fintech Regulatory & Operational Continuity (DNB / AFM)**
   - Before roles exit, verify that reduced Operations & Support capacity still meets regulatory and SLA obligations (KYC/AML/CDD reviews, transaction monitoring, incident response, complaints handling) and that IT access revocation for sensitive financial/PII systems is planned securely and respectfully.

## 2. Phased Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal, Operational & Comms Preparation (Weeks 1–2 — *Pre-Announcement*)
*Keep this strictly confidential among the core team (CEO, CFO, Head of People, Ops/Support leads, Dutch employment counsel).*

- [ ] **Lock the economic rationale (*bedrijfseconomische noodzaak*):** Document the financial figures, runway/margin targets, and why 22 roles in Ops/Support are impacted (needed for both UWV and OR).
- [ ] **Role mapping & *afspiegeling*:**
  - Identify exact functions impacted and determine which roles are interchangeable (*uitwisselbare functies*).
  - Run *afspiegelingsbeginsel* calculations with counsel.
  - Flag protected employees (*opzegverboden*: illness, pregnancy, OR members) and IND visa holders.
  - Map all current and near-term open roles across the company for *herplaatsingsplicht*.
- [ ] **Prepare formal filings:**
  - Draft the **OR *adviesaanvraag*** (Art. 25 WOR).
  - Draft the **WMCO notification** for UWV and relevant trade unions.
- [ ] **Design the exit package / Social Plan framework:**
  - Baseline: statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding* — 1/3 monthly gross salary per year of service, pro-rated).
  - Typical Amsterdam fintech enhancements for VSOs: sign-on uplift above statutory minimum, paid garden leave (*vrijstelling van werk*) during notice period, legal fee reimbursement (€750–€1,500 ex. VAT) for independent VSO review, outplacement/career coaching budget, waiver of non-compete clauses, and IND/visa timing support where feasible.
- [ ] **Operational continuity & security plan:**
  - Define what Ops/Support work will be **stopped, automated, or deprioritized** so the remaining team isn't handed an impossible workload.
  - Confirm DNB/AFM/compliance SLAs remain covered during and after transition.
  - Align IT/InfoSec on access revocation timing for sensitive production/banking systems.
- [ ] **Comms readiness:** Finalize all-hands script, staff email (Section 3 below), FAQ document, and manager talking points.

---

### Phase 2: Filing, Announcement & Consultation (Weeks 2–6)

**Day 0 — Announcement Day (Sequencing matters):**
1. **08:30** — Submit WMCO notification to UWV & trade unions, and formally deliver the *adviesaanvraag* to the OR (if not already pre-briefed under NDA).
2. **09:30** — Brief department heads and Ops/Support managers so they are not blindsided and know how to handle questions.
3. **10:30** — **All-hands meeting** (CEO, live + video for remote staff). Keep it direct, calm, and under 20 minutes.
4. **10:50** — Send the written **Staff Announcement email** (Section 3) + FAQ immediately after the all-hands.
5. **11:30** — **Department-level Q&As** for Operations and Support led by their respective VPs/Directors + People team.
6. **Afternoon** — Leadership & People team hold open drop-in office hours; open an anonymous Q&A form.

> **Do not** name affected individuals or issue VSOs on Announcement Day. Wait until the OR advice process and WMCO consultation requirements are properly completed.

**Weeks 2–6 — Consultation Period:**
- Conduct formal consultation with the **OR** (Art. 25 WOR) and **trade unions** (WMCO).
- Finalize the Social Plan / VSO terms.
- Complete the **redeployment (*herplaatsing*)** assessment across the company.
- Send **weekly written updates** to all staff (even if the update is simply "consultation is ongoing, next milestone is X") to prevent rumor spirals and anxiety.

---

### Phase 3: Final Decision, Individual Notifications & Exits (Weeks 6–8)
*Only after OR advice is received, the board confirms the final decision, and the WMCO 1-month waiting period / union sign-off is satisfied.*

- [ ] **Communicate final decision** to the OR and all staff, including the exact window when 1:1 notifications will occur (e.g., "All affected colleagues will be contacted between 09:00 and 12:00 today").
- [ ] **Individual 1:1 meetings** (direct manager + People partner, private, in-person or video—never email-only):
  - Deliver the news clearly in the first 60 seconds; do not bury it in small talk.
  - Walk through the written VSO offer, transition package, outplacement, legal-review budget, and IND/visa support (if applicable).
  - Remind them of the **statutory 14-day reflection period (*bedenktermijn*)** after signing a VSO—never pressure anyone to sign on the spot.
- [ ] **UWV fallback track:** For any impacted employee who chooses not to sign a VSO, file the formal UWV dismissal permit (*ontslagvergunning*) application.
- [ ] **Dignified offboarding:** Agree on handovers, internal/external goodbye messages, references/LinkedIn endorsements, and proportionate IT offboarding.

---

### Phase 4: Stabilization & Reset (Weeks 8–12)
- [ ] **Re-baseline Operations & Support:** Explicitly publish the updated operating model—what processes are sunsetted, what SLAs changed, and what priorities remain.
- [ ] **Manager & team check-ins:** Hold 1:1s with remaining Ops/Support staff within 2 weeks of exits. Survivor fatigue and attrition risk peak 30–60 days post-restructuring.
- [ ] **30/60-day compliance & SLA audit:** Verify KYC/AML, support response times, and incident handling metrics against regulatory and customer commitments.

Team,

I’m writing to follow up on what I just shared in our all-hands meeting.

Today, we are announcing a **proposed restructuring** that would reduce our team by **22 roles** out of our current 180 people, primarily within **Operations and Support**.

I know this is painful and unsettling news, and I want to be as direct as possible about why we are proposing this, how the process works, and what happens next.

### Why we are proposing this
`[Insert 2–3 direct, honest sentences from the board rationale—e.g., "Over the past year, our cost structure has grown faster than our revenue, and we need to reach a sustainable operating model by [quarter/year]. Over the last few weeks, leadership and the board reviewed multiple non-headcount options to close this gap. While we are also reducing [e.g., vendor spend / discretionary budgets], those measures alone are not enough to get us where we need to be."]`

This is not a reflection on the effort or dedication of our Operations and Support teams, who have carried an enormous amount of work for our customers and the business. It is a structural decision about the size and shape of the organization we can sustain going forward.

### How the process works (and what is *not* decided yet)
Because we operate under Dutch employment law, this is currently a **proposed decision (*voorgenomen besluit*)**:
- Today, we have formally submitted a **request for advice (*adviesaanvraag*)** to our **Works Council (OR)** and notified the **UWV** and relevant **trade unions** under the Collective Redundancy Notification Act (WMCO).
- No individual employment contracts are being terminated today, and no final decision will be made until the consultation process with the OR and unions is completed.
- Selection for any interchangeable roles will follow Dutch statutory rules (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*), and we will actively review internal redeployment (*herplaatsing*) opportunities across the company.

### Support for affected colleagues
As part of our consultation with the OR and unions, we are putting together a fair transition package for anyone whose role is ultimately impacted. Our intention is that this includes:
- Financial severance above/in line with the statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding*) `[confirm exact framing with counsel before sending]`
- A budget for independent legal advice to review any settlement agreement
- Outplacement and career transition support
- Dedicated guidance and practical support for international colleagues on **Highly Skilled Migrant (IND)** visas

### What happens next
- **Today at `[Time]`:** `[Operations Lead]` and `[Support Lead]` will host dedicated Q&A sessions with Operations and Support, joined by `[Head of People]`.
- **Today from `[Time]` to `[Time]`:** Leadership and the People team will hold open office hours. You can also submit questions anonymously here: `[Link to FAQ / anonymous form]`.
- **Weekly updates:** We will share a written update every `[Day, e.g., Friday]` on the progress of the OR and union consultation, even if there is no major milestone that week.
- **Estimated timeline:** We expect the consultation process to take approximately `[4–6 weeks]`. We will let everyone know in advance before any individual notifications take place.

I know the hardest part of a period like this is the uncertainty. We cannot remove all of that uncertainty today, but I can commit to treating everyone with respect, being transparent about the timeline, and answering your questions honestly.

Thank you for everything you do for our customers and for each other.

- Can I apply for open internal roles? *(Explain herplaatsingsplicht.)*
- What happens to my Highly Skilled Migrant visa / 30% ruling if I am affected?
- What happens to my equity / stock options / bonus / unused vacation days?
- Who can I talk to confidentially if I need support?
