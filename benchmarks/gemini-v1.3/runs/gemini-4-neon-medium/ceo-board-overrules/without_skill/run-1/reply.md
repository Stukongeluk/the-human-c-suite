### Before You Announce: 4 Dutch Legal & Fintech Guardrails
At **180 employees** and **22 roles** in Amsterdam, two mandatory statutes apply immediately:
1. **WMCO (Collective Redundancy Notification Act):** Because **22 ≥ 20 roles** within 3 months, you must formally notify the **UWV** and relevant **trade unions**—even if you plan to exit everyone via mutual settlement agreements (*VSOs*). This triggers a mandatory **1-month waiting period** unless unions waive it in writing.
2. **WOR (Works Councils Act):** At 50+ employees, a 12% restructuring requires a formal **request for advice (*adviesaanvraag*)** to your **Works Council (OR)** under Art. 25 WOR *before* a final decision is taken. **You must frame the announcement as a *proposed decision* (*voorgenomen besluit*)**, not a fait accompli. *(If you don't have an OR constituted yet, speak to Dutch employment counsel before announcing.)*
3. **Selection & Redeployment:** For interchangeable roles in Operations and Support, you must apply the **reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*)**—not individual cherry-picking—and conduct a genuine **redeployment (*herplaatsing*)** review.
4. **Amsterdam Fintech Specifics:** Account for **Highly Skilled Migrant (IND)** visa holders (3-month search period & 30% ruling impact—garden leave timing matters) and confirm **DNB/AFM/compliance SLAs** (KYC/AML, incident response, complaints) remain covered under the reduced headcount.
