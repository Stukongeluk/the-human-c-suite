# Restructuring Plan & Staff Announcement (Amsterdam — 22 Roles / 12%)

> **Critical Dutch Legal Note — Read Before Announcing Anything**
>
> At **180 employees** and **22 affected roles** in Amsterdam, two Dutch statutory regimes are triggered simultaneously:
>
> 1. **WMCO (*Wet melding collectief ontslag*)**: Because **20 or more employees** are affected within 3 months in one UWV region, you must notify **UWV** and the relevant **trade unions (*vakbonden*)** in advance, consult the unions on a **Social Plan**, and observe a **1-month waiting period** before UWV will process dismissal permits (unless unions waive it in writing). Settlement agreements (*VSOs*) count toward the 20-employee threshold.
> 2. **WOR (*Wet op de ondernemingsraden*)**: At 180 employees (>50), your **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR)** has a statutory **right of advice (*adviesrecht*, Art. 25 WOR)**. The board's decision **must be communicated as an intended/proposed decision (*voorgenomen besluit*)**—not a final decision—until the OR has formally advised. Presenting it as a done deal exposes the company to an injunction at the Amsterdam Enterprise Chamber (*Ondernemingskamer*).
>
> *(If you do not yet have an OR, or if some of the 22 roles are external contractors or naturally expiring fixed-term contracts that bring the WMCO headcount below 20, verify the exact count with Dutch employment counsel **before** filing or announcing.)*

## Part 1: Phased Execution Plan

### Phase 0: Legal & Operational Preparation (Weeks 1–2 — Strictly Confidential)

Complete these steps **before** any company-wide communication:

1. **Engage Dutch employment counsel (*arbeidsrechtadvocaat*)** to validate:
   - Exact WMCO headcount (distinguishing permanent staff, fixed-term contracts ending naturally vs. early, and external contractors/agency workers).
   - **Interchangeable role groupings (*uitwisselbare functies*)** across Operations and Support.
   - Preliminary **reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*)** calculation (first exiting contractors, temp/agency staff, on-call workers, and AOW-age workers in affected roles; then applying age-bracket LIFO—never performance-based cherry-picking).
   - **Dismissal prohibitions (*opzegverboden*)** check (e.g., employees currently on sick leave or pregnancy/maternity leave).
2. **Fintech regulatory & operational continuity check (DNB / AFM / DORA)**:
   - Confirm that the post-restructuring Operations & Support org chart still meets all licensing, SLA, complaints-handling, KYC/AML, transaction monitoring, and **DORA** operational resilience requirements. Document this for both the board and the OR.
3. **Prepare the OR Request for Advice (*Adviesaanvraag*, Art. 25 WOR)**:
   - Business and financial rationale (including why non-headcount measures were insufficient).
   - Current vs. proposed org chart, affected role groups, and impact on workload for the remaining ~158 staff.
   - Proposed **Social Plan** framework and redeployment (*herplaatsing*) approach.
4. **Draft the Social Plan / VSO Framework**:
   - Statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding*: 1/3 monthly gross salary per year of service, pro rata) + any negotiated enhancement or signing incentive for mutual consent (*vaststellingsovereenkomst* / VSO).
   - Legal review budget (typically €750–€1,500 ex. VAT per affected employee).
   - Outplacement / career transition budget and garden leave (*vrijstelling van werk*) policy.
   - Clarity on equity/options vesting, accrued vacation days, bonuses, and equipment.
   - **International staff / IND Highly Skilled Migrant (*kennismigrant*) support**: Ensure HR is ready to advise affected visa holders on the 3-month job-search period (*zoekperiode*, which runs from the formal contract end date), 30% ruling continuity rules, and the company's IND recognized-sponsor (*erkend referent*) reporting duties.
5. **Align Day-0 sequence**:
   - Brief the OR (ideally under NDA shortly before Day 0, or formally submit the *adviesaanvraag* on the morning of Day 0).
   - Prepare the **WMCO filing** to UWV and notification letters to relevant trade unions (e.g., FNV, CNV, De Unie) to send on Day 0.
   - Brief Operations & Support managers 1–2 hours before the all-hands with strict legal guardrails (see Manager Talking Points below).

---

### Phase 1: Formal Notification & Consultation (Weeks 2–6)

- **Day 0 (Announcement Day)**:
  1. Submit formal *adviesaanvraag* to the OR.
  2. File **WMCO notification** with UWV and send formal notices to the relevant trade unions (starts the 1-month WMCO waiting period).
  3. Brief department leads and people managers.
  4. Send the **Staff Announcement** (Part 2 below) and hold a live All-Hands immediately afterward.
  5. Hold dedicated Operations & Support Q&A sessions the same afternoon so the most affected teams are not left waiting in silence.
- **Weeks 2–6 (Consultation Window)**:
  - Conduct OR consultation meetings and answer written questions promptly.
  - Meet with trade unions to discuss the proposed restructuring and negotiate/align on the Social Plan.
  - Run an active **redeployment (*herplaatsing*)** review: map all current and upcoming internal vacancies across the company (and group, if applicable) and assess whether affected roles could transition with reasonable training.
  - Send brief weekly cadence updates to staff on process milestones—even when the update is simply that OR/union consultations remain on track.

---

### Phase 2: Final Decision & Individual Conversations (Weeks 6–8)

1. **Receive formal OR advice** and issue the written **Final Decision (*definitief besluit*)**.
   - *Note*: If the OR advises negatively and you proceed without resolving their objections, a mandatory **1-month waiting period** applies under Art. 25(6) WOR during which the OR can appeal to the Enterprise Chamber.
2. **Lock the final list**: Re-verify the *afspiegelingsbeginsel* calculation, redeployment matches, and any new *opzegverboden* (e.g., illness).
3. **Individual 1:1 notification meetings** (all held within a tight 24–48 hour window):
   - Attended by the employee's manager and HR (in person in Amsterdam where possible; private video call if remote).
   - Deliver the news directly, empathetically, and clearly. Do not debate the business decision; focus on support and next steps.
   - Provide the written confirmation, Social Plan summary, and **VSO** immediately after the meeting.
   - Give employees a reasonable review window (typically 1–2 weeks) to consult a lawyer, plus the statutory **14-day reflection period (*bedenktijd*)** after signing (must be explicitly stated in the VSO, otherwise it extends to 21 days).
   - Ensure VSOs are drafted to be **WW-neutral** (*WW-veilig*) so employees retain Dutch unemployment benefit rights.
4. **UWV Dismissal Permits (*ontslagvergunningen*)**:
   - For any affected employee who declines a VSO (and where no dismissal prohibition applies), submit a complete UWV a-ground (*bedrijfseconomische redenen*) application once the WMCO 1-month waiting period has elapsed (or unions have waived it in writing).

---

### Phase 3: Handover, Offboarding & Reset (Weeks 8–12+)

- **Dignified transitions & Ops/Support continuity**:
  - Plan realistic knowledge transfer (tickets, runbooks, escalation paths, KYC/compliance queues). Avoid expecting departing employees on garden leave to carry full BAUs.
  - Explicitly define what work is **stopping or being automated/deprioritized** so the remaining Ops & Support staff are not simply handed 12% more volume.
- **Security & compliance offboarding**:
  - Coordinate fintech system/data access revocation respectfully with each person's last active working day.
- **IND & administrative compliance**:
  - Submit required IND notifications for affected *kennismigranten* within statutory deadlines and provide written employment/termination documentation promptly to support their job search.
- **Re-stabilizing the remaining team (~158 people)**:
  - Within 1–2 weeks of individual notifications concluding, hold a focused reset for Operations, Support, and the wider company: new team structure, revised SLAs/priorities, and regular manager check-ins.

> **Channel**: Email from CEO/Founder (or leadership) sent immediately before a live All-Hands, followed by dedicated Operations & Support sessions the same day.  
> **Timing**: Send **only after** the OR *adviesaanvraag* and WMCO notifications (UWV & trade unions) are ready/submitted on Day 0.

**Subject:** Important update: Proposed organizational restructuring

Team,

I am writing to share difficult news. Following an extensive review of our financial position and operating structure with the Board, we are proposing a restructuring that would reduce our team by **22 roles** (roughly 12% of our 180 people), primarily within **Operations and Support**.

Over the past weeks, we explored alternatives to headcount reductions. While we have taken steps to tighten spending elsewhere, the Board and leadership team concluded that those measures alone are not enough to put the business on a sustainable footing for the next phase. I know how hard this news is to hear, especially for colleagues who have worked tirelessly to support our customers and keep our operations running.

### This is a proposed decision — what happens next under Dutch law

Under Dutch law, no final decision is made—and no individual roles are terminated—until the formal consultation process is complete:

- **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR):** Today we submitted a formal Request for Advice (*adviesaanvraag*) to the OR outlining the rationale, proposed structure, and impact on the organization.
- **Trade Unions & UWV:** Because more than 20 roles are affected, we have also notified the UWV and the relevant trade unions under the Collective Redundancy Notification Act (*WMCO*) and invited the unions to consult on a **Social Plan**.

Selection for affected roles will follow strict statutory rules (the Dutch reflection principle / *afspiegelingsbeginsel*) and we will actively explore **internal redeployment (*herplaatsing*)** for anyone impacted.

We are committed to handling this process with fairness, transparency, and respect. As part of the proposed Social Plan we are discussing with the OR and unions, our intention is to provide:

- Severance in line with the statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding*) and fair settlement terms
- Budget for independent legal review and career/outplacement support
- Dedicated HR and immigration guidance for international colleagues on **Highly Skilled Migrant (*kennismigrant*) visas**, including support navigating the 3-month search period and administrative requirements

## Part 3: Manager Guardrails (Do's & Don'ts for Day-0 Q&A)

Brief all Operations, Support, and senior managers on these five rules before the announcement:

1. **Always say "proposed / intended" (*voorgenomen*), never "decided" or "final."** Under Art. 25 WOR, implying the outcome is already locked before OR advice creates direct legal risk.
2. **Never speculate on who is safe or who is at risk.** Selection must follow the statutory *afspiegelingsbeginsel* and redeployment review—managers cannot make informal promises.
3. **Never tie this to individual performance.** This is a business-economic restructuring (*bedrijfseconomische redenen*), not a performance review. Mixing the two undermines UWV/VSO defensibility.
4. **Direct legal/Social Plan questions to HR.** Do not improvise answers on severance, VSO terms, or visa rules; log the question and have HR answer accurately.
5. **Acknowledge the human impact.** Keep it calm, honest, and brief—avoid corporate euphemisms ("rightsizing", "exciting new chapter") and give people space today.
