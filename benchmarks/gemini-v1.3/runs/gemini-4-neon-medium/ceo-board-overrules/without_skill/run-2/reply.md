Before you send anything to staff, there are **two Dutch legal triggers** you need to respect because of your exact numbers (**180 staff, 22 roles in Amsterdam**):

1. **WMCO (*Wet melding collectief ontslag*):** At **20+ roles** within 3 months in one UWV region, you must notify **UWV** and the relevant **trade unions (*vakbonden*)** in advance, consult the unions on a **Social Plan**, and observe a **1-month waiting period** before UWV will process dismissal permits (unless unions waive it in writing). Settlement agreements (*VSOs*) count toward the 20-person threshold. *(Have Dutch employment counsel verify your exact contract mix—if some of the 22 are external contractors or naturally expiring fixed-term contracts, your WMCO count could drop below 20.)*
2. **WOR (*Wet op de ondernemingsraden*):** At 180 employees (>50), your **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR)** has a statutory **right of advice (Art. 25 WOR)**. You **must** announce this as a **proposed/intended decision (*voorgenomen besluit*)**—not a final decision—until the OR has advised. Presenting it as final exposes you to an injunction at the Amsterdam Enterprise Chamber (*Ondernemingskamer*).

## Execution Plan (Overview — 8–12 Weeks)

Full checklist and details are in [restructuring-plan-and-announcement.md](file:///G:/agy-bench/ws/4764277a61fd/restructuring-plan-and-announcement.md).

1. **Phase 0: Legal & Operational Prep (Weeks 1–2 — Strictly Confidential, before any announcement)**
   - **Dutch employment counsel:** Confirm exact WMCO count, group **interchangeable roles (*uitwisselbare functies*)** across Ops & Support, run a confidential preliminary **reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*)** calculation (flexible shell/contractors/temp workers exit first; then age-bracket LIFO—no performance cherry-picking), and flag any **dismissal prohibitions (*opzegverboden*)** such as sick leave or maternity leave.
   - **Fintech regulatory check (DNB / AFM / DORA):** Verify that the post-cut Ops & Support structure still covers KYC/AML, transaction monitoring, complaints handling, SLAs, and DORA operational resilience.
   - **OR Request for Advice (*adviesaanvraag*):** Document the business/financial rationale, proposed org chart, and workload impact on the remaining ~158 staff.
   - **Social Plan / VSO framework:** Statutory *transitievergoeding* + any VSO enhancement, legal review budget (typically €750–€1,500 ex. VAT), outplacement, garden leave, equity/options clarity, and **IND Highly Skilled Migrant (*kennismigrant*)** support (3-month *zoekperiode* from contract end date, 30% ruling guidance, and sponsor reporting).

2. **Phase 1: Formal Notification & Consultation (Weeks 2–6)**
   - **Day 0 sequence:** Submit *adviesaanvraag* to the OR → file **WMCO notification** with UWV & trade unions (FNV, CNV, De Unie) → brief managers with strict legal guardrails → send the **Staff Announcement** & hold live All-Hands → hold dedicated Ops & Support Q&A sessions the same afternoon.
   - **Weeks 2–6:** Run OR and union consultations, negotiate/finalize the Social Plan, and document active **redeployment (*herplaatsing*)** checks across all open/upcoming roles. Send brief weekly process updates to staff.

3. **Phase 2: Final Decision & Individual Conversations (Weeks 6–8)**
   - Receive formal OR advice and issue the written **Final Decision (*definitief besluit*)**. *(If OR advises negatively and you deviate, a mandatory 1-month Art. 25(6) WOR waiting period applies.)*
   - Re-verify *afspiegeling* and *opzegverboden*, then conduct all 1:1 notification meetings (manager + HR) within a 24–48 hour window.
   - Issue **WW-neutral VSOs** with a reasonable review window (1–2 weeks) plus the statutory **14-day reflection period (*bedenktijd*)**. For anyone who declines a VSO, file UWV a-ground dismissal applications once the WMCO waiting period has elapsed (or been waived).

4. **Phase 3: Handover, Offboarding & Reset (Weeks 8–12+)**
   - Manage dignified Ops/Support handovers, explicitly define what work is **stopping** (so remaining staff aren't simply absorbing 12% more volume), revoke fintech system access respectfully on the last active day, complete IND sponsor notifications, and hold a post-restructuring reset for the remaining ~158 people.
