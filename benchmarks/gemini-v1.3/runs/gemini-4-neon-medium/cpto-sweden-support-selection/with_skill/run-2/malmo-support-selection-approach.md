# Malmö Support Team Restructuring: Selection Approach (Draft)

> **Note:** This is a management draft, not legal advice. Review every step with Swedish employment counsel and HR before sharing or acting on it.

## 1. Purpose and Scope
- **Proposed change:** Reduce the Malmö support team from 30 to 21 roles (9 roles affected) following AI automation of first-line tickets.
- **Core constraint:** Under Swedish law, we cannot select who stays by ranking individual ticket counts and CSAT. Selection must follow the Employment Protection Act (LAS) and union negotiation under the Co-determination Act (MBL).

## 2. Steps Before Any Involuntary Reduction
Before finalizing a reduction of 9 roles, document and review with union representatives:
1. **Verify AI performance and remaining workload:** Confirm that AI resolution holds up on repeat-contact rate, escalations, and churn—and that 21 people can sustainably handle the harder, non-automated queue.
2. **Test lower-harm alternatives first:** Hiring freeze and natural attrition, internal redeployment and reskilling (e.g., second-line escalations, customer success, QA, AI knowledge-base maintenance), and voluntary exit packages.
3. **Check statutory redeployment duty:** Offer any open internal vacancies to affected staff before issuing notice.

## 3. Compliant Selection Approach (Sweden)
1. **Negotiate first (MBL):** Open formal consultations with the relevant unions before any final decision, sharing the underlying ticket, cost, and capacity numbers.
2. **Notify Arbetsförmedlingen:** Because 9 roles are affected (threshold: 5–25 employees), submit advance notice to the Public Employment Service at least **2 months** before the first termination takes effect.
3. **Apply LAS seniority order (*turordningsregler*):** Unless a collective agreement or a union-negotiated list (*avtalsturlista*) specifies otherwise, selection follows length of service per operational unit and collective agreement area (last-in, first-out). Job category is not a standalone criterion.
4. **Use the 3-person exemption objectively:** Under LAS (2022 reform), the employer may exempt up to **3 employees** of particular importance to continued operations.
   - **Do not use tickets resolved or CSAT.** Ticket volume rewards easy cases and penalizes specialists who handle complex escalations, mentor peers, or fix root causes—the exact skills needed after first-line automation.
   - **Fix criteria before naming anyone:** Define objective, role-critical skills required for the remaining work (e.g., complex technical troubleshooting, key-account escalation handling, AI workflow QA) and document the rationale with counsel.
5. **Honour notice and re-employment rights:** Apply statutory/collective-agreement notice periods (1–6 months by length of service) and track the **9-month re-employment priority** for dismissed staff with more than 12 months' service in the past 3 years.

## 4. Transition Support for Affected Staff
- Activate the job security council (e.g., TRR, if covered by a collective agreement) from the first day of notice so coaching and job-search support start immediately.
- Inform eligible staff about Sweden's public transition study support (*omställningsstudiestöd*, covering roughly 80% of salary up to a ceiling for up to ~1 year of full-time study).
- Where affordable, pair statutory notice with paid job-search time, written references, and a clear handover so remaining staff are not left absorbing unreduced work.

## 5. Sources to Verify with Counsel
- Swedish Employment Protection Act (*Lagen om anställningsskydd*, LAS, including 2022 amendments)
- Swedish Co-determination Act (*Medbestämmandelagen*, MBL)
- Arbetsförmedlingen advance notice rules (*varsel*, 5–25 employees: 2 months)
- CSN (*omställningsstudiestöd*) and TRR / applicable collective agreement
