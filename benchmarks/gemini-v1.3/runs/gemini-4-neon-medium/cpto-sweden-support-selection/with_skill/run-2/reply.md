Do not use tickets resolved and CSAT to select the 9 people leaving Malmö: in Sweden you cannot pick who stays by performance ranking, and individual ticket counts select against the complex-support skills you now need most.

The trap here is treating AI deflection as a quick headcount cut while using a first-line activity metric that rewards easy tickets and penalizes escalations and mentoring.

- **Verify before cutting:** Check whether AI resolution is holding up on escalations and churn, and test attrition, internal redeployment/reskilling (second-line, customer success, QA, AI tuning), and voluntary exits first.
- **Follow Swedish law (confirm with counsel):** Under MBL, negotiate with unions before deciding; under LAS, selection follows seniority (*turordningsregler*) per unit and collective-agreement area unless a union-agreed list (*avtalsturlista*) sets another order.
- **Use the 3 exemptions objectively:** LAS allows exempting up to 3 critical employees—define objective, role-critical skills for the remaining complex work before naming anyone, not ticket counts.
- **Plan statutory timelines:** Cutting 9 roles (5–25 threshold) requires at least 2 months' advance notice to Arbetsförmedlingen, 1–6 months' notice by service, and 9 months' re-employment priority.
- **Support transitions from day one:** Engage your job security council (e.g., TRR, if covered) immediately on notice and flag Sweden's transition study support (*omställningsstudiestöd*).
