We need to pause on HR’s proposal: **in Sweden, you cannot select who to keep in a redundancy (*arbetsbrist*) based on performance metrics like tickets resolved and CSAT.**

Under the Swedish Employment Protection Act (**LAS**), redundancies must follow **last in, first out (LIFO / *turordning*)**—not a performance ranking. Using performance as the selection basis exposes the company to wrongful termination claims and damages.

Here is the compliant selection approach for the Malmö reduction (30 → 21):

### 1. Define the post-AI role & check redeployment (*omplacering*)
* Define the baseline qualifications needed for the 21 remaining roles (e.g., handling complex/escalated tickets rather than L1 volume).
* Before selecting anyone for termination, check for any vacant roles across the **entire Swedish legal entity** that affected employees have *sufficient qualifications* to fill.

### 2. Build the LIFO list (*turordning*)
* Rank the 30 Malmö employees by **total length of employment** with the company (if you have a collective bargaining agreement, this is done per CBA area).
* Employees with longer tenure stay, provided they have **sufficient qualifications** (*tillräckliga kvalifikationer*) for the remaining role after a reasonable onboarding period—they do **not** need to be the top performers.

### 3. Use the lawful levers to retain key skills
You have three legal ways to deviate from strict LIFO:
* **Up to 3 statutory exemptions (*undantag*):** Under LAS, you may exempt up to **3 employees** of particular importance for continued operations before applying LIFO (check your CBA first, as some agreements modify this).
* **Agreed priority list (*avtalsturlista*):** *Only if you have a CBA*, you can negotiate a custom selection list with the union that weighs competence for the post-AI work. Criteria must be objective and non-discriminatory.
* **Voluntary severance:** You can offer voluntary exit packages to reduce the number of forced redundancies.

> **Note on tickets resolved & CSAT:** Even if you use these metrics to justify the 3 exemptions or negotiate an *avtalsturlista*, they are a weak fit here. Once AI takes simple L1 tickets, historical ticket volume rewards speed on easy work rather than complex escalation handling—and raw volume can create discrimination risks (e.g., for employees returning from parental or sick leave). Focus instead on skills required for the remaining complex work.

### 4. Mandatory process steps (before any decision or notice)
1. **MBL consultation:** Consult the union(s) *before* deciding on the reorganization or redundancies (under MBL § 11 if you have a CBA; under MBL § 13 with any union that has members in the team if you don't).
2. **Notify Arbetsförmedlingen (*varsel*):** Because 9 employees are affected (threshold is 5+), you must file a *varsel* at least **2 months** before the first termination takes effect.
3. **Re-employment priority (*företrädesrätt*):** Keep in mind that terminated staff with >12 months' tenure will generally have a 9-month priority right to be rehired if roles open up.

Have Swedish employment counsel or your employer organization (*arbetsgivarorganisation*) review your CBA terms and MBL notice before you start consultations.
