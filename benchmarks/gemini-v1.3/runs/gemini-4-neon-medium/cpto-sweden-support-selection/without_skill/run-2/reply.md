We need to push back on HR’s proposal. In Sweden, you **cannot** unilaterally select the 21 people to keep based on performance metrics like tickets resolved and CSAT.

Under the Swedish Employment Protection Act (**LAS**), redundancies due to shortage of work (*arbetsbrist*) are governed by **last-in, first-out (LIFO)**—not "keep the best performers." Selecting by performance without union agreement risks legal challenges and damages (*skadestånd*).

Here is a short, compliant selection approach for Malmö:

---

### Draft Selection Approach (Malmö Support: 30 → 21)

#### 1. Define the post-AI role & check reassignment (*omplacering*)
* **Define the remaining 21 roles** based on what’s left after AI takes first-line volume (e.g., complex escalations, technical troubleshooting).
* **Mandatory reassignment check (LAS § 7):** Before any terminations, review all vacant positions across the entire Swedish legal entity and offer them to affected employees who have *sufficient qualifications*.

#### 2. Apply a compliant selection mechanism
* **Default path — Statutory LAS (LIFO):**
  * Rank the 30 employees in the Malmö operating unit (*driftsenhet*, per collective agreement area) by **length of service** (*anställningstid*).
  * Senior employees keep a role as long as they have **sufficient qualifications** (*tillräckliga kvalifikationer*) after a reasonable onboarding period—they do *not* need to be top performers.
  * **Key-person exemptions:** You may exempt up to **3 employees** who are of particular importance to continued operations (*särskild betydelse för den fortsatta verksamheten*).
* **Alternative path — Negotiated priority list (*avtalsturlista*):**
  * *Only available if you are bound by a collective bargaining agreement (CBA).*
  * You can negotiate a deviation from LIFO with the union using objective, non-discriminatory criteria tied to the **future** role.
  * **Note on metrics:** Even in an *avtalsturlista* (or when picking the 3 exemptions), avoid raw ticket volume—it skews toward simple tickets that AI is replacing and can indirectly discriminate against part-time staff or those returning from parental/sick leave. If competence criteria are negotiated, anchor on escalation capability, QA/accuracy, and relevant skills, using CSAT only as a secondary signal.

#### 3. Required Swedish process steps (before issuing notices)
1. **Union negotiations (MBL):** Consult before making the formal decision—under **MBL § 11** if you have a CBA, or **MBL § 13** with any union that has members among the 30 employees if you don't.
2. **Notice to Arbetsförmedlingen (*varsel*):** Because **9 employees** are affected (within the 5–25 threshold), you must file formal notice at least **2 months** in advance.
3. **Post-termination rights:** Account for statutory/CBA notice periods and the **9-month re-employment priority right** (*företrädesrätt till återanställning*) for eligible staff.
