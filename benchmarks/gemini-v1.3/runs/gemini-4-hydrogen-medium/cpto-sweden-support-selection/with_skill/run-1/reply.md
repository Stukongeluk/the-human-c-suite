Do not rank the Malmö team on tickets resolved or CSAT—in Sweden that is both unlawful as a unilateral redundancy rule and measures yesterday's first-line work rather than the complex escalations your remaining 21 people will handle.

Here is the selection approach to take to HR and Swedish employment counsel (not legal advice):

- **Exhaust alternatives before cutting 9 roles:** Check internal redeployment (customer success, QA, AI escalation/training), hiring freezes, and voluntary exits first.
- **Negotiate with the union first (MBL):** Under the Co-determination Act, consult the unions with the post-AI ticket data before deciding, and notify *Arbetsförmedlingen* at least 2 months ahead (required for 5–25 dismissals).
- **Use seniority order by default (LAS):** Redundancy selection in Sweden follows length of service per operational unit and collective agreement area, with at most 3 employer exemptions for critical skills.
- **Only use capability criteria if the union agrees (*avtalsturlista*):** If you negotiate a custom list, base it on future tier-2+ escalation and AI-oversight skills—never raw ticket counts.
- **Fund transition from day one:** Activate the job security council (such as TRR) upon notice, track the 9-month re-employment priority, and point staff to *omställningsstudiestöd*.
