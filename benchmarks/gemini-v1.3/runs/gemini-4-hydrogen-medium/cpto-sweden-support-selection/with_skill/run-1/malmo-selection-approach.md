# Malmö Support Team Restructuring: Selection Approach (30 → 21 Roles)

*Note: This is an operational and management draft, not legal advice. Confirm all steps, collective agreement terms, and timelines with HR and Swedish employment counsel before acting.*

## 1. Why "Tickets Resolved and CSAT" Won't Work

- **Swedish law (LAS):** Under the Employment Protection Act (*Lagen om anställningsskydd*, LAS), redundancy selection follows **length of service** (*turordning*) per operational unit and collective agreement area—not individual performance rankings. Employers may unilaterally exempt at most **three** employees. Any other selection order requires an agreed list (*avtalsturlista*) negotiated with the union.
- **Role mismatch:** AI now handles most first-line tickets. Tickets resolved and CSAT measure yesterday's high-volume first-line work, not the complex escalations, edge cases, and AI quality oversight the remaining 21 people will own.

## 2. Before Finalising the Reduction (9 Roles)

1. **Test alternatives first:** Map internal openings (e.g., customer success, QA, product operations, AI escalation/training) and offer redeployment or reskilling before any dismissal. Check whether natural attrition or voluntary exits can close part of the 9-role gap.
2. **Redesign the remaining work:** Define what the 21-person team will stop doing and how AI escalations are routed so the remaining team is not overloaded.

## 3. Consultation & Statutory Notice

- **MBL negotiation:** Under the Co-determination Act (*Medbestämmandelagen*, MBL), negotiate with the relevant unions **before** a final decision is taken. Share the post-AI ticket volume data, the 21-role design, and redeployment options.
- **Public Employment Service (*Arbetsförmedlingen*):** Because 9 dismissals fall in the 5–25 threshold, notify *Arbetsförmedlingen* at least **2 months** in advance.

## 4. Selection Mechanics

- **Default path (LAS seniority + 3 exemptions):** Apply length-of-service order within the Malmö operational unit and collective agreement area, using up to **3 exemptions** only where specific specialist skills are essential to run the post-AI operation.
- **Negotiated path (*avtalsturlista*):** Only if agreed with the union, use objective criteria tied to the *future* work (e.g., complex/tier-2+ escalation capability, product/technical domain depth, coaching/AI-oversight), never raw ticket volume.
- **Re-employment priority:** Track the 9-month statutory priority for re-employment for affected staff with more than 12 months' service in the past 3 years.

## 5. Transition Support from Day One

- **Job security council:** Activate the applicable council (e.g., TRR for private-sector white-collar staff) as soon as notice is given so coaching and job-search support start immediately.
- **Study support:** Inform affected employees about Sweden's transition study support (*omställningsstudiestöd*) for reskilling.
