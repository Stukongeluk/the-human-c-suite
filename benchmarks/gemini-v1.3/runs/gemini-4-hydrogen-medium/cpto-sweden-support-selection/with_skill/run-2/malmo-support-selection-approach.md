# Malmö Support Restructuring: Selection & Transition Approach

**Scope:** Reducing Malmö support from 30 to 21 roles (9 roles) as AI takes over first-line tickets.
**Note:** This is a management draft, not legal advice. Confirm collective agreement terms, operational unit boundaries, and timelines with HR and Swedish employment counsel before acting.

---

## 1. Why we cannot select on tickets resolved and CSAT

- **Legal compliance (Sweden):** Under the Employment Protection Act (*Lagen om anställningsskydd*, LAS), redundancies follow a statutory order of priority by length of service (*turordning*) per operational unit and collective agreement area—not individual performance rankings. Employers may exempt up to three employees from that order, or agree on a different list (*avtalsturlista*) with the union.
- **Operational fit:** Once AI handles most first-line tickets, the remaining 21 roles shift to complex escalations, root-cause analysis, and customer retention. Ranking by tickets resolved rewards high-volume, simple tickets rather than the deeper problem-solving the new team needs.

## 2. Steps before involuntary exits

1. **Validate the workload shift:** Confirm AI deflection rates and escalation volumes are stable enough that 21 people can carry the remaining work sustainably.
2. **Offer internal redeployment first:** Match affected staff to open or emerging roles (e.g., QA, customer onboarding, product feedback, or AI escalation oversight).
3. **Open voluntary options:** Model voluntary exits or reduced-hours arrangements before issuing involuntary notices.

## 3. Compliant selection process

1. **Consult under MBL:** Initiate Co-determination Act (*Medbestämmandelagen*, MBL) negotiations with the relevant union(s) early, sharing the workload data while the plan can still be shaped.
2. **Establish the *turordning* list:** Build the length-of-service order for the Malmö operational unit and applicable collective agreement area.
3. **Handle critical skills transparently:** Either apply the statutory three exemptions using pre-agreed, objective criteria (e.g., documented tier-2/3 escalation skills) or negotiate an *avtalsturlista* with the union.
4. **File statutory notice:** Notify *Arbetsförmedlingen* at least **2 months** in advance (required for 5–25 dismissals).

## 4. Transition & survivor support

- **From day one of notice:** Activate the job security council (e.g., TRR for private-sector white-collar staff) for coaching and transition support, and inform departing employees about transition study support (*omställningsstudiestöd*).
- **Statutory rights:** Honour notice periods (1–6 months by tenure) and the 9-month priority for re-employment for staff with more than 12 months' service in the last three years.
- **Re-scope the remaining 21 roles:** Explicitly stop or hand off low-value tasks so the smaller team is not left with the same workload.
