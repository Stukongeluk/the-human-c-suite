### Two Quick Flags Before Using Tickets + CSAT
1. **Swedish labor law (LAS & MBL):** In Malmö, redundancy (*arbetsbrist*) follows seniority (*turordning* / last-in, first-out), not pure performance ranking. You must consult the union under MBL first, and you can only apply performance/competence selection within legal bounds—either via the limited LAS exemptions (up to 3 key employees) or a union-negotiated *avtalsturlista*.
2. **Metric mismatch:** Since AI is taking first-line tickets, the remaining 21 roles will focus on **complex escalations and edge cases**. Raw *tickets resolved* and *CSAT* reward fast, simple tickets (the work being automated) and penalize agents who handle harder, already-frustrated customers.

---

### Draft Selection Approach (30 → 21)

**1. Define the post-AI role**
Update the job profile for the 21 remaining seats to focus on Tier 2/complex troubleshooting, escalation ownership, and improving AI/knowledge-base quality.

**2. Follow the statutory process**
- Initiate MBL union consultations and establish the *turordning* (seniority) baseline.
- Agree with the union on whether selection uses standard LAS + key-role exemptions, an *avtalsturlista*, or role-matching for newly defined specialist positions.

**3. Use calibrated criteria (instead of raw volume + CSAT)**
Where competence/performance selection applies, score across the past 6–12 months using weighted, tier-adjusted criteria:
- **Complex Resolution & Quality (40%):** QA audit scores and resolution rate on Tier 2/escalated tickets (normalized for ticket complexity, not raw volume).
- **Customer Outcome (25%):** CSAT normalized by ticket category/tier, paired with communication QA.
- **AI & Process Readiness (20%):** Track record of documenting edge cases, updating help articles, and flagging AI/routing gaps.
- **Team Reliability (15%):** Schedule adherence and peer support (strictly excluding protected absences like parental or sick leave).

**4. Fairness safeguards**
- Prorate/normalize all volume metrics for part-time schedules and leave.
- Have two managers independently review and calibrate scores before any union or employee discussions.
