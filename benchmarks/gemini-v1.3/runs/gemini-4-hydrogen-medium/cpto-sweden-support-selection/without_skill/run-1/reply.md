Before using **tickets resolved + CSAT** to pick the 21 people, two important flags:

1. **Swedish labour law (LAS):** In Malmö, redundancies (*arbetsbrist*) default to **last-in, first-out (LIFO)**—not performance ranking. You also have to check redeployment options (*omplacering*) and negotiate with the union (**MBL**) before finalizing decisions.
2. **Metric mismatch:** Since AI now handles first-line volume, the remaining 21 roles will handle complex escalations and edge cases. Raw "tickets resolved" rewards fast, simple closes—the exact work AI just replaced—and penalizes people who took harder tickets.

Here is a short, workable selection approach you can take back to HR:

### Draft Selection Approach (30 → 21)

1. **Define the post-AI role profile first**
   Document what the remaining 21 roles require (e.g., complex troubleshooting, escalation ownership, handling edge cases, reviewing/improving AI responses). Under LAS, anyone kept must have *sufficient qualifications* (*tillräckliga kvalifikationer*) for this updated role.

2. **Build the legal baseline (Seniority + Redeployment)**
   - Check if any of the 9 affected staff can be redeployed elsewhere in the company.
   - Establish the standard LAS seniority list (*turordningslista*) for the Malmö support unit as the starting baseline.

3. **Apply targeted exemptions / balanced criteria (if deviating from LIFO)**
   - Under LAS, you can **exempt up to 3 key employees** from the seniority order (e.g., critical escalation or AI-escalation specialists).
   - Going beyond 3 requires negotiating an agreed list (*avtalsturlista*) with the union. If you propose performance-based criteria for exemptions or an *avtalsturlista*, replace raw volume with:
     - **Quality & complexity:** QA scores or resolution rate on *tier-2 / escalated* tickets (not total ticket count).
     - **Customer impact:** CSAT segmented by ticket complexity/category.
     - **Role readiness:** Technical depth, knowledge-base/AI coaching capability, and documented performance reviews.

4. **Consult the union (MBL) before communicating**
   Present the AI-driven shift in workload, the updated role requirements, the LIFO baseline, and any proposed exemptions to the union before making individual selections final.
