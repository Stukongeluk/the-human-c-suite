At 180 employees in Amsterdam, cutting 22 roles (12.2%, leaving 158) triggers two mandatory Dutch gates before any dismissal is final:
1. **Works Council (OR) advice (Article 25 WOR):** With 50+ employees, you must ask the OR for advice early enough that they can still influence the plan. Do not announce individual exits as a done deal beforehand.
2. **Collective Dismissal Notification (WMCO):** Cutting 20 or more roles within three months in one UWV work area requires notifying UWV and the trade unions and consulting them *before* finalising—including any employer-initiated settlement agreements (VSOs).
