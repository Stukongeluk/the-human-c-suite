# Restructuring Plan and Staff Announcement (22 Roles)

> **Note:** This is a management and communication plan, not legal advice. Have HR and Dutch employment counsel verify all dates, statutory filings, selection pools, and individual notices before taking action.

---

## Part 1: Transition Plan

**Scope:** 22 of 180 roles (12.2%), leaving 158 roles, concentrated in Operations and Support.

### 1. Leadership Accountability First
- **Own the result:** Leadership takes responsibility for the decisions and conditions that brought the company here—both in the Works Council submission and in the staff announcement.
- **Leaders go first:** Before frontline roles are reduced, freeze or cut executive bonuses, leadership pay/perks, and non-essential discretionary spend. Document what leadership is giving up so the sacrifice is visible and credible.

### 2. Dutch Legal & Consultation Gates (Do Not Skip)
Because you have 180 employees in Amsterdam and are cutting 22 roles (≥ 20 within 3 months in one UWV work area), two statutory processes apply:
1. **Works Council (Ondernemingsraad / OR) — Article 25 WOR:**
   - With 180 employees, a Works Council is mandatory. You must submit a formal request for advice (*adviesaanvraag*) with the financial rationale, the alternatives considered, the expected impact on roles, and the proposed social plan—**early enough that the OR's advice can still influence the outcome**.
   - Do **not** present the 22-role reduction to staff as a final, irreversible decision before the OR has given its advice.
2. **Collective Dismissal (WMCO) — UWV & Trade Unions:**
   - Because 20 or more roles are affected within three months in the Amsterdam UWV work area (including any employer-initiated settlement agreements / *vaststellingsovereenkomsten*), you must notify **UWV** and the **relevant trade unions** and invite the unions to consult *before* finalizing dismissals.
3. **Selection & Dismissal Route:**
   - Objective selection follows the Dutch **reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*)** across age brackets within interchangeable roles (*uitwisselbare functies*)—never performance rankings or cherry-picking.
   - Check for dismissal prohibitions (*opzegverboden*), such as employees on sick leave, pregnancy/maternity leave, or OR members.
   - Dismissals proceed either via **UWV permission** or **mutual settlement agreements (VSO)** with independent legal-review budget for each employee.

### 3. Phased Timeline (Indicative)

| Phase | Timing | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Prep & Legal Lock** | Week 1 | Finalise financial case, executive pay/bonus cuts, interchangeable-role mapping, and workload-reduction plan with Ops & Support leads. Engage Dutch employment counsel. |
| **2. Consultation & Filing** | Weeks 2–5 | Submit *adviesaanvraag* to the OR; file WMCO notifications with UWV and trade unions; consult in good faith on alternatives, selection, and the social plan. |
| **3. Internal & Voluntary Window** | Weeks 4–6 | Open internal vacancies first to people in affected teams. If counsel/OR agree, open a short, capped voluntary exit window before involuntary selection. |
| **4. Notification Day** | Week 6–7 | **1:1 meetings first** (own manager + HR, live/in-person) for affected staff, followed the same day by a written summary and an **all-hands + written note** for the whole company. |
| **5. Transition & Workload Reset** | Weeks 7–14+ | Day-one outplacement/training support begins; Ops & Support workflows/SLAs are officially cut or redesigned; 1:1s with all remaining staff within 2 weeks. |
| **6. Post-Transition Review** | Month 6 | Honest review of actual savings vs. hidden costs (severance, lost knowledge, attrition, SLA impact) and planning fixes so this is not repeated. |

### 4. Cut the Work, Not Just the People
Cutting 22 roles in Operations and Support without changing the work will burn out the remaining team and break customer service. Before Notification Day, Ops and Support leadership must explicitly decide and publish:
- **Which queues, manual checks, or low-value workflows stop completely** on Day 1.
- **Which SLAs or response-time targets are formally adjusted** for the next 6 months.
- **Which tools or automation fixes** get priority engineering support so remaining staff are not doing the same volume with 22 fewer colleagues.

### 5. Support Package for Affected Colleagues
Go beyond the statutory minimum (*transitievergoeding* + 1–4 months' notice) where cash allows:
- **Financial:** Statutory *transitievergoeding* plus an enhanced severance top-up (especially via VSO), and paid notice / garden leave so people can search for work without losing income.
- **Legal review:** A dedicated budget (e.g., €750–€1,500 ex. VAT) for each person to have an independent employment lawyer review their VSO.
- **Day-one transition support:** From the day notice is given (not after the last day), fund outplacement, career coaching, CV/LinkedIn support, and a **paid training/upskilling budget** per person.
- **Visas & high-impact cases:** Proactive immigration support for anyone on an **IND Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigranten) visa** or **30% ruling** (extending notice/garden leave where lawful to maximise their 3-month search period, plus immigration counsel). Extra care for people close to retirement or returning from leave.
- **Practical & human:** Allow people to keep their laptop/phone (after wiping company data) where possible; provide written references and commit to positive reference calls; offer priority for rehiring (statutory *wederindiensttredingsvoorwaarde* applies for 26 weeks) and access to confidential psychological and financial counselling.

### 6. Day-of Execution Rules
- **No surprises, no lockouts:** Every affected person hears the news live from their own manager (with HR present), never by email or by discovering their Slack/email access has been revoked. Access changes happen *after* the conversation, with dignity.
- **Same-day written pack:** Every affected employee receives a clear written summary the same day covering next steps, package details, timelines, and HR/counsel contacts.
- **Time to say goodbye:** Give people the choice of how and when to say goodbye to their teams.
- **Support the managers:** Brief and coach the managers delivering the news beforehand, and check in on them afterwards.

### 7. The 158 People Who Stay
- **Same-day clarity:** Tell the remaining team what happened, why, what it means for their roles, and whether this round is final (only state it is final if the board commits to it).
- **Re-plan together:** Hold team sessions in Week 1 post-announcement to stop/re-scope work, and 1:1s with every remaining employee within two weeks.
- **Monitor health:** Track sickness absence, engagement, and regretted exits closely for 6–12 months.
