# Reorganisation Plan & Staff Announcement (Draft)

> **Note:** This is an operational and communications draft, not legal advice. Have Dutch employment counsel and HR review the timeline, Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR) request for advice, and UWV/union notifications before sharing anything with staff.

---

## Part 1: Transition Plan

### 1. Governance & Dutch Legal Sequence (Before Any Individual Notice)

With **180 employees** and **22 proposed role reductions** in Amsterdam, two Dutch statutory thresholds apply (assuming all 22 are in the same UWV work area within three months):

| Requirement | Threshold | What it means for this plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Works Council (*WOR*, Art. 25)** | Mandatory from 50 employees | The OR has a statutory right to give advice on a significant reorganisation. Ask for advice on the *intended* decision (*voorgenomen besluit*) early enough that the OR can still influence it. Do not treat or announce the decision as final before OR advice is received. |
| **Collective Dismissal (*WMCO*)** | 20+ dismissals in one UWV work area within 3 months | Notify UWV and the relevant trade unions and consult them **before** the decision is final. Employer-initiated settlement agreements (*vaststellingsovereenkomsten*) count toward the 20. |
| **Selection & Permits (UWV)** | Business-economic dismissal | Dismissal requires UWV permission or mutual settlement agreements. Where roles are interchangeable, selection follows the reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*), not individual performance rankings. |

### 2. Leadership First

Before asking 22 colleagues to leave, leadership takes the first visible step:
- **Own the result plainly** in both the OR submission and the staff announcement—the shortfall is a leadership responsibility, not an operations or support failure.
- **Executive sacrifice first:** Freeze or cut executive bonuses, pause executive pay increases, and take a visible reduction in executive compensation/perks before frontline roles are cut. Document this in the OR advice request.

### 3. Stop Work Along With Roles

Cutting 22 roles—mostly in operations and support—without cutting the work will overload the remaining team and create compliance and customer risk in a regulated fintech. Before consultation closes, Operations, Product, and Engineering leads must agree on:
- **Which processes, manual checks, or low-value support tiers stop** (or move to self-service) on day one of the new structure.
- **Revised SLAs and response targets** that match the smaller team, signed off by leadership so remaining staff are not held to old volume targets.
- **Compliance and risk guardrails:** Confirm no mandatory regulatory or AML/KYC control loses its backup or four-eyes check.

### 4. Phased Timeline

1. **Week 1 — Legal, Leadership & Scope Prep**
   - Review the plan with Dutch employment counsel and HR; confirm whether an OR is active (or how employee representation is structured if not yet formed).
   - Finalise executive pay/bonus cuts and the list of ops/support work being stopped.
   - Draft the *adviesaanvraag* (request for advice) for the OR, the WMCO notification for UWV and unions, and the proposed social plan / transition package.
2. **Week 2 — Consultation & All-Staff Announcement**
   - Submit the *adviesaanvraag* to the OR and file the WMCO notification with UWV and unions.
   - Hold the all-staff announcement on the same day (or as aligned with the OR) so nobody hears rumours first. Frame it honestly as a **proposed** reorganisation under consultation.
   - Open a **voluntary exit window** and **priority internal redeployment** for all open roles across the company before any involuntary selection.
3. **Weeks 3–5 — OR Advice, Union Consultation & Selection**
   - Run genuine consultation with the OR and unions: share the underlying numbers, consider alternatives they raise, and adjust the plan where a better route is found.
   - Apply voluntary exits and internal transfers first; for remaining roles, apply objective *afspiegelingsbeginsel* criteria with HR and counsel.
4. **Week 6 — Final Decision & Individual Conversations**
   - Once OR advice and WMCO waiting/consultation steps are complete, confirm the final decision.
   - **Individual notifications:** Each affected person hears the news live (in person or video call) from their own manager or a senior leader, with HR present. Never by mass email, and never by revoking IT access before the conversation.
   - Deliver a written summary the same day: next steps, financial package, deadlines, and named HR/support contacts.
   - Support the managers delivering the news with briefing and time to prepare.
5. **Day of Notice Onward — Transition Support & Remaining Staff**
   - Launch transition support **from day one of notice**, not after the final working day.
   - Hold an all-hands / team reset with remaining staff the same day to confirm what happened, that the round is over (only state this if true), and which work is stopping.
   - Schedule 1:1s with every remaining team member within two weeks; monitor workload, sickness absence, and voluntary attrition over the next 6–12 months.

### 5. Transition Package (Beyond the Statutory Minimum)

Offer a single, clear package above the Dutch statutory transition payment (*transitievergoeding*), sized to what the business can afford:
- **Severance:** Statutory *transitievergoeding* plus a fixed top-up (or enhanced multiplier), plus full paid statutory/contractual notice (1–4 months by tenure).
- **Day-one transition budget:** A per-person budget for outplacement, coaching, or accredited retraining—usable from the first day of notice (in the spirit of Swedish job security councils and Finland's change-security training model).
- **Practical support:** CV/LinkedIn review, written references and committed positive reference calls, confidential psychological and financial counselling, and keeping the company laptop/phone where security/compliance policy allows (after wiping company data).
- **Rehiring & vulnerable cases:** 12-month priority for suitable internal openings, an opt-in alumni network, and dedicated legal/HR support for anyone on a work visa, on long-term leave, or close to retirement.

`[Insert 2–3 plain sentences with the real numbers: e.g., what the cash/margin gap is, what we tried first, and why we cannot close it in time without reducing our cost base.]`

We have submitted our formal request for advice to the Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad*) and notified UWV and the relevant trade unions. No individual role decisions will be finalised until that consultation is complete.
