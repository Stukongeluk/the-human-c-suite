### What to lock in before you send it

1. **Dutch employment counsel and HR sign-off (not legal advice):** Confirm your Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR) status, file the WMCO notification with UWV and the relevant unions, and verify that any voluntary settlement agreements (*vaststellingsovereenkomsten*) are counted inside the 22-role threshold.
2. **Leadership goes first:** Finalise the executive pay, bonus, or perk cuts before you announce, and name them in the placeholder in the announcement. Owning the result publicly and taking the first cut is what keeps trust with the 158 people who stay.
3. **Stop work alongside roles:** Have Operations, Product, and Engineering agree on which manual queues, checks, or SLAs stop on day one—without cutting scope, cutting 22 operations and support roles in a regulated fintech shifts overload and compliance risk onto the remaining team.
4. **Voluntary options and day-one support:** Open voluntary exits and internal redeployment before applying statutory selection (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*), and fund coaching/training from the first day of notice rather than after the last working day.

### Key assumptions

- All 22 affected roles are in the Amsterdam/UWV work area and within a three-month window, triggering WMCO.
- An active Works Council exists (or employee representation must be addressed with counsel given the 180-person headcount).
- The company can fund a transition package above the statutory *transitievergoeding* without breaching cash covenants.
