# Restructuring Plan & Staff Announcement (22 Roles — Operations & Support)

> **Context:** Reduction of 22 roles (~12% of ~180 headcount), concentrated in Operations and Support, following Board decision.  
> **Jurisdiction:** Netherlands (Amsterdam)

---

## Part 1: Execution Plan

### 1. Immediate Legal & Governance Checkpoints (Before Any Announcement)
Because this affects **22 roles** in one region (Amsterdam), Dutch collective redundancy rules apply:

- **WMCO (*Wet melding collectief ontslag*):** With 20+ dismissals for economic reasons within 3 months, you must notify the **UWV** and the **relevant trade unions** (*vakbonden*). Note that a 1-month statutory waiting period applies after WMCO notification before UWV permits can be granted or settlement agreements (*vaststellingsovereenkomsten*, VSOs) take effect—unless the unions agree in writing to waive it.
- **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR):** If you have an OR (mandatory at 50+ employees under the WOR), they must be asked for formal advice (*adviesrecht*, Art. 25 WOR) *before* a final irrevocable decision is communicated externally or implemented. Frame the Board's decision internally as an **intended decision** (*voorgenomen besluit*) if OR consultation has not yet happened.
- **Selection & *Afspiegelingsbeginsel*:** Under Dutch law, you cannot simply pick individuals in Operations and Support unless an entire unique role is eliminated. For interchangeable roles (*uitwisselbare functies*), selection must follow the age-bracket reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*), applied per role group. Have your employment lawyer validate the role-mapping and *afspiegelings* matrix before naming individuals.
- **UWV vs. Mutual Consent (VSO):** Prepare the UWV *bedrijfseconomische redenen* (economic grounds) dossier in parallel, while offering fair VSOs (statutory *transitievergoeding* + notice period + outplacement/legal fee contribution, typically €750–€1,500 excl. VAT for independent legal review) so affected employees can exit cleanly without a contested UWV procedure.
- **Regulated Operations Continuity (DNB / AFM):** If any affected Ops/Support roles touch KYC/AML, transaction monitoring, complaints handling, or incident response, document the handover and minimum staffing coverage first so regulatory SLAs are never breached.

### 2. Pre-Announcement Preparation (T-5 to T-1 Days)

| Workstream | Owner | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal & HR Pack** | HR Lead + Employment Counsel | Finalize role-impact list, *afspiegelings* check, WMCO/UWV/OR filings, VSO templates, and transition calculations. |
| **IT & Access** | IT / Security | Prepare offboarding checklist. Do **not** cut access before 1-on-1s unless there is a specific security risk; instead, plan a respectful transition window (e.g., garden leave / *vrijstelling van werk* after handover). |
| **Manager Alignment** | CEO / Ops & Support Leads | Brief direct managers of affected teams 24 hours before the announcement. Run through 1-on-1 scripts and Q&A. |
| **Communications** | CEO / People Lead | Finalize all-hands script, written staff announcement, and an internal FAQ (covering timeline, support, benefits, and what stays the same). |
| **Support Setup** | People Lead | Line up outplacement support, EAP/mental health resources, and a dedicated HR point of contact for affected staff. |

---

### 3. Announcement Day (Day 0)

1. **09:00 — All-Hands / Town Hall (15–20 mins, live + recorded for remote/absent staff):**
   - CEO delivers the message directly (no slides full of spin; clear, human, accountable).
   - State the facts: 22 roles are impacted, primarily in Operations and Support, why the decision was made, and **exactly when and how** people will hear if their role is affected.
2. **09:20 — Written Announcement Sent:**
   - Post the written message (see Part 2) in Slack/email immediately after the call so everyone has the exact details in writing.
3. **09:30–13:00 — 1-on-1 Meetings with Impacted Employees:**
   - Held privately (in-person in Amsterdam where possible, or camera-on video call) with the direct manager + HR.
   - Keep it clear, respectful, and brief: state that their role is impacted, explain the process (VSO/consultation/OR/WMCO timeline as applicable), and confirm they have the rest of the day/week off to process before next steps.
   - Follow up immediately by email with a written summary and HR contact details.
4. **14:00 — Team-Level Check-ins (Remaining Ops & Support Teams):**
   - Ops & Support leaders meet with remaining team members to acknowledge the loss of colleagues, clarify immediate workload/priorities, and give space for questions.

---

### 4. Consultation, Transition & Offboarding (Weeks 1–6+)

- **Week 1:**
  - Deliver individual VSO proposals (if applicable) with a reasonable reflection period (at least 1–2 weeks, plus statutory 14-day cooling-off period after signing).
  - Reimburse independent legal advice for each affected employee.
  - Begin structured knowledge transfer and handover for Ops/Support queues.
- **Weeks 2–4:**
  - Complete OR/WMCO statutory steps (if applicable).
  - Roll out outplacement/career coaching, CV/LinkedIn support, and reference letters.
  - Review Ops & Support tooling, ticket routing, and SLAs with the remaining team—drop or automate low-value work rather than simply expecting 22 fewer people to absorb 100% of the old volume.
- **Week 6+:**
  - Leadership check-in with remaining Ops & Support staff on workload and morale.
  - Close out offboarding and alumni network/re-hire eligibility where appropriate.

## Part 2: Staff Announcement

**Subject:** Important update on our organization and team changes  

Team,

I’m writing to share a difficult decision following our recent board review. To put the company on a sustainable footing for the next stage of growth, we need to reduce our team by **22 roles** out of our current 180, primarily within **Operations and Support**.

I know how hard this news is to hear—especially for the people whose roles are directly affected, and for the colleagues who work closely with them every day. This decision is not a reflection on anyone’s effort or commitment. Our Operations and Support teams have carried a huge load in getting us to where we are today. However, given our current cost structure and the priorities set with the board, we have to make a structural adjustment now rather than later.

### What happens next today
- **1-on-1 conversations:** Starting at **[09:30 CET]**, HR and department leads will meet individually with everyone whose role is affected. If your role is impacted, you will receive a calendar invite shortly.
- **Time and support:** Anyone whose role is affected will not be expected to work for the rest of **[today / this week]** so you have space to process this news.
- **Fair transition:** We are committed to handling every departure with care and fairness under Dutch employment standards. Every impacted colleague will receive a clear transition package, support for independent legal review, and career/outplacement assistance.
- **For Operations and Support teams:** At **[14:00 CET]**, **[Ops/Support Lead Name(s)]** will hold a team session to talk through immediate priorities and how we will support each other through the transition.

### For everyone else
I know changes like this create uncertainty, even if your own role is not affected. Today’s priority is making sure our departing colleagues are treated with respect and supported properly. Later this week, we will share a clear view of how our Operations and Support workflows will be adjusted so the remaining team isn’t simply left carrying the same workload with fewer people.

If you have questions today, please reach out to your manager, **[HR Lead Name]**, or me directly.

Thank you for the care you show one another—today especially.

**[Your Name]**
