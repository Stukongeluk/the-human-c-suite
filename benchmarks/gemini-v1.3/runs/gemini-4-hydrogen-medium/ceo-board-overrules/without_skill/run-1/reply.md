## 1. Execution Plan

### Immediate Dutch Legal & Governance Checkpoints (Before Any Announcement)
Because you are in Amsterdam and cutting **22 roles** out of 180, a few statutory rules apply immediately:

- **WMCO (*Wet melding collectief ontslag*):** At 20+ economic dismissals within 3 months in one UWV region, you must notify the **UWV** and the **relevant trade unions**. A 1-month waiting period applies after notification before dismissals/settlements can take effect (unless unions waive it in writing).
- **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR):** With 50+ employees, if you have an OR, they have a statutory right of advice (*adviesrecht*, Art. 25 WOR) *before* a final irrevocable decision is implemented. If OR consultation hasn’t happened yet, frame this internally as an **intended decision** (*voorgenomen besluit*) until their advice is received.
- **Selection (*Afspiegelingsbeginsel*):** Unless an entire unique role disappears, interchangeable roles in Operations and Support must be selected via the age-bracket reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*), not performance or discretion. Have Dutch employment counsel sign off on your role grouping and matrix before naming individuals.
- **UWV vs. VSOs:** Prepare the UWV *bedrijfseconomische redenen* dossier in parallel, while offering fair settlement agreements (*vaststellingsovereenkomsten*, VSOs) covering statutory *transitievergoeding*, notice period, and a legal-review budget (typically €750–€1,500 excl. VAT).
- **Regulated Ops Continuity (DNB / AFM):** If any affected Ops/Support roles touch KYC/AML, transaction monitoring, complaints handling, or incident response, map minimum coverage and handovers first so regulatory SLAs stay intact.

### Pre-Announcement Prep (T-5 to T-1 Days)
1. **Legal & HR pack:** Finalize the impacted role list, *afspiegelings* check, WMCO/UWV/OR filings, VSO templates, and transition calculations.
2. **Manager alignment (T-1):** Brief direct managers of Operations and Support 24 hours ahead with 1-on-1 talking points and an internal FAQ.
3. **IT & access:** Prepare the offboarding checklist, but do **not** cut access before 1-on-1s unless there is a concrete security risk. Plan for respectful handover or garden leave (*vrijstelling van werk*).
4. **Support setup:** Line up outplacement/career coaching and a dedicated HR contact for impacted staff.

### Announcement Day (Day 0)
- **09:00 — All-Hands (15–20 min, live + recorded):** Deliver the message yourself—clear, direct, and without spin. State the number (22 roles, mostly Ops and Support), the reason, and **when and how** affected people will be notified.
- **09:20 — Written announcement:** Send the email/Slack post immediately after the call.
- **09:30–13:00 — 1-on-1s:** Manager + HR meet privately with each impacted person (in-person in Amsterdam where possible). Keep it brief and respectful, outline next steps, and give them the rest of the day/week off to process. Follow up in writing immediately.
- **14:00 — Ops & Support team check-in:** Department leads meet with the remaining team to acknowledge the loss of colleagues and clarify immediate priorities.

### Weeks 1–6+ (Transition & Stabilization)
- **Week 1:** Issue VSO proposals with a reasonable reflection window (plus the statutory 14-day cooling-off period after signing) and begin structured Ops/Support handovers.
- **Weeks 2–4:** Complete OR/WMCO steps, launch outplacement support, and **explicitly cut or automate low-value Ops/Support work** so the remaining team isn’t expected to absorb 100% of the old volume.
- **Week 6+:** Check back in with Ops & Support leads on workload, SLAs, and morale.
