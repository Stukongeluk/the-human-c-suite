# Restructuring Plan & Staff Announcements (22 Roles — Operations & Support)

> **Context:** Amsterdam-based fintech (~180 employees). Board decision to reduce headcount by 22 roles (~12%), concentrated in Operations and Support.  
> **Note:** Items in `[brackets]` should be filled in or confirmed with HR and Dutch employment counsel (*arbeidsrechtadvocaat*) before execution.

---

## Part 1: Execution Plan

### 1.1 Critical Dutch Legal & Governance Checkpoints (Before Any Individual Notices)

Because you are in the Netherlands and cutting **22 roles**, several statutory thresholds apply. Confirm these with Dutch employment counsel before locking dates:

1. **WMCO (Collective Redundancy — *Wet melding collectief ontslag*):**
   - Dismissing **20 or more** employees for business/economic reasons (*bedrijfseconomische redenen*) within a 3-month period triggers WMCO—including mutual-consent exits (*Vaststellingsovereenkomsten* / VSOs) for economic reasons.
   - **Action:** Counsel must confirm whether WMCO notification to the **UWV** and relevant trade unions is required, and account for the statutory 1-month waiting period (unless waived).
2. **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad* / OR):**
   - With ~180 employees, you fall under the *Wet op de ondernemingsraden* (WOR). Under **Article 25 WOR**, a restructuring of this size requires a formal request for advice (*adviesaanvraag*) from the OR before final execution.
   - **Action:** If the OR has not yet advised on this, frame the board's decision externally/internally as an **intended decision** (*voorgenomen besluit*) until OR consultation is completed.
3. **Selection & Redeployment (*Afspiegelingsbeginsel* & *Herplaatsingsplicht*):**
   - For economic dismissals, Dutch law requires grouping interchangeable roles (*uitwisselbare functies*) and applying the **reflection principle** (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*) across age brackets—not picking individuals based on performance.
   - You must also document a genuine **redeployment check** (*herplaatsingsplicht*) against open roles across the company.
4. **Exit Route (VSO vs. UWV):**
   - Typically handled either via UWV dismissal permits or individual **Settlement Agreements (VSOs)** offering at least the statutory *transitievergoeding*, a 14-day reflection period (*bedenktermijn*), and often an outplacement budget or garden leave (*vrijstelling van werk*).
5. **Fintech Regulatory Continuity:**
   - Before finalizing which Operations/Support roles are reduced, verify minimum coverage for **KYC/AML**, fraud monitoring, complaint handling, and incident/DORA obligations so SLAs and regulatory compliance remain intact.

### 1.3 Day-1 Logistics & Dignity Checklist

- **Timing:** Announce mid-week (Tuesday or Wednesday morning) so people have immediate access to HR, legal/outplacement support, and colleagues rather than sitting alone over a weekend.
- **No surprises via IT:** Do not cut Slack/email access before a person has had their 1-on-1 conversation.
- **1-on-1 setup:** Manager delivers the core message clearly and empathetically; HR is present to handle logistics, VSO/UWV next steps, and questions.
- **Support package ready:** Have exact details on notice period, *transitievergoeding*/severance, legal-fee contribution for VSO review, outplacement support, and garden leave ready in writing.

### 2.3 Manager 1-on-1 Guide (For Affected Employees)

**Structure (15–20 minutes max; Manager + HR present):**
1. **Get to the point within the first 30 seconds** (kind, clear, no small talk that drags out anxiety):
   > *"Thank you for joining. I have very difficult news to share: as part of the restructuring announced today, your role of `[Role Title]` is being eliminated. This is a structural decision about the size and setup of Operations/Support—it is not a reflection on your performance or contribution."*
2. **Pause and let them react.** Don't rush to fill silence or over-explain the board's finances.
3. **Hand over to HR for practical next steps:**
   - Explain the process (`[VSO / UWV / OR consultation status]`), notice period, financial terms, and *herplaatsing* (redeployment) check.
   - Confirm when they will receive the written package and offer `[e.g., today/tomorrow off]` so they don't have to work through shock.
4. **Close respectfully:** Agree on how they would like to say goodbye to their team and when HR will follow up.

**Avoid saying:** *"I know how you feel"*, *"It could be worse"*, *"You'll land on your feet"*, or debating whether the board's decision was right.
