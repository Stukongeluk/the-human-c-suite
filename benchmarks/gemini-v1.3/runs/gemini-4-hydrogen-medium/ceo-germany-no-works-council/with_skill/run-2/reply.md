**The process in Berlin (not legal advice—retain German employment counsel first):**
1. **§17 KSchG threshold:** At 140 staff, 10% is **14 people**. Dismissing 15 within 30 days (including employer-initiated termination agreements) triggers a mandatory **collective dismissal notification** to the *Agentur für Arbeit* *before* issuing any notice. Errors void the dismissals.
2. **No works council:** You skip §§111–112 BetrVG social-plan negotiations, though staff can still initiate a works council election at any time.
3. **Social selection (*Sozialauswahl*):** Under KSchG (>10 staff, >6 months' tenure), you cannot pick by performance. You must document the operational need and select among comparable roles by tenure, age, dependants, and severe disability.
4. **Timeline:** Prep and *Sozialauswahl* take 3–5 weeks. With typical 3-month notice periods, notices must go out by **31 December 2026** to exit by 31 March 2027.
