### What it actually saves (estimate)
Assume ~€90k–€100k average loaded cost per Berlin software role (~€1.35M–€1.5M/year for 15).
- **Timeline:** Business case, selection, and agency filing take ~4–6 weeks, putting notices out around January. With typical 3-month notice periods, payroll drops only from **April 2027** (~9 months of 2027 savings: ~€1.0M–€1.1M).
- **Upfront costs:** Severance (*Abfindung* to settle KSchG risk, often ~0.5 months' salary per year of service), legal counsel, and transition/coaching support (~€200k–€350k total).
- **Net 2027 saving:** Roughly **€650k–€850k**, not €1.4M.

### The process in Berlin (not legal advice—engage German employment counsel)
1. **Exhaust faster levers first:** Cut cloud/licence/vendor waste, freeze hiring, stop low-value projects, and cut leadership bonuses/pay before frontline roles. If the dip is temporary, consider *Kurzarbeit* (note: the 24-month cap reverts to 12 months on 1 January 2027).
2. **Prove operational need & cut work:** Under the Dismissal Protection Act (*KSchG*), you must show roles are permanently gone and check internal redeployment.
3. **Run *Sozialauswahl* (§1(3) KSchG):** Select among comparable roles by tenure, age, dependants, and disability—never performance rankings.
4. **File §17 KSchG notice:** At 140 staff, 15 roles hits the **10% threshold** (14+), requiring advance notification to the *Agentur für Arbeit* before issuing notices or termination agreements. Errors void dismissals.
5. **Deliver decently:** Have managers tell people live with HR, fund transition coaching from day one of notice, and tell survivors what work is stopping.
