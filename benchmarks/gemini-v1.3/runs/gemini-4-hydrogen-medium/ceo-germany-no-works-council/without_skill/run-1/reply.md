**The Process (for a March 31 exit)**
1. **Check contracts now:** Berlin tech roles typically carry 3-month notice periods. To get people off payroll by March 31, 2027, notices must be served by **December 31, 2026** (earlier if contracts specify quarter-end notice).
2. **Keep it confidential:** Without a works council, you skip § 102 BetrVG consultations and a *Sozialplan*. However, if staff hear rumors and initiate a works council election, organizers gain dismissal protection and timelines slip.
3. **Build your *betriebsbedingte Kündigung* file:** Document the structural role eliminations, check for open internal roles, and run a **social selection (*Sozialauswahl*)** across comparable roles (tenure, age, dependents, disability). 4. **File and serve:** Submit the § 17 filing to the *Agentur für Arbeit*, then issue terminations or *Aufhebungsverträge* (mutual separations with severance to waive labor-court claims).
