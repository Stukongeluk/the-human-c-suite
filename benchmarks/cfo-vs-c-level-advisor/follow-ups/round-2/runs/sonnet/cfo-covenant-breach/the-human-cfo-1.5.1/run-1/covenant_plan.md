# Covenant plan for the bank review: draft for the CFO

All figures are illustrative estimates, scaled to a Dutch furniture manufacturer with 260 employees, about EUR 40m revenue, EBITDA of EUR 3.0m and net debt of EUR 10.8m (3.6x). You gave me only 3.6x, 3.5x and 260 employees. Replace every estimate with ledger, payroll and loan-agreement figures before anything leaves the building. Not legal, tax or investment advice: check the covenant definitions with your lawyer and the redundancy steps with employment counsel.

## 1. The problem in numbers

- Covenant: net debt / LTM EBITDA at 30 June 2027 (assumed calendar year; assumed definition, check the agreement).
- Forecast: 3.6x against 3.5x. The gap is small: EBITDA EUR 0.09m higher, or net debt EUR 0.30m lower, closes it.
- Sensitivity: EBITDA 5% lower gives 3.79x; 10% lower gives 4.00x; 5% higher gives 3.43x. The forecast error is bigger than the gap, so the bank will ask for a margin of safety, not just 3.5x.

| Same, downside, lowest estimates, 50% delivered | 10.56 | 2.96 | 3.56x | Narrow miss: this is why you ask the bank for a safety net |

How the job-cut rows are built: 26 jobs at an estimated fully loaded EUR 58k is EUR 1.51m a year. One-off cost is EUR 0.78m (about EUR 27k a head for the statutory transition payment, a social-plan top-up and outplacement, plus EUR 80k advisers), paid when people leave. Payback is about 6 months after exit. By mid-2028 leverage would be about 2.2x. So the cuts would help the covenant, but a year too late for the June test. Savings land after the test date and the cash goes out before it.

|---|---|---|---|
| Receivables 4 days faster and stock down 6% | EUR 0.30m to 0.90m, point 0.60m | Net debt | COO, finance; from December |
| Defer capex that is not safety or maintenance | 0.20 to 0.40, point 0.30 | Net debt | CFO, COO |
| Owner dividend or drawings paused until leverage is under 3.0x | 0.15 to 0.50, point 0.30 (owner to confirm size) | Net debt | Owner |
| Leadership bonus pool paused, management pay cut 10% for six months | 0.10 to 0.20, point 0.15 | EBITDA | Owner, CEO |
| Purchasing, energy and waste (about 1% of an estimated EUR 18m base, part-year in the LTM) | 0.03 to 0.10, point 0.06 | EBITDA | Purchasing |
| Selective price rise of about 1.5% after expected volume loss | 0.04 to 0.12, point 0.08 | EBITDA | Sales director |
| Hiring freeze, leavers not backfilled where work allows (part-year) | 0.06 to 0.15, point 0.10 | EBITDA | HR, line managers |
| Cost: advisers and bank amendment fee | about 0.05 | Net debt | CFO |

Rules for this list: no squeezing small suppliers on payment terms, no cuts to maintenance, safety or training to make the June number, and the working-capital release must be real cash, not a date shift around the test date.

## 4. What to ask the bank

1. Present the plan above, the 13-week cash forecast, and base and downside cases with headroom.
2. Ask for a headroom safety net: a covenant reset (for example 4.0x for the next two test dates) or an equity cure right. Estimated cost EUR 30k to 60k plus possible margin step-up. Cheaper and faster than a restructuring.
3. Ask whether restructuring costs are added back in the covenant EBITDA. This matters if you ever need cuts (rows above).
4. Offer monthly reporting and defined triggers.

## 5. Triggers for a contingent plan

If any trigger is hit, start the people-side process: order intake 8% below plan for two consecutive months; forecast leverage above 3.4x at the end of March; working-capital measures delivering under 50% by end of March. The contingent plan is prepared now with counsel, but not announced. If it is needed, it is one decent round, designed with the works council.

## 6. If job cuts do become necessary (Netherlands)

- Works council: mandatory at 50+ employees. It has an advisory right on reorganisations (art. 25 WOR). Advice must be asked while the decision can still change. Announcing 10% first can invite an Enterprise Chamber challenge and costs trust.
- 26 jobs is above 20 in three months in one UWV work area: notify UWV and the unions (WMCO). Settlement agreements the employer starts count towards the 20.
- Dismissal for business reasons needs UWV permission or settlement agreements; statutory transition payment applies; notice periods run one to four months.
- Who goes follows the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel): per group of interchangeable jobs and per location, by age band and then shortest service first. The owner cannot choose people, and you may lose young craftspeople. Exceptions exist: a unique function or a whole group of jobs disappearing.
- Short-time work (werktijdverkorting) covers only calamities outside normal business risk; a broader crisis scheme was before parliament in 2026. Check whether it has passed, but do not count on it.
- Employment counsel to confirm all of this.

## 8. Key assumptions and what would change this

- Covenant is net debt / LTM EBITDA, tested on calendar quarters; bank definitions of EBITDA and net debt (shareholder loans, leases, add-backs) not yet checked.
- Loaded cost per head EUR 58k; one-off per head EUR 27k.
- Lever estimates are labelled ranges; none counted as certain.
- What would change my mind: a bank that refuses any amendment; a forecast showing the gap is larger than EUR 1m; demand that will not come back.
