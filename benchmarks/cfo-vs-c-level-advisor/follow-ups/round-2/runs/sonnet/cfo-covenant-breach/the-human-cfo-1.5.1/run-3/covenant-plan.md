## 5. What to take to the bank

1. The 13-week forecast above, with your real numbers, base and downside, updated weekly.
2. The covenant calculation under the agreement's own definition, with headroom by scenario.
3. The levers, with owner, date and size, and what each scenario triggers.
4. What the owner and the leadership are contributing, in cash and pay, first.
5. A request: a covenant reset or waiver for the next two test dates (for example 4.0x stepping back to 3.5x), as insurance for the downside. Ask now, while the breach is a forecast, not a fact.
6. Check whether the agreement requires early notice of an expected breach, and what the auditor will need for going-concern purposes.

## 6. Triggers for a later, properly consulted reorganisation

Cuts are not ruled out; they are not a response to a 0.1x covenant gap. If order intake runs more than 10% below plan for two consecutive quarters, or the downside leverage exceeds 3.5x despite the plan, bring a reorganisation proposal to the works council with the numbers and alternatives, in this order: attrition and hiring freeze, redeployment and reskilling, voluntary exits, then involuntary reductions once, with a fair package, support from the day of notice and the statutory selection rules (afspiegeling). Short-time work (werktijdverkorting) currently covers only calamities outside normal business risk, so do not count on it; check whether the new crisis scheme has passed.

## 7. Employment-law flags (take counsel; I am not giving legal advice)

- The works council (mandatory from 50 employees) has an advisory right on reorganisations (article 25 WOR) and must be asked for advice before the decision, at a stage where it can still change it. Announcing first invites an Enterprise Chamber challenge and delay.
- 20 or more dismissals within three months in one UWV region triggers notification of UWV and the unions (WMCO). Dismissals for business reasons need a UWV permit. Employer-initiated settlement agreements count towards the 20.
- Statutory transition payment and notice periods apply (one to four months by service, less time the UWV procedure took).
- Selection follows the reflection principle by age group; leaders cannot choose names.
- Facility agreement, auditor and credit-insurer implications need the bank, auditor and insurer, not assumptions.

## 8. Key assumptions and what would change this

- Test date 13 weeks away; EBITDA 6,000; net debt 21,600; loaded cost 62. All estimates.
- The plan's size rests most on inventory (450) and receivables (493), the two levers that depend on customers and stock, so check them first.
- If covenant EBITDA already allows restructuring add-backs, the cuts look better at the test (3.61x) but still no better than doing nothing.
- If demand is structurally lower, not temporarily, section 6 starts sooner.
