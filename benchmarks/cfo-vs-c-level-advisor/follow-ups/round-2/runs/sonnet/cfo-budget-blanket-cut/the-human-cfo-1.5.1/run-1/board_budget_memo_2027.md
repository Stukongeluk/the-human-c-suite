# Budget 2027: reach about 93% of the 8% opex target without cutting jobs, and keep the rest on a dated decision gate

DRAFT for CFO review. Every figure marked (est.) is a placeholder built from the three facts we have (600 people, payroll 68% of opex, thin positive margin) and must be replaced with ledger and payroll data before the board sees it. Not legal, tax or investment advice; employment-law steps need counsel.

## 1. The target and why

**Board ask:** opex 8% lower next year. **CEO proposal:** every cost centre cuts 8%.

Working base (est.): payroll EUR 51.0M (600 x EUR 85k loaded), opex EUR 75.0M, non-payroll EUR 24.0M, revenue about EUR 78M, margin about EUR 3.1M (4%). The 8% target is then **EUR 6.0M**, almost twice the whole margin.

Three things must be fixed before the paper is final:
1. **Which baseline?** 8% below 2026 actuals, or below the 2027 baseline after CAO/wage indexation? At 3-4% indexation on EUR 51M, the second reading adds roughly EUR 1.5-2.0M (est.) to the gap.
2. **In-year or exit run-rate?** No route reaches a full 8% in calendar 2027 without large job losses, and even those arrive late (section 3). A run-rate exit target for Q4 2027 is reachable.
3. **What is the 8% for?** Margin, a covenant, a valuation or an owner's request. If it is margin, price and utilisation count too.

## 2. Why not 8% everywhere

An even cut ignores where cost creates revenue. In a services company, most payroll is delivery capacity that bills. An 8% cut there is 48 FTE (est.) and falls on the efficient and the bloated alike.

Research warns of higher turnover after downsizing (Trevor & Nyberg), so the 18 is a placeholder.

**Gap left:** EUR 0.42M run-rate (EUR 2.35M in-year). To close it without jobs:
- Price indexation: re-price the 40% of revenue up for renewal by 3% = about EUR 0.9M margin (est.; counts for margin, not opex).
- Utilisation: each point on about 450 billable FTE is roughly EUR 0.8M of sellable capacity (est.), valid only if sold.
- **Decision gate end of Q2 2027:** if delivered savings run below 70% of plan, open voluntary exit and redeployment before any involuntary step, with the works council involved from the start.

## 5. What we protect, and why

Training, security, quality and customer-facing capacity are not in the list. Cutting them is booked with its future cost (rehiring EUR 30-60k per leaver, quality incidents, lost renewals) beside the saving. Billable roles are only left open when utilisation data shows bench.

## 6. What stops or waits

Two internal programmes (named once the list is validated), non-essential travel, new office fit-outs, discretionary consultancy and licences with under-30% use.

## 7. Headcount

Freeze with exceptions for billable roles and critical skills; expected attrition 72 (est.); 25 not refilled; moves from bench to billable roles first. No involuntary reductions in the base plan.

## 8. Leaders first

Leadership team takes the first and largest cut (bonus pool for 2027 plus a temporary fixed-pay reduction), announced before any wider measure. Future bonus scorecard balances profit with customer retention, regretted attrition and quality, with part deferred over three years.

## 9. Risks and sensitivities

- Non-payroll delivery at 12% is the biggest assumption: at 6% the 2027 net falls by about EUR 0.98M.
- Attrition lower than 12% reduces the freeze saving: each point is about 6 leavers, EUR 0.26M run-rate (est.).
- Subcontractor cuts reduce capacity; check against the pipeline before commitment.
- Indexation (section 1) can add EUR 1.5-2.0M to the gap.

## 10. What would change this budget

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Delivered savings below 70% of plan at end Q2 | Voluntary exits and redeployment; counsel and works council engaged |
| Revenue more than 3% below plan | Re-open the target with the board; protect billable capacity |
| Covenant headroom under agreed level | Talk to the bank early; owners' contribution; 13-week cash forecast |
| Regretted attrition above 10% | Pause freeze in the affected team |

## 11. People measures reported with the financials

Regretted attrition, time to fill, utilisation, customer retention, engagement, reported quarterly next to opex.

## 12. Board decisions requested

1. Restate the target: baseline, in-year versus run-rate, and purpose.
2. Approve Option C and the end-Q2 gate.
3. Approve leaders-first measures.
4. Instruct management to involve the works council now, whatever is decided later.
