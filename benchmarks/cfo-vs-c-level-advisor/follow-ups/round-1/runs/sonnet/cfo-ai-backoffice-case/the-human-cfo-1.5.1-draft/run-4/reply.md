| | 2027 net | 2028 net | Cumulative 2027-28 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **Why A loses money.** In Sweden you negotiate with the unions first (MBL). Notice runs one to six months, and dismissing 26 to 100 people must be notified to Arbetsförmedlingen at least four months ahead. In the model only about SEK 9m of saving lands in 2027.
- **Who leaves under A.** The Employment Protection Act (LAS) selects by length of service, not by which tasks AI took over. Apart from three exemptions per employer, you can't choose who goes. The AI also delivers only part of its capacity in 2027, so contractors and overtime fill the gap.
- **Costs on top of that.** Packages, rehiring and extra voluntary leavers. In vendor surveys (Orgvue, Careerminds), 55% of leaders who cut staff for AI said they had made the wrong decisions, and only 8.4% said the result was delivered and repeatable. Treat that as a signal, not a precise rate.
- **Where B ends up.** B turns positive from the first quarter of 2028. The net run-rate saving at end-2028 is about SEK 10m a year in the base case (range roughly 0 to 14m), and it pays back in 2029. That compares with about SEK 24m a year if all 27 FTE came out. In the low case the gate tells you to stop, which costs about SEK 6-7m of pilot rather than a restructuring.

**The recommendation to the board**

1. **Pilot and freeze.** Pilot accounts payable, reconciliations and reporting in H1 2027, with a hiring freeze with exceptions from Q2. Book no payroll saving yet.
2. **Gate in July 2027.** Decide on measured results against a baseline:
   - touchless rate and error rate in accounts payable;
   - reconciliation auto-match rate;
   - close days saved;
   - review burden at or below the assumed 20%;
   - internal audit sign-off on segregation of duties and audit trail.
3. **Scale and release.** Scale only what passed. Release capacity first through attrition (about 9 leavers a year) and redeployment of up to 6 people into growing roles such as controls, data and claims support. Involuntary exits come last, once and with proper support, only if the evidence demands them.
4. **Start early with the unions.** Bring them in at the design stage, with the numbers. For people who do leave, plan transition support from the day of notice through the job security council (TRR for private white-collar staff).
5. **Protect the controls.** As an insurer you carry operational-resilience, outsourcing and model-governance expectations (for example DORA and Finansinspektionen's requirements). Compliance and counsel should confirm which apply to each use case.
6. **Leaders first.** If results disappoint, leaders' bonuses are cut before frontline jobs. Don't reward headcount reduction itself. Reward close quality, control outcomes and retention over more than one year.

One honest caution. If the board wants "AI replaces 30%" and the pilot says 15%, handing back 30% with a later date would be the real risk. A number the board can rely on is worth more than the one it asked for, and this plan gets it for about SEK 6-7m.

Two questions would change the numbers most:
- What is the 30% for: a SEK saving, a cost-income ratio, or capacity? By when? If it is cash, there are cheaper levers I haven't sized for lack of data (vendors, licences, working capital, close processes).
- Can HR confirm attrition, the applicable collective agreement for your white-collar staff, and how many open roles you could redeploy into?
