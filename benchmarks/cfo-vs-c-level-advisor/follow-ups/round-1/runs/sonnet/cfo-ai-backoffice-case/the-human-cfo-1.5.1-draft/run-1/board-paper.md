# Finance and back-office: what AI can take over, how fast, and what it costs

Board paper, draft for the CFO. Currency SEK million unless stated. Prepared November 2026 for the 2027 budget.

**Status of the numbers.** We have no pilot data and no payroll extract yet. Every figure below is an estimate, labelled as such, built to be replaced with real data (section 9). Nothing here is legal advice; Swedish employment law and the insurance collective agreement need counsel before any step affecting people.

## 1. Recommendation

Approve a staged programme, not a 30% headcount commitment for 2027.

- Fund the AI programme (estimate SEK 18.4m over 2027-2028, range 11-26m).
- Freeze back-fills in AP, reconciliations and reporting, with named exceptions.
- Let the pilots, not the target, set the number. Our base estimate is that AI can take over the work of about 20 of the 90 roles (22%) by end 2028, within a range of 12 to 29 (13% to 33%). The board's 27 roles (30%) is inside the range, but at its top end and not before 2028-2029.
- Reduce headcount through attrition and redeployment first, in step with capacity actually freed. Revisit involuntary reductions only if the gates in section 6 are passed and attrition cannot keep pace.

The headline: no route delivers a net saving in 2027. The AI programme itself costs about SEK 12m in 2027. Dismissing 27 people in 2027 costs more, in cash, than the staged plan over two years, in the base case and the low case, and roughly the same in the high case.

## 2. What the board asked, and what we can prove today

The ask: AI replaces 30% of a 90-person team (27 roles) next year. At an estimated loaded cost of SEK 750k per person (range 650-850k), that is about SEK 20m a year at full run-rate.

What we can say with evidence today: nothing about the percentage. We can say what the work is, where the automation is most plausible, what it costs, and when we will know. Outside evidence argues for caution on the date: MIT NANDA's 2025 study reported about 95% of enterprise generative-AI pilots with no measurable P&L impact (a criticised method, so a direction rather than a rate); Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by the end of 2027 on cost, unclear value or weak risk controls; and in an Orgvue survey of 1,000+ leaders, 55% of those who cut jobs for AI said they had made the wrong decisions. The last two sources are vendor or analyst surveys: a signal, not a precise rate.

|---|---|---|---|

| Net cash 2028 | 0 | +2.7 | +2.4 |
| Two-year total | 0 | -13.4 | -8.9 |
| Two-year total, low case | 0 | -18.0 | -13.0 |
| Two-year total, high case | 0 | -8.1 | -7.7 |

Limit: attrition alone caps the reduction at about 18 roles over two years. In the high case, where capacity exceeds that, the remainder would come through voluntary exits and redeployment first.

## 6. Gates: when an estimate becomes a commitment

| Gate | When | Evidence required | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Before Q1 2027 | Pilot baseline for AP and reconciliations: volumes, handling time, error rate, exception rate | Release phase-1 funding |
| 1 | End Q2 2027 | Straight-through rate, error rate no worse than the human baseline, reviewer hours, audit-trail test, no control findings | Confirm or resize the base case; no payroll saving booked before this |
| 2 | End Q4 2027 | Freed capacity measured against the 7 FTE plan; close days; regretted attrition | Extend to reporting and close, or stop |
| 3 | Mid 2028 | Run-rate capacity, total cost of ownership vs estimate | Fix the 2028-2029 headcount path; decide whether any involuntary step is still needed |

Freed hours are booked first as capacity (backlog, faster close, better data for claims and underwriting), and only then as headcount.

## 8. Rules and controls an insurer cannot skip

- **Employment (Sweden).** The Co-determination Act (MBL) requires negotiation with the unions before major changes. Under the Employment Protection Act (LAS), redundancy selection follows length of service within each operational unit and collective-agreement area, not skill or performance, with three exemptions per employer. In practice the 27 who leave would be the shortest-serving staff, often the people best placed to work with the new tools. Notice is one to six months by tenure; those dismissed have nine months' re-employment priority. Notification to the Public Employment Service is required at least four months ahead for 26-100 people. A job security council (TRR for private-sector white-collar staff, subject to the applicable insurance-sector agreement) supports people from notice. Counsel to confirm all of this.
- **Financial reporting and supervision.** Finance sits inside the reporting, solvency and internal-control framework supervised by Finansinspektionen, and the external auditor will test it. An AI vendor handling financial data is an ICT third-party arrangement under the EU's Digital Operational Resilience Act (in force since January 2025). Counsel and compliance to confirm any AI Act classification and outsourcing notification.
- **Controls.** Segregation of duties (no model that both codes and approves a payment), complete audit trails, and a named human who signs off anything that moves money or affects a person. Cutting the control and compliance staff before the controls exist in the system is the failure to avoid.

## 9. People: how the change is run

- Open the dialogue with the unions and employee representatives while the plan can still change; MBL requires it before a major change, and it is better done early. Share the real numbers.
- Offer redeployment into growing work: claims accounting, data quality, control testing, AI oversight and exception handling. Redeployment moves cost rather than removing it, so it counts as a saving only where it fills a vacancy we would otherwise have hired for; none is counted above.
- Paid learning time for everyone whose work changes (included in the programme cost), and career conversations before roles move.
- Track the people indicators next to the money: regretted attrition, absence, engagement pulse, time to fill, per quarter.
- Leaders first: the finance leadership's bonus is tied to the gates (control quality, error rate, retention, realised capacity over several years), not to a headcount number. No bonus for the reduction itself.

## 10. Other levers not counted above (unsized: no data yet)

Estimates only, excluded from every table until we have the ledger: renegotiating non-payroll finance and back-office spend (audit, actuarial and other advisers, ERP and data licences; if this is SEK 15-25m, a 5-10% saving is SEK 0.8-2.5m); stopping low-value reports and reconciliations nobody uses (perhaps 1-2 FTE, SEK 0.8-1.5m); AP terms and early-payment discounts once invoices are processed faster. These need to be sized from the ledger before the board relies on them.

## 12. Key assumptions and what would make this wrong

- Loaded cost SEK 750k per FTE (650-850k); attrition 10% a year; team split 20/18/27/25.
- Freed capacity of 12-29 FTE at steady state, reached by end 2028; only about 7 by end 2027.
- Transition package of three months' cost each if dismissals are chosen; contractor cover at 1.3x for 70% of the gap.
- The case is wrong if a pilot shows much higher straight-through rates than assumed (the dismissal route then catches up, as in the high case), if attrition is far lower than 10%, or if programme costs come in below the range.
