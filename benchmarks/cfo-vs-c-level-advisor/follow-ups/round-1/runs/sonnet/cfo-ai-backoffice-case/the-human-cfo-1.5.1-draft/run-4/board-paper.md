# AI in finance and back-office: a gated business case for 2027-2028

Draft for the board. All SEK figures are labelled estimates built on placeholders (see section 8) and must be replaced with ledger, payroll and pilot data before the paper is final. Not legal, tax or investment advice; employment-law and regulatory points need counsel.

## 1. Recommendation

Approve a two-stage programme, not a 30% headcount commitment.

- **Stage 1 (Q1-Q2 2027):** pilot AI in accounts payable, reconciliations and reporting, with a hiring freeze with exceptions from Q2. Cost: about SEK 6-7m (estimate). No payroll saving is booked.
- **Gate (end of Q2 2027):** the board decides on measured results whether to scale.
- **Stage 2 (H2 2027 to 2028):** scale where the pilot proved it. Planning case: about 18 FTE of capacity released (20% of the 90), range 10 to 26 (11% to 29%), delivered mainly through attrition and redeployment, with a run-rate net saving of about SEK 10m a year by end-2028 (range roughly 0 to 14m).
- **30% (27 FTE) is the top of the range, not the middle.** It is reachable only if the high-case task sizing is confirmed by the pilot, and not before 2028.

## 2. What problem are we solving?

A "30% of headcount" target is a solution. The underlying need is one of: a SEK saving (27 FTE is about SEK 24m a year at an assumed SEK 0.9m loaded cost per FTE), a faster and more reliable close, lower error and audit risk, or capacity for work the team cannot do today. These call for different plans. The board should state which it is. We have not yet seen the numbers for the 90 people by activity; that is the first piece of work.

Counsel and compliance should confirm which apply to each use case.

## 5. Options, costed on the same basis (SEK m, estimates)

Both options carry the same AI costs. "Net" is saving minus all costs.

| | 2027 saving | 2027 cost | 2027 net | 2028 saving | 2028 cost | 2028 net | Cumulative net 2027-28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Do nothing** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 (the cost pressure and the workload remain) |
| **A. Board plan: 27 redundancies in 2027** (base task sizing) | 9.0 | 31.9 | -22.9 | 24.3 | 28.2 | -3.9 | **-26.8** |
| A, low / high task sizing | 9.0 | 39.7 / 27.3 | -30.7 / -18.3 | 24.3 | 38.6 / 19.3 | -14.3 / +5.0 | -45.0 / -13.3 |
| **B. Gated plan: freeze, attrition, redeployment** (base) | 1.8 | 12.6 | -10.8 | 12.4 | 6.6 | +5.7 | **-5.1** |
| B, low / high task sizing | 1.0 / 2.6 | 19.4 / 9.1 | -18.4 / -6.5 | 7.0 / 14.3 | 10.0 / 4.7 | -3.0 / +9.6 | -21.3 / +3.0 |

Why A loses money in the first two years:

- **Timing.** In Sweden the union negotiation duty (MBL) comes first. Then the Employment Protection Act (LAS) applies, with notice of one to six months by length of service. Dismissing 26 to 100 people must be notified to Arbetsförmedlingen at least four months ahead. In the model the first 13 exits land in Q3 and the rest in Q4 2027, so 2027 sees only about SEK 9m of saving.
- **LAS order.** Selection is by length of service within the operational unit, not by which roles AI makes redundant. The people who leave are not the people whose tasks were automated, apart from the three exemptions per employer. The board cannot choose who goes.
- **Work still has to be done.** The AI delivers only part of its capacity in 2027. The gap is covered by contractors and overtime, assumed at 1.3 times the cost of an employee.
- **One-off and follow-on costs.** Packages and transition support (assumed SEK 0.35m per person, SEK 9.5m in total), rehiring (25% of roles refilled in 2028, as a prudent reading of surveys in which most AI-driven cutters were already rehiring), and extra voluntary exits (3 FTE a year). Surveys by Orgvue and Careerminds, both vendors, point the same way: 55% of leaders who cut for AI said they had made wrong decisions, and 8.4% said the result was delivered and repeatable as planned. Treat these as a strong signal, not exact rates.

Why B is slower but safer: it releases capacity as the pilots prove it, uses about 9 natural leavers a year (assuming 10% attrition, to be checked with HR), redeploys up to 6 people into roles that grow (controls, data, claims and finance business partnering), and keeps involuntary exits as the last step. In the base case B is cumulatively about SEK 5m negative by end-2028 and positive from 2028 Q1 on a quarterly basis, with payback in 2029. If AI costs come in at the high end while benefits stay at base, B's cumulative 2027-28 result is about -15m (2028 alone remains positive at about +2m).

Run-rate net saving at end-2028 under B, after all running costs: about SEK 9.7m base, -0.8m low, +14.2m high. In the low case the programme should be stopped or narrowed at the gate, and the gate is how we find that out cheaply.

## 6. Gate at end of Q2 2027 (proposed)

Scale only the use cases that show, against the baseline measured before the pilot:

- accounts payable: share of invoices processed without touch and the error rate after human review, with agreed thresholds (for example, at least 60% touchless at an error rate no worse than today; thresholds to be set with internal audit);
- reconciliations: share of items auto-matched and time to clear exceptions;
- reporting: close days saved and number of post-close adjustments;
- review burden: does it stay at or below the assumed 20% of freed hours?
- controls: internal audit and compliance sign-off that segregation of duties and audit trail hold;
- people: regretted attrition and sick leave in the pilot teams.

Only then does a use case convert from "capacity freed" to a payroll commitment. The pilot cost is sunk either way; this is the price of a number the board can rely on. Base rates argue for caution: about 95% of enterprise generative-AI pilots in one widely cited study showed no measurable P&L impact (the method is criticised, so a direction only), and Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by end-2027.

## 7. People and legal path

- Involve the unions early (the exact union and agreement for white-collar insurance staff to be confirmed by HR and counsel) while the plan can still change. MBL requires negotiation before major changes; bring the pilot design and the numbers.
- Use the freed capacity first for the backlog, faster close, better controls and growth in claims and underwriting support.
- If involuntary reductions are ever needed, they happen once, decently, with transition support from the day of notice through the job security council (TRR for private white-collar), study support, and priority for re-employment (generally nine months). Over 85% of displaced Swedish workers found work within a year in an OECD study, but earnings still fall on average. Counsel must confirm all notice, notification and agreement terms.
- Leaders go first: if results disappoint, leadership bonuses are cut before frontline jobs. No bonus is paid for headcount reduction as such; link incentives to the close quality, control outcomes, customer measures and retention over more than one year.
- AI Act: obligations to inform workers before using high-risk AI at work (for example monitoring or evaluating staff) apply from 2 December 2027 after the 2026 Digital Omnibus delay. Do not use the tools to rank or monitor individual employees.

| Contractor premium | 1.3 times | Assumed |
| Rehiring after cuts | 25% of roles | Assumed |

Model basis: a quarterly model computed in code; capacity ramp in the base case is 15% of steady state in Q3 2027, 30% in Q4 2027 and 100% in Q4 2028. Steady state is only reached at the end of 2028 in all cases.

What would change this: a pilot showing high-case task sizing early (scale faster, and 27 FTE of capacity becomes realistic in 2028); a regulatory or audit finding on controls (slow down); attrition well below 10% (add voluntary exits before any involuntary step); an unexpected revenue or solvency pressure that makes cash the binding constraint (reopen the cost options in order of least harm, including spend, vendors and working capital, none of which are sized here for lack of data).
