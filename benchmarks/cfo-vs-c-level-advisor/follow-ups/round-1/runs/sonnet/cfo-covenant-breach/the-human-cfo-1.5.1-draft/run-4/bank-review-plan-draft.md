Every figure in EUR thousand unless stated. **All figures are labelled estimates scaled to the one fact we have (3.6x forecast against 3.5x at the end of Q2). ## 1. The size of the problem

Assumed base (estimate): revenue 45,000; EBITDA 4,500 (10% margin); net debt at 30 June 2027 16,200 (= 3.6x).

| Way to close the gap | Amount | Share of base |
|---|---|---|
| Net debt lower by | 450 | 2.8% of net debt |
| or LTM EBITDA higher by | 129 | 2.9% of EBITDA |
| Sensitivity: each 1% EBITDA miss adds roughly | 0.05x | |

The gap is about 0.1x. A 10% job reduction (26 people) is a response sized for a much larger problem than the one in the forecast.

## 6. What to ask the bank for

1. Early dialogue now, before the review, with this plan, rather than a surprise after the Q2 numbers.
2. A covenant reset or holiday for the 30 June and 30 September tests (for example 4.0x for two tests), or a waiver agreed in advance, as insurance against the downside rows above. Expect a fee; the amount is a labelled 30 to 60 in the plan costs.
3. Agreement that the actions in section 7 are the plan, with monthly reporting of net debt, stock and receivables.
4. Confirmation of the covenant definition: gross or net debt, treatment of one-off and restructuring costs, leases, subordinated shareholder loans and any equity cure.

Before talking to the bank, have counsel read the facility agreement for any duty to notify the bank of an expected breach, and for cross-default clauses.

## 8. If the plan falls short: the people decision

A structural reduction is not off the table. It is a decision with a trigger and a process, not an announcement.

- Trigger (to agree with the owner): if by end of February the forecast LTM EBITDA is more than about 8% below the base, or the levers in section 3 are delivering below the low case, or order intake shows demand is structurally lower and not seasonal.
- Process in the Netherlands (check with employment counsel): the works council has a right to give advice on a reorganisation before the decision is taken (article 25 WOR) and may go to the Enterprise Chamber; 26 dismissals in one UWV work area within three months trigger notification to UWV and the unions (WMCO); who goes is set by the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel) by age band within groups of interchangeable jobs, not chosen by management, and within each age band the shortest-serving go first, which can mean the newest hires, including people with skills you need. Employer-initiated settlement agreements count towards the 20.
- Time: advice, notification, permit and notice usually put the first exits months out, so a reduction decided in February or March helps the 30 September and 31 December tests, not 30 June.
- If it happens: once, with a package above the statutory minimum, support from the day of notice, leaders' pay cut first, and the work reduced, not only the people.
