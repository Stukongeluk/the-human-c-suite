# Board pack: savings model for the proposed 120 redundancies

**Status: draft for the finance director. Every figure marked (E) is an estimate and must be replaced with payroll, HR and counsel data before it goes to the board. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.**

## 1. The answer in two numbers

- **Run-rate (full year 2028 onward):** about €9.0m gross (120 x €75,000). The CEO's €9m is this number.
- **In 2027, on a realistic timeline:** about **€3.0m gross and about -€4.1m net cash** (base case). The €9m is a full-year figure for people who have already left; in 2027 almost nobody has, and the costs of getting them out come first.

Cumulative cash turns positive in **Q3 2028**. As proposed, the programme does not deliver €9m in 2027 under any timeline: even if all 120 left on 31 March, 2027 gross is €6.75m before a euro of severance.

## 2. Why 2027 is so far below €9m

1. **Consultation and notice come before any saving.** Announcing next month starts the clock; it does not stop anyone's pay. Dutch works council advice (art. 25 WOR) and, above 20 dismissals in one UWV work area within three months, notification and consultation under WMCO; Dutch notice runs 1 to 4 months. German collective dismissal notification (§17 KSchG), reconciliation of interests and social plan with any works council (§§111-112 BetrVG), social selection (§1(3) KSchG) and notice up to 7 months (§622 BGB). Savings start on the exit date, not the announcement date.
2. **The costs land in the same year.** Severance, transition payments or social plan, outplacement, advisers.
3. **The work does not vanish with the people.** Overtime, contractors and rehiring eat part of the gross saving.

## 8. What would change this model

- A real structural overcapacity: the work for these 120 roles is gone (stopped contracts, sites, services). Then the run-rate saving is real and a smaller, properly consulted programme belongs in the 2028 plan.
- Actual notice lengths, tenure and works council agreement: they move savings by quarters.
- Covenant terms: if the €9m protects a covenant test, talk to the bank now; a waiver may cost less than €4m of net cash outflow in 2027.

## 9. Humans, law and process

- Works councils: Dutch advice right and German co-determination apply while the decision can still change. Announcing a fixed number before consultation risks challenge and delay. Confirm whether a German works council exists and which collective agreements apply.
- Counsel and auditors to confirm thresholds (WMCO, §17 KSchG), selection rules, notice, provisions and disclosure.
- Pre-mortem and people plan: support funded from the day of notice, one round only, work removed with the roles.
