I've built the model, but it does not support "€9 million in 2027" and I would not put that line in the board pack. The €9m is the full-year run-rate (120 x €75,000). In 2027 the base case gives about **€3.0m gross and about -€4.1m net cash**, and cumulative cash turns positive only in Q3 2028.

**Why 2027 is so far below €9m**
- Announcing next month starts consultation; it doesn't end anyone's salary. Dutch works council advice and notification (WMCO, 20+ in one UWV area), German co-determination, collective dismissal notification and social selection, then notice periods of 1-4 months (NL) and up to 7 months (DE). Savings begin on the exit date.
- Costs come first: I estimate €5.7m one-off (severance and social plan €4.8m, outplacement €0.5m, advisers €0.4m), assuming a 60/60 DE/NL split. These are estimates, not data.
- The work doesn't disappear: overtime, contractors, rehiring and extra leavers take about €1.4m in 2027 and about €1.7m in 2028.
- Even if all 120 left on 31 March, 2027 gross is only €6.75m before severance.

**Base case (€ thousand)**

| | Q1 27 | Q2 27 | Q3 27 | Q4 27 | FY27 | FY28 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gross saving | 0 | 225 | 1,013 | 1,800 | 3,038 | 9,000 |
| One-offs | -728 | -2,048 | -1,848 | -1,056 | -5,680 | 0 |
| Hidden costs | 0 | -244 | -531 | -649 | -1,424 | -1,688 |
| **Net cash** | **-728** | **-2,067** | **-1,366** | **95** | **-4,066** | **7,312** |

Severance +/-40% moves 2027 net between -€2.2m and -€6.0m. In 2028, roughly €29m of lost revenue (at an assumed 25% margin) would wipe out the net saving. If announced with a formal plan before year-end, the severance is likely provided in the 2026 accounts (check with the auditors), which would make 2027 P&L about +€1.2m but cash still -€4.1m.

**Options on the same basis:** do nothing (€0, €9m gap open); the 120 redundancies (-€4.1m cash in 2027, €7.3m from 2028, irreversible); or a no-job-cuts package of third-party spend, pricing leakage, hiring freeze and attrition, and leadership pay and bonus cuts first (indicatively about +€4.7m in 2027, built on assumed spend and revenue bases you need to replace from the ERP). That second package doesn't close €9m either, and overlaps with the redundancies if both are counted. If the work truly is gone, a smaller, consulted programme belongs in the 2028 plan, alongside the first package now.

My suggestion for the board: show the net-by-quarter table, ask for authority to open consultation rather than announce a fixed number (announcing 120 before the works councils have advised risks delay and challenge), and put leadership pay on the first page.

Full draft with assumptions, scenarios, sensitivities and legal flags: `board-pack-savings-model.md`. This is not legal, tax or accounting advice; counsel and auditors need to confirm thresholds, notice, provisions and selection rules.
