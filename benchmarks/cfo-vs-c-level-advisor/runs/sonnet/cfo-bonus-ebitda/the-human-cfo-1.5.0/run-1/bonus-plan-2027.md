# 2027 Management Bonus Plan (DRAFT for Remuneration Committee)

Company: PE-owned healthcare software company, about 350 people. Draft for the board; not legal, tax or investment advice. All weights, thresholds and percentages below are proposals to be calibrated against the 2027 budget; none are derived from company data, which I have not seen.

## 1. Purpose

Reward management for growing a profitable, durable business that a buyer will pay for: retained customers, recurring revenue, safe and reliable software, and a team that stays. EBITDA remains the largest single measure, but not the only one.

## 2. Who participates

Executive team and direct reports (to be listed by the Remuneration Committee). Participants are named in the plan schedule, not in this draft. Employees outside the plan keep their existing arrangements; no one's pay is reduced to fund this plan.

## 4. Definition of Bonus EBITDA

1. Start from reported EBITDA in the audited (or review-ready) accounts. Every plan figure is shown next to it in a reconciliation table, each adjustment named and explained.
2. **Restructuring costs stay in.** Severance, notice, consultation, outplacement, advisers' fees and related one-time costs reduce Bonus EBITDA. The company has restructured in each of the last three years, so these costs are part of running the business; calling them one-off would overstate profit and make each cut look free.
3. Credit-agreement or valuation EBITDA may still add restructuring back. That is a separate definition for lenders and the exit model; it is not used for pay. Both are reported side by side.
4. Permitted exclusions, only with Remuneration Committee approval and disclosure: acquisition transaction costs, sponsor fees, and a named, board-initiated programme that is genuinely first-of-its-kind AND passes a payback test (cost recovered from net savings within 18 months, verified after 12). A charge similar to one in the prior two years cannot be excluded.
5. Capitalised development: capitalisation policy frozen at the 2026 basis; any increase in the capitalised share counts as expense for bonus purposes.
6. M&A: acquired EBITDA included only from the date of acquisition, on budgeted basis.
7. No payment is made for headcount reduction as such. Savings from reductions are counted only after the one-off cost, and only to the extent they appear in actual EBITDA.
8. The reconciliation is reviewed by the Audit Committee or the auditors before payout.

## 5. Payout, deferral and leaders first

- 75% paid in cash after the 2027 results are final; 25% deferred and vesting in equal parts over the following 2 years (so each year's award is earned over 3 years in total).
- Deferred amounts are forfeited on cause termination; at an exit, unvested amounts accelerate only if the quality/security and customer-retention gates were met for the last full year (to avoid a pre-sale cost cut being rewarded). Exit mechanics to be agreed with counsel.
- **Malus and clawback:** deferred and paid bonuses may be reduced or recovered for restatement, misconduct, or results later found to have been achieved at the cost of customers, security or the team (for example a cut that produced churn in the following two years). Look-back: 3 years.
- **Leaders first:** if EBITDA is below threshold, executive bonuses are zero before any involuntary reduction is proposed; if the company ever has to cut jobs, the executive team takes the first and largest cut in pay and bonus, and says so. This is written into the plan in advance.
- **No bonus on a cut that costs more than it saves:** where a restructuring's actual net saving at 12 months is below its cost, the relevant leaders' EBITDA payout is reduced by the shortfall.

## 6. Restructuring governance

Any restructuring proposed in 2027 needs a board paper showing the saving net of all costs, by quarter, the alternatives tried first (spend, pricing, hiring freeze, attrition, redeployment, shared sacrifice), the revenue and retention risk, and the legal process required (consultation, works council or employee representation, notice and severance rules in the countries concerned; take legal advice). The Remuneration Committee sees the same paper.

## 7. Governance

Remuneration Committee owns the plan; Audit Committee/finance verifies the numbers; management cannot change definitions mid-year. Mid-year changes need a recorded decision with reasons. Plan reviewed after year-end; findings go to the next plan.

## 8. Open items before sign-off

1. Jurisdiction(s) and employment-law and works-council constraints on bonus terms.
2. Participant list and target bonus levels (% of salary).
3. 2027 budget, the 2024 to 2026 restructuring costs and savings actually delivered, and covenant EBITDA definition.
4. Expected exit window, to set vesting and acceleration terms.
5. Baselines for retention, attrition, engagement, uptime and security metrics.
6. Tax and accounting treatment of deferral (advisers).

Weights and ranges are placeholders; 18-month payback and 90%/108% EBITDA range are estimates. Section 4 point 4 is a fallback offered to the board, not my first recommendation.
