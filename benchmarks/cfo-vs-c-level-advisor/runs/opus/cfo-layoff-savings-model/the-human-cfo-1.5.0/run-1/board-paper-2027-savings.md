# 2027 savings: what 120 redundancies would deliver, and a staged alternative

*Draft for the board pack. Prepared by Finance, 21 November 2026. Model: `savings-model-2027.xlsx` (every figure below comes from it). All amounts in euros. Apart from headcount, the 120 roles, the €75,000 average cost and the legal rules, every input is an estimate (listed in section 9) until payroll, the ledger, contracts and counsel confirm it.*

## 1. Recommendation

**Do not put 120 redundancies into the 2027 plan as €9.0 million of savings.** On the timetable Dutch and German law allows, they save €3.3 million gross in 2027 and cost €5.1 million up front. Between December 2026 and December 2027 they take €2.9 million more cash out of the business than they save. They pay back in 2028, at €6.7 million net.

**Instead, start a staged plan on 1 January 2027:** leaders' pay first, third-party spend, pricing and revenue leakage, and a hiring freeze with exceptions plus redeployment. On our estimates it adds €4.6 million to the 2027 P&L (€3.3 million in cash; P&L range €2.1–5.9 million) and about €8.4 million in 2028, with no dismissals. Any structural reduction should then be decided once, at the April 2027 board, on validated numbers. That decision should follow early talks with the works councils, with voluntary routes first.

**No option reaches €9 million inside 2027.** If €9 million in 2027 is a hard number, for example a covenant test, the conversation to have now is with the bank.

## 2. What the board is solving for

The €9 million target came without its purpose. Three answers would change the plan:

- **What the €9 million protects.** If it is a covenant test, cash and the bank come first, and redundancies make 2027 cash worse. If it is a budget promise to owners or a margin gap, pricing and spend come first. If it funds a payout or an exit valuation, the board should see that trade-off stated openly.
- **Inside 2027, or as a run-rate by the end of 2027.** Both routes reach or come close to a €9 million run-rate from 2028 (C about €8.4 million, B2 about €12 million a year). Neither delivers €9 million inside 2027.
- **Which work stops.** A saving only sticks if the work goes with the roles. A contract, site or service line that is ending makes a stronger and different case: a targeted reduction there, rather than 120 roles spread across the group.

Reading the numbers:

- **Timing.** Nobody can leave before consultation and notice have run their course. We assume Dutch exits from April to September 2027 and German exits from June to December 2027 (section 4).
- **One-off costs of €5.1 million.** This covers:
  - severance and social plans of €3.7 million. In the Netherlands we assume 1.5 times the statutory transition payment, about €25,000 per person. In Germany we assume 0.75 months' pay per year of service, about €36,000 per person. Both assume 10 years' average service.
  - €0.6 million of transition support, funded from the day of notice.
  - €0.8 million for counsel, works council advisers, conciliation board costs and the project team.
- **Hidden costs of €1.2 million in 2027 and €2.3 million in 2028.** These are:
  - work that still has to be done;
  - lost contribution on €1.5 million of revenue at risk;
  - 12 roles refilled in 2028;
  - about 19 extra voluntary leavers who must be replaced.

  The last estimate is cautious. Trevor and Nyberg (2008) found that a 1% downsizing was associated with 31% more voluntary turnover the following year. For an 8.6% reduction we assume a total rise of only 25%.
- **P&L versus cash.** The December announcement may set out the plan's main features to the people affected. If it does, the auditors may require the €4.3 million provision in the 2026 accounts (under IFRS this follows IAS 37 and IAS 19; each entity's framework needs checking). The 2027 P&L would then show +€1.5 million while 2027 cash is −€2.8 million. If the provision falls in 2027, the 2027 P&L is about −€2.8 million. The pack should show both years and both measures. Any adjusted EBITDA that leaves out the charge must be reconciled to reported EBITDA. If the group booked restructuring costs in 2025 or 2026, this charge should not be called non-recurring.
- **After 2028:** about €7.1 million a year. That is €9.0 million less residual cover, refilled roles and lost contribution.

## 4. Why the saving arrives late

| | Netherlands (assumed 60 roles) | Germany (assumed 60 roles) |
|---|---|---|
| Employee representatives | Works council advice on the intended decision (art. 25 WOR), asked while it can still change the plan. Implementation waits one month if the decision departs from the advice | A reconciliation of interests and a social plan negotiated with each works council (§§111–112 BetrVG). If talks fail, a conciliation board can impose the social plan |
| Collective dismissal rules | 20 or more dismissals in one UWV area within three months: UWV and the unions are notified (WMCO). Settlement agreements count towards the 20, and there is a one-month waiting period | Consultation, then notification to the employment agency before notices go out (§17 KSchG). Errors can make the dismissals void |
| Who is selected | The reflection principle across age groups within interchangeable roles | Social selection by service, age, dependants and severe disability (§1(3) KSchG). Some protected groups need prior approval |
| Notice | 1–4 months by length of service, minus the time a UWV procedure takes | Up to 7 months to the end of a month (§622 BGB). Collective agreements may differ or protect older staff |
| Assumed in the model | Settlements or notices in March 2027; exits April–September | Agreement with the works councils by the end of April; notices in May; exits June–December |

**What next month's announcement can be.** In both countries, an announcement in December can only be an *intended* decision that opens consultation. Presented as a final decision, it invites an appeal to the Enterprise Chamber in the Netherlands. In Germany, implementing before trying for a reconciliation of interests exposes the company to compensation claims (§113 BetrVG). Either way, it can also bring the provision into 2026. It should not land in the week before Christmas. HR and counsel still need to confirm three things that change both timing and cost:

- whether every German site has an elected works council;
- which collective agreements apply;
- whether a standing social plan already exists.

- **2027:** net cash is negative in every case. To break even in 2027, every exit would have to come more than four months earlier than the legal path allows.
- **2028:** about €19 million a year of extra lost revenue (at a 35% contribution margin, on top of the €1.5 million assumed) would wipe out the 2028 saving.
- **Two years:** about €7.3 million a year of extra lost revenue from July 2027 would wipe out the whole net saving to December 2028. That is roughly 3–4% of estimated revenue.
- The two numbers that matter most are **exit timing**, driven mainly by consultation in Germany, and **package level**.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

| B. 120 redundancies, announced December | −2.9 | 1.5 (after −4.4 in 2026) | 6.7 | 3.8 | 7.1 | 5.1 | 120 | No |
| B2. B plus leaders' pay, spend and pricing | −0.7 | 4.9 (after −4.4 in 2026) | 12.4 | 11.7 | 12.2 | 5.5 | 120 | Partly |
| **C. Staged plan (recommended)** | **3.3** | **4.6** | **9.0** | **12.3** | **8.4** | **0.75** | **0** | **Yes, until April** |

- B2 and C use the same levers that don't depend on headcount, so they compare like for like. The only difference between them is the headcount route.
- C beats B2 by about €4.0 million of cash in 2027 and roughly matches it over two years (12.3 against 11.7).
- B2 earns about €3.8 million a year more from 2029, but only if the work behind the 120 roles really disappears.
- "Do nothing" is not free. Whatever the €9 million protects stays at risk.

**The case for announcing now, at its strongest.** It is one clear decision instead of a drawn-out one. It gives a higher recurring saving and a clear signal to owners and lenders. A freeze can drift, or bite in the wrong places. If the work behind the 120 roles is truly ending, every month of delay costs money. If all 120 roles turn out to be needed, starting four months later costs about €2.4 million over 2027–2028. That is the price of the April checkpoint, and we think it is worth paying, for three reasons:

- Announcing now does not bring the saving into 2027.
- It commits €5.1 million and 120 people's jobs before we know which work stops.
- The levers that carry option C are needed under either route.

Range for 2027 P&L: €2.1 million if spend and pricing deliver half and attrition is 5%; €5.9 million if they deliver 30% more and attrition is 8%. For 2028 the range is €4.3–10.8 million.

**Steps and decision gates**

1. **December 2026 board:** approve the plan. The leaders announce their own pay cut first, and the board agrees the April trigger below.
2. **January 2027:** share the financial position and the plan with the Dutch works council and the German works councils, and ask for their ideas while the plan can still change. Start the freeze, spend and pricing work.
3. **By the end of January:** gather the data that validates the estimates:
   - spend by supplier;
   - a review of contract indexation clauses;
   - attrition by site;
   - an analysis of which work could stop, and where.
4. **February 2027 board:** validated sizes, with an owner and a date for every lever.
5. **April 2027 board, with Q1 actuals: the checkpoint.** A structural reduction is triggered if either:
   - the validated plan is tracking more than €1.5 million short of a €9 million run-rate for 2028; or
   - specific work is ending.

   If triggered, consultation opens on that specific scope. Internal vacancies are offered first, then voluntary departures and early retirement; involuntary reductions cover only what remains, in one round. For example, 40 voluntary departures from Q3 2027 would cost about €0.9 million net in 2027 and add about €2.8 million in 2028.

**If the €9 million is really about cash or a covenant:**

- **Working capital.** Ten days less in receivables on an estimated €180–220 million of revenue frees about €5–6 million of one-off cash (an estimate, not counted above).
- **Capex timing.**
- **An early conversation with the bank**, with a 13-week cash forecast and covenant headroom under base and downside cases.

**If the drop in demand is temporary:** short-time work is available in Germany. From 1 January 2027 its maximum duration is 12 months unless extended, and it needs works council agreement. The Dutch scheme currently covers only calamities.

## 8. People

- **Option B** affects 120 people (8.6% of the workforce), plus an estimated 19 colleagues who leave on their own. Within each pool the law decides who goes, not management, so we cannot promise to keep particular skills.
- **Option C** ends nobody's contract in 2027, but it leaves about 49 vacancies unfilled. Workload has to be re-planned and some work stopped; otherwise the freeze turns into overtime and burnout.
- **If a reduction follows in April**, it should meet these standards:
  - one round only;
  - support funded from the day of notice: coaching, a training budget and job-search help;
  - packages above the statutory floor where the company can afford it;
  - every person told by their own manager, in person;
  - the people who stay told the same day what changes for them and what work stops.

## 10. What the board should see every month

- **Money:** net savings by lever (P&L and cash) against this plan; cash and covenant headroom.
- **People:** regretted attrition, absence, overtime hours and agency spend (early signs that the freeze is overloading people), safety incidents, and an engagement pulse every quarter.
- **Customers:** on-time performance or service levels, complaints, renewals and acceptance of price changes.

## 11. What would change this recommendation

- **The €9 million protects a 2027 covenant test.** Then the bank and cash come first; redundancies make 2027 cash worse.
- **A contract, site or service line is ending.** Then consultation on that scope should start now, not in April.
- **Validation puts spend and pricing at the low end.** Then a structural reduction in April becomes more likely.
- **Attrition is well below 7%, or falls where the work is growing.**
- **Most affected roles are in the Netherlands.** Then option B is faster and cheaper (2027 cash −€1.3 million), but it is still negative in 2027.
