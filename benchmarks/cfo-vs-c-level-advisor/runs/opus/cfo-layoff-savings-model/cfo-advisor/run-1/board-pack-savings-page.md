**Scenarios:**
- **Downside:** German negotiations go to the Einigungsstelle (conciliation board), so German notices go out at the end of September. The Dutch works council (OR) advises against the plan, so Dutch notices go out at the end of July. Social plans are richer, leakage is 20%, and 15 billable roles are cut while demand holds.
- **Base:** German agreement by the end of April, notices at the end of May. Dutch termination agreements at the end of April. Mid-range social plans, 10% leakage.
- **Upside:** Fast agreement in both countries, near-statutory severance, 5% leakage.

**Reading:** No scenario delivers €9m in 2027, and none delivers €9m a year even at run-rate. Even if every notice in both countries went out by the end of January, which the consultation rules make impossible, 120 exits would save at most about €5.2m in 2027.

## 6. Alternative path for the board to weigh

**Option B: hiring freeze with exemptions, no compulsory exits.** Assumes 8% voluntary turnover and half of all leavers not replaced, with safety-critical and billable roles exempt. In the base case:
- about 56 fewer roles by Dec 2027 and about 112 by Dec 2028
- about **€1.5m net in 2027**, after a €0.2m internal-mobility budget and with no severance
- **€6.6m cumulative over 2027-28**, against €5.4m for the redundancy route

Its weaknesses: it cannot choose which roles go, turnover falls in a downturn, and it reaches the same headcount a year later. Downside (6% turnover, 35% not replaced): €0.5m in 2027.

**Recommended structure:**
1. Freeze from December with exemptions.
2. Cut agency, contractor and overtime spend.
3. Run a voluntary leaver scheme.
4. Use compulsory redundancies only for the residual, sized role by role during consultation.

The pieces are not additive: every role that goes through the freeze or the voluntary scheme is one fewer compulsory exit and one fewer severance payment.

## 7. Risks not in the numbers

- Resignations of people the business wants to keep, from the moment a number is announced.
- Lower productivity during 5-10 months of consultation and notice.
- Revenue loss if billable technicians are cut while demand holds.
- Procedural risk. German collective-dismissal errors can make dismissals invalid. UWV refuses Dutch permits if the OR procedure is incomplete. Protected groups reduce the pool and add delay: works council members, severely disabled staff, pregnancy and parental leave, long-serving staff protected by collective agreements, and Dutch staff on sick leave.
- If the group has a European Works Council, its agreement probably requires information and consultation as well.

## 8. Decisions requested from the board

1. **Note** that the 120-role proposal is estimated to deliver €0.8-3.9m of underlying savings in 2027 (base €2.9m), against €3.1-8.1m of one-off costs (base €5.1m), and €6.1-8.6m a year from 2028.
2. **Decide** whether to open consultation on a proposal, rather than announce a decision, and on which path: A (compulsory, 120) or a blended path starting with a hiring freeze now.
3. **Ask** management to bring a bridge from the 2027 redundancy savings to the €9m target, using non-headcount levers, to the next meeting.
