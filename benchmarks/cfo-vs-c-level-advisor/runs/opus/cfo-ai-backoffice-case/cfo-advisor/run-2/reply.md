**CFO Advisor: Business case for AI in finance and back office (2027)**

**Bottom line**

I built the case the board asked for: 27 of 90 roles replaced by AI in 2027. The numbers don't support it. A gated programme that automates first and shrinks through attrition gives the board a better deal:

- 8–9 fewer roles in 2027, almost all by not backfilling leavers.
- About 22 fewer roles (24%) by end-2029 in the base case, and about 30 in the upside.
- A positive five-year return. Making 27 redundancies next year most likely gives a five-year loss.

Confidence: Medium on the conclusion. Low on the exact SEK figures until they are re-run on your payroll and process data.

Tags: **[Verified]** = checked against a cited source; **[Medium]** = one source or a reasoned inference; **[Assumed]** = my input, to be replaced with yours.

**What**

- **Every version loses money in 2027.** The recommended path nets −SEK 13.5m in 2027. The board's version nets −SEK 30.3m. Severance (SEK 10.3m) and consultants covering work the tools can't do yet (SEK 12.4m) cost more than the half-year of savings. [Assumed inputs; method in the attached paper]
- **27 roles in 2027 isn't reached even in my upside.** The upside is about 14 roles. The base case is 8–9. Outside evidence points the same way:
  - Gartner predicted that by 2026, 90% of finance functions would use AI but [fewer than 10% would see headcount reductions](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-09-12-gartner-predicts-that-90-percent-of-finance-functions-will-deploy-at-least-one-ai-enabled-tech-solution-by-2026).
  - Even best-in-class AP teams process only [49.2% of invoices touchless](https://www.apexanalytix.com/resources/blog/ardent-partners-key-ap-metrics-2025/). [Verified]
- **Swedish law means the earliest exits are mid-2027, and you can't choose who goes.**
  - You must negotiate under MBL before deciding.
  - Varsel to Arbetsförmedlingen takes at least [4 months for 26–100 people](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/for-arbetsgivare/anmal-varsel-och-permittering).
  - Under [LAS](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-1982-80.text), notice runs 1–6 months and selection is last in, first out with at most three exemptions. Those dismissed also have 9 months' priority for re-employment.
  - So the leavers will be your shortest-tenure staff, not the people whose work was automated. If the AI underdelivers, you must offer the jobs back to them first. [Verified; your collective agreement can change details]
- **Five-year NPV at 8% favours the recommended path in every scenario.**
  - Recommended path: +SEK 4.9m in the base case and +SEK 22.7m in the upside. In the downside, if we stop at Gate 1, the loss is capped at about −SEK 12.6m.
  - Board's version: −SEK 24.7m in the base case, or −SEK 2.7m if the remaining 63 people absorb half the gap. [Assumed]
- **One unmeasured number decides the case: how much capacity the tools actually free.** Below about 85–90% of my base assumptions, even the recommended case turns negative. So the first ~SEK 2m pays for a proof of value on your own invoices and reconciliations, before the big spend. [Assumed]

**Why this matters**

The board will hold you to whatever number lands in the 2027 budget. The board is probably thinking of a SEK 23m saving (27 × SEK 0.85m). In practice it becomes a SEK 30m cost in 2027. The FY2027 close and the Solvency II annual reporting would then be done by 63 people on unproven tools. That outcome costs a CFO credibility. Klarna and Commonwealth Bank of Australia both [publicly reversed AI-driven cuts](https://www.itpro.com/business/business-strategy/australias-biggest-bank-cut-staff-for-ai-then-it-backtracked-and-its-one-of-many-scrapping-plans-for-automated-customer-support-teams).

**How to act**

1. Pull the data that replaces my assumptions: payroll by team, 36-month attrition and retirements due 2027–29, invoice and reconciliation volumes, current tools, and finance consultant spend. **Owner:** you, the Head of HR and the Head of Accounting. **Deadline:** 11 Dec 2026.
2. Put vacancy control on all 90 roles, so nothing is backfilled without your sign-off. Each role not backfilled saves SEK 0.66–0.99m a year. **Owner:** you. **Start:** 1 Dec 2026.
3. Have labour counsel review the plan against your collective agreement, and include an MBL negotiation on the AI rollout itself. **Owner:** General Counsel and Head of HR. **Deadline:** 18 Dec 2026.
4. Classify the finance processes and vendors under DORA, and get indicative vendor quotes. **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer, CISO and CIO. **Deadline:** 18 Dec 2026.
5. Re-run the attached model with real figures and take Option B to the board. Then start Gate 0: a 2-week time study plus a proof of value with 2–3 vendors. **Owner:** you and the Head of Finance Operations. **Deadline:** Gate 0 decision by end-Feb 2027.

**Risks**

- **The tools free less capacity than planned.** Then you've spent SEK 12m in 2027 for 3–4 roles. Trigger: at Gate 1 (Jul 2027), capacity below 50% of plan. Response: cancel wave 2 and keep AP.
- **The message "AI replaces 30%" gets out.** Your IFRS 17 and Solvency II specialists, the people you need to configure and test the tools, leave first.
- **DORA, GDPR or audit work is left out of the plan.** Go-live slips a quarter, which roughly halves the 2027 savings.

**Your decision**

- **Option A: the board's version, 27 redundancies in 2027.** The headline is SEK 23m a year. Realistically, 2027 nets −SEK 30m and the five-year NPV is about −SEK 25m in the base case. It carries the highest legal, close-quality and reputational risk.
- **Option B: automate first, shrink through attrition, gated.** You get 8–9 roles in 2027 and about 22 by 2029 in the base case (30 in the upside). Five-year NPV is about +SEK 5m in the base case and +SEK 23m in the upside, with about one forced exit.
- **Option C: AP only in 2027, decide the rest in 2028.** It has the lowest spend and risk but the smallest gain, 3–5 roles, and the board may read it as timid.
- **My recommendation is Option B.** Restate the 30% as a capacity target for end-2029, with hard 2027 targets the board can check every quarter.

**Critic pre-screen** (this affects about 30% of the team, so I stress-tested my own case)

- **Weakest point:** my capacity percentages are informed assumptions, not measurements. They could be too low as well as too high.
- **Missing perspective:** what is behind the board's 30%? It could be a cost-ratio target, peer pressure, or a solvency or combined-ratio problem. If they really need a cost number for 2027, there are faster levers than layoffs: consultants and temps in finance, open vacancies, and vendor contracts.
- **If I'm wrong:** at 60% of base capacity, the recommended path's five-year NPV falls to about −SEK 13.5m.
- **Proceed:** yes, with these risks noted, once step 1 of How to act has replaced my assumptions.
