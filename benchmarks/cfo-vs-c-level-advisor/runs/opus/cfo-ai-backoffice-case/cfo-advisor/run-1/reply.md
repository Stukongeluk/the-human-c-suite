📊 CFO: Business case for AI in finance & back-office (90 FTE)

**BOTTOM LINE**
I can't build an honest case for AI replacing 27 of 90 people in 2027: in the base case that plan loses about SEK 35m over 2027–29. The case I'd take to the board instead is a gated programme. It releases about 16 FTE (17%) by end-2029 in the base case and reaches 30%+ only if the pilots show bull-case rates. Confidence: 🟡 medium. Swedish law and cost rates are verified; every company figure is a placeholder until you load your actuals.

**WHAT**
• **30% within 2027 isn't reachable in any scenario I can defend.** Net capacity released by Dec 2027 is −2 FTE (bear), 4.5 (base) or 17.5 (bull, 19%). To reach 27 net by year end, about 63% of *all* team hours would have to be automated within months of go-live, reporting included, even on bull assumptions. 🔴 These rates are my assumptions, and the evidence points lower: 91% of finance functions report low or moderate initial AI impact ([Gartner, Nov 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-11-18-gartner-survey-shows-finance-ai-adoption-remains-steady-in-2025)), and a field study found that about 8.5% of accountants' time moved off routine work ([MIT Sloan](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/how-generative-ai-can-make-accountants-more-productive)). 🟢
• **Swedish process limits 2027 savings to about 4 months, even if the AI worked from day one.** MBL negotiation comes first. Varsel to Arbetsförmedlingen must be given at least 4 months before the first exit when 26–100 people are affected. Individual notice is 1–6 months. That puts exits around Sep 2027. Turordning selects the shortest-tenure staff, not the roles AI automated, with at most 3 exemptions. Leavers keep 9 months' re-employment priority. 🟢 ([LAS](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-1982-80.text), [Lag 1974:13](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-1974-13.text))
• **Cutting 27 before the capacity exists leaves about 24 FTE of work uncovered in late 2027 and about 19 FTE in 2028.** Covering that work with consultants and then rehires makes Option A cost SEK 28.2m in 2027 and SEK 34.6m over 2027–29 in the base case. Its 5-year NPV is −SEK 21.4m in the base case and −SEK 67.1m in the bear case. Only the bull case is positive, at +SEK 30.7m. 🔴 (model on placeholder inputs)
• **The gated route matches or beats Option A in every scenario.** It means freezing hiring into automatable roles, reducing through attrition and redeployment, and offering voluntary packages only for surplus the pilots have proven. In the base case 2027 is an investment year (−SEK 12.5m), 2027–29 comes to −SEK 3.3m, the 5-year NPV is +SEK 8.3m, and 15.7 FTE are out by end-2029. The bull case reaches 30.6 FTE (34%) with an NPV of +SEK 39.1m. In the bear case, stopping at a June 2027 gate caps exposure at about SEK 7.5m. 🔴
• **The prize is real but smaller than the headline.** The team costs SEK 79.2m loaded. 27 FTE at the team average would be SEK 23.8m a year, but turordning means the leavers are the cheapest staff, so Option A saves SEK 19.3m a year. At steady state the gated route saves SEK 12.6m a year gross, or SEK 8.8m net of run costs. 🔴

**WHY THIS MATTERS**
Suppose the board signs off on "27 people in 2027" and we land on the base case. We then either miss the Q4 close and Solvency II deadlines, or buy the capacity back at about twice the cost, partly from the people we just let go. After that, the board discounts every number we bring them. Companies that went first are a warning: 55% of leaders who made AI-driven redundancies say they got those decisions wrong ([Orgvue, 2025](https://www.orgvue.com/news/55-of-businesses-admit-wrong-decisions-in-making-employees-redundant-when-bringing-ai-into-the-workforce/)), and Klarna started hiring human agents again in 2025 after its CEO said the AI cost drive had gone too far ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-08/klarna-turns-from-ai-to-real-person-customer-service)).

**HOW TO ACT**
1. Replace my placeholders in the model with actuals (payroll by sub-function, attrition, invoice and reconciliation volumes, close calendar) and run a 4-week activity baseline → Finance ops lead + HR → 15 Jan 2027
2. Freeze hiring into AP and reconciliation roles and backfill on fixed-term contracts only → You + HR → 1 Jan 2027
3. Inform the unions under MBL and start DORA due diligence on a bought (not built) AP and reconciliation tool → You + General Counsel + CIO → Feb 2027
4. Pilot AP and bank/balance-sheet reconciliations against the baseline, with hard kill criteria at Gate 1 → You → Jun 2027
5. Take Option B to the board with Option A side by side and a 30%-by-2029 stretch target tied to the gates → You → next board meeting

⚠️ **RISKS**
• Bull case under Option B: savings arrive later. 2028 is about SEK 16.5m behind Option A, recovered in 2029; the 3-year totals are identical (+SEK 11.0m each).
• If attrition is below 8%, Option B is slower (at 5%, the NPV is +SEK 4.5m instead of +SEK 8.3m). Voluntary packages at Gate 3 close the gap.
• DORA contracting, and an FI notification if this counts as outsourcing a critical or important function, can take 2–4 months. The Q4 close and QRT deadlines don't move.
• Weakest point of my own recommendation: the automation rates are assumptions, not measured on your processes. The pilot exists to replace them.

🔑 **YOUR DECISION**
Option A: Commit to 27 compulsory redundancies in 2027. This matches the board's ask, but it costs SEK 34.6m over 3 years in the base case, can't be undone, and pays off only in the bull case.
Option B: Run the gated programme. 2027 is an investment year. Net release is about 17% by 2029 in the base case and 30%+ if the gates show bull-case rates, with no compulsory redundancies unless proven surplus remains after attrition.
Recommendation: Option B. It matches or beats A in every scenario and caps the downside at about SEK 7.5m instead of about SEK 67m.
