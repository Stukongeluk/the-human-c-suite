## The short answer

- **The 30% is about the right size.** Automation and AI can realistically take out about 26 FTE of work from the function (range 17–33). You'll need about 3 people to run and control the tools, so the net is about 24 roles (26%). 27 roles (30%) is achievable in the upper half of that range, by 2029.
- **"Next year" doesn't work.** By end-2027 the tools will realistically have removed about 8 FTE of work. Cutting 27 roles in 2027 means letting go of about 22 people whose work is still there. You'd then pay consultants about 1.5x to do it, right through the 2027 year-end close and the first Solvency II cycle under the amended rules.
- **No option saves money in 2027.** It's an investment year whichever path you take.

The recommended path wins in all three automation scenarios I modelled, including the optimistic one. The board's version only comes out ahead if the high case comes true *and* about 75–80% of it is live by Q4 2027. That means every workstream, reporting included, working at close to full effect within about nine months.

## Swedish rules that set the earliest date

- **MBL §11:** you must negotiate with the unions *before* deciding. A board resolution that "27 roles go" made before that negotiation is a risk in itself.
- **Varsel:** with 26–100 people affected, you must notify Arbetsförmedlingen at least 4 months ahead. Notice under LAS is up to 6 months for staff with 10+ years' service, which describes most of an insurer's back office. In practice, payroll savings start around Q4 2027 at the earliest.
- **Turordning:** selection goes by length of service, and you can exempt only 3 people. So you may lose your newest, most digital staff rather than the people whose work was automated.
- **Re-employment priority:** dismissed staff keep priority for re-employment until 9 months after they leave, and MBL §38 requires negotiation before you bring in contractors. Backfilling with consultants will be contested.

## What I'd put to the board

Keep the ambition. Change the timing and the method.

1. Approve about SEK 28m one-off plus about SEK 6m a year in run costs, released in tranches at each stage gate.
2. Once a process's automation has proven itself, stop backfilling those roles, and move people into open roles elsewhere in the group.
3. Set the target at 27 roles by end-2029, with a checkpoint in Q3 2028. At that point a voluntary programme closes any gap to *proven* capacity. Compulsory redundancy is the last resort.
4. Report quarterly on touchless invoice rate, auto-match rate, close days, on-time Solvency II and IFRS 17 delivery, verified hours released, and roles removed against proven capacity.

A line you could use in the room: *"We can take out roughly 30% of the work by 2029, and we'll remove the roles as the work goes. Cutting first costs us about SEK 19m more in NPV and puts year-end and Solvency II reporting at risk."*
