# 2027 cost programme: what a 120-role reduction would save, and the options

**For:** Board meeting, [date] · **Prepared by:** Finance Director · **Prepared:** [date] November 2026 · **Status:** Draft for discussion · **Decision needed by:** [date]

Model: `savings-model.xlsx` (inputs, scenarios, monthly phasing and reconciliation checks). All amounts in € million unless stated. Figures marked as estimates or placeholders will be replaced with payroll, HR and ledger data before the board decides.

## 1. Summary

- **€9.0m is a run-rate, not a 2027 saving.** 120 roles at an average fully loaded cost of €75,000 is €9.0m a year, but only once all 120 people have left. Allowing for roles that will have to be refilled or covered, the lasting saving is about €7.9m a year, from 2028.
- **2027 gross saving: €4.5m in the base case** (range €2.3m–€6.4m). The consultation, notification and notice steps required in Germany and the Netherlands mean most exits take effect between June and August 2027.
- **One-off costs: €5.7m in the base case** (range €3.3m–€9.3m), almost all paid in cash in 2027.
- **2027 net cash effect: about −€1.9m in the base case.** The programme pays back around April 2028.
- **Reaching €9m of gross savings in 2027 through redundancies alone** would take about 240 exits at base-case timing, and the one-off costs would rise with them.
- **Recommendation.** The board confirms the savings target and the problem it addresses, but no number of redundancies is announced in December. Management opens consultation with the German and Dutch works councils on the financial position, the target and the options. It also starts reversible measures in January and comes back in [February 2027] with a combined plan sized from company data. Any compulsory reductions in that plan cover only what the other measures can't, and are carried out in one round.

**Responsibility.** The gap this programme has to close is the result of management decisions, and management owns it. The executive team proposes that its 2027 bonuses be suspended before any frontline role is affected [and, for the board to decide, a reduction in executive base pay of [x]%]. This is a statement of responsibility, not a material saving.

### Accounting timing

If the plan is formally announced and detailed before 31 December 2026, the restructuring provision may have to be recognised in the 2026 accounts. Whether it does depends on the framework (for example IAS 37 and IAS 19 under IFRS) and on how firm the plan is at year-end, so this is to be confirmed with the auditor. That would show 2027 at about €3.8m (base). It moves the P&L charge between years; it doesn't change the cash.

### Not in the model

- **Lost revenue** if any of the 120 roles are billable while demand remains. For the savings above to hold, this must be close to zero.
- The productivity and safety risk on sites during six to nine months of uncertainty between announcement and exit.
- The 2027 pay round, which raises the saving and the severance slightly.
- Claims if the process is faulty. In Germany, a mass-dismissal notification filed before consultation with the works council is complete makes the dismissals invalid (Federal Labour Court, 1 April 2026).

## 4. The process and timeline the model assumes

Subject to confirmation by employment counsel in both countries. Collective agreements may set different notice periods.

**Germany (base case: exits 31 July 2027)**

| Step | Basis | Base-case timing |
|---|---|---|
| Inform the works council and negotiate a reconciliation of interests and a social plan; a social plan can be imposed by a conciliation board | §§111–112 BetrVG | Dec 2026 – Feb 2027 (2 months fast, about 6 months if a conciliation board is needed) |
| Consult the works council and notify the employment agency of collective dismissals, only once consultation is complete | §§17–18 KSchG; Federal Labour Court, 1 April 2026 | March 2027 |
| Hear the works council on each dismissal | §102 BetrVG | March 2027 |
| Social selection among comparable employees: length of service, age, maintenance obligations, severe disability | §1(3) KSchG | With the above |
| Notice: 4 months to the end of a month at 10 years' service (1 to 7 months by service) | §622 BGB | Notices in March, exits 31 July |

**Netherlands (base case: exits 31 May 2027)**

| Step | Basis | Base-case timing |
|---|---|---|
| Ask the works council for advice while it can still change the decision | Art. 25 WOR | Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 |
| If the decision departs from the advice: a one-month suspension, with possible appeal to the Enterprise Chamber | Art. 25(6) and 26 WOR | Feb 2027 |
| Notify UWV and the unions (20 or more dismissals in one UWV work area within three months; settlement agreements count) | WMCO | Jan – Feb 2027 |
| UWV dismissal permits for business-economic reasons, with selection by the reflection principle, or settlement agreements | Art. 7:669 BW; Ontslagregeling | Feb – Apr 2027 |
| Notice of 1 to 4 months by service, reduced by the UWV procedure time (at least one month remains) | Art. 7:672 BW | Exits 31 May |
| Statutory transition payment: ⅓ monthly pay per year of service | Art. 7:673 BW | At exit |

Announcing a fixed number before consultation doesn't speed this up, because in both countries the legal clock starts with consultation either way. It does make a contested process more likely, and the slow case costs about €1.9m more in 2027 than the base case.

**From each seat.**
- *Customers:* site continuity and key-person risk on service contracts.
- *Finance:* one-off cash in 2027 against a lasting saving from 2028, and the provision timing.
- *Operations:* the same work with fewer people unless work stops, and safety during the months of uncertainty.
- *Technology:* scheduling and planning systems should show where capacity is idle and where subcontractors fill gaps.
- *People:* who is selected is set by law, not by management choice, and the trust of the 1,280 who stay.

## 7. Decisions requested

1. **Note** that €9m from 120 roles is a 2028 run-rate. In 2027 that plan delivers about €4.5m gross and about −€1.9m in cash in the base case.
2. **Confirm** the target, whether it is meant as 2027 in-year, cash or run-rate, and the problem it addresses (section 2).
3. **Approve** opening information and consultation with the German and Dutch works councils in December on the financial position, the target and the options, not on a fixed number of redundancies.
4. **Approve** reversible measures from 1 January 2027: a hiring freeze with exemptions for safety-critical roles and billable roles where demand exists; a review of third-party, subcontractor and agency spend; and the suspension of 2027 executive bonuses.
5. **Ask** management to return on [February 2027] with a combined plan sized from company data. Any compulsory reductions in it cover only what the other measures can't close, are carried out in one round, and come with a social plan and transition support from the day of notice.

## 8. Assumptions, and what would change this recommendation

- If the €9m protects a 2027 covenant test or liquidity, and the other measures can't be sized and started in time, the case for a larger formal process gets stronger. That process would still go through consultation.
- If the drop in demand is temporary, short-time work in Germany and hours-based measures make exits unnecessary.
- If the 120 roles are work that genuinely stops, such as a contract or site that closes, refilling falls towards zero and the run-rate rises towards €9m.
- If the €75,000 includes allocated overhead that stays, the saving falls in proportion.
- If any of the roles are billable while demand remains, the plan reduces revenue as well as cost and may lower margin.
