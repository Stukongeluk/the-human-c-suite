# 2027 Management Bonus Plan

**[Company name] — DRAFT v0.1 for Remuneration Committee and Board discussion**

Status: draft, not approved. Items in [square brackets] are for the Board to decide. Annex A uses illustrative figures only. Before this goes to participants, it needs employment-law and tax review in every country where participants are employed.

---

## Decisions needed before approval

1. **Restructuring treatment (section 5.2).** This draft counts restructuring as an operating cost and offers a bonus-neutral route for exceptional, Board-designated programmes. That is a change from the "excluded as one-off" approach in the brief. The reasons are in Annex A, and a fallback clause is in Annex B if the Board keeps the current approach.
2. **Payout curve (section 6).** Threshold, maximum and payout percentages.
3. **Participants and target bonus levels (section 3).**
4. **Optional cash-conversion gate (section 7.2).** Yes or no.
5. **Change-of-control treatment (section 10).** To be aligned with the exit timetable and the management equity plan.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Plan rewards the management team for delivering the 2027 EBITDA target in the Board-approved Budget. Performance is measured on the earnings the business actually generates, on the basis a lender's or buyer's quality-of-earnings review would use. That way management is paid for value the shareholders can actually realise.

## 3. Eligibility and bonus opportunity

3.1 **Participants.** The CEO, the executive team, and senior leaders nominated by the CEO and approved by the Committee by [31 January 2027]. That is about [N] people. Each participant gets a participation letter stating their tier and target bonus.

3.2 **Commission plans.** Anyone in a sales commission plan may also join this Plan only with Committee approval, and at a reduced target.

3.3 **Target bonus,** as a % of base salary earned in 2027:

| Tier | Roles | Target bonus | Maximum (at [200]% payout) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CEO | [75]% | [150]% |
| 2 | Executive team | [50]% | [100]% |
| 3 | VPs and Directors | [30]% | [60]% |
| 4 | Senior managers | [15]% | [30]% |

3.4 **Joiners and tier changes.** Anyone joining after [1 October 2027] does not take part in the 2027 Plan. Earlier joiners, and anyone who changes tier during the year, are pro-rated by days.

## 4. Performance measure: Plan EBITDA

4.1 **Definition.** Plan EBITDA is consolidated earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation for 2027, taken from the audited consolidated financial statements and adjusted only as set out in section 5.

4.2 **Self-funding.** Plan EBITDA is measured after the full cost of this Plan and of all other bonus, commission and incentive plans, so payouts above target pay for themselves. The Target includes the cost of target bonuses. The actual result is measured after the bonus cost at the payout level it produces, which is calculated iteratively, and the CFO's model will show the solution.

4.3 **Consistent basis.** Plan EBITDA uses the accounting policies, judgements and [budget FX rates] used to prepare the 2027 Budget. If a policy changes during the year, its effect is removed from the actual result (or the Target is restated), so the change neither increases nor reduces payouts.

4.4 **Target.** The Target is 2027 Budget EBITDA, prepared on the same basis as Plan EBITDA. It includes the costs and expected savings of any restructuring the Board has already approved for 2027 when it approves the Budget.

## 5. Adjustments

5.1 **Permitted adjustments.** Only the adjustments below may be made, and each is applied whether it raises or lowers payouts.

- (a) **Shareholder costs:** monitoring fees and costs charged by the shareholder or its affiliates.
- (b) **Transaction costs:** external costs of acquisitions, disposals, refinancings and any exit process approved by the Board.
- (c) **Acquisitions and disposals:** the Target is increased by the budgeted EBITDA from the Board-approved deal case of an acquired business, from completion. For a disposal, the Target is reduced by the disposed business's budgeted EBITDA. Either way the deal is bonus-neutral in the year it completes. Integration costs fall under 5.2.
- (d) **Accounting changes:** the effect of changes in accounting standards or of policy changes required by the auditors (section 4.3).
- (e) **Share-based payments:** non-cash charges for the management equity plan.
- (f) **Capitalised development:** Plan EBITDA uses the share of development cost capitalised in the Budget, which is [X]%. If the actual rate is more than [2] percentage points above or below that, the difference is reversed for Plan purposes.
- (g) **Restructuring:** only as set out in section 5.2.

5.2 **Restructuring costs**

- (a) **Default: counted.** Restructuring costs are operating costs. They are included in Plan EBITDA in full and are not added back, whether or not they are described as one-off.
- (b) **Exceptional Programmes: bonus-neutral.** When the Board approves a restructuring programme that is not in the Budget, it may designate it an Exceptional Programme at the moment of approval, before any cost is committed. For an Exceptional Programme:
  - (i) its Qualifying Restructuring Costs (5.3) incurred in 2027 are excluded from Plan EBITDA; and
  - (ii) the Target is increased by the 2027 savings stated in the business case the Board approved.

  The programme therefore neither increases nor reduces 2027 payouts, and its savings go into the 2028 Budget baseline.
- (c) **No retrospective designation.** A programme cannot be designated after its costs have been committed or after year-end.
- (d) **Cap.** Total costs excluded under (b) may not exceed [5]% of the Target. Any costs above the cap are included in Plan EBITDA.
- (e) **Refilled roles.** If a role eliminated under an Exceptional Programme is refilled before Plan EBITDA is finalised, whether by a hire, a contractor or outsourcing, the exclusion for that role is reversed.
- (f) **Reporting.** Each quarter the CFO reports to the Committee on restructuring costs, designations, and savings delivered against the business case.

5.3 **Qualifying Restructuring Costs** are only:

- statutory, contractual and agreed severance and notice pay for roles that are permanently eliminated;
- costs of exiting leases or sites;
- contract termination penalties; and
- directly attributable external legal and advisory fees.

They exclude performance-related exits, normal attrition, recruitment and replacement costs, retention payments, ongoing transformation, consulting or systems costs, and any cost not identified in the approved business case.

5.4 **Items that are not adjusted.** For the avoidance of doubt, all of the following stay in Plan EBITDA:

- costs of data breaches, security incidents, regulatory fines and remediation;
- customer credits and SLA penalties;
- bad debts;
- litigation in the ordinary course of business;
- recruitment, retention and contractor costs; and
- any other cost described as "one-off" that is not listed in 5.1.

5.5 **Discretion.** The Committee may make an adjustment that is not listed above only if the event was outside management's control, the amount is material (more than [2]% of Target), and the adjustment is applied symmetrically.

5.6 **Process.** The CFO prepares a line-by-line reconciliation from audited reported EBITDA to Plan EBITDA, with evidence for each adjustment. [The external auditor performs agreed-upon procedures on the reconciliation.] The Committee, not management, approves the final Plan EBITDA and payout percentage.

## 6. Payout curve

6.1 **Curve.**

| Plan EBITDA as % of Target | Payout (% of target bonus) |
|---|---|
| Below [95]% | 0% |
| [95]% (Threshold) | [50]% |
| 100% (Target) | 100% |
| [110]% or above (Maximum) | [200]% (cap) |

Payouts are interpolated in a straight line between the points. With the bracketed values, each 1% of Target between threshold and maximum is worth 10 points of payout.

6.2 **Individual payout** = target bonus × payout %. There is no individual performance modifier, in line with the Board's 100% EBITDA design. The Committee may reduce payouts under section 7. [Option: an individual modifier of 0–125%, with total payouts capped at the funded pool.]

6.3 **Calibration.** Before approval, the CFO will show the Committee what the Plan costs at threshold, target and maximum, and the EBITDA delivered at each point, to confirm that every point is affordable after the cost of the Plan.

## 7. Malus and optional gate

These provisions do not change the 100% EBITDA weighting.

7.1 **Malus.** The Committee may reduce the payout of any or all participants, including to zero, if during 2027:

- (a) a patient-safety incident linked to the company's software caused, or could have caused, serious harm;
- (b) a reportable personal-data or health-data breach resulted from a failure of controls;
- (c) the company received a material regulatory sanction or finding, or lost a certification [for example from a data-protection, health or medical-device regulator];
- (d) the participant committed misconduct or a material breach of company policy; or
- (e) results were materially misstated.

7.2 **[Optional cash-conversion gate.]** Payouts above 100% require operating cash conversion (operating cash flow before tax ÷ Plan EBITDA) of at least [X]%. This stops EBITDA from being earned through a build-up of receivables or through capitalisation.

## 8. Payment

8.1 Payment is in cash through payroll, less tax and deductions, within [30] days after the Board approves the 2027 audited accounts (expected [April 2028]).

8.2 To be paid, a participant must be employed and not under notice (given or received) on the payment date, except as set out in section 9.

8.3 The bonus is not pensionable [subject to local law].

## 9. Leavers

9.1 **Good leavers** leave because of death, ill health or disability, redundancy (including under any restructuring), or retirement, or for any other reason the Committee decides. Their bonus is pro-rated to the leaving date, based on actual Plan EBITDA, and paid at the normal time.

9.2 **All other leavers,** including those who resign or are dismissed for cause, receive no payment.

## 10. Change of control

10.1 If a sale of the company completes before the end of 2027:

- performance is measured on Plan EBITDA to the last month-end before completion, against the phased Target for the same period;
- the resulting payout is pro-rated to the completion date and paid within [30] days of completion; and
- the Committee agrees with the buyer, as part of the transaction, how the rest of the year is treated.

10.2 Treatment is to be aligned with the management equity plan and any transaction bonus, so the same performance is not rewarded twice.

## 11. Clawback

The Committee may recover all or part of a payout within [3] years of payment if it was based on materially misstated results or a calculation error, or if the participant committed misconduct.

## Annex A — Why restructuring is counted (illustrative figures, in millions)

**Background.** The company has restructured in each of the last three years. When a cost recurs every year, it is part of the cost of running the business, not a one-off.

**Worked example.**

Assumptions:

- 2027 Target EBITDA is 24.0.
- In Q1 2027 management proposes another restructuring. It costs 3.0 and saves 2.0 in 2027 (about 4.0 a year from 2028).
- The rest of the business lands exactly on budget.
- Reported EBITDA is therefore 24.0 − 3.0 + 2.0 = 23.0.

| | "Excluded as usual" | Recommended: counted (default) | Recommended: designated Exceptional Programme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reported EBITDA | 23.0 | 23.0 | 23.0 |
| Restructuring cost added back | +3.0 | 0 | +3.0 |
| Plan EBITDA | 26.0 | 23.0 | 26.0 |
| Target | 24.0 | 24.0 | 24.0 + 2.0 savings = 26.0 |
| % of Target | 108.3% | 95.8% | 100.0% |
| **Payout** | **183%** | **58%** | **100%** |
| Payout if no restructuring is done | 100% | 100% | 100% |

**What the example shows.**

- **"Excluded as usual":** the restructuring lifts the bonus from 100% to 183% in a year when reported EBITDA fell by 1.0. Management is paid for the savings while the shareholders bear the cost. A bonus built this way makes another restructuring the quickest route to a maximum payout, every year.
- **Counted (default):** management bears the net in-year cost, just as the shareholders do.
- **Exceptional Programme:** a restructuring that the Board thinks is worth doing despite its in-year cost can be designated, which makes it bonus-neutral. Management is then not put off a sound long-term decision.

**Exit check.** Restructuring add-backs for [2024, 2025 and 2026] total [X]. A buyer's quality-of-earnings review is likely to treat a cost that recurs every year as a normal operating cost. Each 1.0 of recurring cost presented as one-off overstates the EBITDA a buyer will pay for by 1.0, which at an illustrative [12]x multiple is 12.0 of enterprise value the buyer will probably negotiate away. Paying bonuses on the basis a buyer will use keeps management focused on EBITDA that survives diligence.

**Pay at budget is unchanged.** The Target is set on the same basis as the measurement, so delivering the Budget still pays 100%. The change removes only the payout from savings whose cost was excluded. It is not a pay cut.

## Annex B — Fallback clause if the Board keeps "excluded as one-off"

If the Board decides to keep excluding restructuring costs, these safeguards should replace section 5.2 as a minimum:

> Restructuring costs may be excluded from Plan EBITDA only where:
> (i) the programme was approved by the Board, with a business case stating its costs and savings, before any cost was committed;
> (ii) the costs are Qualifying Restructuring Costs (5.3);
> (iii) total exclusions do not exceed [5]% of Target; and
> (iv) the Target is increased by the programme's 2027 savings as stated in its business case.

Safeguard (iv) is the one that matters most. Without it, the Plan pays out on savings whose cost has been excluded, which is the 183% outcome in Annex A. If the Board drops (iv), its reasons should be recorded in the minutes.
