# 2027 Management Bonus Plan - DRAFT for board discussion

Status: draft for the Remuneration Committee. Not legal, tax or employment advice; have employment counsel and the auditors review before it is issued. All thresholds are placeholders to be set from the 2027 budget.

## 1. Purpose
Pay management for building a company a buyer will want to own: profit that is real, customers who stay, a team that stays, and software that is safe and secure. Not for the cheapest year.

## 2. Who is covered
Executive team and senior managers (assumed about 25-40 of 350 people; confirm). Same scorecard for everyone, so leaders and managers are pulled the same way.

## 3. Scorecard

| Measure | Weight | Threshold (50% payout) | Target (100%) | Stretch (150%, cap) | Why it is here | Gaming risk and guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bonus EBITDA (adjusted EBITDA after restructuring costs; see section 4) | 50% | 90% of budget | 100% of budget | 110% of budget | The board's core measure | Cutting training, security, maintenance: guard is the gates and the quality measure below. Relabelling costs: every adjustment reconciled to reported EBITDA and approved by the Remuneration Committee |
| Cash conversion (operating cash flow / bonus EBITDA) | 15% | set from budget | set from budget | set from budget | Profit that is not collected is not profit | Delaying supplier payments: measured on a normalised payables position |
| Net revenue retention and logo churn (customers) | 15% | set from budget | set from budget | set from budget | A buyer pays for retained recurring revenue | Discounting to retain: measured with gross margin per customer |
| Regretted attrition and engagement | 10% | set from budget | set from budget | set from budget | Restructured three years running; knowledge and trust are assets | Pressure on survey answers: use an independent survey, anonymous, team-level only |
| Quality and safety: uptime/SLA, security incidents, open patient-safety or compliance findings | 10% | set from budget | set from budget | set from budget | Healthcare customers buy trust | Under-reporting: incident counts reviewed by the audit/risk owner, not by the budget holder |

Payout between points is linear. Total payout capped at 150% of target.

## 4. Definition of bonus EBITDA
- Starting point: reported EBITDA from the audited ledger.
- Restructuring, severance, advisory and related costs are INCLUDED (deducted). Restructuring has occurred in each of the last three years, so it is a cost of running this business, not a one-off.
- The board may keep a separate "adjusted EBITDA before restructuring" for lender and exit reporting, but must show it next to reported EBITDA with each adjustment named. The bonus does not use it.
- Only a genuinely single event (for example, a named acquisition integration approved by the Remuneration Committee in advance) may be excluded, and only up to a cap fixed now (placeholder: 2% of budget EBITDA). Anything above the cap counts.
- Savings are credited only net of their full cost (severance, notice, rehiring and ramp-up, lost revenue).
- Fallback if the board will not include restructuring in full: include the average of the last three years' restructuring charges as a fixed deduction. Estimate required from the ledger.

## 5. Gates (all-or-nothing)
- Serious patient-safety, data-breach or regulatory event attributable to management: Remuneration Committee may reduce the whole bonus, up to zero.
- Cash or covenant headroom below the agreed minimum: no payout above target.
- No bonus is paid for headcount reduction as such, and no measure rewards it.

## 6. Deferral
Executive team: 70% paid in cash after the year-end audit; 30% deferred and vesting equally over the next 2 years (placeholder; the playbook-style norm is 3 years for the deferred share, to be aligned with the exit horizon and any management equity plan). Senior managers: paid in cash after the audit.

## 7. Malus and clawback
Deferred amounts can be reduced (malus), and paid amounts recovered (clawback) for 3 years, for: restatement or material misstatement, results later shown to be bought by cutting security, quality or people investment, misconduct. Check enforceability under local employment law.

## 8. Leaders first
- If bonus EBITDA is below threshold, there is no executive bonus, and management bonuses are reduced before any involuntary reduction is proposed.
- If the board orders cost reductions in 2027, executive team pay and bonus are reduced first and by the largest percentage; the cuts and their order are published to staff.
- Executive bonus targets do not exceed those for the next layer by more than an agreed ratio (placeholder).

## 9. Governance
- Targets set by the Remuneration Committee before 31 January 2027; no change during the year except by recorded decision with reasons.
- Reconciliation of bonus EBITDA to reported EBITDA, signed by the CFO and reviewed by the auditors, goes into every quarterly board pack.
- People indicators (regretted attrition, time to fill, engagement) shown next to the financials each quarter.
- Employee representatives or the works council: check whether consent or consultation rights apply to the plan (works council in the Netherlands; confirm jurisdiction).
