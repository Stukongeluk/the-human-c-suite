**Do not put a €9.0M 2027 savings figure from 120 redundancies into the board pack: in Germany and the Netherlands, a programme started next month will likely cost around €2.7M net in 2027 (base case) and only deliver €0.3M net even in a fast, cooperative best case.** The €9.0M (120 × €75,000) is a gross **2028** run-rate, not a 2027 cash or P&L saving.

The trap is treating a gross annualised headcount calculation as in-year 2027 savings — and announcing redundancies before statutory consultation, which in both jurisdictions risks injunctions, void notices, and higher settlement costs.

### The 2027–2028 Reality Check (120 roles at €75k fully loaded = €750k/month)

Assuming consultation opens properly in December 2026 as a *proposed intention* (not a final decision), DE/NL works council and union processes typically take 3–5 months, followed by 1–4 months' notice in the Netherlands and up to 7 months in Germany. Severance/social plan costs hit up front while payroll savings start in Q3–Q4 2027:

| Scenario (€M) | Avg. exit | 2027 gross payroll saved | 2027 one-off & friction* | **2027 Net** | **2028 Net** (after leakage) | Payback |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Best** (cooperative, fast) | 1 Jul 2027 (6 mo) | +4.50 | -4.20 | **+0.30** | +8.55 (5% leak) | ~13 mo |

### What Actually Delivers Cash in 2027

Before touching involuntary redundancies, three reversible levers hit the P&L in Q1–Q2 2027 with near-zero severance drain:
- **Non-payroll spend, subcontractors, overtime & rework:** est. **+€2.0M–€4.0M** in 2027.
- **Contract pricing, inflation indexation & unbilled scope recovery:** est. **+€1.5M–€3.0M** in 2027.
- **Hiring freeze, natural attrition (50–70 roles) & internal redeployment:** est. **+€1.9M–€2.6M** in 2027 (**+€3.75M–€5.25M** in 2028).
- **Combined:** **+€5.4M–€9.6M** net in 2027. If the pressure is a temporary order dip at German sites, *Kurzarbeitergeld* bridges payroll immediately (note: max duration reverts from 24 to 12 months from 1 January 2027 unless extended, and needs works council agreement). If results disappointed, leadership cutting executive bonuses and perks first sets the trust needed for everything else.
