### The trap in one sentence
`120 × €75,000 = €9.0M` is an annualised gross payroll figure that assumes everyone leaves on 31 December 2026 at zero cost, ignoring German/Dutch consultation, notice periods, severance, overtime cover, revenue at risk and follow-on attrition.

### Three things to flag before the board pack goes out

1. **You cannot legally "announce 120 redundancies" as a done deal in December.** (Not legal advice — get German and Dutch employment counsel in before any communication.) Under EU Directive 98/59/EC, Dutch WOR (Art. 25 works council advice) and WMCO (UWV/union notification for 20+ dismissals in 3 months, *before* a final decision), and German BetrVG (§§111–112 *Interessenausgleich* and *Sozialplan* where a works council exists) plus §17 KSchG notification, announcing a decision before consultation invites void dismissals, Enterprise Chamber appeals and months of delay.
2. **Start with a private conversation with the CEO.** Steelman their concern first: if a specific site or service line is in *structural* decline, a hiring freeze won't fix it, and permanent restructuring may eventually be needed. But even then, it is a 2028–2029 payback decision, not a 2027 fix. A line you can use:
   > *"I ran the 120-role model by quarter with German and Dutch timelines. Because exits don't land until H2 and severance hits first, it's a €3.7M net cash drain in 2027 rather than a €9M saving. If we need €9M in 2027, we need non-payroll spend, pricing/unbilled work, leadership bonuses, working capital and attrition first — and if a specific site is structurally underwater, let's size that separately with counsel."*
3. **If the dip is temporary in Germany, use hours before exits.** *Kurzarbeit* (with works council agreement under §87(1) no. 3 BetrVG) preserves trained technicians without severance. Note that statutory maximum *Kurzarbeitergeld* duration reverts from 24 months to 12 months from 1 January 2027 unless extended.

### Key assumptions behind these estimates
- Exits phased 50% end of Q2 2027 and 50% end of Q3 2027 (Q1 consultation + average 3-month notice); average severance/social plan €40k/person (~8 years' tenure); outplacement €4k/person; 16% H2 overtime/contractor cover; 30% contribution margin on lost/delayed revenue; +2.5% extra voluntary attrition at 0.5× salary replacement cost (Gallup).
