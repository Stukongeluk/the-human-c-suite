# 2027 Cost & Cash Plan: Redundancy Model and Alternatives

**Prepared for:** Board of Directors & Executive Team  
**Status:** Working draft for review (all figures below are **estimates** pending payroll, ERP, CRM and legal counsel inputs)  
**Date:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Recommendation:** Do not announce 120 redundancies in December 2026 as a way to deliver €9.0M of savings in 2027. Under German and Dutch employment law, consultation and notice periods push actual exits into the second half of 2027, while severance, social plan, legal and transition costs hit earlier. On a realistic net basis, **120 redundancies will drain €3.75M of cash in 2027** (peaking at **-€4.94M in Q3 2027**) and will not break even on cash until **late Q3 2028**.

Instead, before any compulsory reductions are considered, launch a **blended non-headcount and workforce-flow plan** (vendor renegotiation, pricing and unbilled-work recovery, leadership bonus/pay reset, and a hiring freeze with natural attrition and redeployment). This delivers an estimated **+€6.18M of recurring net cash and EBIT in 2027** (positive from Q1) and **+€8.40M in 2028**, plus **€2.5M–€3.5M of H1 2027 working-capital cash release** — closing the €9.0M cash gap in 2027 without compulsory exits or irreversible capacity loss.

If there is a structural, permanent drop in demand at a specific site or service line that these measures cannot resolve, size any restructuring to that specific unit *after* proving the gap and completing statutory consultation — and model it as a 2028–2029 payback decision, not a 2027 saving.

## 3. Option A: 120 Involuntary Redundancies — Net Model by Quarter

### Why the €9.0M headline does not land in 2027
€9.0M (`120 × €75,000`) is the annualised gross payroll run-rate assuming all 120 roles leave on 31 December 2026 at zero cost. In Germany and the Netherlands, starting consultation in December 2026 / January 2027 means:
- **Q1 2027:** Works council and union consultation, German *Interessenausgleich* and *Sozialplan* negotiation (where a works council exists), and Dutch WOR Art. 25 advice / WMCO notification / UWV filing. Zero exits.
- **Q2 2027:** Notices served; average 3-month notice period runs (in the Netherlands, 1–4 months by tenure; in Germany, up to 7 months after 20 years under §622 BGB).
- **Exits (Estimate):** Tranche 1 (60 roles, shorter tenure) exits 30 June 2027 (off payroll in Q3–Q4); Tranche 2 (60 roles, longer tenure) exits 30 September 2027 (off payroll in Q4). Full-year run-rate begins in 2028.

### Sensitivities & Break-Even (Option A)
- **Consultation/notice delay (+1 quarter):** If works council negotiations, *Sozialplan* arbitration or a Dutch Enterprise Chamber / UWV dispute pushes exits back by one quarter, 2027 gross savings fall by **€2,250k** (from €3,375k to €1,125k), worsening 2027 net cash to roughly **-€5.5M**.
- **Severance factor:** Every €10k change in average severance moves 2027 net cash by **±€1,200k** (at €25k/person: **-€1,945k** in 2027; at €55k/person: **-€5,545k** in 2027).
- **2-year break-even:** At a 30% contribution margin, **€4.52M of additional lost revenue over two years** (or **36 additional regretted exits** at €37.5k replacement cost) wipes out the entire +€1,355k two-year net cash saving.
- **Evidence context:** Research finds downsizing is associated with lower subsequent profitability (Guthrie & Datta, *Organization Science*, 2008), a 1% downsizing correlates with 31% higher voluntary turnover the next year (Trevor & Nyberg, *Academy of Management Journal*, 2008), and replacing an employee costs 0.5× to 2.0× annual salary (Gallup).

## 4. Option B: Blended Non-Headcount & Workforce-Flow Plan

Rather than cutting payroll first, Option B sequences actions from least to most human harm, landing cash in Q1 2027 and preserving billable delivery capacity:

1. **Working-capital bridge (cash only, not in P&L table):** Tighten receivables (dispute resolution, invoicing cadence, terms enforcement) and defer non-safety capex for **+€2.5M–€3.5M in H1 2027** — without stretching payments to small suppliers.
2. **Non-payroll spend & vendor renegotiation:** Zero-base subcontractors, fleet/leasing, IT/software licences, consultants, energy and facilities (**+€2,100k in 2027; +€2,400k in 2028**).
3. **Pricing discipline & revenue leakage recovery:** Enforce contract indexation, capture unbilled field hours and change orders, tighten discount approvals (**+€1,600k in 2027; +€2,000k in 2028**).
4. **Leaders first:** Freeze executive salaries and reset 2027 management bonus pool (**+€600k in 2027**), restoring bonuses only on a multi-year balanced scorecard (profit, cash, customer retention, safety/regretted attrition).
5. **Hiring freeze, natural attrition & internal redeployment:** Assuming ~8% normal voluntary attrition (~112 exits/year), hold ~50% of non-critical vacancies unfilled (reaching 56 FTEs by Q4 2027) by explicitly stopping low-value work and redeploying staff internally (**+€2,175k in 2027; +€4,200k in 2028**).
6. **Conditional cyclical lever (Germany):** If demand in German sites is temporarily soft rather than structurally gone, use *Kurzarbeit* (short-time work, requiring works council agreement under §87(1) no. 3 BetrVG) to bridge hours without losing trained technicians. *Note: Statutory maximum Kurzarbeitergeld duration falls back from 24 months to 12 months from 1 January 2027 unless extended by the federal government.*

## 5. Legal, Works Council & Governance Checkpoints

*This section is a financial-planning flag, not legal advice. German and Dutch employment counsel must review any restructuring proposal before internal or external communication.*

- **Do not announce 120 redundancies as a final decision in December 2026.** Under EU Directive 98/59/EC and national laws, employee representatives must be consulted *in good time* while the plan can still change.
- **Netherlands:** A works council (*Ondernemingsraad*) is mandatory from 50 employees and holds an advisory right on reorganisations (Art. 25 WOR), with appeal to the Enterprise Chamber. Dismissing 20 or more employees within three months in one UWV work area triggers the Collective Dismissal Notification Act (WMCO) — requiring advance notification and consultation with UWV and unions *before* the decision is final (employer-initiated settlement agreements count toward the 20). Dismissal requires UWV permission (using the reflection principle, *afspiegelingsbeginsel*) or settlement, plus statutory *transitievergoeding*.
- **Germany:** Where a works council (*Betriebsrat*) exists, major operational changes (>20 employees) require negotiating a reconciliation of interests (*Interessenausgleich*) and a social plan (*Sozialplan*, §§111–112 BetrVG). Collective dismissals above statutory thresholds require advance notification to the *Agentur für Arbeit* (§17 KSchG); procedural errors can render dismissals void. Selection must follow *Sozialauswahl* (§1(3) KSchG: tenure, age, maintenance obligations, severe disability) — not performance rankings. Notice periods reach up to 7 months after 20 years (§622 BGB). Note also supervisory board codetermination rules (1/3 worker seats from 500 employees).
