### Why the 30% Target Breaks on Its Own Numbers

Board pressure to improve the expense ratio—especially when peers are announcing AI gains—is real and legitimate. The trap is treating a pre-set 30% headcount target as a Year 1 business case before diagnosing the work or piloting the tools: in Sweden, the legal timeline alone pushes payroll savings into late Year 1 while pulling AI, legal, and transition costs forward.

Using illustrative estimates (**90 FTEs at 0.90 MSEK/FTE/yr = 81.0 MSEK/yr**, **~8% natural attrition**—all to be replaced with your ERP/HRIS actuals):

| Option | Yr 1 Net | Yr 2 Net | Yr 3 Net | 3-Yr Total | Yr 3 Headcount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **0. Do nothing** | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.0 | **0.0** | 90 FTE |
| **1. Licence/vendor & report cleanup only** | +1.3 | +3.5 | +3.5 | **+8.3** | 90 FTE |
| **2. Phased AI + freeze, attrition & reskilling (Recommended)** | **0.0** | **+8.6** | **+14.1** | **+22.7** | **74 FTE (-16 via attrition)** |
| **3a. 30% forced cuts in Yr 1 (Base case)** | **-4.4** | **+18.2** | **+18.2** | **+32.0** | **67 FTE (-27 cut, +4 oversight)** |
| **3b. 30% forced cuts in Yr 1 (Regret/rehiring case)** | **-4.4** | **+11.3** | **+12.8** | **+19.7** | **72 FTE (-27 cut, +9 rehired)** |

Three Swedish and insurance-specific realities drive this gap *(not legal advice—verify with Swedish employment counsel and HR)*:

1. **Year 1 cash timing:** Mandatory MBL union negotiations, the **4-month Arbetsförmedlingen *varsel*** triggered at 26–100 redundancies (27 crosses that line), and 1–6 month LAS/collective-agreement notice periods mean exits leave payroll roughly 6–9 months into Year 1 (~8.1 MSEK gross payroll saved), while AI build (~6.0 MSEK), severance/*avtalsturlista*/legal (~4.5 MSEK), and transition cover (~2.0 MSEK) hit immediately.
2. **LAS *turordning* works against AI adoption:** Unless you negotiate an *avtalsturlista* with the union, statutory length-of-service rules (with only three statutory exemptions since 2022) remove your newest, often most digitally fluent staff while retaining senior staff who still need retraining—and dismissed staff hold 9 months' re-employment priority.
3. **Tasks ≠ whole jobs in regulated finance:** AI can automate standard AP invoices and draft reconciliations/commentary, but exceptions, root-cause breaks, DORA audit trails, and Solvency II / Finansinspektionen sign-off stay human. Two in three companies that cut jobs for AI were already rehiring within a year (Careerminds, Feb 2026); if you have to rehire 9 FTEs/contractors (Option 3b), forced cuts underperform Option 2 by **3.0 MSEK** over three years while damaging trust and triggering survivor turnover.
