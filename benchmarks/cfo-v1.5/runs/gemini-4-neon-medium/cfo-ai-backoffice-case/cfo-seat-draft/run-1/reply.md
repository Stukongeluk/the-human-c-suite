### Why the 30% "next year" case breaks down

The board's instinct to tackle expense-ratio pressure and manual toil in AP, reconciliations, and reporting is right. The trap is starting from a headcount percentage and a 12-month clock instead of the underlying cost problem:

- **Swedish law moves the savings past next year.** Cutting 27 people (30% of 90) crosses the 26-person threshold requiring **4 months' advance notice to *Arbetsförmedlingen***, on top of primary union negotiations under *MBL* and **1 to 6 months' notice** under *LAS*. Almost no payroll leaves the P&L before Q3, while AI build, integration, DORA testing, and legal/transition costs hit in Q1–Q3.
- **LAS seniority order fights the AI plan.** Under *LAS*, selection runs by length of service per unit/agreement area with only three statutory exemptions unless you negotiate an *avtalsturlista* with the union. You risk losing newer, data-fluent analysts while retaining roles that still need retraining — and dismissed staff hold **9 months' re-employment priority** if you later need to backfill.
- **Insurance controls are non-negotiable.** Under Finansinspektionen oversight, Solvency II, IFRS 17, and DORA, you cannot remove segregation of duties, audit trails, or human sign-off on payments and statutory reporting. AI can speed up invoice matching, standard reconciliations, and first-draft variance checks; it cannot own regulatory sign-off.
- **The hidden costs eat the cushion.** In the 24-month model, Option A's entire net cushion is **0.98 MSEK** `[Estimate]`. One extra backfilled role, a one-quarter delay, or a single audit remediation wipes it out — and survey evidence from Careerminds (2026) and Orgvue (2025) suggests most firms that cut jobs upfront for AI end up rehiring or regretting it.

*(This flags statutory and regulatory constraints for financial planning, not legal or compliance advice — have Swedish employment counsel, HR, and Compliance verify the exact *MBL*, *LAS*, collective-agreement, and DORA requirements before any commitment.)*
