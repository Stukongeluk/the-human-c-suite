### Why the CEO's shortcut backfires

Your CEO's instinct makes sense—a flat 8% across every cost centre is fast and avoids political fights between department heads. The trap is that **a uniform cut looks fair on a spreadsheet, but in a B2B services firm it cuts billable delivery alongside overhead, shrinking revenue faster than cost.**

Three numbers make the case for your private conversation with the CEO before the board pack goes out:

1. **You are profitable, not in a cash crisis.** A thin positive margin needs resilience, not panic. Cutting billable teams (where payroll is often 75%–85% of cost) risks pushing a thin margin negative.

Affecting 20+ roles in one UWV work area within three months—including employer-initiated settlement agreements—triggers **WMCO** notification and consultation with UWV and unions *before* the decision is final.
