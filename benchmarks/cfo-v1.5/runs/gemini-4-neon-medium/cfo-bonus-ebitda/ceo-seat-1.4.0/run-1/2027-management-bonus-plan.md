# 2027 Management Bonus Plan (Draft for Board Review)

**Status:** Draft for Remuneration Committee / Board review  
**Scope:** Executive Leadership Team and Senior Management  
**Plan Year:** FY2027 (1 January 2027 – 31 December 2027)  
**Company Profile:** Healthcare Software (~350 FTEs)

---

## 1. Purpose and Design Rationale

The 2027 Management Bonus Plan aligns leadership incentives with **durable enterprise value, real cash generation, and exit readiness**.

In each of the past three financial years (2024–2026), the company has incurred restructuring costs that were excluded from Adjusted EBITDA as non-recurring items. Continuing to pay 100% of management bonuses on Adjusted EBITDA with restructuring excluded creates three commercial risks:

1. **Exit valuation and Quality of Earnings (QoE) risk:** A fourth consecutive year of "one-off" restructuring will be treated as a recurring operating cost by prospective buyers and lenders in due diligence, reducing normalised EBITDA and compressing the exit multiple.
2. **Misaligned in-year incentives:** Excluding severance and reorganisation costs from the bonus metric allows management to meet an Adjusted EBITDA target through late-year headcount reductions even when underlying revenue, product execution, or cash flow has missed plan.
3. **Clinical software and retention risk:** In healthcare software, repeated annual restructurings erode domain knowledge (clinical workflows, hospital integrations, regulatory compliance), increase customer churn risk, and elevate operational and patient-safety exposure—all of which are scrutinised in buyer due diligence.

Accordingly, the 2027 plan keeps profitability and cash as the majority driver (**60%**), brings restructuring costs **inside** the earnings definition, and balances financial delivery with customer durability (**25%**) and organisational stability (**15%**).

## 2. 2027 Scorecard and Weightings

All metrics are measured at the **company or system level** (no individual activity metrics) against targets locked by the Board prior to 1 January 2027.

| Pillar | Weight | Metric | Definition & Measurement Basis | Data Source |
|---|---:|---|---|---|

## 3. Rules on Restructuring and Adjustments

To ensure earnings quality and eliminate perverse incentives:

1. **No restructuring add-back:** All FY2027 severance, notice-period, outplacement, and reorganisation expenses are charged directly against EBITDA for bonus calculation purposes.
2. **Narrow, pre-approved exclusions only:** The only permitted adjustments to EBITDA are genuinely non-operating items approved in writing by the Board **before** they are incurred (for example, third-party M&A transaction fees or sponsor monitoring fees). No retroactive adjustments at year-end.
3. **Leadership accountability on involuntary reductions:** If the company undertakes involuntary headcount reductions in FY2027 due to missed commercial or operational targets, the Remuneration Committee will apply a mandatory downward review to executive bonus payouts before any payment is authorised.

## 4. Payout Curve and Gates

### 4.1 Payout Curve (Per Metric)

Each metric is scored independently on a linear scale between Threshold, Target, and Stretch:

| Performance Level | Achievement vs. Target | Payout Factor |
|---|---:|---:|
| Below Threshold | < 85% | 0% |
| Threshold | 85% | 50% |
| Target | 100% | 100% |
| Stretch (Cap) | ≥ 115% | 150% |

*Linear interpolation applies between 85% and 100% (+3.33% payout per +1% achievement) and between 100% and 115% (+3.33% payout per +1% achievement). Overall plan payout is capped at 150% of target bonus.*

### 4.2 Hard Gates (Circuit Breakers)

No bonus is payable—regardless of scorecard score—if any of the following occur:

- **Liquidity & Covenant Gate:** Breach of any senior debt covenant or year-end cash falling below the Board-approved minimum liquidity buffer of `[TBD]`.
- **Patient Safety, Regulatory & Security Gate:** A severe clinical-safety software failure, major regulatory sanction, or reportable data breach caused by neglected controls triggers mandatory Remuneration Committee review, with authority to reduce or cancel payouts (malus/clawback).

## 5. Illustrative Payout Scenarios

The table below illustrates how the 2027 plan behaves across three operating scenarios (using the linear curve above):

| Metric (Weight) | Scenario A: On-Plan Delivery | Scenario B: Plan Missed, Cut-to-Hit Attempted* | Scenario C: Strong Organic Execution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unadjusted EBITDA (40%)** | 100% ach. → 100% × 40% = **40.0%** | 88% ach. → 60% × 40% = **24.0%** | 106% ach. → 120% × 40% = **48.0%** |
| **FCF Conversion (20%)** | 100% ach. → 100% × 20% = **20.0%** | 85% ach. → 50% × 20% = **10.0%** | 106% ach. → 120% × 20% = **24.0%** |
| **NRR (15%)** | 100% ach. → 100% × 15% = **15.0%** | 88% ach. → 60% × 15% = **9.0%** | 106% ach. → 120% × 15% = **18.0%** |
| **Reliability (10%)** | 100% ach. → 100% × 10% = **10.0%** | <85% ach. → 0% × 10% = **0.0%** | 100% ach. → 100% × 10% = **10.0%** |
| **Regretted Attrition (10%)** | 100% ach. → 100% × 10% = **10.0%** | <85% ach. → 0% × 10% = **0.0%** | 100% ach. → 100% × 10% = **10.0%** |
| **Team Stability (5%)** | 100% ach. → 100% × 5% = **5.0%** | <85% ach. → 0% × 5% = **0.0%** | 100% ach. → 100% × 5% = **5.0%** |
| **Total Payout (% of Target)** | **100.0%** | **43.0%** *(subject to mandatory Committee reduction)* | **115.0%** |

*\*In Scenario B, under a 100% Adjusted EBITDA plan with restructuring excluded, late-year headcount cuts could have produced a ~100% payout despite missed organic performance, cash drain from severance, rising attrition, and degraded service reliability.*

## 6. Governance and Implementation

1. **Definition Lock:** Final metric definitions, baselines, and targets are approved by the Remuneration Committee before 1 January 2027 and cannot be re-weighted mid-year.
2. **Quarterly Tracking:** CFO and Chief People Officer present quarterly progress against all six metrics to the Board using verified system-of-record data.
3. **Audit Verification:** Final FY2027 financial payouts are released only after sign-off of audited financial statements.
4. **Legal & Consultation Review:** Prior to rollout, employment counsel should verify alignment with existing executive service agreements, local employment law, and any applicable works council or employee-representative consultation requirements in the company's operating jurisdictions.
