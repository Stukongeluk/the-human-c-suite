# 2027 Management Bonus Plan (Draft)

**Prepared for:** Board of Directors & Remuneration Committee  
**Company:** Healthcare Software Group (~350 employees, PE-backed)  
**Status:** Draft for Board Review — illustrative thresholds marked `[TBD]` pending 2027 budget sign-off

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Recommendation

**Recommendation:** Reject a 100% Adjusted EBITDA bonus that excludes restructuring costs, and adopt a **balanced 2027 scorecard** anchored on **Unadjusted EBITDA (including any restructuring), cash conversion, customer retention, people retention, and platform quality**, with **40% of the award deferred over three years**.

Why this protects exit value for the fund:
1. **Three years of restructuring is not "one-off."** Restructuring in 2024, 2025, and 2026 makes reorganisation a recurring cost of running the business. Excluding a fourth year from bonus calculations overstates underlying earnings and makes further cuts look free to management. Under US SEC non-GAAP rules (Regulation S-K Item 10(e)(1)(ii)(B))—a sound benchmark even for private companies—an item cannot be excluded as non-recurring if a similar charge occurred in the prior two years or is reasonably likely within the next two.
2. **PE buyers price durability, not adjusted slides.** In exit due diligence, buyers strip out serial "one-off" adjustments and discount the valuation multiple when they see repeated reorganisations, rising customer churn, deferred engineering maintenance, or key-person attrition.
3. **100% EBITDA plans pay management to mortgage 2028–2029.** In a 350-person healthcare software business, the fastest way to hit a single-year EBITDA target is to cut R&D, customer support, security, and training. The saving lands in 2027; the churn, outages, and rehiring bill land in 2028.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Option A: Board Initial Brief** | 100% Adjusted EBITDA (restructuring excluded) | Simple; familiar PE template | **Rejected.** Incentivises a 4th year of cuts, hides true cash cost of turnover/severance, invites quality/security underinvestment, and creates a valuation discount at exit. |

## 4. Profit Definition & Restructuring Rules

To end the cycle of annual restructuring:

1. **No "one-off" exclusion for restructuring.** Because the company has restructured in 2024, 2025, and 2026, all severance, notice, consultation, outplacement, and reorganisation costs in 2027 are treated as **normal operating expenses** inside EBITDA.
2. **Full-cost business case required.** Any proposed headcount reduction must be modelled net of severance, paid notice/consultation, rehiring/ramp-up costs (replacing a skilled software employee typically costs 0.5x–2.0x annual salary), and revenue at risk—and compared against non-payroll cost levers first.
3. **No bonus for headcount cuts.** Restructuring milestones or headcount reductions may never be used as a bonus KPI or trigger.
4. **Reconciliation rule.** Management may still report *Adjusted EBITDA* to lenders where required by existing credit agreements, provided every board pack shows **Reported EBITDA** alongside **Adjusted EBITDA** with a full line-by-line reconciliation.

---

## 5. Payout Mechanics, Deferral & Downside Governance

* **Circuit Breaker (Gateway):** No bonus is payable under any metric if (a) the company breaches or requires a waiver on its bank debt covenants, or (b) a material regulatory, clinical-safety, or patient-data breach occurs due to neglected controls.
* **Multi-Year Deferral (Exit Alignment):**
  * **60%** paid in cash following completion of the FY2027 external audit.
  * **40%** deferred into time-vested phantom equity or deferred cash vesting equally over **3 years** (one-third each in 2028, 2029, and 2030, or accelerating upon a qualifying exit).
* **Malus & Clawback:** Unvested deferred awards (malus) and paid cash bonuses (clawback) are subject to recovery for 3 years in the event of financial restatement, fraud, material compliance failure, or short-term cost cuts that demonstrably cause subsequent customer or operational failure.
* **Leaders Go First:** If 2027 EBITDA misses Threshold or the company initiates involuntary redundancies during the year, executive management bonus pools are reduced or zeroed first before any frontline or engineering roles are cut.
