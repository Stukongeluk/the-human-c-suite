### Why push back on the board's brief (in the fund's own terms)

1. **Three consecutive years of restructuring is an operating cost, not a one-off.** Calling a fourth year "one-off" makes layoffs look free on the bonus sheet while the company pays the real cash bill in severance, notice, and replacement hiring (which Gallup estimates at 0.5x to 2x annual salary per role). Even US SEC non-GAAP rules (Regulation S-K Item 10(e)(1)(ii)(B)) bar excluding charges as non-recurring when similar charges occurred in the prior two years—a sound standard for private reporting too.
2. **PE buyers price durability, not adjusted slides.** At exit, due diligence strips out serial restructuring adjustments and discounts the multiple when it sees rising churn, deferred maintenance, or key-person departures.
3. **100% EBITDA plans pay management to mortgage 2028.** In a 350-person healthcare software firm, the fastest way to hit a one-year profit target is to starve R&D, customer support, and security—leaving the churn and clinical/data risk for next year.

### The Options & Proposed Scorecard

* **Option A (Board's brief — rejected):** 100% Adjusted EBITDA, restructuring excluded. Invites a fourth round of cuts and weakens exit quality.
* **Option B (Recommended):** **55% Financial / 45% Value Drivers**, with **40% deferred over 3 years** (vesting 2028–2030 or on exit), **3-year malus/clawback**, and a **leaders-first downside rule** (executive bonuses cut/zeroed before frontline redundancies).
* **Option C (Financial-heavy compromise):** 70% Financial / 30% Value Drivers, 30% deferred—viable if the fund insists on higher margin gearing, **provided restructuring still stays inside EBITDA**.

**Recommended 2027 Scorecard (Option B):**
* **40% Reported EBITDA (including restructuring):** Reconciled line-by-line if Adjusted EBITDA is still shown to lenders.
* **15% Operating Cash Conversion (FCF / EBITDA):** Measured on trailing 4-quarter average working capital so payables aren't stretched.
* **20% Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** With a **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) floor** so price hikes can't mask customer losses.
* **15% Regretted Attrition & Engagement:** Protects scarce healthcare-software talent and stops cut-and-rehire cycles.
* **10% Platform Quality, Uptime & Security:** Verified by CTO/compliance audit so margin isn't bought with technical debt or patient-data risk.
