# 2027 Management Bonus Plan (Draft for Board & Remuneration Committee)

**Company Profile:** Private-Equity-Owned Healthcare Software (~350 employees)  
**Plan Year:** FY 2027  
**Status:** Draft for Board Review  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Rationale

For the past three consecutive years, the company has executed restructurings and excluded those costs from Adjusted EBITDA as "one-off" items. A fourth restructuring cannot credibly be treated as non-recurring. Continuing to exclude restructuring costs from the management bonus calculation creates a structural misalignment: management is insulated from the financial and operational cost of reorganisations while capturing the short-term payroll reduction in the bonus numerator.

In healthcare software, enterprise value at exit is driven by three things that buyers test rigorously in due diligence:
1. **Quality of earnings** (EBITDA that converts to cash without recurring "one-time" add-backs).
2. **Revenue durability** (Net Revenue Retention and low gross churn).
3. **Operational & clinical resilience** (system uptime, low defect rates, and retention of domain-expert engineers and implementation staff).

This 2027 plan aligns management incentives with **total enterprise value** rather than single-year accounting EBITDA. It keeps EBITDA as the anchor metric (50% weight), eliminates the restructuring carve-out, and pairs it with customer retention, product reliability, and team stability counter-metrics.

---

## 2. Plan Structure & Metric Weights

| Component | Weight | Metric Definition | Data Source | Owner |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **1. Quality EBITDA** | **50%** | **EBITDA after restructuring & severance costs** (GAAP/IFRS Operating EBITDA, excluding only board-approved M&A transaction fees). | Audited GL / ERP | CFO |
| **2. Revenue Durability** | **25%** | **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** on existing customer cohorts (including upsell, contraction, and churn). | Billing / CRM | CRO / VP CS |
| **3. Product & Clinical Reliability** | **15%** | **SLA Uptime & Critical Incident Rate** (e.g., ≥99.9% core availability and ≤ target P1/escaped clinical-safety defects). | Monitoring / Incident Log | CTO / VP Eng |
| **4. Organisational Stability** | **10%** | **Regretted Attrition Rate** across engineering, product, clinical, and customer delivery teams. | HRIS | VP People |
| **Total** | **100%** | | | |

### Why This Mix Works
- **Separates Covenant Reporting from Bonus Accounting:** The CFO continues to report Bank/Covenant Adjusted EBITDA to lenders under existing credit agreements. For management compensation, however, **restructuring costs are treated as an operating expense**.
- **Prevents "Hollowing Out" Before Exit:** A 10% payroll cut that boosts short-term EBITDA while triggering a 5-point drop in NRR or a spike in P1 healthcare incidents will reduce, rather than inflate, the bonus payout.
- **Protects Institutional Knowledge:** In a 350-person healthcare software business, losing tenured engineers or clinical implementation leads creates multi-quarter delivery delays and customer escalations.

## 3. Payout Curve & Calibration

Each metric is scored independently on a **0% to 150%** payout curve, with linear interpolation between Threshold, Target, and Stretch.

| Performance Level | Metric Achievement | Payout Multiplier |
|---|---|---:|
| **Below Threshold** | < 90% of Target (or below floor) | **0%** |
| **Threshold** | 90% of Target | **50%** |
| **Target** | 100% of Target | **100%** |
| **Stretch (Cap)** | ≥ 110% of Target | **150%** |

*(Note: For inverse metrics such as Regretted Attrition and P1 Incidents, Threshold/Target/Stretch correspond to maximum acceptable rates rather than minimums—e.g., Target ≤ 10% regretted attrition, Stretch ≤ 7%, Floor > 14% = 0% payout.)*

---

## 4. Mandatory Gates & Governance Guardrails

No bonus is earned or paid unless the following company-wide gates are met:

1. **Debt Covenant & Cash Gate:** Minimum cash balance and senior debt covenant headroom must be maintained at year-end. If a covenant breach occurs, total bonus payout is **0%**.
2. **Patient Safety, Regulatory & Security Gate:** Zero preventable material regulatory sanctions, HIPAA/GDPR reportable data breaches, or unresolved clinical-safety failures caused by deferred maintenance or understaffing. Any such event triggers a Board discretionary reduction up to **100%**.
3. **Gross Retention Floor:** If Gross Dollar Retention falls below the Board-approved floor (e.g., 88%), the non-EBITDA components are capped at **50%** regardless of upsell performance.
4. **Leadership Accountability Rule (Involuntary Cuts):** If the company initiates involuntary layoffs or a fourth restructuring during FY 2027 (outside of board-approved M&A overlap), the executive management team's total bonus payout is **capped at 0%** for the year, and executive variable pay is reduced before any broader employee bonus pool.

## 5. Illustrative Scenarios (Worked Comparison)

Assume a **$10.0M Target Bonus EBITDA** and **100% Target NRR = 105%**.

### Scenario A: "Repeat the Past" (Cost-Cutting to Hit Target)
- Management executes a $1.2M restructuring in Q2 to trim payroll.
- Reported Adjusted EBITDA (excluding restructuring) = **$10.4M** (104% of target).
- **Bonus EBITDA (inclusive of restructuring)** = $10.4M − $1.2M = **$9.2M** (92% of target → **60%** payout on the 50% weight = **30.0%**).
- Regretted attrition spikes to 16% (below floor → **0%** on 10% weight).
- NRR slips to 100% (Threshold → **50%** on 25% weight = **12.5%**).
- Reliability hits Target (**100%** on 15% weight = **15.0%**).
- **Result under old 100% Adjusted EBITDA plan:** **120.0%** payout (management rewarded for cutting).
- **Result under 2027 Plan:** **57.5%** payout (and **0%** if the Leadership Accountability Rule for involuntary cuts is triggered).

### Scenario B: "Operational Discipline" (Growth, Retention & Waste Reduction)
- Management avoids restructuring ($0 restructuring cost), reduces cloud/vendor waste by $0.6M, and improves implementation cycle time.
- **Bonus EBITDA** = **$10.2M** (102% of target → **110%** payout on 50% weight = **55.0%**).
- **NRR** = **107%** (102% of target → **110%** payout on 25% weight = **27.5%**).
- **Reliability** = Target (**100%** on 15% weight = **15.0%**).
- **Regretted Attrition** = 8.5% (between Target and Stretch → **125%** on 10% weight = **12.5%**).
- **Result under 2027 Plan:** **110.0%** payout (55.0% + 27.5% + 15.0% + 12.5% = 110.0%), backed by durable recurring revenue and an intact team.

## 6. Implementation & Review Rhythm

- **Q4 2026:** Finalise exact numerical Threshold/Target/Stretch bands from the 2027 Operating Plan; confirm definitions in writing with Audit Committee and CFO.
- **Quarterly Board Review:** Track all four metrics side by side—never EBITDA in isolation—so early warning signs in NRR, P1 incidents, or regretted attrition are addressed before they hit revenue.
- **No Mid-Year Re-Baselining:** Targets remain fixed for FY 2027 unless a board-approved acquisition or divestiture materially alters the perimeter.
- **Local Compliance:** Ensure plan terms and any workforce-related provisions comply with local employment law and, where applicable, works council or employee representative consultation requirements.
