**Prepared for:** Board of Directors

## Executive Summary

**Recommendation:** Do **not** announce 120 redundancies in December 2026. A 120-person reduction cannot deliver €9.0 million in 2027: once German and Dutch consultation, notice periods, severance, backfill, and attrition are included, **120 redundancies produce a net cash outflow of €2.0 million in 2027** (and only **+€2.2 million** on P&L even if the €4.2 million severance charge is booked in 2026). Payback does not occur until **Q2 2028**.

Instead, **close the 2027 gap through working capital, non-payroll spend, pricing/leakage, a selective hiring freeze, and leadership bonus deferral first**, delivering **€4.6M–€6.6M in P&L savings and €6.6M–€10.6M in cash in 2027** with zero severance and full reversibility. Only if a specific business line has permanent structural overcapacity should we bring a targeted, smaller restructuring proposal to the works councils *before* any public announcement.

## 1. Why 120 Redundancies Will Not Deliver €9.0M in 2027

Multiplying 120 roles by €75,000 gives **€9.0M** only if every person leaves on 1 January 2027 with zero severance, zero legal delay, zero operational backfill, and zero revenue loss. In an industrial services group operating in **Germany and the Netherlands**, four realities change the maths:

1. **Consultation and notice push exits into Q2–Q4 2027:**
   - **Netherlands:** A works council (*Ondernemingsraad*, mandatory at ≥50 employees) has an advisory right on reorganisations (Article 25 WOR). Dismissing ≥20 people in one UWV work area within three months triggers WMCO notification to UWV and unions, a one-month waiting period, UWV review (or negotiated settlement agreements), and 1–4 months' statutory notice.
   - **Germany:** Major operational changes require negotiating an *Interessenausgleich* and *Sozialplan* with the works council where one exists (§§111–112 BetrVG), social selection (*Sozialauswahl*, §1(3) KSchG — based on tenure, age, dependants, and disability, not performance), mass dismissal notice to the Agentur für Arbeit (§17 KSchG), and statutory notice of up to 7 months for 20+ years' service (§622 BGB).
   - **Result:** Virtually no payroll leaves in Q1 2027. Even with an aggressive schedule, average exits land mid-year, cutting 2027 gross payroll savings from **€9.00M to ~€4.88M** (estimate).
2. **Severance and transition costs arrive upfront:** At an estimated €35k average per FTE (social plan / *transitievergoeding* / settlement uplift), severance alone consumes **€4.20M** in 2027 cash, plus **€0.80M** for outplacement, retraining, and legal/advisory fees.
3. **Industrial services work doesn't vanish when headcount is cut:** Without explicitly ending contracts or unprofitable sites, cutting 8.6% of a 1,400-person workforce creates overtime, subcontractor backfill, and SLA/renewal risk.
4. **Second-order attrition and rehiring:** Research shows a 1% downsizing is associated with 31% higher voluntary turnover the following year (Trevor & Nyberg, *Academy of Management Journal*, 2008), and replacing an employee costs 0.5x to 2x annual salary (Gallup).

### Break-Even & Sensitivities

- **Break-even:** On a steady-state 2028 basis (€9.0M gross), losing **~€15M of revenue** at a 40% margin plus €3.0M of backfill/attrition wipes out the saving. At 40% contribution margin, **every €1.0M of lost revenue erases €400k of net saving**.
- **Sensitivity 1 — Exit timing:** Each **1-month delay** in average exit dates reduces 2027 gross savings by **€750k** (120 × €6.25k/month).
- **Sensitivity 2 — Severance level:** Each **+€10k/FTE** in negotiated *Sozialplan* / VSO terms adds **€1,200k** to 2027 cash outflow.

### Breakdown of Option 1 (Least Harm, Fastest Cash)

1. **Working capital (€2.0M–€4.0M one-off cash in Q1–Q2 2027):** Tighten invoicing cadence, resolve disputed WIP, enforce customer payment terms, and defer non-critical capex (without stretching small suppliers).
2. **Zero-based non-payroll spend (€1.5M–€2.5M in 2027):** Challenge external consultants, subcontractors, fleet/fuel contracts, IT licences, travel, and indirect vendors.
3. **Pricing & revenue leakage (€1.0M–€2.0M in 2027):** Enforce contract indexation, capture unbilled variations/change orders, and eliminate unauthorised discounting.
4. **Selective hiring freeze + natural attrition (€2.1M in 2027; €4.2M annualised):** At ~8% attrition on 1,400 staff (~112 exits/year), holding back 50% of non-billable/overhead replacements saves ~56 FTEs across 2027 with **€0 severance**.
5. **Leadership first:** Freeze executive/senior management bonuses and discretionary perks for 2027 before asking frontline teams to carry any sacrifice.

## 4. Legal & Governance Guardrails (Not Legal Advice)

- **Do not pre-announce 120 redundancies in December 2026** as a fixed decision before engaging the Dutch Works Council (Art. 25 WOR) and German Works Council (§111 BetrVG). Doing so risks injunctions, Enterprise Chamber proceedings, and voided dismissal notices (§17 KSchG).
- **If a temporary demand dip is the driver:** Note that Germany's extended 24-month *Kurzarbeitergeld* maximum expires on **31 December 2026** (reverting to 12 months from 1 January 2027 unless extended), and the Netherlands' *werktijdverkorting* covers only non-business-risk calamities (verify status of the *Wet personeelsbehoud bij crisis* bill with Dutch counsel).
