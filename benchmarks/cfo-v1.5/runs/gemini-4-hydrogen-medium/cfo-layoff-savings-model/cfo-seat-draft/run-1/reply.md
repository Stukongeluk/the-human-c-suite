### Why the €9.0M maths breaks

The trap here is treating gross annualised payroll (`120 × €75,000 = €9.0M`) as a 2027 saving—a headcount target without a diagnosis of the underlying cash or margin problem usually ends up cutting capacity you have to buy back as overtime, contractors, and lost contracts.

In **Germany and the Netherlands**, works council consultation (NL Article 25 WOR; DE §§111–112 BetrVG), collective dismissal notifications (NL WMCO / UWV; DE §17 KSchG), and statutory notice periods (up to 4 months in NL, up to 7 months in DE under §622 BGB) push actual exit dates into **Q2–Q4 2027**. Using an illustrative 50/50 DE/NL ramp (0 exits Q1, 40 off payroll in Q2, 100 in Q3, 120 in Q4):

| 120 Redundancies (€k, estimates) | Q1 2027 | Q2 2027 | Q3 2027 | Q4 2027 | **2027 Total** | **2028** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gross payroll saving (from exit dates) | 0 | +750 | +1,875 | +2,250 | **+4,875** | **+9,000** |
| Severance & social plan (€35k/FTE avg) | 0 | -1,400 | -2,100 | -700 | **-4,200** | 0 |
| Outplacement, transition & legal fees | -200 | -250 | -250 | -100 | **-800** | 0 |
| Overtime / contractor backfill (16% / 10%) | 0 | -120 | -300 | -360 | **-780** | -900 |
| Margin lost on €1.5M revenue at risk (40%) | 0 | -100 | -200 | -300 | **-600** | -600 |
| Rehiring & ramp-up (15 roles by 2028) | 0 | 0 | 0 | -100 | **-100** | -1,325 |
| Extra voluntary attrition (10 / 15 exits) | 0 | 0 | -200 | -200 | **-400** | -600 |
| **Net cash effect** | **-200** | **-1,120** | **-1,175** | **+490** | **-2,005** | **+5,575** |

- **Even excluding €4.2M severance as "one-off"** (which should not be hidden if restructuring recurs), 2027 net P&L is only **+€2.20M**—€6.8M short of €9.0M.
- **Sensitivity:** Every 1-month delay in average exit dates costs **€750k** in 2027 gross savings; every +€10k/FTE in negotiated severance adds **€1.20M** to 2027 cash outflow.

1. **Non-payroll, working capital, pricing & attrition first (Recommended):** Working capital tightening without squeezing small suppliers (**+€2.0M–€4.0M cash**), zero-based non-payroll spend (**+€1.5M–€2.5M**), contract indexation/unbilled variation recovery (**+€1.0M–€2.0M**), selective hiring freeze on non-billable attrition (**~€2.1M** in 2027, €0 severance), and **freezing leadership bonuses first**. 2. **Temporary demand bridge (if volume dip is cyclical):** German *Kurzarbeit* (requires works council agreement under §87(1) no. 3 BetrVG; note the 24-month maximum reverts to 12 months on 1 Jan 2027 unless extended) plus Dutch flex reduction/redeployment and Option 1 spend cuts (**+€3.5M–€5.0M P&L**, **+€5.5M–€7.5M cash** in 2027).
3. **Targeted structural exits (40–50 non-billable roles) + Option 1:** Only if specific contracts or sites are permanently closing, consulted with the Dutch *Ondernemingsraad* and German *Betriebsrat* **before** any public announcement.
4. **Do nothing:** €0 saving, preserves capacity, leaves the gap open.
