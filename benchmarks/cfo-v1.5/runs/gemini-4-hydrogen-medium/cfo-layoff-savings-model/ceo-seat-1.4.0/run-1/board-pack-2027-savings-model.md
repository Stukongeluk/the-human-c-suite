## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

**What is at stake:** We need to close a **€9,000k (€9.0M) P&L / cash gap in 2027** *[insert specific driver: e.g., margin compression, covenant headroom, or customer volume drop—and what happens if we miss it]*.

**Key finding:** Announcing **120 redundancies (8.6% of workforce)** in December 2026 will **not** deliver €9.0M of net savings in 2027. While `120 × €75k` equals €9.0M on an annualised run-rate basis, statutory consultation in Germany and the Netherlands, notice periods, and upfront severance/transition costs mean **realistic net 2027 savings are €25k to €700k** in the Base Case (and negative in a contested scenario). The full run-rate does not land until **2028**.

**Recommendation:** Do not announce a 120-person redundancy target next month. Instead:
1. **Before any announcement**, quantify the exact €9.0M problem (structural demand loss vs. temporary dip vs. margin/cost leakage) and lock in faster, reversible 2027 levers (non-payroll/subcontractor spend, contract repricing, hiring freeze/attrition, and executive pay/bonus restraint).
2. **If a structural headcount reduction is still required**, engage the German *Betriebsrat* and Dutch *Ondernemingsraad* early with the real numbers, pair any reduction with explicit contract/work exits so remaining teams aren't backfilled with agency labour, and reset the board's 2027 P&L expectation to realistic timing.

## 4. Legal, Works Council & Governance Reality (Not Legal Advice)

Announcing "120 redundancies" externally or internally before consulting employee representatives creates severe legal and execution risk in both jurisdictions:

- **Germany:**
  - **Works Council (*Betriebsrat*):** Where established, §§111–112 *BetrVG* require negotiating an *Interessenausgleich* (reconciliation of interests) and *Sozialplan* before implementing major operational changes (>20 employees).
  - **Mass Dismissal Notification (§17 *KSchG*):** Advance notification to the *Agentur für Arbeit* is mandatory above statutory thresholds (e.g., 10% or >25/30+ employees within 30 days). Procedural errors can render dismissals void. Employer-initiated termination agreements count toward thresholds.
  - **Selection & Notice:** Operational dismissals require *Sozialauswahl* (§1(3) *KSchG*: tenure, age, maintenance obligations, severe disability—not performance rankings). Statutory notice (§622 *BGB*) runs up to 7 months after 20 years' service.
- **Netherlands:**
  - **Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad*):** Mandatory at ≥50 employees. Article 25 *WOR* grants a formal advisory right on reorganisations *before* a final decision is taken, with appeal to the Enterprise Chamber (*Ondernemingskamer*) if bypassed or presented as a *fait accompli*.
  - **Collective Dismissal (WMCO) & UWV:** Dismissing ≥20 employees in one UWV work area within 3 months requires advance notification and consultation with UWV and unions (employer-initiated settlement agreements count toward the 20). Dismissals require UWV permission or mutual settlement, following the reflection principle (*afspiegelingsbeginsel*), with statutory *transitievergoeding*.

## 5. Human Impact & Transition Safeguards (If Any Reduction Proceeds)

If consultation confirms structural redundancies are unavoidable:
1. **Leaders go first:** Freeze or cut executive bonuses and discretionary perks before any frontline role is affected.
2. **Cut work, not just people:** Explicitly name which contracts, sites, or non-billable projects stop so the remaining 1,280 staff do not absorb 120 people's workload.
3. **Do it once, decently:** Offer voluntary exits, early retirement, and internal redeployment first; fund outplacement, coaching, and paid retraining from the day notice is given.
4. **Communicate at human speed:** Brief works councils and managers first; tell affected staff personally via their own manager with HR present and a same-day written package—never via mass email or surprise access lockouts.
5. **Track post-change health:** Monitor regretted attrition, sickness absence, overtime/agency spend, and customer SLA scores monthly for 12 months.

## 6. Key Assumptions & Questions for the Board

- **Assumption 1:** The €9.0M target is a 2027 P&L/cash need, not a 2028 run-rate target. *Would change recommendation:* If the board only needs a €9.0M exit run-rate by Q4 2027, a smaller, phased attrition + voluntary programme is viable without forced cuts.
- **Assumption 2:** Average severance is ~€27.5k and average exit is 1 July 2027. *Would change recommendation:* Actual tenure/age profiles from HRIS could swing 2027 upfront cash cost by ±€1.0M+.
- **Open questions before finalising the pack:**
  1. What specific problem is driving the €9.0M target—a temporary demand dip, structural contract losses, or margin erosion?
  2. How much of the €9.0M can we capture in Q1–Q2 2027 from subcontractors, non-payroll spend, and loss-making contract exits before touching permanent headcount?
