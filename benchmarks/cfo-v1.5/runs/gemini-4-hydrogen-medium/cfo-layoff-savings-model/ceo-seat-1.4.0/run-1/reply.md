### The Trap in the CEO's Ask
`120 × €75,000 = €9.0M` is a **2028 annualised run-rate**, not a 2027 in-year saving—and jumping straight to a headcount number before diagnosing whether the €9M gap is temporary demand, contract margin, or subcontractor spend locks in upfront cash costs and legal risk without closing 2027.

### What the Honest 2027 Model Shows (Base Case, €k)

| Line Item | 2027 Base Case (Avg. Exit 1 Jul 2027) | Basis / Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross 2027 payroll savings (6 months)** | **+4,500** | `120 × €75k × (6 / 12)` after 2–4 mo consultation + notice |
| Severance / *Sozialplan* / *Transitievergoeding* | -3,300 | Est. €27.5k/FTE (range €20k–€35k depending on tenure) |
| Outplacement, training & legal/advisory | -500 | Est. €2.5k/FTE transition support + €200k counsel |
| **Net 2027 savings (before backfill)** | **+700** | **€8,300k short of the €9.0M target** |
| Operational leakage (overtime / agency cover) | -675 | Est. 15% of gross if contracts/work are not stopped |
| **Net 2027 savings (after backfill)** | **+25** | **Essentially break-even in 2027; €9.0M lands in 2028** |

*(In a contested downside—avg. exit 1 Sep 2027, €35k severance—2027 is a **-€1.80M to -€2.40M net outflow**.)*

### What to Put in Front of the CEO Before the Board Sees This
1. **Reset the 2027 levers to what actually hits Q1–Q2 P&L:** Non-payroll/subcontractor squeeze, repricing or exiting loss-making contracts, a hiring freeze with natural attrition (zero severance), and executive pay/bonus restraint first. If the demand drop is temporary in Germany, *Kurzarbeit* bridges payroll faster (note: max duration reverts from 24 to 12 months on 1 January 2027 unless extended, and requires *Betriebsrat* consent).
2. **Respect the DE/NL legal floor (verify with employment counsel, not legal advice):** Announcing "120 redundancies" before consulting the *Ondernemingsraad* (Art. 25 *WOR*, Enterprise Chamber appeal risk) and *Betriebsrat* (§§111–112 *BetrVG* *Interessenausgleich* & *Sozialplan*), or mistiming WMCO / UWV (NL) and §17 *KSchG* / *Agentur für Arbeit* (DE) notifications, can delay exits or void dismissals. Selection must follow *Sozialauswahl* / *afspiegelingsbeginsel*—not performance rankings.
3. **If structural cuts are still needed:** Pair every eliminated role with stopped work so the remaining 1,280 staff aren't backfilled with expensive agency labour.

### Two Questions That Would Change This Recommendation
1. **Is the €9.0M a 2027 in-year P&L/cash requirement, or a Q4 2027 exit run-rate?** (If it's run-rate, attrition + voluntary exits can get you there with far less cash burn.)
2. **How much of the €9.0M can we take out of subcontractors, non-payroll spend, and loss-making contracts in Q1–Q2 2027 before touching permanent staff?**
