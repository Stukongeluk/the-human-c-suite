# Leverage covenant: our plan for the bank review

Note to [owner's name] from [CFO's name] · Draft, 20 November 2026

## 1. Recommendation

We don't announce job cuts before the bank meeting. We take the bank a costed plan that brings leverage back below 3.5x by the Q2 test, with headroom to about 3.3x, and in which the family and the management team contribute first.

This forecast is the result of decisions we took as owner and management team. Fixing it is our job, not our employees'. If the work in the next weeks shows that demand has fallen for good, we review the size of the workforce properly, with the works council, as part of the plan for 2028.

## 2. The gap

On current forecasts, net debt / EBITDA is 3.6x at the end of Q2 against a limit of 3.5x. This note assumes the test is at 30 June 2027 on twelve-month EBITDA; the definitions in the facility agreement decide.

| Target | Net debt lower by (EBITDA unchanged) | or EBITDA higher by (net debt unchanged) | Our figures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.5x (the limit) | 2.8% | 2.9% | €[ ]m / €[ ]m |
| 3.3x (headroom) | 8.3% | 9.1% | €[ ]m / €[ ]m |

Example: with twelve-month EBITDA of €5.0m and net debt of €18.0m, reaching 3.5x takes €0.5m less net debt or €0.14m more EBITDA. Reaching 3.3x takes €1.5m less net debt or €0.45m more EBITDA.

## 3. Why announcing a 10% cut now doesn't help the Q2 test

### It lands after the test

| Step | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| Works council advice on a major reduction (art. 25 WOR) | 4–8 weeks; if we decide against its advice, a further month before we may act |
| Collective dismissal notice to UWV and the unions (WMCO) | No dismissals until one month after notice; runs in parallel |
| UWV dismissal permits on business-economic grounds | Often 4–8 weeks |
| Notice periods | 1–4 months by length of service, minus the UWV procedure, at least 1 month |

Announced in December, people would leave around April to June 2027.

### The money (estimates)

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Annual saving, 26 roles at €55–60k fully loaded | €1.43–1.56m |
| Saving captured by the end of Q2 (one to two months) | €0.12–0.26m |
| One-off cash, paid first: transition payments (average 12 years' service is about four months' pay) plus legal and advice, before any social plan | €0.41–0.52m |

In the example above (mid case: €0.19m saved, €0.47m one-off cost), leverage at the Q2 test would be about **3.52x** if restructuring costs can be added back to EBITDA, and about **3.87x** if they can't. Doing nothing gives 3.60x.

The fair counter-argument: a year later, at full run-rate, the same cut would take the example to about 2.6x. That effect is real, but only if the work has gone for good. If volume comes back, we will have paid to lose skilled people we then have to find again. That's why the diagnosis comes before any decision about jobs.

### The accounting

Under IFRS (IAS 37, paragraph 72), and similarly under Dutch GAAP (RJ 252), a restructuring provision is booked once there is a detailed formal plan and its main features have been announced to the people affected. An announcement in December would put the cost into this year's results, inside the twelve months tested at Q2. Whether it can be added back, and up to what cap, depends on the facility agreement. To confirm with our auditor.

### The law (to check with employment counsel)

- **Works council.** A major reduction needs the works council's advice, asked "at a time when it can still have a significant influence on the decision" (art. 25(1)(d) and (2) WOR). If we decide against its advice, it can appeal to the Enterprise Chamber, which can order us to withdraw the decision or ban steps to carry it out (art. 26 WOR). Announcing a decided cut before asking is the clearest case for that appeal.
- **Collective dismissal.** Twenty or more dismissals in one work area within three months must be notified to UWV and to every union with members here, and we consult them on avoiding, limiting and softening the dismissals. Skipping this can make the dismissals void.
- **Who goes.** Agency workers first, then the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel): within interchangeable roles, by age group, last in first out. We can't choose.
- **No public short-time scheme.** Werktijdverkorting covers only calamities outside normal business risk. The proposed Wet personeelsbehoud bij crisis (bill 36940) is still before the Tweede Kamer and couldn't take effect before 1 January 2029. Any reduced-hours arrangement would have to be agreed with the works council, the unions and staff.

### People and reputation

- Downsizing 1% of a workforce was associated with a 31% rise in voluntary turnover the following year (Trevor and Nyberg, 2008). The people who leave first are the ones with options, usually our best craftspeople.
- Skilled trades take years to rebuild, and our name in the region is part of how we hire.
- News of cuts reaches customers and suppliers. If suppliers' credit insurers lower their limits on us, we'll be asked to pay faster, and net debt goes up.

### What the bank needs from us

Liquidity (a 13-week cash forecast), covenant projections in base and downside, concrete actions with leads and dates, and what the owner is contributing. An announcement about jobs, with its timing, cost and legal steps unresolved, answers none of these.

**Bank.** I call our relationship manager this week, so the meeting holds no surprises. In the meeting we ask for a temporary reset of the Q2 and Q3 2027 tests to [x]x, stepping back to 3.5x, as a safety net against forecast risk. We offer monthly reporting and expect a fee. **Works council and staff.** We brief the works council in confidence on the forecast and the plan before the bank meeting and ask for its ideas on waste and cost. No announcement about jobs. Once the plan is agreed, staff hear it from us, in our words, before rumours do.

## 5. The bank meeting: outline

1. What happened and why, owned by us as owner and management.
2. Twelve-month EBITDA, net debt and the covenant projection by quarter to Q4 2027, base and downside.
3. Liquidity: the 13-week cash forecast.
4. The actions: lead, date, size, and what's already done.
5. What the family and the management team are contributing.
6. Our ask: a temporary reset as a safety net, and the reporting we offer.
7. If demand looks structural: a review with the works council, with a date to report back.

## 6. When a smaller workforce would be on the table

If, by the close of Q1 2027, order intake shows a lasting drop that these measures can't absorb, and leverage stays above 3.5x in the downside case. Then we would:

- bring the works council in at the problem stage, with the real numbers;
- try voluntary routes first: turnover, early retirement, a voluntary leaving scheme;
- agree a social plan with the unions;
- fund training, coaching and job-search support from the first day of notice;
- do it once, sized properly.

## 8. Assumptions and what would change this

- The Q2 test is at 30 June 2027 on twelve-month EBITDA, and 3.6x is the base case.
- Cost per role (€55–60k fully loaded), average service (about 12 years) and the procedure timings are estimates.
- This changes if the facility defines EBITDA or net debt differently, if liquidity rather than the covenant is the real constraint, if the bank has already said it expects headcount action (then we show the review process, not an announcement), or if the lost volume is large and permanent.
