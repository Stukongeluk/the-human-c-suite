**Don't announce cuts before the meeting. Take the bank a costed plan for a 0.1x gap, with the owner's money going in first.**

The gap is about 3%: net debt down 2.8%, or EBITDA up 2.9%, gets you to 3.5x. Aim for 3.3x (net debt down about 8%) so a forecast miss doesn't put you back here.

A 10% cut (26 people) mostly misses the Q2 test:

- **Timing.** Works council advice (art. 25 WOR), the collective-dismissal notice to UWV and the unions (WMCO), UWV permits and notice periods mean people leave four to six months after an announcement. My estimate: the test catches one or two months of a roughly €1.5m annual saving (€0.1–0.25m), while €0.4–0.5m of transition payments and advice goes out first and raises net debt.
- **Accounting.** Announcing can force a restructuring provision into the twelve months being tested. Unless the facility allows an add-back, covenant EBITDA falls. Ask your auditor.
- **Law.** Announcing a decision before asking the works council's advice is what the Enterprise Chamber can block, and the reflection principle, not management, decides who goes.
- **People and market.** Skilled staff, customers and suppliers' credit insurers hear it too. Downsizing 1% of a workforce was followed by 31% more voluntary leavers the next year (Trevor & Nyberg, 2008).

What I'd do in the three weeks:

1. **Talk to the owner privately first:** "I want what you want: the bank leaving convinced we're in control. An announcement can't deliver that by Q2. A plan with numbers, with the family going first, can."
2. **Read the facility agreement:** EBITDA add-backs, equity cure, waiver terms.
3. **Release cash:** inventory, overdue receivables, non-critical capex, a paused dividend. Have some of it done by the meeting.
4. **Protect EBITDA:** prices and surcharges, loss-making products, scrap and energy, overtime and agency hours (with honest notice), a hiring freeze; normal turnover may cover a good part of 10% within a year.
5. **Call your relationship manager this week,** then bring a 13-week cash forecast, base and downside covenant projections, and the actions. Ask for a temporary reset as a safety net; agreed months ahead, it usually costs less than a waiver after a breach.
6. **Own it:** tell the bank the forecast is management's and the owner's to fix, with management bonuses suspended. Brief the works council in confidence beforehand.

If demand has fallen for good, a smaller workforce may be part of the 2028 answer: decided on the facts, with the works council, voluntary routes first, once.
