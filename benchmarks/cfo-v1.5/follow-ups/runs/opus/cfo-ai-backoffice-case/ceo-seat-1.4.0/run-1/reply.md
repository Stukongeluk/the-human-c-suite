I wouldn't take a case for replacing 27 people in 2027 to the board. I'd take a case for freeing up to 30% of the team's capacity by the end of 2029. Headcount falls from 90 to about 68 through attrition, a backfill freeze and moves into open roles elsewhere in the company, and nobody is made redundant because of the programme before the end of 2028. On my planning estimates that comes to about SEK +5m over 2027–29 and about SEK 16m a year from 2030. The 27-redundancies version comes to about SEK −15m over the same three years, and it can't be undone if the AI underdelivers.

The board paper is drafted on that basis in `outputs/business-case.md`. It covers the options, financials, sensitivities, human impact, the Swedish legal steps, gates and a communication plan.

**Why the 2027 version doesn't hold up**

- **The work won't be gone yet.** My central estimate is that automation removes about 6% of the team's work by the end of 2027, 20% by the end of 2028 and 29% by the end of 2029, after counting the new oversight work it creates. Even top AP functions still handle about half their invoices by hand: 49.2% touchless ([Ardent Partners 2025, via Corpay](https://www.corpay.com/resources/blog/ap-team-productivity-benchmarks)).
- **Swedish process sets the clock.** MBL negotiation comes first. Arbetsförmedlingen must be notified at least four months ahead when 26–100 people are affected ([fact sheet, May 2026](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/download/18.446992bb1922f85547c1f0ec/1779803325065/Faktablad%20om%20varsel%20(engelska).pdf)). Notice runs up to six months under the FAO–Forena agreement. So most exits land around Q4 2027 at best, which means at most a quarter of the annual saving in 2027, while the costs arrive in full.
- **The remaining work still has to be done.** Consultants covering it would cost more than the payroll saving in 2028. If the people left cover it, it shows up as late closes, errors and resignations.
- **You don't choose who leaves.** LAS selection follows length of service across the operational unit and agreement area, not the tasks being automated.
- **2027 is a reporting-change year.** The Solvency II amendments apply from 30 January 2027, including an audit of the SFCR balance sheet unless you're exempt ([William Fry, Oct 2026](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/solvency-ii-reform-key-changes/)).

The board is right about the direction. Top AP functions run with less than a third of the staff of the weakest, and doing nothing is a real risk. Klarna shrank through a hiring freeze and attrition rather than layoffs ([Fortune, Feb 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/02/17/klarnas-ceo-dario-amodei-ai-white-collar-workforce-shrink-2030/)), and still had to bring humans back where quality slipped. As Gartner put it this month: "AI-generated capacity is a resource, not a return" ([4 Nov 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-11-04-gartner-idnetifies-three-steps-for-cfos-to-turn-ai-time-saavings-into-business-value)).

The trap is that a case built to prove a number chosen in advance will be tested by the unions, your auditor and possibly FI. If the tools aren't ready, you own a saving that never arrives.

**Options (SEK m, planning estimates)**

| Option | 2027 | 2027–29 | Per year from 2030 | Reversible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Do nothing | 0 | 0 | 0 | Yes |
| B. 27 redundancies in 2027 | −20.9 | −14.9 | +18.9 | No |
| C. Automate, freeze, redeploy (recommended) | −10.6 | +4.7 | +16.2 | Yes, at each gate |
| D. Automate, keep everyone, reinvest | −12.9 | −21.9 | −3.5 plus value of new work | Yes |

B only beats C over three years if AI removes about twice the work I estimate. If it removes half, B loses about SEK 43m and C about SEK 4m.

**Human impact.** Under C, roles change rather than end. Everyone gets paid learning time and a career conversation in Q1, there's first call on open roles across the company, and union representatives are involved from the diagnostic onwards. The pressure point is workload during parallel running. The paper tracks overtime, sick leave and regretted leavers next to the savings, at team level only. It also states that finance leadership owns the result: if the programme underdelivers, the answer is to re-plan, not to cut faster.

**Two questions that would change the case**

1. What is the 30% meant to solve: an expense-ratio gap, a profit or capital problem, or a view of what AI can do? And what SEK figure, by when? If it's about SEK 16–20m a year from 2030, C gets there. If it's SEK 24m of cash in 2027, no AI-based plan does, and we should look at other levers.
2. Can you get the real inputs before this goes out? I need the split of the 90 by process, three-year attrition, consultant and temp spend in finance, invoice volumes and e-invoice share, and which collective agreement applies. Attrition decides the most: at 5% instead of 8%, the 2029 reduction falls to about 17 roles; at 11% it reaches about 26.

**Key assumptions:** a fully loaded cost of about SEK 0.9m per FTE, attrition of 8% a year, consultants at about SEK 1.6m per FTE-year, a programme costing about SEK 22m over three years, and no cash or capital emergency. Each is flagged in the paper for your actual figures. This isn't legal advice: check the MBL, LAS and agreement points with employment counsel before the paper goes out.
