# Q2 Leverage Covenant Action Plan & Bank Review Brief

**Prepared for:** CFO & Managing Owner (Private — pre-bank review)  
**Situation:** Forecast Q2 leverage of **3.60x** vs. **3.50x** covenant limit; bank review in **3 weeks**  
**Company profile:** Family-owned furniture manufacturer, Netherlands, 260 employees  
**Note on figures:** Because actual general ledger, payroll aggregate, working-capital and loan-agreement figures are not yet loaded, all euro amounts below are **labelled illustrative estimates** scaled to a 260-employee Dutch furniture manufacturer (€40.0M revenue, €4.00M LTM EBITDA, €14.40M forecast Q2 Net Debt, €60k average fully loaded payroll cost per employee). Replace with actuals from ERP, payroll aggregates and the facility agreement in Week 1.  
**Legal disclaimer:** References to Dutch employment law (WOR, WMCO, statutory notice and transition payments) and loan covenants are for financial planning only and are **not legal or tax advice**. Verify all legal steps with Dutch employment counsel and banking counsel before acting.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Do not announce 10% job cuts (26 roles) before the bank meeting.** Under Dutch employment law (mandatory works council advice under Art. 25 WOR, collective dismissal notification and consultation with UWV and unions under WMCO for 20+ roles, plus 1–4 months' notice), payroll savings will **not arrive in Q2**, while legal and severance costs hit first. Announcing cuts before consultation also creates legal risk (including Enterprise Chamber intervention) and signals panic rather than control to the bank.
- **The Q2 gap is 0.10x — a 2.8% gap, not a 10% payroll crisis.** On the illustrative baseline (€14.40M Net Debt / €4.00M EBITDA), closing the gap to 3.50x requires **€400k of Net Debt reduction** (or €115k of EBITDA); reaching comfortable headroom at **3.40x** requires **€800k of Net Debt reduction**.
- **Recommended path (Option B):** Close the Q2 gap through **working-capital cash release, non-safety capex deferral, non-payroll spend/pricing discipline, and paused family dividends/leadership bonuses**, backed by a **13-week cash forecast** and a **proactive temporary waiver request** (e.g., 3.75x–4.00x for Q2–Q3) as downside insurance. Keep headcount levers (hiring freeze, natural attrition, and early works council dialogue) as pre-agreed triggers if order intake weakens further.

**Hidden costs not yet in the table above:**
- **Regretted attrition:** Research by Trevor & Nyberg (2008) found a 1% downsizing was associated with **31% higher voluntary turnover** the following year. In a regional Dutch furniture plant, losing skilled woodworkers, upholsterers or CNC operators is hard to reverse.
- **Rehiring cost:** Replacing a skilled employee typically costs **0.5x to 2x annual salary** (Gallup). If demand recovers in 12–18 months, rehiring even half the roles wipes out the entire year-2 saving.
- **Break-even risk:** Losing just 1–2 mid-sized retail accounts (~€1.5M revenue at 35% contribution margin = €525k EBITDA) due to delivery delays or quality drops more than wipes out the first year's net saving.

## 4. Dutch Legal, Governance & Loan Agreement Checklist

Verify these four items in Week 1 with Dutch employment counsel and banking counsel (not legal advice):

1. **Works Council (OR — *Wet op de ondernemingsraden*, WOR):** Mandatory from 50 employees. Any material reorganisation requires prior written advice under **Art. 25 WOR** while the decision can still change. Bypassing the OR risks appeal and suspension at the Enterprise Chamber (*Ondernemingskamer*).
2. **Collective Dismissal (WMCO):** Dismissing **20 or more employees** within one UWV work area within three months requires advance notification to UWV and unions *before* the decision is final, followed by consultation and a waiting period. Employer-initiated settlement agreements (*vaststellingsovereenkomsten*) count toward the 20.
3. **Collective Agreement (CAO) & Flex Rules:** Check the applicable CAO (likely *CAO Meubelindustrie en Meubileringsbedrijven*) for social-plan expectations, overtime/hours flexibility, and agency-worker rules. Standard Dutch short-time work (*werktijdverkorting*) covers calamities outside normal business risk only; check with counsel whether the *Wet personeelsbehoud bij crisis* (before parliament in 2026) has taken effect and applies.
4. **Loan Agreement Definitions:** Check the exact definition of **Net Debt** (cash netting, leases/IFRS 16, subordinated shareholder debt) and **Covenant EBITDA** (whether restructuring charges are added back or deducted, and whether an equity cure clause exists).

## 5. Three-Week Plan to the Bank Review

### Week 1: Establish the Real Numbers & Align the Owner
- Pull actuals from ERP, general ledger, payroll (aggregated), order book and loan agreement to replace every illustrative estimate above.
- Build a **13-week rolling cash forecast** (base and downside cases).
- Meet privately with the owner: walk through Option A vs. Option B side by side, confirm dividend/bonus pause, and test whether a subordinated shareholder loan is available if needed.
- Verify covenant definitions with banking counsel.

### Week 2: Execute Immediate Cash Levers & Draft Bank Pack
- Launch overdue receivables sprint, slow-moving inventory clearance, and non-safety capex hold (with named owners and weekly targets).
- Audit top 20 non-payroll vendors and unapproved sales discounts.
- Prepare the bank pack:
  1. **13-week cash forecast** (base and downside)
  2. **Q2–Q4 covenant bridge** showing path to ≤3.40x
  3. **Owner & leadership commitment** (dividends/bonuses paused; optional shareholder support)
  4. **Pre-agreed downside triggers** (if Q3 order intake drops >X%: selective hiring freeze, natural attrition, flex-labour adjustment, and early OR consultation before any structural headcount change)
  5. **Specific ask:** confirmation of covenant compliance under base case, plus a **pre-emptive temporary waiver / reset (e.g., 3.75x–4.00x for Q2–Q3)** as downside insurance.

### Week 3: Bank Review & Governance Cadence
- Send the pack to the relationship manager **3–5 business days ahead** of the meeting (banks reward early transparency and hate surprises).
- Hold the bank review; lock in any waiver terms in writing.
- Brief the management team and **inform the OR proactively** (under Art. 24 WOR general financial position update) on the focus on cash, working capital and order-book discipline — building trust early so that if structural measures are ever needed later, you have a credible partner.

## 6. Ongoing Tracking (Financial & People Indicators)

Review weekly (cash) and monthly (P&L and people) with the owner and management team:

- **Financial:** 13-week cash headroom, Net Debt / LTM EBITDA (actual & 2-quarter forecast), DSO, inventory days, gross margin / discount leakage, order-book coverage (weeks).
- **Operational & People:** On-time delivery, scrap/rework rate, preventive maintenance completion, safety incidents, sickness absence, voluntary attrition (overall and skilled trades).
