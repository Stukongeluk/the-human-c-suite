### Why the Board's 30% Case Fails on Its Own Numbers
The board is right to push on expense ratio and manual toil in AP, reconciliations, and reporting—but **30% next year is a slogan, not a business case**:
1. **Swedish law pushes payroll savings past the Year 1 deadline.** Dismissing 27 people triggers MBL union negotiations, a **4-month statutory *varsel*** to Arbetsförmedlingen (26–100 people), and **1–6 months of paid notice** (est. 4 months average). Payroll exits don't land until Q3, while AI integration, severance/settlement packages (*avtalsturlista*), and interim cover hit immediately. *(Not legal advice—confirm exact timelines and collective agreements with Swedish employment counsel.)*
2. **LAS seniority rules (*turordning*) work against an AI transition.** With only three statutory exemptions, involuntary redundancies follow length of service rather than role or AI fluency—risking the exact specialists you need to run and audit new tools, while 9-month re-employment priority (*företrädesrätt*) complicates rehiring if AI under-delivers.
3. **Tasks, not whole jobs, are automatable in a regulated insurer.** Sizing by function puts realistic addressable capacity at **15%–23% over 24–30 months**—highest in AP (25%–35%), lower in insurance reconciliations (15%–25%), and lowest in IFRS 17 / Solvency II reporting (10%–15%), where segregation of duties, DORA vendor oversight, and human sign-off for Finansinspektionen cannot be delegated to a model.

### How to Frame the Board Conversation
- **Open with the problem, not a refusal:** Agree with the goal of lowering finance cost and manual toil, then show all four options side by side on the same quarterly cash basis (Board's 30% cut, Gated AI + Attrition, Non-Payroll Cleanup, and Do Nothing).
- **Book capacity first, payroll second:** Commit zero headcount reduction until the **Q2 pilot gate** proves accuracy and net hours freed on your own ledgers (after human review and exception handling). Capture proven capacity via natural attrition (~10%/yr) and internal redeployment, supported by early MBL union co-design and Sweden's transition study support (*omställningsstudiestöd*).
- **Bring CTO and Audit/Risk into the room:** Have IT size the policy-admin/ERP integration and DORA controls, and have Internal Audit sign off on the Q2 gate criteria before any role changes.
