# Board Business Case: AI & Automation in Finance and Back-Office Operations

**Prepared by:** Chief Financial Officer  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Scope:** 90 FTEs across Accounts Payable (25), Reconciliations (35), and Financial & Regulatory Reporting (30)  
**Currency:** SEK (thousands, kSEK) — *All unit costs, timelines, and headcount projections marked `[Est.]` are planning estimates pending payroll, vendor, and pilot-gate verification.*

---

## 1. Executive Recommendation

**Do not commit to a 30% headcount reduction (27 FTEs) next year.** Under Swedish employment law and realistic AI delivery timelines, forcing 27 exits in Year 1 produces a **net cash outflow of -5,150 kSEK in Year 1** and only **+5,050 kSEK cumulative net over two years**, while creating severe Solvency II, IFRS 17, and audit-control risks.

**Recommended path (Option B — Staged, Task-Sized AI + Flow-Based Transition):**
1. **Size tasks, not headcount.** Pilot AI in three gated waves—Accounts Payable first, then Reconciliations, then Reporting—booking benefits as **hours freed** before reducing any role. Realistic task-level capacity release over 18–24 months is **18–23 FTE equivalents (20%–25%)**, not 30% in Year 1.
2. **Capture payroll reductions through natural attrition, a selective hiring freeze, and internal redeployment** rather than forced redundancies. This avoids Swedish collective-redundancy delays, *turordning* (last-in, first-out) disruption, and rehiring penalties.
3. **Expected financial outcome:** **-1,350 kSEK net in Year 1**, **+11,100 kSEK net in Year 2**, and **+9,750 kSEK cumulative over two years**—nearly double the 2-year net cash return of the forced-cut option, with capped downside if any pilot misses its quality gate.

## 2. What Is at Stake & Baseline Cost Structure

Before selecting a target, the Board needs clarity on the underlying cost base and what happens if we miss:
- **Baseline team (`[Est.]`):** 90 FTEs at an average fully loaded cost of **800 kSEK/FTE/year** (200 kSEK/quarter, assuming ~550 kSEK gross salary plus ~45% for *arbetsgivaravgifter*, ITP occupational pension, holiday pay, and IT/seat costs) = **72,000 kSEK/year** (18,000 kSEK/quarter).
- **Functional split (`[Est.]`):**
  - **Accounts Payable (25 FTEs | 20,000 kSEK/yr):** Invoice capture, PO matching, coding, supplier queries, payment runs. High automation readiness for standard invoices; human judgement needed for exceptions and fraud/duplicate controls.
  - **Reconciliations (35 FTEs | 28,000 kSEK/yr):** Bank, premium, claims, and technical-account reconciliations. Rule-based matching is automatable; complex claims/reinsurance breaks require domain knowledge.
  - **Reporting (30 FTEs | 24,000 kSEK/yr):** Month/quarter close, IFRS 17, Solvency II QRTs, Finansinspektionen filings, management & board packs. AI can assist data aggregation and first-draft commentary; human sign-off, judgement, and audit trails remain mandatory.
- **Systems of record to verify before finalising:** General ledger / ERP, payroll aggregates (by unit and tenure), vendor/licence ledger, and audit/incident logs.

## 3. Why a Forced 30% Cut Next Year Fails the Numbers (and Swedish Law)

### A. Swedish Legal & Operational Timeline Constraints
*Note: This section outlines operational planning assumptions, not legal advice; employment counsel and HR must confirm specifics against our collective agreements.*
1. **Co-determination (MBL):** Major organisational changes and new workplace technologies require prior negotiation with our trade unions before a final decision is taken.
2. **Public Employment Service (Arbetsförmedlingen) Notice:** Dismissing **26 to 100 employees** (27 FTEs = 30% of 90) requires **at least 4 months' advance notice** to Arbetsförmedlingen (versus 2 months for 5–25 employees).
3. **Selection Order (LAS *Turordning*) & Notice Periods:** Redundancies follow length-of-service order per operational unit and collective-agreement area (with up to 3 exemptions since the 2022 reform). We cannot simply select who stays based on AI-readiness. Statutory/collective notice periods run **1 to 6 months** by tenure. Combined with MBL and Arbetsförmedlingen timelines, **no forced-exit payroll savings land before Q3 of Year 1**.
4. **9-Month Re-employment Priority (*Företrädesrätt*):** Employees dismissed for lack of work with >12 months' service in the prior 3 years hold priority for re-employment for 9 months—creating legal and operational friction if AI underperforms and we need to rehire.
5. **EU AI Act:** Emotion recognition in the workplace is prohibited (since Feb 2025). Specific transparency duties for high-risk workplace AI (Article 26(7)) take effect **2 December 2027** (following the 2026 Digital Omnibus), while national MBL co-determination rules apply immediately.

### B. External Base Rates on AI & Headcount Cuts
- **AI pilot conversion:** MIT NANDA's *State of AI in Business 2025* reported that ~95% of enterprise generative AI pilots showed no measurable P&L impact yet (directional signal; methodology debated). Gartner (June 2025) forecasts >40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end-2027 due to escalating costs, unclear value, or inadequate risk controls.
- **Regretted AI layoffs:** In an Orgvue survey (2025, >1,000 senior leaders), 39% made redundancies due to AI and **55% said they made the wrong decision**. Careerminds (Feb 2026, 600 HR leaders) found **two in three** companies that cut jobs for AI were already rehiring (52% within six months), and for **30.9%**, rehiring costs exceeded savings. (Both are vendor surveys; treat as strong directional warnings.)
- **Turnover contagion:** Trevor & Nyberg (*Academy of Management Journal*, 2008) found a 1% workforce downsizing was associated with a **31% increase in voluntary turnover** the following year among remaining staff. Gallup estimates replacement costs at **0.5x to 2.0x annual salary**.

## 5. Break-Even & Sensitivities

1. **Rehiring sensitivity (Option A):** If AI fails to sustain quality in Reconciliations or Reporting and **14 FTEs** (half the cut) must be rehired or covered by contractors in Year 2 instead of 7 FTEs, Year 2 net drops by **5,600 kSEK** to **+4,600 kSEK**—leaving Option A **-550 kSEK cumulative negative** over two years.
2. **Pilot-gate protection (Option B):** Because no role is eliminated until a pilot proves ≥95% straight-through accuracy and passes internal audit review, a failed pilot in Reporting caps the loss at that module's build cost (~1,000 kSEK) with zero severance or rehiring damage.
3. **Break-even:** Option B pays back its -1,350 kSEK Year 1 investment by **Year 2 Q1**, whereas Option A does not pay back its -5,150 kSEK Year 1 cash hole until **Year 2 Q3** under optimistic assumptions.

## 6. Governance, Controls & People Plan

- **Controls that stay non-negotiable:** Segregation of duties in AP payments, immutable audit trails for Solvency II / IFRS 17, and named human sign-off on all financial postings, reserve adjustments, and regulatory filings.
- **Union & employee engagement (MBL + TRR):** Engage union representatives at the problem stage—before vendor lock-in—to co-design task automation and reskilling pathways. Where roles do eventually become redundant in Year 2+, activate **TRR** (job security council) from day one of notice (historically >85% of displaced Swedish workers find new work within a year, per OECD) and pair with *omställningsstudiestöd*.
- **Quarterly Review Gates:** Track both financial and people/control indicators every quarter:
  - *Financial:* Net cash vs. budget, AI run-rate spend, contractor/overtime spend.
  - *Operational/Risk:* Close cycle time, reconciliation break backlog, AP error/duplicate rate, audit findings.
  - *People:* Regretted attrition, engagement, internal redeployment rate.

## 7. Decision Requested from the Board

1. **Approve Option B** with a **Year 1 net investment ceiling of 1,350 kSEK** (gross AI capex/opex of 4,000 kSEK) and a **Year 2 net saving target of 11,100 kSEK**, subject to quarterly pilot gates.
2. **Mandate a zero-based non-payroll spend review** (Option C's vendor/consultant levers) in Q1 to self-fund the Year 1 AI investment.
3. **Defer any involuntary redundancy target** until Q3–Q4 pilot data proves sustained capacity release beyond natural attrition and redeployment.
