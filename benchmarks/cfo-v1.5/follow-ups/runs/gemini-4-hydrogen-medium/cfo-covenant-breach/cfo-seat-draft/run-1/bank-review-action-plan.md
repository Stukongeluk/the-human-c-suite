# Q2 Covenant & Bank Review Action Plan

**Prepared for:** CFO & Owner (Family-Owned Furniture Manufacturer, Netherlands — 260 FTE)  
**Target:** Bank review in 3 weeks | Q2 Leverage Covenant: **3.5x** (current forecast: **3.6x**)  
**Status:** Draft for internal alignment — figures marked `[To verify from GL / Loan Agreement]` must be populated before the bank meeting.

---

## 1. Decision Summary: Hold the Layoff Announcement

**Do not announce a 10% headcount reduction before the bank review.**

1. **It worsens Q2 cash and leverage.** At 260 employees, a 10% cut (26 roles) triggers the Dutch Collective Redundancy Notification Act (**WMCO**, threshold: 20+ dismissals within 3 months in one UWV region) and mandatory Works Council advice (**WOR Art. 25**). Consultation, UWV/union notification, and statutory notice periods (1–4 months) mean payroll savings land in Q3/Q4 at the earliest—while legal fees and statutory transition payments (*transitievergoeding*) increase cash outflows and **Net Debt** in the short term.
2. **Announcing first creates legal and credit risk.** Publicly or internally announcing a 10% cut before consulting the Works Council (*Ondernemingsraad*) risks an Enterprise Chamber (*Ondernemingskamer*) challenge and union friction. A bank's credit committee reads an unconsulted restructuring as loss of governance, not discipline.
3. **3.6x vs. 3.5x is a ~3% gap.** Closing a 0.1x leverage gap requires moving Net Debt down or EBITDA up by roughly 3%—a working-capital, pricing, and spend problem, not a reason to permanently lose 26 skilled craftspeople and operators.

## 4. What to Put in the Bank Pack

Credit committees respond to **visibility, control, and skin in the game**:

1. **Root-Cause Bridge:** Why leverage moved to 3.6x (e.g., working-capital build, raw material timing, or softer order intake) and whether it is temporary or structural.
2. **13-Week Cash Forecast:** Weekly cash inflows/outflows under **Base** and **Downside** cases, showing minimum liquidity headroom.
3. **Q2 Action Plan:** Named owners, euro amounts, and dates for working capital, capex deferral, and spend reductions.
4. **Trigger Framework:** Explicit pre-agreed triggers (e.g., *"If order intake falls below €X for 6 weeks, we activate Y"*), so the bank sees discipline without premature layoffs.
5. **Owner Commitment:** Formal confirmation that dividends/drawings are paused and leadership bonuses are suspended until leverage returns comfortably below 3.5x.

## 5. Questions to Answer Before Finalising Numbers (Internal Check)

- **Loan Agreement:** How does the facility define *Net Debt* (e.g., cash netting, lease liabilities) and *EBITDA* (are restructuring charges excluded or included)?
- **Root Cause:** Is the 3.6x forecast driven by a **margin/volume drop** or by **cash tied up in inventory and receivables**?
- **CAO & Works Council:** Which collective labour agreement (*CAO Meubelindustrie* or similar) applies, and when does the *Ondernemingsraad* next meet?
- **Employment Counsel:** Verify any current statutory or *CAO* rules (including any updates to Dutch crisis/short-time work schemes) before taking any formal HR steps.
