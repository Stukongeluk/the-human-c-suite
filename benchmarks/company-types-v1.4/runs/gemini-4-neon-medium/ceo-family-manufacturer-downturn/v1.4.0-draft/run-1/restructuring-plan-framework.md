# Restructuring Plan Framework (Credit Line Renewal — February 2027)

**Prepared for:** Managing Director (Family-Owned Machine Builder, Stuttgart Region — 210 employees, incl. 14 apprentices)  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Status:** Working framework — figures marked `[ERP / BWA]` must be populated from current accounting, order book, and aggregated HR records before submission to the bank.  
**Legal notice:** This document is a management and financial framework, not legal or tax advice. Verify all employment, co-determination, and insolvency-law points with a German employment lawyer (*Fachanwalt für Arbeitsrecht*) and restructuring accountant (*Steuerberater* / *Wirtschaftsprüfer*) before acting.

---

## 1. Situation & Leadership Ownership

Two major automotive-supplier customers have reduced orders by 35% this year. Critically, one has confirmed that volumes will not return to prior levels as it transitions to electric drivetrains. The house bank requires a credible restructuring plan ahead of the February 2027 credit-line renewal.

Two realities must be held together:
1. **This is a leadership concentration risk, not a shop-floor failure.** Over-reliance on two combustion-era automotive customers built up over years. Leadership owns that exposure and takes the first financial cut (managing-director remuneration, bonuses, and family dividends) before asking employees to carry the adjustment.
2. **Part of the volume loss is structural, not cyclical.** Treating a permanent drivetrain shift as a temporary dip would burn through liquidity and put all 210 jobs at risk. At the same time, a blunt immediate cut of 45 people (~21.4% of headcount) is commercially and financially counterproductive before February: in Germany, consultation, notice periods, and severance drain cash first and deliver savings mostly in the second half of 2027, while stripping out the skilled trades needed to win non-automotive business.

## 4. Recommended Two-Track Restructuring Plan

### Track 1: Stabilize Cash & Earn the Bank's Confidence (Nov 2026 – Feb 2027)
1. **Leadership goes first:** Immediate reduction in managing-director salary/draw, suspension of management bonuses, and suspension of family dividends until liquidity and profitability targets are met. Put this on page 1 of the bank pack.
2. **Working capital & non-people spend:** Release cash from inventory and overdue receivables; cut discretionary spend, energy waste, scrap/rework, external subcontracting (bring work back in-house where your own machines sit idle), and defer non-critical CapEx.
3. **Eliminate buffers before core jobs:** Stop overtime, wind down agency/temp contracts with honest notice, and freeze all external hiring (except mission-critical replacements approved by the MD).
4. **Pricing & service cash:** Review underpriced legacy parts and accelerate high-margin maintenance, retrofit, and spare-parts offerings to existing installed-base customers.
5. **Targeted *Kurzarbeit* for the temporary dip only:** Apply for *Kurzarbeitergeld* for lines hit by cyclical volume drops (noting the 24-month maximum duration runs until **31 December 2026** and reverts to **12 months from 1 January 2027** unless extended again). Use idle hours for structured cross-training and machine/process improvement rather than waiting passively.

### Track 2: Close the Permanent EV Gap & Pivot (Q1 2027 – 2028)
1. **Quantify the true structural capacity gap:** Once Track 1 savings, commercial pivot targets (EV components, medical/industrial machinery, energy, defence/precision subcontracting), and 24-month natural attrition/retirements are modelled, determine the exact remaining payroll gap — if any.
2. **Redeploy & reskill first:** Map skills across the 196 non-apprentice staff and offer internal transfers and paid retraining into growth lines and service/retrofit roles.
3. **Voluntary measures before compulsory exits:** Model partial retirement (*Altersteilzeit*), early retirement bridges, voluntary hours reductions, and voluntary exit agreements (*Aufhebungsverträge*) — with legal oversight, because employer-initiated termination agreements count toward §17 KSchG collective-dismissal thresholds.
4. **If involuntary reductions remain unavoidable:** Execute **one** well-prepared reduction rather than repeated waves; cut the underlying work/lines alongside roles; follow fair, legally compliant *Sozialauswahl* (§1(3) KSchG); and fund day-one transition support (such as a *Transfergesellschaft*, coaching, and paid retraining) so displaced staff land in new jobs quickly.
5. **Protect the 14 apprentices (*Azubis*):** Retain all 14 apprentices (both legally protected post-probation under German vocational training law, BBiG, and vital to the company's 5-year skill base and regional standing).

## 6. Key German Legal & Co-Determination Guardrails (As of November 2026)

*Check every item with a Fachanwalt für Arbeitsrecht before taking action:*
- **Works council (*Betriebsrat*):** Mandatory only where elected. Where one exists, *Kurzarbeit* requires co-determination consent (§87(1) no. 3 BetrVG), and major operational changes in companies with >20 employees require negotiating an *Interessenausgleich* and *Sozialplan* (§§111–112 BetrVG).
- **Collective dismissal notification (§17 KSchG):** In an establishment of 60–499 employees, dismissing 10% or more than 25 employees within 30 days requires advance notification to the *Agentur für Arbeit*. Employer-initiated termination agreements count toward the threshold; procedural errors can invalidate dismissals.
- **Social selection (*Sozialauswahl*, §1(3) KSchG):** Operational dismissals require weighing tenure, age, maintenance obligations, and severe disability among comparable employees — performance rankings are not a legal selection criterion.
- **Notice periods (§622 BGB):** Statutory employer notice grows with tenure, up to 7 months to the end of a month after 20 years' service (or longer under applicable collective agreements).
- **Apprentices (BBiG):** After the probationary period, ordinary operational termination of training contracts is effectively barred except on closure of the training operation.

## 7. Monthly Governance Metrics (Financial & People)

Review together every month with leadership and employee representatives:
- **Cash & Liquidity:** 13-week rolling cash headroom, credit-line utilization, DSO (receivables days), inventory days.
- **Commercial Pivot:** Non-automotive / EV-compatible order intake, quote conversion rate, contribution margin by customer group, top-2 customer concentration %.
- **Operations:** Machine utilization by line, scrap/rework rate, on-time delivery, safety incidents.
- **People:** Voluntary regretted attrition, sickness absence rate, training/reskilling hours completed, apprentice progression, and quarterly short pulse check on trust and clarity.
