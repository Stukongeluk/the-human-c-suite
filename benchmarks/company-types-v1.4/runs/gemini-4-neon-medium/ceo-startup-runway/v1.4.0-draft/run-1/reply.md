### Why "30% this month" fails in Sweden (not legal advice—call Swedish employment counsel now)
- **Arbetsförmedlingen notice:** Dismissing 5–25 people requires at least **2 months' advance notice** to the Public Employment Service.
- **MBL & LAS:** You must consult unions under MBL before deciding, and LAS sets selection order by length of service per operational unit (at most **3 statutory exemptions** unless unions agree otherwise). You cannot simply pick who stays.
- **Notice & rehiring:** Statutory notice runs **1–6 months**, and dismissed staff with >12 months' service generally hold **9 months' re-employment priority**.
- **Cash reality:** Payroll savings arrive in **3–6 months**, while legal costs, disruption, and top performers quitting hit immediately.
