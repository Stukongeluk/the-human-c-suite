## 1. The Situation and Accountability

We currently have **9 months of cash runway** (through approximately August 2027), while our Series B timeline has moved to **summer 2027 at the earliest**. Reaching a Series B process with fewer than 6 months of cash is unacceptable—it weakens our negotiating position and puts the company at risk. As CEO, I own that we did not build a larger buffer earlier, and I am taking immediate action to extend runway to **15–18+ months** (into 2028) while protecting our **~4% monthly revenue growth** (~60% annualized).

Two investors have proposed a **30% headcount reduction (~14–15 roles) this month**. I share the urgency behind that proposal—we must act now on burn—but executing a 30% layoff this month is neither legally possible in Sweden nor the fastest way to save cash.

## 2. Why a 30% Cut This Month Does Not Solve the Near-Term Cash Problem

Under Swedish employment law (subject to confirmation with our employment counsel):

- **Advance authority notice:** Dismissing 5 to 25 employees requires notifying the Swedish Public Employment Service (*Arbetsförmedlingen*) at least **2 months in advance**.
- **Union consultation (MBL):** Major organisational changes and redundancies require formal consultation with relevant unions under the Co-determination Act (*Medbestämmandelagen*) before decisions are finalised.
- **Selection order (LAS):** Redundancies follow length-of-service order (*turordningsregler*) per operational unit unless otherwise agreed with unions, with at most **3 statutory exemptions**. We cannot simply select who stays.
- **Notice periods and re-employment rights:** Statutory notice runs **1 to 6 months** by tenure, and affected staff with over 12 months' service generally hold a **9-month priority right to re-employment**.

**Cash impact:** Because of consultation, statutory notice, and transition costs, cash savings from involuntary reductions typically do not begin arriving for **3 to 6 months**, while productivity loss, customer risk, and regretted departures of key staff hit immediately.

## 3. Proposed 14-Day Plan to Reach 15–18 Months of Runway

Rather than jumping straight to an irreversible cut before checking faster levers, I propose we execute **immediate, reversible cash savings over the next 14 days** and bring a fully costed 18-month cash model back to the board on **[Date + 14 days]**.

### Immediate Actions (Cash impact within 30 days)
1. **Executive pay first:** Starting next payroll, I will take a **[X]% salary cut** (and executive leadership will take **[Y]%**, with all executive bonuses and perks frozen) until Series B closes or we reach cash-flow breakeven.
2. **Non-payroll burn audit:** Freeze discretionary spend and cut cloud/infrastructure waste, idle software licences, travel, office extras, and non-essential external vendors/contractors (giving fair notice to any contractors affected). Target: **[SEK X / month]**.
3. **Immediate hiring freeze:** Pause all open requisitions and redeploy internal capacity from lower-priority work toward customer delivery and revenue.
4. **Stop non-core work:** Pause projects that do not directly support our 4%/month revenue trajectory or core Series B milestones over the next 12 months.
5. **Revenue, working capital, and non-dilutive capital:**
   - Pull forward cash via annual prepayments, tighter invoicing/receivables, and pricing review.
   - Accelerate applications for non-dilutive Swedish/EU climate-tech grants (e.g., Vinnova, Energimyndigheten, EIC/EU Innovation Fund) and evaluate whether an insider bridge makes sense alongside cost reductions.

### Contingency: If a Cash Gap Remains After Day 14
If the audited 18-month cash model shows that the steps above—combined with our revenue trajectory and any confirmed bridge or grant funding—still leave us short of **15–18 months of runway**:

- We will model **voluntary measures** (reduced hours, voluntary exits) first.
- If involuntary reductions are genuinely unavoidable to keep the company alive, we will plan **one** focused, legally compliant restructuring—cutting the associated work alongside the roles, consulting unions early under MBL, working with Swedish employment counsel on LAS, and providing day-one transition support (including TRR if applicable and awareness of *omställningsstudiestöd*).
