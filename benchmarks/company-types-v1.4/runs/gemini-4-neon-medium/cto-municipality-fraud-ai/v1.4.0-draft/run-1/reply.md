The trap here is treating a process and investigator-capacity problem as a prediction problem, and shifting the cost of false positives onto vulnerable residents.

### Why this proposal will fail you and the alderman

- **The math breaks down on rare events.** "90% accuracy" hides the base rate. On a 2,000-person caseload with 2% true fraud (40 cases), 90% sensitivity and 90% specificity flags 36 fraud cases and 196 honest citizens—**84.5% innocent**. That consumes investigator capacity rather than saving it.
- **Data linking and public law (not legal advice—bring in your FG and legal counsel).** Pulling voluntary debt-counselling (*schuldhulpverlening*) and housing data into fraud scoring clashes with GDPR purpose limitation (*doelbinding*) and deters people from seeking debt help early. The Hague District Court struck down SyRI in 2020 under Article 8 ECHR for opaque, disproportionate dataset linking, and the *toeslagenaffaire* showed the human and political fallout of risk profiling. Under the EU AI Act, welfare-benefit AI is high-risk (main obligations apply from December 2027), while GDPR DPIA, *Algoritmeregister* expectations and Works Council (*OR*) consultation apply today.
- **Six months is not enough** to do procurement, a DPIA, bias/proxy testing, OR consultation and human-oversight design lawfully.

### What to do next

1. **Speak with the alderman privately first**, before any council debate. Steelman their goal—protecting public money with limited investigator capacity—and show them you are protecting them from a legal and political crisis.
2. **Pull your own baseline data** over the next two weeks: separate administrative/reporting mistakes from deliberate fraud, and ask frontline investigators where their time actually goes.
3. **Propose a six-month alternative that delivers:** fix the top causes of honest reporting errors (clearer letters, simpler change-of-circumstances reporting), and give investigators transparent, legally grounded rule-based checks on benefits data only—co-designed with investigators, your FG, legal counsel and the OR.
