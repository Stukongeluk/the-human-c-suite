### Why the board's three measures will backfire

Your Supervisory Board is right about one thing: a 7% structural deficit on a 650-person organisation is serious and will drain reserves if left unaddressed. Their diagnosis is the problem — they jumped to three control-and-cut solutions before looking at where the money actually leaks:

1. **Replacing 15% of district nurses with lower-qualified carers** looks cheaper per hour, but district nurses (*wijkverpleegkundigen*) do the clinical triage, *indicatiestelling*, and coaching toward client self-reliance. KPMG's 2015 study of Buurtzorg for the Dutch Ministry of Health found that self-managing teams of higher-qualified nurses were a low-cost provider precisely because they used *fewer* hours per client. Hospital research also links diluted nurse staffing to higher burnout and clinical risk (Aiken et al., 2002 — hospital data, so a directional warning rather than a home-care forecast). Financially, notice periods (1–4 months), transition payments, and survivor turnover (Trevor & Nyberg, 2008: 1% downsizing associated with 31% higher voluntary turnover the next year) will eat Year 1 savings and push you into expensive ZZP/agency cover.
2. **A minute-by-minute visit-tracking app** hits a hard legal wall: under **Article 27(1)(l) WOR**, your Works Council has a **right of consent** (*instemmingsrecht*) over any system suited to monitoring employee attendance, behaviour, or performance. It also raises GDPR/AVG proportionality issues and destroys professional trust — driving up sickness absence and exits in a tight nursing market. *(If the app uses AI to allocate tasks or monitor staff, the EU AI Act's duty to inform workers and reps applies from 2 December 2027; Dutch WOR consent applies today.)*
3. **Centralising all planning** adds central planner payroll and software overhead while stripping out local route and client knowledge. It hurts continuity of care and schedule stability, and — like a major role restructuring — triggers your OR's **advisory right under Article 25 WOR** (with appeal to the Enterprise Chamber), plus Client Council consultation.

*(This is not legal advice — have HR and Dutch employment counsel verify WOR, WMCO — which applies from 20+ dismissals including employer-initiated settlement agreements in 3 months — CAO VVT, and GDPR/AVG before any formal step.)*
