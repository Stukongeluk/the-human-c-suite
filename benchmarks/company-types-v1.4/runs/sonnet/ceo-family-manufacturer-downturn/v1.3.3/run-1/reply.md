**Don't commit to 45 now, and don't promise the bank "no layoffs" either. Give the bank a plan with a staged path and a decision date, built to close the gap that is permanent rather than the one that will pass.** Your adviser's 45 is 21% of the company (about 23% of the 196 who aren't apprentices). It is one number, picked before anyone has shown what it is meant to fix.

**Your grandfather's record isn't the test.** What matters is that the firm survives and that whatever you do, you do once and do decently. A company that fails protects no one, apprentices included. But a cut sized by a rule of thumb usually costs more than it saves, because the skilled people you lose are the ones who are hard to rehire at the next upturn.

**The real problem is two problems, and they need different tools**
- **A cyclical dip:** the part of the 35% that returns when automotive investment recovers.
- **A structural loss:** the drivetrain volume that won't return. Short-time work does not fix this. Germany's IAB warned in 2026 that extending it in a structural crisis delays the changes a company needs.

Also, Kurzarbeitergeld's maximum duration is 24 months only until 31 December 2026. It falls back to 12 months from 1 January 2027 unless the government extends it (checked November 2026; confirm with the Arbeitsagentur). Your February bank date sits right on that change.

**What plan B contains, in this order**
1. **You go first.** Say in the plan and to the staff that the result is leadership's responsibility. Cut your own pay and bonuses, and the management team's, before anyone else's, and say by how much.
2. **Waste and revenue:** vendor spend, energy, inventory, idle space; spare parts, service and retrofit work, which keeps earning while new-machine orders are low; pricing.
3. **Focus:** stop low-value projects, so the workload really falls rather than being spread thinner.
4. **Hiring freeze and natural attrition.** In a firm your age, retirements may close a large part of the gap without a single dismissal.
5. **Overtime and time accounts first, then Kurzarbeit** for the cyclical part, with training during the hours freed. In Germany this needs the works council's agreement if you have one (§87 BetrVG).
6. **Voluntary exits and Altersteilzeit** for those who want them, with a proper package.
7. **The structural fix, the part that decides your future:** which sectors beyond automotive can use your machines and know-how (medical, packaging, batteries, energy)? Your skilled workforce is the asset that lets you move; dismissing them removes it.
8. **If a gap still remains** after steps 1 to 7, one targeted reduction, once, with a package that goes beyond the legal minimum and support from the day of notice. That is the point at which a number like 45 could still prove right, or turn out smaller. I'd rather you reach it with evidence than start from it.

**Apprentices:** keep all 14 through their training. They are your future skilled workers, they are cheap relative to their value, and apprentices generally have strong protection against dismissal; check with counsel. Cutting them would barely move the gap and would damage your recruiting for years.

**For the bank:** banks want evidence of cash and a credible path, not a headcount. Bring a 13-week cash forecast, a scenario view (what if the second customer's volume never returns?), covenant headroom, and dated milestones with a review in the first quarter. Ask them directly what they would need to see. A plan with a clear trigger ("if X hasn't happened by date Y, step 8 begins") is often stronger than a number you picked in a panic.

**Law and process (not legal advice, confirm with employment counsel before anything is announced)**
- If you have a Betriebsrat, involve it now, with the real numbers, while it can still change the plan. If you don't have one, ask whether staff want one; don't wait for the announcement.
- 45 dismissals within 30 days exceeds the notification threshold for your size (§17 KSchG, more than 25 in a firm of 60 to 499), and counts agreed exits that you initiate. A mistake can void the dismissals.
- A cut of this size is likely a Betriebsänderung, which means negotiating an Interessenausgleich and a Sozialplan (§§111-112 BetrVG).
- Dismissals need a Sozialauswahl (length of service, age, dependants, disability). Performance rankings are not a legal selection method, and it will limit who you can choose.
- Notice periods grow with service up to seven months, so the saving arrives later than the adviser's slide implies.
