**Don't let 45 people go now.** Use the ten weeks until February to build the plan the bank needs, separating the dip that will pass from the volume that is gone. Decide on job cuts only for the part that is truly gone: once, against a trigger the bank can see.

Two numbers behind that:

- **45 people is 21% of your workforce.** That only matches your drop if those two customers made up about 60% of your sales and none of the volume ever comes back. So far, only one has said it won't.
- **Dismissals announced now save nothing before February.** At 45 people you must file a mass-dismissal notification (§17 KSchG), and a works council can insist on a social plan (§112a BetrVG). With negotiation and notice periods of up to seven months (§622 BGB), most exits land around mid-2027. On rough estimates (€75,000 per person fully loaded, seven years' average service, severance of 0.75 months' pay per year), the cut saves about €0.5 million net in 2027 and €3.4 million a year from 2028. That is real money, so size it to the structural gap, not the whole drop.

Your adviser has a point: carrying capacity for volume that is gone for good burns cash the bank won't fund, and one clear decision beats small cuts every six months. I differ on the size and the order.

**Between now and February**

1. **Go to the bank now.** Ask exactly what they need: often a restructuring concept to the IDW S 6 standard, built around a credible picture of the company profitable again ([Ebner Stolz, January 2025](https://www.ebnerstolz.de/de/unser-angebot/leistungen/wirtschaftspruefung/krisenfrueherkennung/idw-s-6-anforderungen-sanierungskonzepte-82786.html)). A headcount number isn't that. Bring a 13-week cash forecast in December.
2. **Split the 35%.** Ask the other customer how much comes back, and when. Ask the one moving to electric drivetrains what it will buy for those new lines. Put spare engineering hours on service, retrofits and growing sectors like defence, aerospace and medical technology ([VDMA via produktion.de, October 2026](https://www.produktion.de/wirtschaft/maschinenbau-ki-und-verteidigung-geben-impulse/2751715)).
3. **Own it and go first.** Depending this heavily on two automotive customers is a leadership outcome, so say so to the bank and your staff. Stop your own draw and dividend and cut management pay first. Bring the works council in now with the real numbers, while they can still shape the plan.
4. **Free up cash that isn't people:** stock and work in progress, down payments on new orders, capex that can wait, outside spend and overtime. Then agency staff, with honest notice.
5. **Bridge the dip with hours, not exits.** Use working-time accounts, then short-time work (Kurzarbeit) for the share that will come back. It needs the works council's agreement and only covers a temporary loss ([Arbeitsagentur, January 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-8b-kurzarbeitergeld_ba015388.pdf)). The 24-month maximum applies only until 31 December 2026, so check the limit from January ([Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/bezug-von-kurzarbeitergeld-2326616)). Under the metal-industry collective agreement, if it binds you, a temporary 30-hour week with dismissals excluded is possible with IG Metall and employer-association approval, as is partial retirement from 57 ([IG Metall, October 2026](https://www.igmetall.de/tarif/tarifrunden/metall-und-elektro/tarifliche-regelungen-zur-beschaeftigungssicherung)).
6. **Freeze hiring, count the retirements due within 24 months and offer voluntary routes.** Your age profile may close much of the gap without a dismissal.
7. **Keep all 14 apprentices.** After probation they can only be dismissed for an important reason anyway (§22 BBiG), and they are your skilled workforce for the recovery the VDMA expects in 2027, with output up 3% ([September 2026](https://www.produktion.de/wirtschaft/maschinenproduktion-vdma-senkt-prognose-fuer-2026/2738100)).

| Option | Saves | Lands | Who carries it | Reversible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner and managers first | Little money, much trust | December | You, your leaders | Yes |
| Cash and spend | Often the most cash | 4–12 weeks | Suppliers, agency staff | Mostly |
| Hours: time accounts, Kurzarbeit | Cost of idle hours | January | Everyone a little | Yes |
| Freeze, retirements, voluntary exits | Depends on age profile | 6–24 months | Volunteers | Partly |
| New revenue: e-drive, service, new sectors | Slow; it is the future | 6–24 months | Sales, engineering | Yes |
| One cut, sized to the structural gap | ~€75k per role a year | Mid-2027 | Those who leave | No |

**For the bank:** steps 1 to 7 already running, plus a conditional decision: "If the lost electric-drivetrain volume is confirmed gone and new orders haven't reached X by [date], we cut capacity by up to N roles, once, by [date]." If that day comes, use a social plan and a transfer company that gives people up to 12 months of paid support and retraining (§111 SGB III). The legal social selection (Sozialauswahl) means you can't simply choose who goes; it usually hits your shortest-serving people. And expect more of the people you keep to quit afterwards (Trevor and Nyberg, 2008).

**Your grandfather** protected a company that keeps its people through bad years. Short-time work for the dip, an honest plan for what is gone and leaders paying first are the modern version of that. If some jobs truly can't be saved, cutting them once, as the last step and decently, is no betrayal. Losing the credit line would hurt all 210.

**A line for your staff:** "Our two biggest customers have cut orders by a third, and one says the volume won't come back. Depending so much on them is my responsibility, and I'm taking the first cut. Here are the numbers; I want your ideas before we decide anything."

This is not legal advice. Before you announce anything, see an employment lawyer or Südwestmetall. If the credit line keeps you liquid, also ask a restructuring lawyer about your duties as managing director.
