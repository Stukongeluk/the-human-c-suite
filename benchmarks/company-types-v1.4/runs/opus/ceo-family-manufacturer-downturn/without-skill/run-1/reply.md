**Short answer:** Don't sign off on 45 dismissals now. Don't promise that nobody will ever leave, either. Split the order loss into the part that is temporary and the part that is permanent. Bridge the temporary part with Kurzarbeit and the metal-industry collective-agreement tools. Size the permanent part from real numbers, and let the number of exits, if any, come out of that work. Go to the bank now with a plan that is visibly under way, and make sure the owners are seen to contribute first.

Your grandfather's principle and the bank's demand are less opposed than they look. What the bank needs is a credible plan that shows the company can survive. Layoffs are one possible measure in such a plan, not the requirement.

## 1. Why "45 now" is the wrong starting point, even though your adviser is right to be in a hurry

**The number has to be worked out, not assumed.** 45 people is 21% of your workforce. Since apprentices can't be dismissed for operational reasons once their probation ends, it is about 23% of your regular staff. Ask how that figure follows from the order loss.

- If those two customers were 40% of your revenue, a 35% cut means roughly 14% less total revenue.
- If they were 70%, it means roughly 25% less.

Those two cases lead to very different headcounts. Your adviser may also be fixing a cost problem that existed before this year. That may be legitimate, but it should be stated openly.

**In Germany, "now" doesn't mean now.**
- At 45 people you are above the mass-dismissal threshold. Firms with 60–499 staff must consult the works council and notify the Agentur für Arbeit when they dismiss 10% or more than 25 people within 30 days ([§17 KSchG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__17.html)).
- You are also above the threshold at which the works council can force a social plan through the conciliation board. For firms with 60–249 staff that threshold is 20% or at least 37 people, and separations by mutual agreement that you initiate count towards it ([§112a BetrVG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__112a.html)).
- The process involves negotiating a reconciliation of interests and a social plan (Interessenausgleich and Sozialplan), then social selection, then notice periods of up to seven months for long-serving staff.
- Realistically, the savings arrive in the second half of 2027, but the severance cash goes out early. As an illustration: assume 45 people with an average of 12 years' service and about €5,000 gross a month, and a social plan of 0.5 to 1.0 monthly salaries per year of service. That comes to roughly **€1.35–2.7 million**, paid out at exactly the moment the bank is watching your liquidity.

**Social selection may take the wrong people.** The law ranks people by length of service, age, dependants and severe disability. The people who leave are therefore often the younger skilled workers you will need when volumes recover. Also check whether you are bound by the Baden-Württemberg metal collective agreement. If you are, older employees with long service have special protection against ordinary dismissal (the "Alterssicherung"), which IG Metall [kept in 2021](https://www.stuttgarter-zeitung.de/wirtschaft/alterssicherung-erhalten-symboltraechtiger-konflikt-in-metallindustrie-entschaerft-79013761.html). That makes the selection pool even more skewed towards the young. Have a labour lawyer check exactly how this applies to you.

**Where your adviser is right:** if part of the loss is permanent, every month you wait costs money. If you delay until the cash runs out, the decision will be made by the bank or an insolvency administrator, and that is the worst outcome for all 210 people. So preparation starts this week. What you decide this week is the method for getting to a number, not the number itself.

## 2. Split the problem into temporary and permanent

- **The customer moving to electric drivetrains:** treat its lost volume as permanent unless it shows you otherwise. Before writing it off, ask it what production equipment it will need for e-drive parts. Its transition needs machines too. If you aren't the one building them, someone else will be.
- **The other customer:** ask for a written outlook of its volumes for 2027–2029, not just a remark in a meeting.
- **The market:** the VDMA expects a 2% fall in production for 2026 and [3% growth in 2027](https://www.infranken.de/deutschland/deutsche-maschinenbauer-erwarten-erst-2027-produktionsplus-art-6396512). In its Q3 survey, defence, aerospace and medical technology were providing positive momentum, while segments heavily dependent on automotive were [especially hard hit](https://www.produktion.de/wirtschaft/maschinenbau-ki-und-verteidigung-geben-impulse/2751715). Defence is the fastest-growing market. Whether your family wants to build for it is a values decision, so make it deliberately rather than by default.
- **Build a capacity bridge by department, in hours.** Start with the hours lost, split into temporary and permanent. Then subtract:
  - temp agency workers and subcontracted work you can bring back in-house,
  - expected retirements and other natural turnover over 24 months,
  - new business with a credible pipeline.

  What remains is your real structural gap. It may well be smaller than 45, but you only find out by doing the arithmetic.

## 3. The order of measures

1. **Owners and management first.** Suspend distributions, cut management pay, and decide what capital or shareholder loan the family can put in. The bank and the workforce will both look for this. A family owner who visibly contributes has negotiating credibility with the works council that an anonymous group headquarters never has.
2. **External flexibility.** End temp agency and contractor work, stop overtime, run down working-time accounts, and bring outsourced work back in-house.
3. **Hiring freeze, natural turnover and voluntary partial retirement.** In the metal sector, partial retirement under TV FlexÜ is available from age 57, with the employer topping pay up to roughly 85–89% of previous net pay ([IG Metall](https://www.igmetall.de/tarif/tarifrunden/metall-und-elektro/tarifliche-regelungen-zur-beschaeftigungssicherung)).
4. **Kurzarbeit, for the temporary part only.**
   - It is not free: you still pay social-security contributions on the lost hours, plus any top-up.
   - The 24-month special rule expires on 31 December 2026. From 2027 the limit goes back to 12 months unless a new regulation is passed ([TK](https://www.tk.de/firmenkunden/fachthemen/versicherung-fachthema/bis-ende-2026-doppelte-bezugsdauer-bei-kurzarbeitergeld-2189160)). Plan on 12 months.
   - Kurzarbeit is only allowed for a temporary loss of work. Courts also treat it as evidence that a shortage is temporary. So keep the "temporary" and "permanent" departments clearly separated in your plan.
5. **Tools from the metal collective agreement,** if you are bound by it or can join through Südwestmetall:
   - Collective reduction of working hours under the job-security agreement (TV Beschäftigungssicherung), for example from 35 to 30 hours. Dismissals for operational reasons are excluded during the reduction, and IG Metall and the employers' association must approve it.
   - Converting the annual collective supplement T-ZUG A into six days off for everyone when orders are short.
   - Using the transformation payment (T-Geld) to partly compensate for the lower pay.
   - Supplementary or restructuring agreements (Ergänzungs- or Sanierungstarifverträge): temporary concessions in exchange for job and site guarantees ([IG Metall](https://www.igmetall.de/tarif/tarifrunden/metall-und-elektro/tarifliche-regelungen-zur-beschaeftigungssicherung)).

   The 2026 bargaining round is still under way, so check the current terms.
6. **Lending staff to other employers in the region.** Since March 2025, the Baden-Württemberg agreement on regional staff deployment (Tarifvertrag zum regionalen Personaleinsatz) lets metal firms temporarily lend surplus staff to other participating firms in the state. It is voluntary, pay continues, and the works council must agree. There is also a trial-placement variant of up to six months for people who might move permanently ([details](https://www.humanresourcesmanager.de/arbeitsrecht/zeitarbeit-zum-erhalt-von-arbeitsplaetzen/)). Firms in growing sectors are the natural partners.
7. **Retraining towards new markets.**
   - *Qualifizierungsgeld:* at a company with fewer than 250 employees, it is available once 10% of the workforce needs retraining because of structural change. You need a works agreement, and the training must exceed 120 hours. The Agentur pays 60/67% of the lost net pay and you pay the course costs. Training that is mainly company-specific is excluded ([BA fact sheet](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-qualifizierungsgeld-arbeitgeber-arbeitnehmer_ba047767.pdf)).
   - *Employee training subsidy (§82 SGB III):* at your size it covers 50% of course costs (100% for staff aged 45 and over) plus a 50% wage subsidy ([BA](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/foerderkonditionen_beschaeftigtenqualifizierung_ba196097.pdf)).
8. **Only then, if a structural gap remains:**
   - Run a voluntary programme first, but cap it so that your best people don't all take the money.
   - Offer a transfer company. Transfer-Kurzarbeitergeld runs for up to 12 months, and employers usually top it up to around 80–85%. Because employment ends by agreement, it gives you legal certainty, and it gives people a bridge and help finding a new job ([CMS](https://cms.law/de/deu/legal-updates/transfergesellschaft-instrument-zur-restrukturierung)).
   - Agree a fair social plan.
   - Keep training all 14 apprentices and plan to take them on. They are the cheapest way to renew your workforce, and cutting them is the clearest signal that you've given up on the future.

## 4. What to give the bank

- **Go now, not in February.** Ask exactly what they expect: a full IDW S6 restructuring concept or something lighter. Then ask for a meeting in mid-January to review your draft. As one law firm puts it, ["banks finance viability, not hope"](https://cms.law/de/deu/legal-updates/transformation-finanzieren-wie-banken-stabilitaet-schaffen).
- **What goes into the plan:**
  - a 13-week liquidity forecast, starting immediately,
  - an integrated plan (P&L, balance sheet and cash) for 2027–2028, with a base case and a downside case,
  - an honest account of the cause, which is customer concentration,
  - a target picture: which markets, which products, which customers,
  - a list of measures with their € effect, timing and who is responsible,
  - a contribution from each side: owners, employees (through collective-agreement concessions) and the bank.
- **State guarantees** from the L-Bank and the Bürgschaftsbank can cover up to 50% of working-capital loans. Companies formally "in difficulty" under EU rules are excluded, and so are loans already granted and simple extensions ([Förderdatenbank](https://www.foerderdatenbank.de/FDB/Content/DE/Foerderprogramm/Land/Baden-Wuerttemberg/buergschaftsprogramm-allgemeine-buergschaft.html)). Ask your bank early whether a new or larger credit line could qualify.
- **Your personal duty.** As managing director you are legally required to monitor developments that threaten the company's survival and to take countermeasures ([§1 StaRUG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/starug/__1.html)). Get a restructuring adviser with IDW S6 experience to assess, early, whether the company can continue as a going concern. That protects you as well as the company.

## 5. About your grandfather

I would not promise "no layoffs, ever" unless you are certain you can keep that promise. A broken promise costs more trust than an honest hard message. What you can promise, and keep, is an order of measures:

- the owners contribute first,
- temporary tools for a temporary problem,
- voluntary measures before compulsory ones,
- dignity and real support for anyone who has to leave.

Breaking with a family tradition in order to save 165 jobs is not the same as breaking it to hit a cost target. A clean no-layoff record that ends in insolvency protects no one.

Tell people early. Your staff can see what is happening at Bosch, ZF, Mahle and Porsche ([overview](https://www.produktion.de/wirtschaft/trumpf-voith-bosch-amp-co-industrie-spart-massiv-jobs-ein/2581599)). Uncertainty makes your best people leave first. Tell them what you know, what you don't know yet, and the order in which you will act.
