**Between now and February**

1. **Split the 35% by customer and product line:** what comes back, when, and what won't. Ask the customer going electric what machines its new lines need; its shift may be a market for you too.
2. **Own it and go first.** The dependence on two automotive customers grew under the family's leadership; say that plainly to your people and the bank. Cut your own pay and management bonuses, stop distributions, and state what the family will contribute (fresh equity, or shareholder loans left in). Banks read that as the strongest signal.
3. **Free cash outside payroll:** inventory, receivables, down payments, investment you can defer, supplier spend, spare-parts pricing.
4. **Bring the works council in now, with the real numbers.** Short-time work needs its agreement (§87 BetrVG); cutting 45 jobs means a reconciliation of interests and social plan (§§111–112 BetrVG) plus advance notice to the employment agency (§17 KSchG). Told early, they may find savings you haven't.
5. **Bridge the temporary part with hours:** run down working-time accounts, then Kurzarbeit, which only covers a temporary loss of work. The 24-month rule ends on 31 December 2026 ([KugBeV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kugbev_4/BJNR1520A0025.html)); an extension was still open on 16 November ([Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/personal/entgelt/kurzarbeitergeld_78_702968.html)), so plan on up to 12 months. If you're bound by the metal collective agreement, add shorter hours under the job-security agreement (for example 35 to 30), T-ZUG days off and Altersteilzeit ([IG Metall, Oct 2026](https://www.igmetall.de/tarif/tarifrunden/metall-und-elektro/tarifliche-regelungen-zur-beschaeftigungssicherung)).
6. **Shrink the structural part without dismissals first:** a hiring freeze; ending agency and fixed-term contracts with proper notice (that's someone's income too); retirements (how many by 2028?) and voluntary packages. Retrain people for new markets with Qualifizierungsgeld: with under 250 employees, 10% of staff needing training because of structural change is enough, courses must run over 120 hours, and the agency pays 60–67% of lost net pay while you pay the course ([Arbeitsagentur](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-qualifizierungsgeld-arbeitgeber-arbeitnehmer_ba047767.pdf)). Machine builders see growth in defence, aerospace and medical technology ([produktion.de, Oct 2026](https://www.produktion.de/wirtschaft/maschinenbau-ki-und-verteidigung-geben-impulse/2751715)).
7. **Keep training your 14 apprentices.** They're your 2030 workforce, and after probation the law largely protects them anyway (§22 BBiG).
8. **Write a plan the bank can sign.** Expect them to want an IDW S 6-style restructuring concept: causes, a picture of the company afterwards, measures, and integrated profit, balance-sheet and cash planning ([Ebner Stolz](https://www.ebnerstolz.de/de/unser-angebot/leistungen/wirtschaftspruefung/krisenfrueherkennung/idw-s-6-anforderungen-sanierungskonzepte-82786.html)). Ask about an L-Bank liquidity loan with a Bürgschaftsbank guarantee of up to 80%; it isn't open to firms "in difficulty" under EU rules ([Förderdatenbank](https://www.foerderdatenbank.de/FDB/Content/DE/Foerderprogramm/Land/Baden-Wuerttemberg/liquiditaetskredit.html)).
