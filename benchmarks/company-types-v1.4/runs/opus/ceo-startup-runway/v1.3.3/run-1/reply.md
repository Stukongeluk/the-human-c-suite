**Act this month, but don't make a 30% cut your first move.** Start with fast, reversible steps, leadership first. Ask the two investors to fund a bridge against a written plan, and agree now on the trigger for one smaller, well-run reduction if the plan misses. On illustrative numbers, a 30% cut only moves your runway from about 10 to about 13 months. That doesn't get you safely to a Series B that could slip again.

The investors are right that nine months is an emergency, and that one early cut beats two late ones. But "cut 30%" is a solution. The problem is that you need roughly 18 months of runway to close the B with room to spare, without breaking the 4% monthly growth the B depends on.

**Why the cut buys less than it looks**

In Sweden, a cut decided this month saves almost nothing before March:
- MBL negotiations must finish before anyone gets notice. That means negotiating with your union if you have a collective agreement, otherwise with the union of each affected member ([verksamt.se](https://verksamt.se/en/employees-recruitment/termination-employment-notice-period/shortage)).
- For 14 people, Arbetsförmedlingen needs notice at least two months before the first person leaves ([Arbetsförmedlingen](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/for-arbetsgivare/anmal-varsel-och-permittering)).
- Notice periods run one to six months by length of service. Selection follows last in, first out, with three exemptions.
- Severance is paid out before the savings start. Survivors leave too: each 1% of downsizing came with 31% more voluntary turnover the next year (Trevor & Nyberg, 2008).

**Options** (illustrative, not your numbers)

| Option | Runway | Forced exits | Reversible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | ~10 months | 0 now, all at risk later | n/a |
| Cut 30% (14 people) now | ~13 months (~12 if growth slows to 2%) | 14 | No |
| Spend cuts, leaders' pay, hiring freeze, voluntary hours and exits | ~14 months | 0 | Mostly |
| The same plus an insider bridge of 3–4 months' burn | 22+ months | 0 | Yes |
| The same plus one cut of 5–7 roles in month 6 if triggers miss | ~16–17 months | 5–7 | No |

*Assumptions: payroll is 70% of costs; gross profit covers 25% of costs and grows 4% a month; nine months is cash divided by today's burn. The package: spend down 20%, leadership pay down 25%, 4 leavers not replaced, 6 people on 80% hours, 3 voluntary exits.*

The package only matches the cut if spend really falls about 20% and some people volunteer. Neither reaches 18 months alone; financing closes the gap. Waiting has a price too: delaying a 14-person cut by two months costs about a month of runway.

**Next three weeks**

1. **This week:** build a 13-week cash forecast and an 18-month model with three cases: the B closes next summer, at year-end, or never. That model, not the 30%, should size any cut.
2. **Leaders first:** you and the leadership team take the first and largest pay cut, and drop bonuses. At about 3% of costs, this is a signal, not the savings plan. Tell the team and the board plainly that the runway gap is leadership's result, not theirs.
3. **Reversible moves now:**
   - Freeze hiring except revenue-critical roles.
   - Cut cloud, licences, vendors and office.
   - Have your CTO and product lead stop the work that doesn't move the B metrics.
   - Push annual prepayments and collections.
   - Check you claim the R&D deduction: for staff working at least half-time on R&D, 20% of their pay comes off employer contributions, up to SEK 3m a month ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/forskningsavdrag.4.8dcbbe4142d38302d7cb4.htm)).
4. **See the two investors privately, before the board.** *"I agree we must act this month. A 30% cut decided today buys about three months and saves almost nothing before March. Here's a plan that buys as much, protects our growth, and says exactly when and how much we'd cut if it misses. If you want us to reach the B, help bridge it."* Bridges are normal: 32% of Series A-stage rounds on Carta in 2026 so far were bridges ([Carta, Q3 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q3-2026-full-report/)).
5. **Bring people in now:** share the real runway with your union or employee representatives and the team while they can still shape the plan. Offer voluntary 80% hours and exits, and decline volunteers you can't lose. Public short-time work support covers unforeseeable outside events, so a funding delay is unlikely to qualify ([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/stodvidkorttidsarbete)).
6. **Set the trigger:** for example, no committed bridge by mid-January, or a model showing under 15 months. Then make one reduction sized to the remaining gap, and stop the work along with the roles. Finish MBL negotiations first, and give people a decent package plus TRR or similar support from the day of notice.

Confirm the process with employment counsel; this isn't legal advice.
