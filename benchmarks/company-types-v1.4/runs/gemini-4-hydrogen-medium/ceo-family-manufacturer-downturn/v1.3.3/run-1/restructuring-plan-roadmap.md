# Restructuring & Liquidity Roadmap (November 2026 – February 2027)

**Prepared for:** Managing Director, Family-Owned Machine Builder (Stuttgart region)  
**Workforce:** 210 employees (196 regular staff, 14 apprentices)  
**Trigger:** 35% order reduction from two largest automotive supplier customers (one temporary/cyclical, one structural shift to electric drivetrains); credit line renewal due February 2027.  
**Note:** Legal references below reflect German law as of November 2026 and are not legal advice. Confirm all labour-law and works-council steps with employment counsel (*Fachanwalt für Arbeitsrecht*) before acting.

---

## 1. Why "Cut 45 People Now" Fails Both Your People and the Bank

Your adviser's proposal to cut 45 people (~21.4% of total headcount, or ~23.0% of your 196 non-apprentice staff) treats a revenue and structural-transition problem as a simple headcount math exercise. In Germany, it also backfires on cash before February:

- **Collective dismissal rules (§17 KSchG):** In an establishment of 60–499 employees, dismissing more than 10% or more than 25 employees within 30 days triggers mandatory advance notification to the *Agentur für Arbeit* (*Massenentlassungsanzeige*). Errors can void the dismissals.
- **Works council co-determination (§§111–112 BetrVG):** If you have a works council (*Betriebsrat*), reducing 45 roles is a major operational change (*Betriebsänderung*) requiring a reconciliation of interests (*Interessenausgleich*) and a social plan (*Sozialplan*)—which takes months to negotiate and creates immediate severance liabilities.
- **Social selection & notice periods (§1(3) KSchG, §622 BGB):** You cannot simply pick who leaves. *Sozialauswahl* weighs tenure, age, maintenance obligations, and severe disability. Long-tenured *Facharbeiter* carry statutory notice periods of up to 7 months to month-end (after 20 years' service).
- **Cash impact before February:** Severance, social-plan reserves, and notice-period payroll mean firing 45 people in Q4 2026 increases cash outflow in Q1 2027 rather than saving cash by February.

## 2. Separate the Two Problems

| Problem | Nature | Right Tool | Wrong Tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer A (volume dip, likely to return)** | Cyclical / temporary | *Kurzarbeitergeld* (bridges hours and keeps skilled machinists ready for recovery) | Permanent layoffs (you will pay to rehire and retrain in 12–18 months) |
| **Customer B (shift to electric drivetrains)** | Structural decline (volume won't return) | Commercial pivot, redeployment, reskilling, voluntary exits / *Altersteilzeit*, and—only if a gap remains—targeted reduction | *Kurzarbeit* alone (Germany's IAB warned in 2026 that using *Kurzarbeit* for structural decline only delays needed change) |

## 3. Options Side by Side (Ordered from Least to Most Human Harm)

| Option | Cash & Timing Impact (by Feb 2027) | Who Is Affected | Hidden Costs & Risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Working capital, vendor & capex freeze** | Immediate cash release (weeks 1–6): WIP/inventory reduction, faster receivables, defer non-essential capex, renegotiate supplier terms | Vendors, internal budget owners | Pushing suppliers too hard can risk critical component supply | High |
| **2. Commercial & pricing action** | Margin & cash lift in 1–6 months: repricing custom/low-volume legacy parts, aftermarket/service & retrofit push, pivoting sales to non-ICE sectors | Sales, engineering, customers | Takes 3–9 months to replace large OEM volume | High |
| **3. Leadership & owner sacrifice first** | Modest direct cash, high credibility with bank and workforce (effective immediately) | Managing director, family dividends/drawings, senior leadership bonuses/perks | None—essential trust signal before asking staff to share the load | High |
| **4. Hiring freeze, temp/agency review & natural attrition** | Stops cost growth immediately; saves as roles vacate or temp contracts end | Open requisitions, agency/temp workers (give honest notice) | Slow for a 35% drop on its own; watch bottlenecks on remaining orders | High |
| **5. *Kurzarbeit* + paid reskilling (for cyclical gap)** | Immediate payroll relief proportional to reduced hours (subject to *Betriebsrat* agreement under §87(1) no. 3 BetrVG or individual consent; note max duration is 24 months until 31 Dec 2026, reverting to 12 months from 1 Jan 2027 unless extended) | Affected production and support teams; **keep all 14 apprentices** | Doesn't fix permanent ICE volume loss; employees lose part of net income | High |
| **6. Voluntary exits, *Altersteilzeit* & early retirement** | Cash cost up front (incentive), structural payroll reduction in 3–9 months | Employees who choose to leave or are near retirement | Risk of losing your best *Facharbeiter* if not scoped carefully | Low |
| **7. Targeted involuntary reduction (last resort)** | Net cash outflow in Q1–Q2 2027 (notice + severance); savings arrive H2 2027+ | Affected roles after *Sozialauswahl* | Lost institutional knowledge, survivor attrition, works-council friction, brand damage in Stuttgart | Irreversible |

## 4. 10-Week Action Plan to February 2027

### Weeks 1–2 (Late November – Early December 2026): Get the Real Numbers
- **Build a 13-week rolling cash-flow forecast** and a 2027 P&L under three demand scenarios (base, downside, severe).
- **Quantify the exact Euro gap**—not a headcount guess—by separating Customer A's temporary dip from Customer B's permanent loss.
- **Freeze non-essential capex and hiring**, and launch a working-capital sprint (overdue receivables, excess raw material/WIP).
- **Cut leadership pay/bonuses/owner drawings first** and put that in writing for both the bank and your team.
- **Check your legal baseline with employment counsel:** confirm whether you have a *Betriebsrat*, check applicable *Tarifvertrag* / *Kurzarbeit* rules, and verify notice-period profile across your 196 regular staff.

### Weeks 3–5 (December 2026): Engage People and Customers Early
- **Bring your *Betriebsrat* (or employee representatives/all staff if none) into the room with the real numbers** before locking any plan. Frame the problem honestly: *two customers cut 35%, one permanently; we want to protect as many jobs and all 14 apprenticeships as possible—help us shape how.*
- **Apply for *Kurzarbeit* where demand is cyclical**, using the freed hours for structured cross-training (EV/hybrid tooling, CNC automation, service/retrofit).
- **Audit your machine park and engineering skills** for adjacent demand in Baden-Württemberg: EV/battery/powertrain components, energy/grid equipment, medical, defence/aerospace subcontracting, and paid maintenance/retrofits for your installed base.
- **Protect the 14 apprentices:** they are heavily protected under German law (§22 BBiG) and are your skilled workforce for 2028–2030.

### Weeks 6–8 (January 2027): Finalise the Bank Package & Any Voluntary Measures
- **If structural overcapacity remains** after commercial pipeline, *Kurzarbeit*, and natural attrition, open **voluntary options** first (reduced hours, *Altersteilzeit*, targeted voluntary agreements).
- **Only if voluntary measures cannot close the 2027 structural gap:** prepare one clean, decent reduction—never "salami slicing" in multiple rounds—with outplacement/coaching funded from day one of notice.
- **Assemble the February Bank Pack** (see Section 5).

### Weeks 9–10 (February 2027): Bank Renewal & Execution Rhythm
- Present the plan to your *Hausbank* with weekly cash tracking already running.
- Set a monthly review cadence with leadership and employee representatives tracking both **cash/margin** (liquidity headroom, order intake outside ICE, WIP days) and **people** (sickness absence, voluntary turnover, training hours completed).

## 5. What Your Bank Actually Needs to See in February

A German *Mittelstand* credit officer wants proof of **liquidity control and a credible path to break-even**, not a rushed layoff announcement:

1. **Owner & leadership commitment:** Written reduction in owner drawings/executive pay before asking staff or the bank for concessions.
2. **13-week cash-flow & 2027–2028 liquidity bridge:** Showing you can service debt and stay inside the credit line without relying on fantasy severance savings.
3. **Working-capital & cost measures already underway:** Euro amounts already released from inventory, receivables, vendor renegotiations, and frozen capex.
4. **Two-track capacity plan:** *Kurzarbeit* for the temporary dip + concrete, costed plan for the permanent EV shift (sales pipeline outside ICE, reskilling, and—only if numbers require it—a properly budgeted, legally compliant structural adjustment).
5. **Governance & milestones:** Monthly KPIs you will report to the bank so there are no surprises.
