**Prepared:** November 2026  
**Company:** [Company Name], Stockholm (48 FTEs)  
**Purpose:** Board and leadership decision brief on extending cash runway from August 2027 into Q4 2027 / Q1 2028 without breaking our 4% monthly revenue growth.

## 1. The Situation & The Real Gap

- **Current runway:** 9 months (cash out: ~August 2027).
- **Series B timeline:** Slipped to Summer 2027 at the earliest. Allowing 3–5 months from term sheet to cash in bank, Series B proceeds would arrive in **Q4 2027 at the earliest**.
- **Revenue trajectory:** Growing ~4% month-over-month (~60% annualised).
- **The gap:** We do not have a "headcount" problem in isolation—we have a **3–6 month timing gap** between August 2027 and when Series B cash can realistically close, plus the need to hit Series B metrics along the way.

### Why an immediate 30% cut (~14–15 people) this month does not solve the Q4/Q1 cash problem

Under Swedish employment law (check specifics with employment counsel before acting):
1. **Mandatory advance notice to Arbetsförmedlingen:** Dismissing 5–25 employees requires notifying the Public Employment Service at least **2 months** in advance.
2. **Union consultation (MBL):** Major organisational changes require prior negotiation under the Co-determination Act (*MBL*).
3. **Selection rules (LAS):** Redundancies follow length-of-service order (*turordning*) per unit/agreement area unless otherwise agreed with the union, with up to **3 exemptions** per employer.
4. **Paid notice periods:** Statutory and collective-agreement notice periods run **1–6 months** depending on tenure.

**Financial implication:** Initiating a 30% cut in late November 2026 yields **minimal net cash savings before Q2 2027**, while immediately cutting ~30% of product, engineering, and commercial capacity—jeopardising the 4% monthly revenue growth that our Series B valuation depends on.

## 3. Recommended Plan: Two-Stage Approach

### Stage 1 (Immediate — Next 30 Days): Execute Option A
Leadership takes responsibility for the burn first and executes all reversible, non-destructive levers before touching team headcount:

1. **Leadership goes first:** Founders and executive team take a **[20–30]% salary reduction** (or deferment) effective December 1, 2026, and pause all executive bonuses/perks.
2. **Hard hiring freeze:** Pause all open requisitions and backfills immediately.
3. **Non-payroll spend audit:** Cut or renegotiate cloud/compute waste, unused SaaS licences, external contractors/agencies, travel, and office/facility extras. Target: **[SEK X]/month**.
4. **Commercial cash pull-forward:**
   - Offer **[5–10]% discount for annual upfront payment** on renewals and new contracts.
   - Tighten receivables (14–30 day terms) and milestone billing.
   - Review pricing on underpriced tiers/pilots.
5. **Scope focus:** Pause [1–2] non-core R&D or pilot tracks that do not contribute to 2027 ARR or Series B milestones.

### Stage 2 (Decision Gate — 31 January 2027)
Review actual cash burn, Q4/January collections, and Series B investor feedback against a single threshold:
- **If runway reaches ≥ Q4 2027 (12+ months from Jan 2027):** Stay the course, protect the 4% MoM growth engine, and prepare the Series B data room for spring/summer.
- **If runway still falls short of Q4 2027:**
  - First ask existing investors for a **[3–6] month milestone-based bridge**.
  - If no bridge is available and cuts are unavoidable, execute **one** lawful, well-planned reduction (Option C) sized strictly to reach Q1 2028—never multiple rounds. Pair it with scoped-down product commitments, full MBL/LAS compliance, and day-one transition support via *TRR (Trygghetsrådet)* or equivalent outplacement.
