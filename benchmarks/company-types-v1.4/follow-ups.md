The released version 1.4.0 is step 4 plus three small changes that were not rerun: the CPTO's first step now says to rank the customers' top problems by evidence, and two wording fixes in the company-types reference (the Gap result came on top of two weeks' notice; the nurse-staffing study is about patients per nurse, not skill mix).

**Read with care.**
- These are one or two runs per setup.
- Web search was unavailable to the step 3 and step 4 runs (the session's search budget was used up), so both sides of each comparison ran without it, and the scores aren't comparable with the main round, where Opus searched heavily.
- Different graders read the check "ranks the customer problems" differently: the step 2 grader passed both replies, the step 3 and 4 graders failed every reply on it. That check, and other compound checks the graders flagged, are the first candidates to split in the next round.
