**Don't adopt the three measures as proposed. Thank the board for taking the gap seriously, then bring your own plan that starts from where the 7% comes from.** If your annual costs are €35–45 million, the gap is roughly €2.5–3 million (my estimate; your controller has the real figure).

**The three proposals**

- **Fewer district nurses:** if you have about 100, 15% is 15 people. At roughly €15–20k a year less per FTE (estimate), that saves about €0.2 million, under a tenth of the gap, and only after works council advice and notice periods. Your district nurses do the needs assessments insurers expect from level-5 nurses, and in [KPMG's 2015 study](https://www.commonwealthfund.org/publications/case-study/2015/may/home-care-self-governing-nursing-teams-netherlands-buurtzorg-model) Buurtzorg's nurse-led teams used fewer hours per client, not more. Scarce nurses you push out are hard to win back.
- **Minute app:** billing needs registered care time. Timing every visit and reading it per nurse is something else: it rushes visits and pushes people out. A system that can monitor staff needs your works council's consent (art. 27(1)(l) WOR). If care goes unbilled, fix that at team level.
- **Central planning:** it may cut travel time, but it costs continuity (more strange faces at a client's door) and the autonomy that keeps nurses. Pilot central planning support in two or three teams, with the teams keeping their schedules, and measure travel time and continuity.

**What to do instead**

1. **Talk to the board chair privately first,** before the next meeting. You run the organisation; the board supervises and approves.
2. **Split the 7%** into price (tariffs below cost price), volume (revenue caps, care delivered but not billed) and your own costs (absence, agency cover, turnover, overhead). Ask the teams too; they know where time leaks.
3. **Negotiate with your full cost price.** Municipalities must pay a fair price that reflects collective-agreement wages (Wmo 2015, art. 2.6.6); work with ActiZ or Zorgthuisnl on the insurers.
4. **Leadership goes first:** management layers and overhead, starting with your own pay, before any frontline role.
5. **Bring in the works council and client council now,** with the real numbers, while they can still change the plan.

**A line for the chair:** "This gap is ours as leadership, and I'll close it. Give me six weeks to show where the 7% comes from and a plan that closes it without weakening the care we're paid to deliver."

**What would change my answer:** is the gap one year or structural, and how big are your reserves? If it's one year and reserves are sound, bridge it while the plan lands.
