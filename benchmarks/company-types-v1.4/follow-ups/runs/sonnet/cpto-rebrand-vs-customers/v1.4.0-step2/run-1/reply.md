Plan Q1 as two tracks: a deliberately small rebrand (new name, legal entities, domain and redirects), and protected capacity for the three complaints that took NPS from +28 to +4. Bring it to the CEO as help making the decision well, not as a protest.

**Why this is the stronger case**
- A new name doesn't fix slow invoicing or a broken integration. One name after a merger is a fair goal, but if the whole quarter goes to it, a 24-point NPS fall will likely reach renewals before it reaches a slide.
- "Any decision beats no decision" holds for cheap-to-undo choices, so decide those fast. The design system and marketing site can be phased. The domain move is harder to reverse (email, SEO, integrations), so do it carefully, not first.

**Q1 shape**
1. **Rebrand must-haves:** name in the app, legal entities, domain with redirects and email continuity. Design system starts with tokens and the busiest screens. Marketing site: a light refresh now, the full version in Q2.
2. **Customer fixes:** invoicing speed, the accounting integration, the top missing reports. Each gets a named team, a measure (latency, error rate, revenue at risk) and a date.
3. **Slack:** room for incidents, plus any debt that blocks the fixes.

**Before proposing a split**
- Rank the complaints with evidence: NPS comments, churn reasons, support volume, ARR on accounts that raised each.
- Ask each team for its own estimate of rebrand and fix work. Where they don't fit, cut scope or move items to Q2. No overtime.
- Check which teams carry both; they'll be the bottleneck.

**The CEO conversation**
Have it privately, before any wider meeting. Open with their goal: "I want the rebrand to land in Q1 without losing customers while it does." Then bring the NPS fall and the revenue at risk, and ask what would change their mind. A line you can use: "I'm not asking for a different decision, just for the rebrand's must-have part to be sized so the three complaints still get a team." Offer a two-week window for team estimates, so leadership still decides, with real numbers.

**If the answer is still "everything on the rebrand"**
Get the trade-off written down: what is deferred, which customers are exposed, and a mid-quarter date to review NPS, churn and open tickets. Then run it and report the numbers honestly.
