Plan Q1 in two tracks: a small, must-have rebrand slice, and the three customer problems (slow invoicing, the broken accounting integration, missing reports) as the first priority. Don't plan it as "everyone on the rebrand". NPS going from +28 to +4 while those complaints sit open is lost revenue that hasn't reached the slide yet. A new name won't fix an unhappy customer.

I haven't seen your churn, support or capacity data, so the split below is a starting hypothesis.

**What I'd bring to the CEO**

1. **Split the rebrand into must-have and phase-later.** After a merger, one name is a legitimate goal. The must-have part is the name in the app, legal entities, domain and redirects. The new design system and full marketing site can follow in phases. Ask each team to estimate its own part; don't assign a number from above.
2. **Name the top three customer problems with evidence.** For each: NPS comments, churn reasons, tickets and revenue at risk. Give each an owner, a measure and a date. For example: invoice time to p95 under X seconds, integration error rate, the top missing reports shipped.
3. **Build the plan from real capacity.** For each team, take headcount minus leave, incidents, on-call, debt and meetings. Then see what is left. If the rebrand and the fixes don't both fit, cut scope or sequence. Overtime is not a plan.
4. **Keep reliability and debt visible**, and don't plan any team at 100%.

**Handling "leadership decides" and "any decision beats no decision"**

The second line is right for choices that are cheap to undo. A phased rebrand is one of those. Whether the whole org spends the whole quarter on it is expensive to undo. So don't oppose the decision; ask for the reasons to be written down:

- What problem does the all-hands rebrand solve, and by when?
- What happens to the customer complaints in the meantime?
- What would change the plan?

**First steps**

- This week, talk to the CEO privately, before any wider forum. Don't open with "no". Open with: "I want the rebrand to land. Can I bring you a two-track option with the numbers by Friday?"
- Pull the customer evidence and a one-page capacity estimate from the teams.
- Bring two or three options: (A) rebrand-only, (B) must-have rebrand plus customer fixes, (C) fixes first, rebrand in Q2. Show cost, customer risk and reversibility.
- Agree indicators: NPS and churn, delivery health, team health. Review monthly.

A line you can use: "Customers told us what they need. I can deliver the new name and fix invoicing, the integration and reports, if we agree what the rebrand must include in Q1."
