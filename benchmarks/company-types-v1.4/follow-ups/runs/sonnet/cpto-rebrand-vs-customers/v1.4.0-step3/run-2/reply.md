**What I'd propose for Q1**

1. **Three customer outcomes, each with an owner, a measure and a date.** Invoice time to a target the team sets, the accounting integration working again, and the first missing reports shipped. Attach revenue at risk to each, from churn reasons, support tickets and renewal dates.
2. **Rebrand, minimum slice:** the name in the app, legal and contract changes, and domain redirects. A short, fixed list.
3. **Phased after that:** the full design system, adopted screen by screen as teams touch them anyway, and the marketing site, which can run on a lean version first.
4. **Protected time for reliability and debt.** Don't plan any team at 100%.

**Two things to check this week**
- The domain change can break the accounting integration, through callback URLs, authentication and customers' allow-lists. So the rebrand and the integration fix are tied together, and the integration team needs a seat in the domain plan.
