Plan for about €0.6M net this year, not the €1.3M annual figure the board will quote, and only if notices go out by the end of January. This is not legal advice; involve a German employment lawyer first.

**Process (no works council, so no co-determination duty)**
1. Decide which work stops, then which roles.
2. Sozialauswahl: among comparable roles you select by tenure, age, dependants and disability, not by "weakest". The Kündigungsschutzgesetz applies at your size, so each dismissal needs a documented operational reason.
3. Check protected people: pregnancy, parental leave, severe disability (Integrationsamt approval), data protection officer.
4. 15 of 140 exceeds the 10% threshold in §17 KSchG if within 30 days, so notify the Agentur für Arbeit first. Confirm with counsel.
5. Statutory notice (§622 BGB) runs one to seven months by tenure, longer if contracts say so.
6. Offer voluntary exit agreements first. Severance isn't mandatory, but half a month's salary per year of service is the norm, and employees have three weeks to sue.
