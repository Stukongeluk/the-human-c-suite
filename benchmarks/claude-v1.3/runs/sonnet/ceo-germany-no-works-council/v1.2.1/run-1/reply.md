**Short answer:** 15 roles by March is feasible only with short notice periods, and it saves roughly €0.5-0.8M net in 2027, not the €1.4M annual run-rate. Try cheaper levers first.

**Process (Germany, no works council; check with employment counsel, not legal advice)**
- **Unfair-dismissal law applies** (over 10 employees). You must show a genuine operational need, no suitable vacancy, and a documented social selection (tenure, age, dependants, disability). You can't simply pick "low performers".
- **Mass-dismissal notice:** 15 of 140 is above 10%, so you likely must notify the employment agency (§17 KSchG) before notices go out, and dismissals can't take effect for a month afterwards.
- **Notice periods** run from one month to seven with tenure, or longer by contract. Notices by late February only end employment in March for the shortest.
- No works council means no legal duty to negotiate a social plan, but severance (customary: about 0.5 monthly salary per year of service) in exchange for a signed settlement is the norm. Don't obstruct employees who want to elect a council.

**Savings (my estimates: €75k average salary, €95k loaded, calendar 2027)**

| 30 June (realistic) | €0.71M | €0.23M | ~€0.48M |

This excludes legal costs, unused leave, and lost productivity or extra attrition among those who stay.

**Show the board the earlier options first:** vendor, cloud and licence spend, pricing, stopping low-value projects, a hiring freeze, voluntary exits on the same package, and a visible executive pay cut. Kurzarbeit fits only a temporary dip. If cuts remain necessary, do one well-planned round.
