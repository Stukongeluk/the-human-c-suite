# Staff announcement (draft)

Send only on notification day, after every affected colleague has had their individual conversation. Before then, the works council has been informed and consulted. Fill in the brackets; do not send with any left in.

Subject: Changes to our organisation: what is happening and what happens next

Dear all,

Today I have to tell you something hard. [Company] is reducing the organisation by 22 roles out of 180, mainly in Operations and Support. Colleagues in those teams heard this personally this morning from their own manager. If you did not receive a conversation invitation, your role is not affected.

Why. [Plain statement with real numbers: the gap, the date, what it puts at risk.] The board looked at other ways to close the gap, including [what was considered]. They were not enough or not fast enough, and the board decided that a reduction in roles is necessary. I will not pretend that this is anything but a painful decision, and the responsibility for it is mine and the leadership team's.

What leadership is doing. [Specific: bonuses, pay, perks the leadership team is giving up, and for how long.]

What we are doing for the colleagues who are leaving.
- [Notice, severance and statutory transition payment, in plain terms.]
- Support from today, while they are still working with us: paid time for job search, coaching and outplacement, and a training budget of [EUR].
- A written reference and a positive reference call, the chance to keep their laptop and phone, and priority if we hire again.
- Confidential psychological and financial support.
- Open vacancies were offered first to affected colleagues.

How this was done. The works council has been informed and asked for its advice, and [what changed because of that advice]. Selection followed documented, objective criteria that comply with Dutch law, applied by HR. [Describe in one line.]

What changes in our work. [Operations and Support work that stops or changes, and what that means for customers.] We will not simply hand the same work to fewer people. Managers will sit down with their teams in the next two weeks to decide what we stop and what changes.

What I cannot promise. [Either: "The board has committed that this is the only round, and the numbers we have support that." or: "I cannot promise there will be no further changes. If that becomes necessary, you will hear it from me early, with the numbers."] Use only the one that is true.

What happens next.
- Today: questions to your manager or [HR contact]. I am hosting an open Q&A at [time/date], and questions can be sent anonymously to [channel].
- Within two weeks: a 1:1 with your manager for everyone who is staying.
- [Date]: update on workloads and priorities in Operations and Support.
- In [3 and 6] months: I will report back on how this turned out, including what it cost us.

To the colleagues who are leaving: you were part of what we built here, and this decision is not a judgement of your work. To those who are staying: I know this is difficult, and I want to hear what worries you, not only what is going well.

[Name], CEO
