The board decided, so I have not reopened the alternatives. What I have done is make this one as decently and as safely as it can be done. The full plan, the announcement messages and a manager script are in `plan-and-announcement.md`. Nothing has been sent to anyone.

**What matters most, in order**

1. **Works council first (art. 25 WOR).** At 180 people you will have one. Its advice has to be requested before the decision is final and early enough to change it. If staff hear "decided" and notices follow, you risk a challenge at the Enterprise Chamber, which costs more time than the consultation. The announcement is therefore worded as a proposal that you will only finalise after reading the council's advice, and it is honest that you will change the plan if the council finds a better route.
2. **22 triggers the collective dismissal rules (WMCO).** Twenty or more in one UWV work area within three months means notifying UWV and the unions and consulting them. Dismissals then follow the UWV route or settlement agreements, with the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel) for selection, and the statutory transition payment. Your employment counsel should confirm all of this before the file goes to the council.
3. **Cut the work, not just the people.** Operations and support are where a fintech's customer service and regulatory duties sit (complaints, customer due diligence, monitoring). Each team lead writes down what stops, shrinks or is automated, and compliance signs off before the file goes out. Otherwise 22 fewer people means the same work and failing service levels within months.
4. **Leaders go first, and say it up front.** I left the amount to you, with a line in the announcement for it. If staff hear about leadership pay after the council meets, it will look like an afterthought.
5. **Package beyond the legal minimum**, with support from the day of notice, not the last day. I have proposed elements but not figures, because I have no salary or budget data. HR and finance need to cost it, so you and the board see the net saving and payback before the council does.

**What is in the document:** a week-by-week sequence; selection rules; the package; the day of the conversations (own manager, HR present, no mass email, no access cut first); the plan for the people who stay; indicators; the all-hands message, a manager briefing and a message for those who stay; and a script for the individual conversation.

**Gaps you must fill:** the real reason and figures, dates, package budget, leadership pay decision, and the list of affected roles. I have left these in [brackets] rather than invent them.
