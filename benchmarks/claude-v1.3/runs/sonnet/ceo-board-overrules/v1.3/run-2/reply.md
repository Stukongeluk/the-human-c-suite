I've written both, with one change to the order: the staff announcement can't go out as a final decision. In the Netherlands, 22 roles at a company of 180 is a proposed decision until the works council has given its advice, and the 22 also likely triggers the collective-dismissal notification to UWV and the unions (20 or more within three months). The announcement is therefore written for the day the council gets the formal request, and it says honestly that the need to reduce is the board's decision while the how, when and support are open to the council's advice. Counsel should confirm this before anything is sent.

What I put in the plan:
- **Sequence:** counsel review, then T0 (request for advice plus the all-staff message), then an advice period of about six weeks, UWV and union notification in parallel, then the board's written answer, then individual conversations. Notice, severance and consultation mean the first-year saving will be well below the annual cost of 22 roles, so please have finance model that.
- **Selection:** the Dutch reflection principle by exchangeable function group and age cohort. No performance ranking, and no individual activity metrics.
- **Leaders first:** executive pay and bonus cuts announced the same day, with leadership stating plainly that the result is theirs. The amounts are placeholders for you and the board to decide.
- **Package:** transition payment plus extra severance, paid notice, a coaching and training budget from the first day of notice, devices, references and rehiring priority. The amounts are placeholders.
- **Work stops too:** each affected team decides what it stops doing, so the people who stay don't get the same work with fewer colleagues.
- **Afterwards:** team-level indicators for 12 months (customer wait times, complaints, regretted exits, absence), and an honest review at 6 and 12 months.
