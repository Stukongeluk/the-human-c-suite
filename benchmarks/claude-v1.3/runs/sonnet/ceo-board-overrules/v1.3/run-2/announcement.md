# Staff announcement (draft, to send on T0)

Send only after the works council has the formal request for advice, the managers of affected teams have been briefed, and counsel has read this. Items in [brackets] must be filled in or confirmed.

**Subject: A hard decision about the size of our company, and what we will do about it**

Dear all,

Today I have to tell you something difficult. Our board has decided that we need to reduce the company by 22 roles, from 180 to 158 people. Most of these roles are in Operations and Support.

I want to be direct about why. [One paragraph in plain words with the real numbers: the size of the gap, what drives it, by when, and what happens if we do not close it. Do not use vague phrases such as "market conditions" unless they are the whole truth.]

This is not a judgement of the people in those teams. They did their jobs well. The responsibility for the position we are in sits with the leadership team and with me, not with them.

We did look at other routes first, including [spending and vendor costs, pricing, stopping lower-value work, a hiring freeze, voluntary options]. [One or two sentences on why they were not enough or fast enough.] The board has decided that a reduction is still necessary.

What this means in practice:

- **No one is receiving notice today.** Today is a proposal. Under Dutch law, the works council now has the right to give advice before this becomes final, and we have asked for it today with the full numbers. Their advice can change the plan, and we will take it seriously. [Expected timeline: about six weeks.] I do want to be honest with you: the need to reduce is the board's decision. The works council can shape how, where, when and with what support.
- **Nobody will be chosen by a performance ranking.** Selection will follow the rules for such cases in the Netherlands, and the method will be shared with the works council before any names exist.
- **If you are in an affected role, you will hear it from your own manager, in person, with HR present, and before anything is shared more widely.** Not by email. [Date range.] Until then, nothing is decided about any individual.
- **Support starts on your first day of notice, not your last.** It includes [severance above the legal minimum, paid notice, an individual budget of EUR x for coaching and training, outplacement, keeping your laptop and phone, a written reference and priority for rehiring, and confidential psychological and financial support]. We will publish the full package on [date].
- **Leadership goes first.** [The executive team and I are reducing our own pay by x% and cancelling our bonuses for 2026 and 2027, starting today.] This does not fix the gap, and it is not meant to. It is the minimum we owe you.
- **We will cut work, not only people.** The teams will decide with their leads what we stop doing, so that colleagues who stay are not handed the same work with fewer hands. [Leads to confirm.]

What happens next:

- [Date]: all-hands meeting where you can ask me anything, in person and online. Questions can also be sent anonymously to [address], and I will answer them in writing every [week].
- [Date]: the works council gives its advice, and we publish the board's answer to it.
- [Date]: individual conversations begin, if the plan goes ahead.

I know that "support" and "process" do not take away the worry some of you have tonight. If you are afraid, or if you need to talk to someone outside the company, [independent provider, contact details] is free, confidential, and not shared with us.

I will not tell you this is the last change we will ever make. I will tell you that [only if the board confirms it is true: "the board has confirmed that no further round of reductions is planned"; otherwise: "I will tell you early and honestly if our situation changes"].

Thank you for your work, and for listening to a hard message.

[Name]
CEO

## Talking points for managers delivering individual conversations (after the council's advice and the final decision)

1. Say it in the first two sentences: "I have difficult news. Your role is one of those that will end." No small talk first.
2. Explain the reason once and briefly. Do not debate it, and do not say "it's nothing personal" if the person was chosen.
3. State what happens next: written summary today, notice period and dates, package, a named HR contact, support available from today.
4. Let silence be silence. Let them react. Do not rush to the practical details if they are upset.
5. Do not promise anything not in the written package. Do not speculate about other people.
6. Close with something true about their work, and what you will do to help (reference, introductions).
7. Afterwards: report back to HR, take a break, and talk to someone yourself.
