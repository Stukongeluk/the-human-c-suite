The plan and the staff announcement are written, built around one change: announce 22 roles as the board's intended decision, not a final one, until your works council has given its advice. The board's decision stands and I am not reopening the alternatives.

At 180 people you have a mandatory works council, and 22 dismissals in one UWV area is over the 20 threshold, so unions and UWV must be involved too. An announcement of a finished decision, with consultation afterwards, can be challenged at the Enterprise Chamber and can stop the whole thing for months. Done in the right order, the cost is a few weeks, and the works council may find something you can use.

The document, in `plan-and-announcement.md`, has three parts:

- **Plan:** timeline, selection by the Dutch reflection principle, the package, support for the people who stay, and the risks to watch.
- **Announcement:** a live all-hands text in your voice, saying plainly what is decided and what is open.
- **Manager brief:** a one-page script for the briefing before you speak.

Three things are in it that you should know about:

- **Leadership goes first.** The plan and announcement say the responsibility is yours and the executive team's, and state a pay, bonus and perk contribution. The figures are blank because they are yours to set. Please settle them before you send anything; without them, the message will sound hollow.
- **Cut the work, not only the roles.** Name what Operations and Support stop doing before you announce. Have Compliance confirm the remaining teams can still meet your regulatory duties, since in a fintech that is a licence risk.
- **Blanks marked in brackets** are facts only you have: the real numbers behind the decision, the severance formula, the dates. I did not invent any.

This is not legal advice. Employment counsel needs to confirm the route (UWV permission or settlement agreements), the function groups and the dates.
