**Short answer:** 15 roles cost about €1.27M a year; notice and settlements eat much of year one. With three-month notice, gone by 31 March means letters by 31 December: **€0.6–0.75M saved in 2027**. Letters in February save €0.4–0.55M. Nothing lands in 2026. A hiring freeze could save €0.35–0.6M in 2027, without severance or court risk.
*Estimates: €70k average salary, 21% on-costs, three-year average tenure, 8–10% attrition. Swap in your payroll.*

**The process**
1. Decide which work stops; courts check the jobs really disappear. Offer any fitting open role first.
2. Select by service, age, dependants and disability among comparable staff, not by performance (§1(3) KSchG). It tends to hit newer hires.
3. Pregnant, parental-leave and severely disabled staff need authority approval first, which can take months.
4. 15 of 140 within 30 days crosses the 10% threshold: notify the Agentur für Arbeit *before* any letter. Notices without it are void ([Federal Labour Court, 1 April 2026](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/en/presse/massenentlassung-rechtsfolge-von-fehlern-im-anzeigeverfahren/)).
5. Hand-signed letters. Notice runs 1–7 months by service (§622 BGB), often longer by contract.
6. Expect claims within three weeks; most settle around 0.5–1 month's pay per year of service.
7. Staff can elect a works council anytime; if one exists before you decide, you must negotiate with it first (§111 BetrVG).

**Before choosing:** revenue fell on leadership's watch, so leadership owns it and takes the first pay cut. Then cloud, licences, vendors and the freeze. For a temporary dip, Kurzarbeit, which here needs each employee's consent; the benefit [drops to 12 months from 2027](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/bezug-von-kurzarbeitergeld-2326616) unless extended.

If cuts remain: one round, support from the day of notice, each person told by their own manager.
