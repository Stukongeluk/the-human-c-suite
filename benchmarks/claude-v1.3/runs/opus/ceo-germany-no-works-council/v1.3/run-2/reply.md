**The process** (not legal advice; hire an employment lawyer now):

1. **Decide which work stops,** document it, and offer affected people any open roles (§1(2) KSchG).
2. **Social selection** within comparable roles: tenure, age, dependants, severe disability (§1(3) KSchG). Performance isn't a criterion.
3. **Special protection:** pregnancy, parental leave and severe disability need prior authority approval, which takes weeks.
4. **Mass-dismissal notification:** 15 exits in 30 days crosses the 10% threshold. Notify the Agentur für Arbeit before any notice or employer-initiated termination agreement. Errors void the dismissals (BAG, April 2026).
5. **Written notices** with wet-ink signatures (§623 BGB); notice runs four weeks to seven months by tenure or contract (§622 BGB).
6. **Settlement:** most claims (filed within three weeks, §4 KSchG) settle at about 0.5–1 monthly salary per year of service.
