**It will save about €0.57–0.75M in 2027, not the €1.27M a year those 15 roles cost.** Exits on 31 March save nine months (€0.95M), minus severance (€0.13–0.26M) and legal and outplacement (€0.08–0.13M). Estimates: €70k average salary, 21% employer costs, three years' average tenure.

**The process (the Kündigungsschutzgesetz applies):**

1. Document which work stops for good, and offer any open role to affected people first.
2. Select by tenure, age, dependants and disability across comparable roles, not by performance; you may exempt a few people whose skills you can show you need.
3. 15 of 140 within 30 days requires a mass-dismissal filing with the Agentur für Arbeit before any notice (§17 KSchG); termination agreements you initiate count too. Since April 2026 the Federal Labour Court voids dismissals after a missing or defective filing.
4. Notices on paper, signed by hand. Notice runs 1–7 months by tenure, so serve by 31 December for a 31 March exit; each month later costs about €0.1M.
5. Pregnancy, parental leave and severe disability need authority approval; expect those to slip past March.
6. People can sue within three weeks; settlements usually start at half a month's pay per year of service.

No works council means no social plan, but staff can elect one anytime, and those who call the election gain dismissal protection. Be open early, not fast.

**The trap:** a headcount target without a euro target ends with cutting what's easiest to count. A strict hiring freeze at 7–10% attrition (estimate) could save €0.4–0.6M in 2027 without severance or lawsuits. Cut leadership pay, contractors and cloud spend first; if a gap remains, cut once, with job-search support from day one.
