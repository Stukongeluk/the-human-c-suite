On a calendar year, this saves nothing in 2026. In 2027, 15 exits by 31 March net roughly **€0.4–0.6M**, not the €1.35M run rate the board may be counting. A hiring freeze plus normal attrition could save about as much, without severance.

**The process (no works council means no social plan; the rest still applies)**
1. Decide which work stops, not just who goes; courts test that.
2. Offer open roles first, then select among comparable roles by tenure, age, dependants and disability (§1 KSchG). You can't simply pick.
3. 15 of 140 crosses the mass-dismissal threshold (10% within 30 days, §17 KSchG; employer-prompted exit agreements count). Notify the Agentur für Arbeit before any notice: since the [Federal Labour Court's 1 April 2026 ruling](https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/en/presse/massenentlassung-rechtsfolge-von-fehlern-im-anzeigeverfahren/), notices without it are void.
4. Severely disabled, pregnant or parental-leave staff need authority approval first: weeks to months.
5. Notice on paper with a wet signature, from their own manager. Anyone on three months' notice must have it by 31 December.
6. People have three weeks to sue; most cases settle on severance.

A works council elected before notices go out (staff's right) must be consulted and heard first, adding weeks.

**2027 savings (estimate: €75k average salary, ~€90k loaded, 4 years' tenure)**

| | |
|---|---|
| Payroll, April–December | +€1.01M |
| Severance, 0.5–1.0 month per year | −€0.19–0.38M |
| Counsel, outplacement, transition support | −€0.10M |
| Three people you'd keep also quit | −€0.11M |
| **Net** | **≈ €0.4–0.6M** |

Exits that slip past March cut this further.

**Before committing:** leaders take the first pay cut, freeze hiring, offer a decent voluntary package. If the dip is temporary, Kurzarbeit bridges it; without a works council each employee must agree.

"15 roles" is a solution; the problem is a revenue gap. Two questions decide this: how big is the gap in euros, and is the drop temporary or structural?
