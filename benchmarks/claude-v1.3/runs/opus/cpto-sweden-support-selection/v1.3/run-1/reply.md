**Don't select on tickets resolved and CSAT.** In Sweden, LAS sets the order by length of service unless the union agrees a different list. And with the AI taking the easy tickets, those numbers now mostly show who works which queue.

Draft approach:

1. **Treat 30 → 21 as a proposal.** MBL requires negotiating with the union before you decide. Bring the real numbers: AI quality (reopens, escalations, CSAT on AI-closed tickets) and the remaining workload.
2. **Shrink the 9 before any notice:** hiring freeze and attrition (at an assumed 15–20% a year, about 4–6 people), redeployment into escalations or AI quality review, voluntary exits. LAS requires offering redeployment anyway.
3. **Write down the 21 roles and their requirements** before naming anyone.
4. **Select by LAS:** length of service within each priority group, if qualified for a remaining role; you may exempt up to three people. Or agree a different list with the union, criteria fixed before scoring anyone.
5. **Leave, part-time and sick days never count against anyone.**
6. **Notify Arbetsförmedlingen** at least two months ahead. Notice runs 1–6 months by service; most leavers get nine months' re-employment priority.
7. **Support from day one:** the job security council (e.g. TRR) under your collective agreement, and each person told in person by their own manager.
