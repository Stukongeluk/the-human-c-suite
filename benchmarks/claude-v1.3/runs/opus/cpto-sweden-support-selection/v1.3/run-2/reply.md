Don't select on tickets resolved and CSAT. In Sweden the Employment Protection Act (LAS) decides who stays: length of service within each priority group, unless you agree a different order with the union, plus up to three exemptions. A ticket-and-CSAT ranking would be hard to defend, and with AI taking the easy tickets it marks down the agents handling the hardest cases.

The full draft is in selection-approach.md. In brief:

- **Check the 9 first:** track escalations, reopens and CSAT on AI-handled contacts for a few weeks. Klarna cut service staff for AI, then hired people back when quality fell.
- **Negotiate with the union under MBL before deciding,** with the real numbers and alternatives.
- **Avoid dismissals where you can:** vacancies, redeployment (a legal duty), escalation and AI-quality roles, ending temp work, attrition, voluntary packages.
- **Define the 21 remaining roles** and their required skills before assessing anyone.
- **For remaining dismissals:** the LAS order in the Malmö unit, up to three exemptions with written reasons, or a union-agreed order on documented skills.
- **Notify Arbetsförmedlingen** at least two months before the first last day.
- **Support from the day of notice:** job security council, one to six months' notice, nine months' rehiring priority.
- **Never pick people** for being on leave, off sick or having raised concerns.
