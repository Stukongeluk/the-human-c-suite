# Malmö Support: staffing change and selection approach (draft)

**Status:** draft for negotiation with the union (MBL §11). Nothing is decided yet. Employment counsel must review it before use. Owner: [name]. Date: [date].

## Proposal

AI now resolves most first-line tickets, and management proposes reducing Malmö Support from 30 to 21 roles. Before anything is decided, we will check that number, reach it with as few dismissals as possible, and agree the approach with the union.

## 1. Check the need (weeks 1–4)

- Weekly ticket volume handled by people, before and after AI, including escalations from AI.
- Quality of AI-handled contacts: reopen rate, escalation rate, CSAT and complaints.
- Cover the 21 must provide: opening hours, languages, holidays, sickness and peaks.
- If the data supports fewer than 9 reductions, the number changes. Klarna cut customer service staff after launching AI and later began hiring people again because service quality had suffered.

## 2. Negotiate before deciding

- Start MBL negotiations before any decision. Share the volume data, the costs and the alternatives below.
- Agree with the union what employees are told and when.

## 3. Avoid dismissals where possible

1. Offer open vacancies in the company to the Malmö team first. LAS §7 also requires us to consider redeployment.
2. Create roles for the new work: escalation specialists, knowledge-base upkeep, AI quality review.
3. End temporary, agency and contractor work in support before permanent roles.
4. Use natural attrition and a hiring freeze in support.
5. Offer voluntary departure packages, and reduced hours to anyone who wants them.

Dismissals still needed after steps 1–5: [x].

## 4. Define the remaining roles

Describe the 21 roles, the work they cover and the qualifications they need. Write this down before anyone is assessed.

## 5. Selection, if dismissals remain

- **Priority group:** the Malmö operating unit, with a separate group for each collective agreement area (LAS §22).
- **Order:** total length of employment; with equal service, the older employee goes first.
- **Qualifications:** an employee keeps their place if they have sufficient qualifications for the remaining work after reasonable training.
- **Exemptions:** up to three people of particular importance to continued operations, each with written reasons. No further exemptions are allowed for terminations within three months.
- **Alternative:** an order agreed with the union, based on documented skills for the roles in section 4.
- **Not used:** tickets resolved or CSAT per person. AI now handles most simple tickets, so people get very different case mixes, and CSAT mostly reflects the customer's issue. Parental leave, sick leave, part-time work, union activity and raising concerns are never factors.

## 6. Notice and support

- Notify Arbetsförmedlingen at least two months before the first employee's last day (5–25 people).
- Each person hears the news from their manager, with HR present, and gets a written summary the same day.
- Notice runs from one to six months by length of service (LAS §11), or as the collective agreement sets.
- Support starts on the day of notice: the job security council (TRR, or Kammarkollegiet's basic support without a collective agreement), plus a company budget for coaching and training of [amount] per person.
- Priority for re-employment for nine months, for people with more than 12 months' employment in the last three years.
- The 21 who stay hear the same day what happened, and their work is re-planned with them.
