I wouldn't select on tickets resolved and CSAT. In Sweden, LAS sets the order by length of service unless you agree a different list with the union, and those numbers reward easy tickets and penalise anyone on parental leave, sick leave or part-time, a discrimination risk. This approach holds up and may need fewer than nine dismissals.

**Selection approach, Malmö support (draft)**

1. **Size the team from the remaining work.** Check two to three months of reopens, escalations and CSAT on AI-handled tickets. If hard tickets now land on fewer people, 21 may be too few.
2. **Negotiate under MBL before deciding.** Give the union the numbers and the proposal while it can still change.
3. **Shrink the number first.** Hiring freeze, attrition, moves into the new work (AI quality review, knowledge base, second line), reduced hours, then voluntary exits with an enhanced package.
4. **Then select lawfully.** Define the remaining roles, then apply the LAS order among those qualified, with up to three exemptions, or a union-agreed list on objective, skills-based criteria. No activity rankings.
5. **Give notice decently.** Notify Arbetsförmedlingen at least two months ahead. Job security council support (TRR, via your collective agreement) from day one; nine months' re-employment priority.
6. **Phase it over two quarters** and keep a human route: Klarna rehired agents in 2025 after admitting service quality suffered.

Not legal advice; check the details with employment counsel.
