**How roles are selected**

Dutch law sets the method, and we follow it. Roles are grouped by comparable work. Within each group, the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel) keeps the mix of age groups the same, and within each age group the most recent hire goes first. Performance ratings, illness, parental leave and whether someone has raised concerns play no part. HR will explain the method at the Q&A sessions and on [intranet page].

**What we will do for colleagues who leave**

- a career coach and a training budget of one month's salary, from the day they hear the news, not from their last day
- a contribution towards their own legal advice
- their laptop and phone to keep, a written reference and a reference call from their manager
- priority if we rehire in the next 12 months
- confidential psychological and financial support
- extra help for colleagues whose residence permit is linked to their job, including advice from an immigration lawyer at our cost
