## 5. Legal and consultation steps (Netherlands)

Not legal advice. Counsel confirms each point for our situation.

- **Works council (WOR).** With 180 employees we are required to have a works council. It has an advisory right on a reduction like this (article 25 WOR), and the request must come at a point where its advice can still substantially influence the decision. The request sets out the reasons, the consequences for staff and the planned measures. If the final decision departs from the advice, we wait one month before carrying it out, and the council can appeal to the Enterprise Chamber (articles 25(6) and 26 WOR).
- **Collective dismissal (WMCO).** Dismissing 20 or more people within three months in one UWV work area means notifying UWV in advance and the unions with members in the company, and consulting the unions. Settlement agreements count towards the 20. Settlement agreements, notices and court dissolutions have to wait one month after UWV confirms the notification is complete, unless the unions confirm in writing that they were consulted and agree ([UWV](https://www.uwv.nl/werkgevers/werkgever-en-ontslag/ik-wil-ontslag-aanvragen/detail/collectief-ontslag); [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/regels-voor-collectief-ontslag/)).
- **Dismissal route.** Either a UWV permit for business-economic reasons or a settlement agreement. For a permit we show the business reason, a selection that follows the reflection principle, and that redeployment within a reasonable period isn't possible.
- **Notice periods** (article 7:672 Civil Code): one month under five years of service, two months for five to ten, three for ten to fifteen, four for fifteen or more. The time a UWV procedure took can be deducted, leaving at least one month.
- **Transition payment.** Statutory: one third of monthly salary per year of service, pro rata. The maximum in 2026 is €102,000, or the annual salary if that is higher ([Rendement, 19 December 2025](https://www.rendement.nl/transitievergoeding/nieuws/maximale-transitievergoeding-is-in-2026--102000.html)). Check the 2027 maximum once it is published, because most exits fall in 2027.
- **Settlement agreements.** The employee can withdraw within 14 days of signing (article 7:670b Civil Code). End dates respect the notice period so that unemployment benefit (WW) isn't put at risk.
- **Residence permits.** As sponsor, we report the end of employment to the IND. A highly skilled migrant who loses their job has a search period of three months, or six months for people who have held their permit for at least two years and whose job ends on or after 22 May 2026; the search period starts when the contract ends ([Mynta](https://www.mynta.nl/en/knowledge-base/possibly-longer-search-period-for-some-settled-highly-skilled-migrants); [IamExpat](https://www.iamexpat.nl/career/employment-news/ind-extends-job-search-period-work-permit-holders-netherlands)). Counsel confirms per person.
- **Regulator.** Compliance checks whether any affected role is a key function or part of a function we have reported to [DNB/AFM], and whether that triggers a notification.

## 6. How we decide who is affected

- **Role groups, not names.** Roles are grouped by comparable content, skills, level and pay. The plan fixes how many roles go per group. Nobody draws up a list of names.
- **Order within each group,** as UWV sets it out ([UWV](https://www.uwv.nl/werkgevers/werkgever-en-ontslag/ik-wil-ontslag-aanvragen/detail/ontslag-via-uwv/ontslagaanvraag-wegens-bedrijfseconomische-redenen/rekening-houden-met-de-ontslagvolgorde-afspiegelingsbeginsel)):
  1. external workers in the group (agency staff, freelancers, secondees)
  2. employees who have reached state pension (AOW) age
  3. on-call (zero-hours) workers
  4. fixed-term contracts ending within 26 weeks of UWV's decision
  5. permanent contracts, and fixed-term contracts running longer
- Groups 2 to 5 are split into five age bands (15–25, 25–35, 35–45, 45–55, 55+). Each band loses its proportional share, and within a band the most recent hire goes first.
- **Exceptions are narrow and documented.** "Indispensable" knowledge needs written proof; counsel reviews every exception.
- **Never used:** performance ratings, illness, parental or other leave, having raised concerns, or any protected characteristic.
- **Volunteers** in a role group reduce the number of forced exits in that group, if the social plan includes a voluntary window. Counsel checks how this fits the reflection principle.
- **Redeployment first:** people in affected groups get first claim on open roles they can do with reasonable training, and we pay for the training.

## 8. The individual conversations (Tuesday 26 January)

- Each person hears it from their own manager, with HR, in person or on a live video call. 20 to 30 minutes, in a private room, early in the day. Managers get breaks between conversations.
- The news comes in the first minute, then the reason, what happens next and the package.
- Everyone gets a written summary in the conversation: the decision, the package with amounts, the timeline, deadlines and contacts. Nobody signs anything that day. A follow-up with HR within two working days. Their own lawyer, at our cost.
- People choose whether to go home or stay, and how and when colleagues hear. They can say goodbye.
- In affected teams, people whose roles stay hear that from their manager the same morning.
- Access: privileged and production access (payments, customer data, admin rights) is removed straight after the conversation, never before it. Email, chat and HR tools stay on until the agreed last working day.
- Managers debrief at the end of the day and have access to the same confidential support.

## 11. What we measure

Reviewed monthly for six months by the leadership team, and shared with the works council.

- **Money:** run-rate saving against plan; one-off costs against budget.
- **People who stay:** regretted exits, sickness absence, a pulse survey at six weeks and three months, internal moves.
- **Customers and operations:** service levels, backlog, KYC queue, complaints, incidents.
- **People who left:** how many have new work at three and six months (aggregated, from the outplacement provider).
- **Trigger:** if regretted exits exceed [x] or service levels are missed for [two] months running, we re-plan the work. We don't cut further.

## 13. Risks and what would change this plan

- **The works council's advice changes the spread, timing or package.** We change the plan; that is what consultation is for.
- **Compliance finds a capacity gap.** The spread of the 22 changes.
- **A leak before 1 December.** We announce on the next working day, in the same order: works council first.
- **Leadership's share isn't decided.** Staff in Operations and Support will ask about it, and the announcement says leadership is responsible.
- **Assumptions:** all 22 roles are in Amsterdam (one UWV work area); there is no collective agreement with its own rules; there were no other dismissals in the last three months that count towards the WMCO total; the works council exists and hasn't yet given its advice.
