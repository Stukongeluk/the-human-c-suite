That order isn't me reopening the board's decision. It's Dutch law:

- **Works council.** With 180 people you're required to have one, and article 25 of the Works Councils Act gives it an advisory right on a reduction like this. The request has to come while its advice can still change things. If you announce "22 roles go" as final before that, the council can appeal to the Enterprise Chamber, and you lose its trust right when you need it.
- **UWV and unions.** 22 exits within three months is above the threshold of 20 in the collective dismissal act (WMCO). You have to notify UWV and the unions, and settlement agreements and notices wait a month after UWV confirms the notification ([UWV](https://www.uwv.nl/werkgevers/werkgever-en-ontslag/ik-wil-ontslag-aanvragen/detail/collectief-ontslag)).
