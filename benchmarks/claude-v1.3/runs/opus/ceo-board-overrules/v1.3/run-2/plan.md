## 2. Legal and consultation track (Netherlands)

- **Works council (article 25 WOR).** It has an advice right on a significant reduction of the workforce. Ask for its advice while that advice can still substantially change the decision, and discuss the matter in at least one consultation meeting (overlegvergadering) before it advises. If the final decision departs from the advice, implementation waits one month, during which the works council can appeal to the Enterprise Chamber.
- **Collective dismissal (WMCO).** The rules apply when an employer plans to dismiss 20 or more employees within three months in one UWV work area. Settlement agreements count towards the 20. Notify UWV in advance, and notify and consult the unions with members in the company (consultation is required if they respond in time). The one-month waiting period starts when UWV confirms that the notification is complete. No contract ends before then, settlement agreements included, unless the unions declare in writing that they were consulted and agree. ([UWV](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/ontslag/collectief-ontslag), [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/timing-is-everything-zo-bepaalt-de-wmco-wachttijd-uw-reorganisatie))
  - If some of the 22 are open vacancies rather than people, counsel confirms whether the WMCO still applies. Run everything else the same way.
  - Don't spread exits over separate three-month windows to stay under 20. Apart from the legal risk, a drip of small rounds does more damage than one well-run round.
- **Selection: the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel).** Within each category of interchangeable roles, apply it per age group (15–25, 25–35, 35–45, 45–55, 55+), with the shortest service going first within each group. Hired-in staff in those categories go first, then employees of state pension age, on-call workers and temporary contracts with at most 26 weeks left. The principle doesn't apply where a whole category, or a unique role held by one person, disappears. An employee covered by a dismissal ban, such as illness or pregnancy, is skipped and the next person in the same age group is selected. Performance ratings, leave and having raised concerns are never criteria. ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/ontslag/afspiegelingsbeginsel))
- **Redeployment.** Before anyone is dismissed, offer them suitable vacancies, with training where that is reasonable.
- **Routes.** One route is a settlement agreement (vaststellingsovereenkomst), which carries a 14-day cooling-off period if the agreement states it (three weeks if it doesn't). The other is a UWV permit application for business-economic reasons. Set settlement end dates no earlier than the notice period would allow, or the person's unemployment benefit (WW) starts later.
- **Notice periods.** Under 5 years' service: 1 month; 5–10 years: 2; 10–15 years: 3; 15 years or more: 4. In the UWV route the procedure time may be deducted, but at least one month must remain. ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/ontslag/vraag-en-antwoord/iemand-ontslaan-opzegtermijn-werkgever))
- **Transition payment.** One third of a monthly salary per year of service, from the first day. The maximum is €102,000 in 2026, or one year's salary if that is higher ([Rendement](https://www.rendement.nl/transitievergoeding/nieuws/maximale-transitievergoeding-is-in-2026--102000.html)). The cap is updated on 1 January, and most end dates fall in 2027.
- **Visa holders.** A highly skilled migrant whose job ends keeps their permit for a search period of up to 3 months, or up to 6 months if they became unemployed on or after 22 May 2026 and have held the permit for 2 years or more. The search period starts on the day the contract ends ([IND](https://ind.nl/werk/werken-in-nederland/paginas/kennismigrant.aspx)), so the end date matters to them.
