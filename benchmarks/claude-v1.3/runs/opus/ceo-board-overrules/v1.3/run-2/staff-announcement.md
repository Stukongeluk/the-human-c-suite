# Staff announcement

**Subject: An intended decision to reduce 22 roles: why, what happens next, and what we'll do**

Hi everyone,

This morning I shared a proposal with the works council, and the managers of the teams most affected spoke to their teams in person. You've just heard it from me in the all-staff session. Here it is in writing, so you can read it again and share it with the people at home.

**What we intend to do**

We intend to reduce the number of roles at [Company] by 22, from 180 to 158. Most are in operations and support: [n] in [team], [n] in [team] and [n] in [team].

**Whose responsibility this is**

It is ours, the leadership team's, and mine in particular. [One or two honest sentences on what went wrong, for example: "We planned and hired for X; what happened was Y."] That was our call. It is not the result of anything the people in operations and support did. They have kept our customers served through [a specific, true example], and I want that to be clear before anything else.

[The leadership team and I are taking a [x]% pay cut for the next 12 months, and there will be no leadership bonuses for [2026 and 2027]. It is a small part of the savings. We're doing it because the people who made the plans should carry the cost first.]

**Why**

[The numbers, plainly: for example, costs of €X a month against revenue of €Y, Z months of runway, and what has to be true by when.] We have already [the savings already made outside jobs, with amounts]. Together these save about €[x] a year, which doesn't close the gap. The leadership team and the board also looked at other ways to avoid job losses, including [the two or three main ones], and concluded [the honest reason they weren't chosen]. The works council will get the full analysis, and I'll go through it with anyone who wants to.

**What is decided and what is still open**

This is an intended decision. Under Dutch law, the works council gives its advice first, while that advice can still change the plan, and I want it to. Still open:

- how many roles per team
- how the work is reorganised, so that the people who stay aren't left with the same work and fewer colleagues
- the support package
- the timing

If you see a better way to do any of this, tell your manager, the works council or me. When we decide, we'll tell you what we changed because of your input, what we didn't, and why.

Because 20 or more roles are involved, we have also notified UWV and the unions today, as the law requires.

**How people will be selected**

We will follow Dutch law: the reflection principle (afspiegelingsbeginsel). Within each group of interchangeable roles, selection is spread across age groups, with the shortest service going first within each age group. Contractors and agency staff in the same roles end first. Selection is not based on performance reviews, on anyone being on leave, or on anyone having raised concerns. Before anyone leaves, we will offer suitable open vacancies to people in affected roles, with training where needed.

**When**

- **Every Thursday:** a written update from me, even when there's little news.
- **Over the holidays:** nothing happens to anyone's job, pay or access.
- **Late January:** the works council's advice, then our final decision, which we'll share with all of you.
- **Not before 2 February:** individual conversations. Nobody will be told individually before then. If your role is affected, you'll hear it from your own manager, with HR present, in person or on a live call. Never by email, and never by finding your access changed.
- **Not before the end of March:** the first last working days. For many people it will be later, depending on notice periods.

**What we'll do for colleagues who leave**

This is our proposal to the works council, and it may get better in consultation:

- a payment above the statutory transition payment, with a minimum of [3] months' salary
- full notice paid, with paid time to look for work
- a personal budget for coaching, training or outplacement, starting the day you hear, not after your last day
- a contribution towards your own lawyer to review any agreement
- keeping your laptop and phone, a written reference and a reference call
- priority if we hire again in the next 12 months
- confidential psychological support and financial advice
- extra help for colleagues whose residence permit depends on their job, including paid immigration advice

[We are not planning another round of reductions.]

**Where to go with questions**

- Your manager, today or any day
- HR drop-in sessions today, 14:00–17:00, and [times] this week
- The works council: [names and contact]
- Our confidential counsellor (vertrouwenspersoon): [name and contact]
- Confidential psychological support, for everyone, not only those affected: [provider and number]
- The FAQ on [intranet link], which we'll keep updated

I know this lands hard, and that some of you will spend the coming weeks worried, at home as well as at work. I'm sorry. We'll do this once, carefully, and in the open, and I'll keep telling you what I know when I know it.

## FAQ (publish on the intranet with the announcement)

**Is my role affected?**
If your team is in scope, your manager told you this morning. Nobody knows yet who individually will be affected. That follows from the selection rules after the works council has advised, and nobody will hear individually before 2 February.

**How are people chosen?**
By Dutch law: within each group of interchangeable roles, selection is spread across five age groups (15–25, 25–35, 35–45, 45–55 and 55+), with the shortest service going first within each group. Contractors and agency staff in the same roles end first. Performance reviews, leave and having raised concerns play no part.

**Can I volunteer to leave?**
We're discussing with the works council whether volunteers in affected roles can take a place, and on what terms. We'll answer this by [date].

**What will I receive if my role ends?**
The proposal is above the statutory minimum (see the announcement). The final package is agreed in consultation with the works council and the unions, and we'll publish it before any individual conversation.

**What happens to my work if colleagues leave?**
We're deciding with each team what stops, what changes and what service levels change, so the remaining work fits the team that remains. Your input is part of that, and you'll see the result before anyone leaves.

**I'm ill, on leave or pregnant. What does this mean for me?**
Being on leave is never a reason for selection, and Dutch law protects you against dismissal during illness and pregnancy. HR can explain what applies to you, in confidence.

**My residence permit depends on my job. What happens?**
Talk to HR in confidence. We'll pay for immigration advice and take your permit's search period into account when setting any end date.

**Will there be more cuts after this?**
[We are not planning another round.] We'll tell you if anything changes, and we won't do it by surprise.

**Why not cut [something else] instead?**
[Short, honest answer on the savings already made and the alternatives considered.] Ask us at any Q&A, and the works council has the full analysis.

**Who can I talk to in confidence?**
Our confidential counsellor (vertrouwenspersoon) [name], the psychological support line [number], HR, or any works council member.
