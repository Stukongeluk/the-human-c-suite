To everyone at [Company],

This is hard news, and I want you to hear it plainly. You've just heard it from me at the all-hands; this is the written version, so you can read it again in your own time.

## What we're proposing

The board and the leadership team intend to reduce [Company] by 22 roles. That is about 12% of our 180 colleagues. Most of these roles are in operations and support:

- Operations: [n] roles, in [role types]
- Support: [n] roles, in [role types]
- [Team]: [n] roles, in [role types]

If your team and role type are not on this list, your role is not part of this proposal.

This is a proposed decision, not a final one. This morning we asked our works council (OR) for its formal advice. The OR can change this plan, and we will take its advice seriously. No decision about anyone's individual role will be made until we have received and weighed that advice.

## Why

[Our revenue this year is €[x], [y]% below our plan. Our costs are €[z] a year, and [most] of that is salaries. If nothing changes, we would [run out of cash by (month, year) / fall below the capital our licence requires by (month, year)].] To stay healthy, we need to lower our yearly costs by €[x] by [date].

This is also the result of choices the leadership team made, including mine. [We hired for growth that came more slowly than we planned.] I own that.

## What we did first

Before proposing this, we [cut spending on (vendors, software, office) by €[x]; stopped hiring in (teams) since (month); (other steps)]. Those steps save €[x] a year and they continue, but they don't close the gap in time. [The leadership team, including me, will take a [x]% pay cut and give up our bonuses for [year].]

## What happens next

- **From today:** the OR reviews the proposal. It has the full numbers and can bring in its own adviser at our cost. We have also informed UWV and the unions, as Dutch law requires when 20 or more jobs may be affected.
- **Every [weekday] until this is settled:** I'll send you a short update, also when there is no news.
- **Around [date]:** we expect the OR's advice. We'll then take the final decision and tell you what changed because of the advice, and why.
- **[Dates]:** if the plan goes ahead, colleagues in the affected role types can choose to leave voluntarily, with the same package.
- **[Date]:** everyone in an affected role type hears personally whether their role is affected, from their own manager with HR, in person or on a live call. Nobody will find out by email or because their laptop stops working. Colleagues in those teams whose roles continue will hear the same morning.

## How selection works

Within each type of role, Dutch law sets the order. First, assignments of agency workers and freelancers in those roles end. After that, colleagues are grouped by age band, and within each band the people who joined most recently are affected first. This is called the reflection principle. It is not based on performance ratings or on who anyone likes, and being on leave or having raised concerns plays no part.

For every affected colleague we will first check whether there is another suitable role here. From today, open vacancies go to colleagues in affected roles before anyone outside.

## What we'll do for colleagues who leave

This is our proposal to the OR and the unions, and their input may improve it:

- The statutory transition payment plus [x]%, with a minimum of [two] months' salary, so that colleagues who joined recently aren't left with very little.
- Your full notice period, paid, with time to look for work, or release from work if you prefer.
- Support from the day you hear, not after your last day: a personal budget of €[x] for coaching, outplacement or training, and paid time to use it.
- A contribution of €[x] towards your own lawyer to review the agreement.
- You keep your laptop [and phone], once company data has been wiped.
- [Good-leaver treatment under the option plan, with [x] months to exercise vested options.]
- A written reference, and a reference call from your manager if you'd like one.
- Priority if we hire for similar roles within [12] months, and a place in our alumni network.
- Free, confidential support through [provider] for [six] months, and a session with a financial adviser.
- If you are here on a highly skilled migrant permit, we'll pay for advice from an immigration lawyer, help you look for a new sponsor, and take your permit into account when we set dates. [HR name] is your contact.

## Questions and answers (sent with the announcement)

**Is my role affected?**
If your team and role type are listed above, you'll know on [date], from your own manager. If they're not listed, your role is not part of this proposal.

**Why can't you tell me today?**
Two reasons. The OR advises first, and its advice can change the plan. And Dutch law sets the selection order, which we can only apply properly once the plan is final. We would rather tell you once and correctly than give you a guess.

**Can I volunteer to leave?**
We propose a voluntary window after the OR has advised, with the same package. If you're thinking about it, you can talk to HR in confidence now. Nothing you say will count against you.

**Will there be more rounds?**
[We are not planning any. / Honest answer.]

**I'm on leave or off sick. What does this mean for me?**
Your manager will call you personally. Extra legal protections may apply to you, and HR will explain them.

**What happens to my holiday balance, pension and options?**
Unused holiday is paid out. Your pension accrual stops on your last day, and HR will explain your options. [Option plan: good-leaver treatment, as above.]

**What does this mean for our customers?**
We're planning support coverage for the whole period and will tell customers in advance about any change in service.

**Who decides in the end?**
The board takes the final decision after receiving the OR's advice. If that decision departs from the advice, the law requires us to wait one month before carrying it out, and the OR can go to court during that time.
