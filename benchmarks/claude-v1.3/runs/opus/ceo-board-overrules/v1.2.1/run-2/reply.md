My recommendation is to make one announcement and present it as a **proposed decision** that your works council advises on before it becomes final, with a package above the legal minimum. At 180 staff and 22 roles, both the works council's advice right and the collective dismissal rules (20 or more people within three months) apply. If you announce on Tuesday 1 December, the individual conversations land on 2 February and the earliest last working days on 31 March 2027.

I've written both documents and left out the alternatives:
- **reduction-plan.md**: the legal steps, scope, selection, package, timeline, the Day 0 run of show, the people who stay, and indicators.
- **staff-announcement.md**: the announcement plus a short Q&A. Read it at a live all-hands first and send the written version straight after.

One thing I couldn't write the way the board put it. If staff hear "the decision stands" before the works council has advised, the council can go to the Enterprise Chamber, and UWV treats dismissal applications as premature until that procedure is done. So ask the board to minute it as a proposed decision, and leave real room for the advice to change something.

Three things that matter before Day 0:
- 22 roles is not the same as 22 dismissals. By law, agency and freelance assignments in the same role types end first, and closing open vacancies also counts. The plan shows how to work out the real number.
- For short tenure, the statutory transition payment is small: €3,600 for 2.5 years at €4,000 a month. That's why the plan proposes a minimum of two months' pay.
- KYC/AML, transaction monitoring and complaints handling need enough people left after the cuts to meet what your licence requires.

Nothing has been sent. Have your employment lawyer check the legal steps; this isn't legal advice.
