### Why the current three moves backfire on a PE exit
Treating a customer and integration crisis as a quick cost-cut and rebrand problem is the trap here. In an 18-month exit window, buyers price Net Revenue Retention, churn, NPS trajectory, and team stability—not just one-year EBITDA. A fourth restructure in two years will trigger another productivity dip and regretted departures, a Q1 rebrand will consume capacity customers need now, and under German labour law (*BetrVG* reconciliation of interests/social plans where a works council exists, *KSchG* *Sozialauswahl*, and *BGB* notice periods—all to be verified with German employment counsel, not legal advice) headcount cuts cost cash upfront before delivering net savings.

### Your options for the board
1. **Current leadership proposal (Q1 rebrand + 4th reorg + cut all contractors & juniors):** High upfront cash cost, delayed net savings, high churn risk, and a hollowed-out delivery and succession bench in buyer due diligence. Irreversible.

   - **Months 1–3:** Freeze executive perks/discretionary bonuses and tie variable pay to NPS, churn, and EBITDA. Audit and cut duplicate SaaS, cloud, vendor, and facility spend across the four acquired entities, and stop unbilled services scope creep.
