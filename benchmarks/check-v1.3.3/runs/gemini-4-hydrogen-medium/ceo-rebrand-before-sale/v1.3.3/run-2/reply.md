Contractors across four recently merged logistics companies often hold critical integration and legacy-system knowledge; cutting them all at once risks SLA breaches and outages.

### Human & German legal reality (flag for HR and counsel)
* **Works councils (*Betriebsräte*) & co-determination:** At ~900 employees across four German entities, check which sites have works councils (and whether supervisory board co-determination under the *Drittelbeteiligungsgesetz* applies). Major operational changes (§§111–112 BetrVG) require early negotiation of an *Interessenausgleich* and *Sozialplan* *before* implementation.
* **Dismissal rules & timing:** Collective redundancies (§17 KSchG) require advance notice to the *Agentur für Arbeit*, and operational dismissals require formal *Sozialauswahl* (§1(3) KSchG)—you cannot simply target management layers. Combined with statutory notice periods (§622 BGB) and severance, layoffs rarely deliver net cash savings inside an 18-month exit window. *(Verify all specifics with German employment counsel.)*
