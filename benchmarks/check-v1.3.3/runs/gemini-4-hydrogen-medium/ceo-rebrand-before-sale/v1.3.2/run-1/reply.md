### The Trap to Name in the Room
Your leadership team is treating a post-merger integration and service failure as a headcount-cost problem—stacking a fourth reorganisation on top of three that have not yet settled.

### The Core Argument for the Board
Private equity buyers in 18 months will value the business on **EBITDA × Multiple**, and in logistics software and services, **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and gross churn** set that multiple.

| Option | Effect on Exit Value | Timing to Cash / EBITDA | Main Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Current 3 moves** (Q1 rebrand + 4th reorg + cut all contractors/juniors) | Hurts NRR and multiple; severance & notice periods delay net savings | 6–12 months (cash-negative upfront) | Senior burnout, lost integration knowledge, worsening churn |
| **B. Status quo** | Margin stays flat; churn erodes revenue | None | Missed EBITDA target and weak equity story |
| **C. Recommended: Stabilise, Consolidate Spend & Sequence** | Protects NRR/multiple while capturing faster non-headcount EBITDA | **1–3 months** (spend & pricing); **3–9 months** (ops) | Requires discipline on 4-entity spend and commercial leakage |

### What to Do Instead (First 90 Days)
1. **Own the result, leaders first:** State plainly to the board and the company that leadership owns the +6 NPS. Tie executive bonuses jointly to **NRR/churn + EBITDA** (never EBITDA alone) and pause discretionary executive bonuses or perks until NPS and churn stabilise.
2. **Fix retention before changing org charts:** Put single-threaded owners on top at-risk accounts across all four companies and clear the top support/onboarding bottlenecks.
3. **Capture faster, reversible EBITDA first:** Consolidate duplicate SaaS licences, cloud hosting, overlapping vendors, and excess office space across the four acquired entities; stop unbilled custom services and enforce contract price indexation.
4. **Freeze and redeploy instead of cutting juniors:** Freeze external hiring in overlapping G&A. Train and redeploy willing juniors into customer onboarding and L1/L2 support bottlenecks—cutting them only pushes routine tickets onto expensive senior engineers.
5. **Review contractors selectively:** Separate non-core contractors (wind down with fair notice and proper handover) from critical integration specialists (retain until internal staff are trained to take over).
6. **Sequence the brand:** Use a low-cost *"Part of [Group Name]"* endorsement where useful for cross-selling, and defer a full rebrand until NPS recovers above +20.

### German Legal & People Guardrails (Verify with Employment Counsel)
- **Works councils (*Betriebsrat*):** Confirm which of the four entities have one. Any major operational change requires early negotiation of an *Interessenausgleich* and *Sozialplan* (§§111–112 BetrVG), and any analytics or AI tool that can monitor performance requires co-determination (§87(1) no. 6 BetrVG).
- **Dismissal protection:** If structural reductions are ever needed later, German *KSchG* requires *Sozialauswahl* (tenure, age, dependants, disability—not performance rankings) and statutory notice up to seven months (§622 BGB), plus *§17 KSchG* advance notifications above thresholds.
- **Supervisory board:** At ~900 employees in Germany, verify compliance with one-third worker representation (*Drittelbeteiligungsgesetz*, applicable from 500 employees).
