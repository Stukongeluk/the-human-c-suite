### Why the current three-move plan puts the exit at risk

1. **You have a retention and integration problem, not a pure headcount problem.** Three restructures in two years across four acquired companies explain a 25-point NPS drop. A fourth restructure and a Q1 rebrand add disruption and cash burn without fixing a single customer defect.
2. **German law makes headcount cuts slow and expensive upfront.** At ~900 employees, your Supervisory Board (*Aufsichtsrat*) includes one-third worker representation (*DrittelbG*), and any major operational change triggers *Betriebsrat* reconciliation of interests and social plans (**§§111–112 BetrVG**), social selection (**§1(3) KSchG**), and statutory notice up to 7 months (**§622 BGB**). Severance and lost productivity hit Q1–Q2 cash while savings arrive late.
3. **Blanket contractor and junior cuts hit the wrong muscle.** Ending all contractors risks stalling live customer implementations; cutting juniors saves little payroll while emptying the pipeline and pushing routine work onto senior staff.
