### The Core Message to the Board
Private equity exits are priced on **Exit EBITDA × Multiple**. Right now, the biggest threat to your 18-month exit isn’t just cost—it’s that **NPS dropped from +31 to +6** and **churn is rising**. In buyer due diligence, deteriorating Gross/Net Revenue Retention (GRR/NRR) and customer references will compress your multiple far more than short-term headcount cuts can add to EBITDA.

If you execute the three agreed moves as currently scoped, you risk spending cash and burning customer trust to save costs that won't even hit run-rate cleanly before the sale.

### Why the Three Proposed Moves Backfire (and Your Pivot)

1. **Q1 Rebrand of All Four Companies → Pause & Switch to an Endorsed Brand**
   * **The trap:** Rebranding in Q1 costs real cash (contracts, UI, legal, marketing) with zero short-term EBITDA upside. Worse, putting one name over four still-unintegrated products/services when NPS is +6 signals disruption to customers ("Are you sunsetting my system?") and accelerates churn.
   * **The board pivot:** Pause the Q1 big-bang rebrand. Use a low-cost **endorsed brand** (*"[Legacy Name], a [Group] Company"*) for customer stability, and unify the group narrative at the holding/investor level for exit.

2. **Fourth Restructure to Remove Management Layers → Freeze Customer-Facing Orgs; Consolidate G&A Only**
   * **The trap:** Three restructures in two years is likely *why* NPS fell to +6. A fourth broad reorg will freeze decision-making for 3–6 months and trigger regrettable attrition of key account and delivery leads. In Germany, broad layoffs also require *Betriebsrat* (*Interessenausgleich/Sozialplan*) negotiations—taking **6–9+ months** and burning **upfront severance cash** right when you need clean EBITDA and cash conversion.
   * **The board pivot:** Freeze customer-facing and delivery reporting lines for 12 months. Restrict any structural consolidation strictly to **duplicate non-customer-facing G&A** (4× Finance, HR, admin, corporate IT) where payback is under 9 months.

3. **Ending All Contractor & Junior Roles → Replace Blanket Bans with ROI & Pyramid Discipline**
   * **The trap:** Cutting *all* contractors overnight breaks active customer implementations and SLAs—pouring fuel on churn. Cutting *juniors* saves almost no money (lowest salaries in a 900-person base) while stripping out L1/L2 support and delivery capacity, forcing expensive senior engineers to handle routine tickets and hurting both margin and NPS. It also raises a talent-pipeline red flag in buyer diligence.
   * **The board pivot:** Run a **30-day contractor ROI audit**—cut unbilled/bench contractors immediately and renegotiate rates, while protecting or selectively converting critical delivery contractors. **Keep juniors** and push routine support/implementation work down the pyramid to free seniors for high-margin billable and product work.
