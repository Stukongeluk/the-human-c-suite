# 18-Month Value Creation & Exit Readiness Plan
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors  
**Prepared by:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Context:** 900-FTE German Logistics Software & Services Group (4-company roll-up, ~18 months to exit)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & The Exit Equation

Over the past 24 months, we assembled a 900-person logistics software and services group from four acquisitions. With an exit targeted in ~18 months, our mandate is clear: **maximize exit enterprise value**.

In private equity exits, **Enterprise Value = Exit EBITDA × Valuation Multiple**. Right now, our headline operational metrics signal a severe threat to the **multiple**—and to deal certainty:
- **NPS has dropped from +31 to +6** this year.
- **Customer churn is rising.**
- The organization has absorbed **three restructures in two years**.

Before I took the seat, the leadership team aligned on three moves: (1) rebranding all four companies under one new name in Q1, (2) a fourth restructure to remove management layers, and (3) ending all contractor and junior roles.

**My recommendation to the Board is to pivot.** Executing those three moves as currently scoped will **depress exit value**, not increase it. They will trigger further customer defection, consume cash and management bandwidth in German works council (*Betriebsrat*) negotiations, and strip out low-cost delivery capacity—all while failing to fix the actual drivers of margin and retention.

Instead, this plan proposes a disciplined **18-month Value Creation Program** that protects our multiple by stabilizing NPS and churn within 90 days, while expanding **quality, sustainable EBITDA** through pricing, utilization, vendor consolidation, and surgical—rather than blanket—cost actions.

## 3. The 18-Month Value Creation Plan

### Pillar 1: Stop the Bleeding — Retention & NPS Stabilization (Months 1–4)
*Goal: Halt churn acceleration and establish a credible NPS recovery trajectory before buyer due diligence.*

1. **Top-30 At-Risk Account War Room (Week 1):**
   - CEO and commercial leads personally review the top 30 accounts by ARR and every account with red/amber health scores.
   - Assign **one clear executive sponsor** per key account across all four legacy entities—without changing underlying org charts.
2. **Fix the Top 3 NPS Root Causes (Days 1–60):**
   - Rapidly audit support ticket backlogs, missed SLAs, and delayed implementations across the four entities.
   - Reallocate internal capacity (including protected juniors and critical contractors) to clear the support/implementation backlog immediately.
3. **Commercial Continuity Guarantee:**
   - Communicate clearly to customers that their products, contracts, and primary contacts remain stable—eliminating rebrand and sunset anxiety.

---

### Pillar 2: High-Leverage Profit Expansion (Months 1–12)
*Goal: Drive rapid, high-quality EBITDA expansion without breaking delivery or triggering prolonged labor disruption.*

1. **Pricing, Indexation & Scope Hygiene (Fastest EBITDA Lever):**
   - Enforce contractual inflation/indexation clauses across all four entities.
   - Identify and reprice legacy under-priced contracts (especially low-margin managed services/custom support).
   - Stop **unbilled scope creep** in services through strict change-order discipline.
2. **Services Utilization & Delivery Pyramid:**
   - Implement weekly billable utilization tracking across all services teams across the four companies.
   - Push routine support and standard implementation tasks to junior roles and standardized playbooks, freeing senior specialists for high-margin work and productized services.
3. **Non-Headcount Procurement & Vendor Consolidation (No *Betriebsrat* Delay):**
   - Consolidate duplicate software licenses, cloud hosting, CRM/ERP instances, telecoms, and external spend across the four acquired companies.
   - Rationalize overlapping office leases/facilities where leases allow.
4. **Surgical Headcount & Contractor Discipline:**
   - **Contractors:** Immediate 30-day audit—terminate non-billable/bench contractors and low-impact external spend; convert essential long-term contractors only where unit economics improve.
   - **Hiring Freeze:** Immediate freeze on non-billable/non-critical backfills; all new hires require CEO/CFO sign-off tied to contracted revenue.
   - **Targeted G&A Consolidation:** Address genuine corporate duplication (e.g., 4 finance/HR/admin setups) through a single, well-prepared *Interessenausgleich/Sozialplan* process scoped to finish cleanly well ahead of exit diligence.

---

### Pillar 3: Platform Integration & Exit Readiness (Months 9–18)
*Goal: Present buyers with one scalable, high-retention German logistics platform—not four stitched-together companies.*

1. **Unified Financial & Operational Reporting:**
   - Single chart of accounts, unified SaaS/Services KPIs (ARR, GRR, NRR, gross margin by segment, utilization), and a clean **EBITDA bridge** separating one-time integration/severance costs from recurring run-rate earnings.
2. **Cross-Sell Engine:**
   - Incentivize account teams to cross-sell complementary modules/services across the 4-company customer base once NPS stabilizes (higher NRR drives multiple expansion).
3. **Clean Exit Narrative:**
   - Demonstrate **3–4 consecutive quarters of recovering NPS** (target: +6 → +25+), stabilized/improving GRR & NRR, and expanding EBITDA margins.
   - Frame any brand unification as a strategic growth opportunity for the *next* owner—or execute a clean group-level identity once product/service integration is proven.
