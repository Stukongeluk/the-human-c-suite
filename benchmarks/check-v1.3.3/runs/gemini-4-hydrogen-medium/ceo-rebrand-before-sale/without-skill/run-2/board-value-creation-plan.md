# 18-Month Value Creation & Exit Readiness Plan

**Prepared for:** Board of Directors  
**Prepared by:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Context:** 900-person German logistics software & services group (4 acquired entities, 24 months post-formation)  
**Objective:** Maximize exit valuation in ~18 months by expanding sustainable EBITDA **and** protecting the revenue multiple (reversing the NPS drop from +31 to +6 and halting customer churn).

---

## 1. Executive Summary: The Exit Math

Our private equity owners want higher profit ahead of an exit in ~18 months. However, **exit valuation is `EBITDA × Multiple`**, and right now our multiple is at risk:

* **The Warning Signal:** NPS has fallen 25 points (+31 → +6) in 12 months and customer churn is rising. In B2B logistics software and services, buyers in due diligence scrutinize **Gross Retention, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), and Quality of Earnings (QoE)**.
* **The Trap:** If we boost short-term EBITDA by cutting muscle while churn accelerates, buyers will discount the multiple (or walk away), wiping out more enterprise value than the cost savings create.
* **The Mandate:** We have **six quarters**—which means the operational fixes must land in **Q1–Q2** so that our trailing 12-month (LTM) retention and EBITDA run-rate are clean when the sell-side process begins in ~12–14 months.

To achieve this, I am asking the Board to **pivot from three blunt, high-risk moves** currently on the table to a **targeted, two-track plan**: (1) immediate churn and NPS stabilization, and (2) surgical EBITDA expansion that respects German labor realities and protects delivery capacity.

## 3. The Four-Pillar Value Creation Plan

### Pillar 1: Churn & NPS Turnaround (Days 1–90 — Protect the Multiple)
* **Top-25 At-Risk Account War Room:** Assign an executive sponsor (including myself) to each of the 25 largest or highest-risk accounts within 14 days. Conduct direct listening sessions and establish 60-day remediation plans.
* **Root-Cause Audit of the +31 → +6 Drop:** Isolate whether churn is driven by post-M&A account handoffs, support SLA misses, billing confusion across the 4 entities, or product roadmap uncertainty—and fix the top two drivers in Q1.
* **Front-Line Stability Mandate:** Explicitly commit to customer-facing delivery and support teams that their reporting lines and territories will remain stable for the next 12 months.
* **Customer Health Early Warning:** Implement a single weekly churn-risk dashboard across all 4 entities tracking ticket backlog, SLA breaches, usage drops, and upcoming renewals (90/60/30 days out).

### Pillar 2: Surgical Margin & Operating Model Expansion
* **Tiered Contractor Rationalization:**
  * *Category A (Non-billable / redundant):* Exit within 30–60 days.
  * *Category B (Billable or critical legacy/product):* Retain short-term, tie to margin/delivery SLAs, and run structured knowledge transfer to internal staff.
* **Delivery & Services Gross Margin Improvement:**
  * Enforce billable utilization targets across services teams; use junior talent in standardized implementation pods to lower blended delivery cost.
  * Eliminate unbilled scope creep through standardized change-order governance across all 4 legacy companies.
* **Targeted G&A & Overhead Consolidation:**
  * Consolidate back-office functions (finance, legal, IT, procurement, overlapping corporate management) across the 4 entities rather than disrupting front-line customer teams.
  * Rationalize duplicate software/cloud tooling and vendor spend.

### Pillar 3: Commercial & Pricing Acceleration (High-Margin EBITDA)
* **Pricing & Contract Harmonization:** Audit legacy contracts across the 4 acquired companies for underpriced renewals, missing indexation/inflation clauses, and unmonetized modules. Apply disciplined renewal uplifts where value is proven (and after NPS stabilizes in key segments).
* **Cross-Sell Playbook:** Equip account managers with a simple, high-margin cross-sell motion (e.g., selling Entity B's warehouse/transport add-on into Entity A's installed base) rather than chasing expensive, long-cycle net-new logos.
* **Software-First Mix Shift:** Package recurring managed services and software add-ons to increase the recurring revenue share of total revenue—directly lifting both EBITDA margin and exit multiple.

### Pillar 4: German Labor Governance & Key-Talent Retention
* **Proactive Works Council (*Betriebsrat*) Strategy:** Any targeted headcount reduction in Germany requires structured *Interessenausgleich* / *Sozialplan* planning. Factor realistic legal timelines and one-off severance costs into Q1–Q2 cash flow so savings are clean and defensible in QoE.
* **Lock In the Critical ~25–30 Leaders:** Identify the indispensable product architects, delivery leads, and top commercial performers across the 4 entities. Put **exit-aligned retention bonuses** (vesting at transaction close) in place immediately so our core value creators do not leave during the 18-month sprint.
