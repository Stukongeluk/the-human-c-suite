### The Core Message to the Board
With an 18-month exit horizon, our valuation is **`EBITDA × Multiple`**. Right now, our multiple is in danger: NPS has dropped from **+31 to +6** and churn is rising. If we cut costs indiscriminately while our revenue base leaks, buyers in due diligence will hammer our Quality of Earnings (QoE) and Net Revenue Retention (NRR), costing us far more in multiple compression than we save in payroll.

Because a sell-side process typically starts 4–6 months before close, we have **two quarters (Q1–Q2)** to land both our cost savings and our retention turnaround so the trailing 12-month (LTM) numbers are clean by exit.

### How to Reframe the Leadership Team's Three Moves
Instead of rubber-stamping three high-risk, blunt moves, ask the Board to back a **surgical pivot**:

1. **Q1 Full Rebrand → Modify to an Endorsed Brand Architecture**
   * *Why:* Renaming four trusted logistics brands while NPS is at +6 signals instability to German Mittelstand/enterprise customers and burns management bandwidth.
   * *The Pivot:* Keep the product/legacy names in Q1 under an endorsed umbrella (*"[Legacy Brand] — a [Group] company"*), unify the commercial pitch for cross-sell, and defer full renaming until retention stabilizes.
2. **4th Restructure → Stop Broad Reshuffles; Target G&A Only**
   * *Why:* Three restructures in two years are likely *why* NPS collapsed. A fourth broad org reshuffle will trigger key-talent flight and months of German works council (*Betriebsrat*) / *Sozialplan* friction.
   * *The Pivot:* Lock down customer-facing support and delivery teams for 12 months. Consolidate only duplicate G&A and corporate management layers, backed by a ring-fenced severance budget so one-off costs are cleanly excluded in QoE.
3. **Blanket Contractor & Junior Cut → Tiered, ROI-Gated Reset**
   * *Why:* Cutting all contractors risks losing critical legacy EDI/ERP/WMS integration knowledge; cutting all juniors removes your lowest-cost delivery tier and forces expensive seniors onto L1/L2 tickets—hurting margins.
   * *The Pivot:* Exit non-billable/low-ROI contractors within 30–60 days, keep critical-path/billable ones on a 90-day knowledge-transfer plan, and redeploy existing juniors into billable implementation and support pods to raise utilization.
