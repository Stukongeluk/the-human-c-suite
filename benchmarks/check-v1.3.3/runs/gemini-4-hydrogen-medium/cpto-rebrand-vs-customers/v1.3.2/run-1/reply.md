### Your first steps

1. **Pull the commercial evidence before you walk in.** From your CRM, billing, and support tools, quantify the Q1/Q2 renewal ARR and ticket volume tied to slow invoicing, the accounting integration, and missing reports.
2. **Size real team capacity.** Subtract unplanned work, incidents, and on-call load from total engineering hours before promising dates. Do not close the gap with overtime or skipped testing.
3. **Take this to the CEO privately first—before any leadership meeting or all-hands.** Don't litigate whether to rebrand or push back on their authority in a group. Work within their own frame (*"any decision beats no decision"*): show that you are committed to the Q1 rebrand launch, and put two concrete options on the table with the revenue and retention trade-offs made explicit.
4. **Give your teams clarity once the call is made.** Publish what is in Q1, what is deferred to Q2, and why, so teams execute on real priorities rather than absorbing unspoken scope.
