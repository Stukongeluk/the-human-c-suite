# Board Memorandum: 18-Month Value Creation & Exit Readiness Plan

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Resetting Our 18-Month Pre-Exit Plan — Protecting the Multiple While Expanding EBITDA

---

## 1. Executive Summary

We have an 18-month runway to exit. Our objective is to maximize enterprise value (**EBITDA × Exit Multiple**).

Following my initial review of the business, I am recommending that we **do not proceed** with the three initiatives previously discussed by the leadership team—a Q1 full rebrand, a fourth organizational restructure, and a blanket elimination of all contractors and junior roles.

**Why:** Our Net Promoter Score has fallen from **+31 to +6** this year and customer churn is accelerating. In a software and services business, **rising churn destroys exit value faster than cost-cutting can create it**. Any buyer conducting commercial due diligence in 12–18 months will heavily discount our valuation multiple—or walk away—if Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) is deteriorating. All three proposed moves would worsen customer disruption, trigger prolonged German labor-law friction, and jeopardize the exit timeline.

Instead, I am proposing a disciplined **three-phase Value Creation Plan** focused on:
1. **Months 1–4 (Stabilize):** Stop the churn bleed, restore operational stability, and capture surgical, non-disruptive cost savings.
2. **Months 5–12 (Expand Margin & Growth):** Drive sustainable EBITDA growth through pricing discipline, services utilization/margin improvement, selective back-office consolidation, and low-friction cross-sell.
3. **Months 13–18 (Exit Readiness):** Enter the sale process with four consecutive quarters of recovering retention, clean Quality of Earnings (QoE), and a credible DACH logistics platform equity story.

| Proposed Initiative | Impact on 18-Month Exit Value | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Buyers see through cosmetic rebrands if products and service remain fragmented. | **Defer full rebrand.** Adopt a lightweight umbrella endorsement (*"[Legacy Brand] — a [Group] Company"*) for enterprise/M&A positioning. Retain junior delivery capacity to protect margin leverage; institute a hiring freeze for non-billable/non-critical roles. |

## 3. The 18-Month Value Creation Plan

### Phase 1: Stabilize & Stop the Bleeding (Months 1–4)
*Goal: Halt churn acceleration, reverse NPS decline, and bank clean quick-win savings without disrupting customers.*

- **Customer Retention War Room (Immediate):**
  - Segment the customer base by ARR, renewal date, and health score; identify the top 25–30 "red" accounts.
  - Assign direct executive sponsors from the leadership team to every at-risk key account.
  - Diagnose and fix the root causes of the NPS drop (+31 → +6)—specifically support SLA breaches, broken account handoffs from prior restructures, and stalled implementations.
- **Organizational Stability & Talent Retention:**
  - Communicate an immediate moratorium on broad restructures to calm the 900-person workforce.
  - Establish clear single-threaded ownership for each legacy product line and customer segment.
  - Identify the top ~30–40 mission-critical engineers, consultants, and account leads; put targeted exit-linked retention incentives in place.
- **Surgical Cost & Cash Discipline (Zero Customer Disruption):**
  - **Contractor Audit:** Classify all external spend within 14 days. Retain only billable or retention-critical contractors; wind down non-essential external spend over 30–60 days.
  - **Non-Headcount Consolidation:** Consolidate overlapping software licenses, cloud infrastructure, telecoms, and third-party vendors across the four legacy entities.
  - **Hiring Controls:** Freeze all net-new overhead hiring; require CEO/CFO sign-off on any backfill.
- **Brand Positioning:**
  - Cancel the Q1 rebrand spend. Roll out a minimal-cost holding-group endorsement for sales collateral and investor communications.

### Phase 2: Margin Expansion & Commercial Execution (Months 5–12)
*Goal: Deliver durable, buyer-grade EBITDA expansion and prove the multi-product thesis.*

- **Pricing & Commercial Hygiene:**
  - Enforce annual CPI/indexation clauses across all four customer bases at renewal.
  - Standardize rate cards for professional services and eliminate unbilled scope creep/custom work.
  - Introduce structured packaging tiers for core software modules to drive Net Revenue Retention (NRR).
- **Services Gross Margin & Delivery Leverage:**
  - Improve billable utilization across consulting and implementation teams (target +5–8 percentage points).
  - Protect and optimize the delivery pyramid—using junior and mid-level consultants for repeatable implementation/support tasks so senior specialists focus on high-margin work and complex accounts.
- **Targeted, Low-Friction Cross-Sell:**
  - Identify the 2–3 highest-fit cross-sell pairings across the four portfolios (e.g., TMS/WMS/telematics/analytics adjacencies).
  - Incentivize trusted legacy account managers to make warm introductions rather than forcing a disruptive sales-force merger.
- **Quiet Back-Office Unification (Data Room Readiness):**
  - Standardize financial reporting, KPI definitions (ARR, GRR, NRR, CAC, Services Margin), and CRM pipeline hygiene across all four entities so QoE and commercial due diligence are seamless.

### Phase 3: Exit Readiness & Equity Story (Months 13–18)
*Goal: Enter the market from a position of strength with verifiable momentum.*

- **Demonstrated Turnaround Metrics:**
  - Present 4+ consecutive quarters of improving NPS (target: back to **+25 or above**), stabilized GRR, and rising NRR.
  - Show clean, recurring EBITDA growth driven by pricing, utilization, and vendor consolidation—minimizing contested "one-time restructuring add-backs" in QoE.
- **Compelling Buyer Narrative:**
  - Frame the group as a resilient, high-retention DACH logistics software & services platform with unified financial visibility, proven cross-sell traction, and clear Phase 2 upside (full brand and deep platform unification) available to the next owner.
- **Vendor Due Diligence (VDD) Prep:**
  - Begin financial and commercial VDD preparation at Month 12 to proactively address the historical NPS dip as a resolved post-merger integration chapter.
