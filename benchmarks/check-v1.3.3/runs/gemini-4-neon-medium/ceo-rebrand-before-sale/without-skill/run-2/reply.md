### 1. Frame the Board Conversation Around PE Exit Math, Not Soft Sentiment

PE boards do not respond to "the team is tired." They respond to **Enterprise Value = Exit LTM EBITDA × Exit Multiple**—and the fact that with an 18-month exit clock, **your Exit LTM period starts in roughly 6 months**.

Right now, your NPS collapse (+31 → +6) and rising churn are a valuation emergency. In Commercial Due Diligence (CDD), buyers will call your customers, inspect cohort GRR/NRR, and discount the multiple heavily—or walk away—if the business looks like a hollowed-out roll-up.

Here is why each of the three proposed moves destroys exit value on an 18-month timeline:

* **Q1 single-name rebrand:** Q1 is weeks away. Rushing a rebrand of four acquired companies burns €0.5M–€2M+ in cash and management bandwidth, destroys trusted brand equity in a conservative German logistics market, and signals to frustrated customers that you care more about logos than fixing their service. **Move:** Cancel it. Keep legacy brands through exit, use a light group endorsement on investor/cross-sell decks, and sell "future brand unification" as upside to the next owner.
* **Fourth restructure to remove layers:** In a 900-person German group, a broad restructure means *Betriebsrat* negotiations, *Interessenausgleich/Sozialplan* (§111–112 BetrVG), *Sozialauswahl*, heavy upfront severance cash, and 6–12 months of internal paralysis. You won't get clean run-rate EBITDA inside your LTM window—you'll get messy add-backs, fleeing top performers, and worse delivery. **Move:** Declare an 18-month moratorium on broad restructures. Fix clear senior duplication quietly and surgically via individual *Aufhebungsverträge* without triggering a *Betriebsänderung*.
* **Ending all contractor and junior roles:** Blanket contractor cuts will break live customer implementations, legacy integrations, and SLAs overnight—pouring gasoline on churn. Cutting juniors removes your lowest-cost delivery capacity, forcing expensive senior staff onto L1 support and routine work (which *reduces* services gross margin and drives senior attrition). **Move:** Audit contractors by revenue/SLA criticality—cut non-delivery corporate contractors immediately, keep/convert delivery-critical ones (while quietly cleaning up any *Scheinselbstständigkeit* risk), and **protect** your junior delivery pyramid.
