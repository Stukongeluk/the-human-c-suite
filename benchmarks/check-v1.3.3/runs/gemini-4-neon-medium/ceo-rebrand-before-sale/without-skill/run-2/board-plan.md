# Board Memorandum: 18-Month Value Creation & Exit Readiness Plan

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Re-sequencing Our 18-Month Pre-Exit Plan to Protect Valuation and Deliver Sustainable EBITDA Growth

---

## 1. Executive Summary

With an anticipated exit window in approximately **18 months**, our mandate is clear: maximize enterprise value at exit. In a B2B logistics software and services business, exit value is a function of **Exit LTM EBITDA × Exit Multiple**.

Our current trajectory puts both variables at severe risk. Over the past 12 months, **NPS has fallen from +31 to +6** and **customer churn is accelerating**. After two years of integrating four acquired companies—and three restructures—the organization is experiencing acute change fatigue, and that internal friction is now visibly hitting customers.

Prior to my arrival, the leadership team aligned around three initiatives intended to lift short-term profitability:
1. Rebranding all four legacy companies under a single new name in Q1.
2. Executing a fourth restructure to remove management layers.
3. Ending all contractor and junior roles.

**My recommendation to the Board is to pause and replace these three moves.** On an 18-month clock, executing them as proposed will destroy more enterprise value than they create: they will accelerate customer churn, trigger significant upfront cash and German labor-law friction, and damage the exact metrics (GRR, NRR, customer references, and EBITDA quality) that buyers will scrutinize in Commercial and Financial Due Diligence (CDD/FDD).

Instead, I propose a disciplined **18-Month Value Creation Plan** focused on four pillars:
1. **Stop the Bleeding:** Stabilize customer retention and recover NPS to +20+ before diligence begins.
2. **High-Quality EBITDA Expansion:** Capture clean, defensible margin gains via pricing/contract hygiene, services utilization, non-delivery cost rationalization, and cross-sell—without starving customer delivery.
3. **Organizational Stability & Unified Governance:** Declare a moratorium on broad restructures, fix accountability surgically, and unify financial/commercial reporting across all four entities.
4. **Exit Readiness:** Enter the LTM exit window (starting in ~6 months) with clean numbers, recovering customer sentiment, and a credible platform growth story.

## 3. The 18-Month Value Creation Plan

### Pillar 1: Commercial Stabilization & NPS Recovery (Months 1–6)
*Objective: Halt churn acceleration immediately and rebuild customer trust ahead of CDD.*

- **30-Day Customer Save War Room:** Identify the top 40–50 at-risk and highest-ARR accounts across all four legacy businesses. I will personally lead executive outreach alongside legacy account leads to listen, own past friction, and agree concrete 90-day remediation plans.
- **Root-Cause Remediation:** Triage the drivers of the +31 → +6 NPS drop (typically support response times, broken SLAs, implementation backlogs, and account-manager turnover) and ring-fence delivery capacity to fix the top 3 operational pain points within 90 days.
- **Account Continuity Guarantee:** Freeze disruptive account reassignments and communicate clearly to customers that their product, brand, and day-to-day contacts are stable.
- **Closed-Loop Retention Cadence:** Introduce weekly churn-risk reviews (red/amber/green health scoring) and quarterly pulse NPS tracking by legacy entity and product line.

### Pillar 2: High-Quality EBITDA Expansion (Months 1–12)
*Objective: Deliver sustainable, buyer-credible EBITDA growth without impairing delivery or ARR.*

1. **Immediate OPEX Avoidance (Quick Win):**
   - Cancel Q1 rebrand spend and reallocate management bandwidth to retention and margin execution.
   - Terminate non-billable, non-critical external contractors (advisory, corporate IT, low-ROI agencies) within 60 days.
2. **Vendor & Overhead Consolidation Across the 4 Entities:**
   - Consolidate overlapping SaaS licenses, cloud infrastructure, telecom, and third-party vendor contracts across the four companies.
   - Standardize procurement approval thresholds and freeze non-essential G&A hiring.
3. **Commercial & Pricing Levers (Highest-Multiple Impact):**
   - **Contract & Pricing Hygiene:** Audit legacy contracts for unexercised CPI/indexation clauses, underpriced maintenance tiers, and unbilled services scope creep. Implement disciplined rate-card enforcement and change-order governance on services engagements.
   - **Services Gross Margin:** Protect and leverage the junior-to-senior delivery pyramid; track billable utilization weekly by team; standardize repeatable implementation playbooks across the four entities.
4. **Targeted, Low-CAC Cross-Sell:**
   - Map customer overlap across the four legacy bases (e.g., TMS, WMS, customs/freight, analytics, managed services).
   - Launch focused cross-sell plays *only* into healthy/stabilized accounts (NPS promoters/passives) to lift Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and prove commercial synergy to future buyers.

### Pillar 3: Organizational Stability & Quiet Governance (Months 1–12)
*Objective: End change fatigue while establishing single-company financial and operational discipline.*

- **No 4th Restructure:** Publicly commit to a stable operating structure through exit so our 900 employees can stop looking inward and focus on customers.
- **Clear Single-Threaded Accountability:** Assign unambiguous P&L, retention, and delivery ownership for each of the four legacy lines, supported by lean central functions (Finance, Commercial Ops, HR/Legal).
- **Targeted Senior Streamlining Only:** Where true management duplication exists at senior levels, resolve quietly and individually (mutual agreements / natural attrition) rather than through disruptive org-wide layers cuts.
- **Unified Data & Reporting Spine:** Harmonize ERP/financial reporting, CRM pipeline/churn tracking, and KPI definitions across all four entities by Month 6—critical for both daily management and FDD/CDD readiness.
- **Contractor Compliance Clean-Up:** Conduct a targeted review of long-term German freelance contractors to mitigate *Scheinselbstständigkeit* exposure prior to legal due diligence.

### Pillar 4: Exit Readiness & Equity Story (Months 9–18)
*Objective: Enter the sale process with clean LTM financials, recovering customer proof points, and a compelling growth narrative.*

- **Protect the LTM Window (Months 6–18):** Every major cost and commercial lever is front-loaded into Months 1–6 so that the LTM exit period reflects clean, run-rate EBITDA with minimal extraordinary add-backs.
- **Build the Buyer Equity Story:** Position the group not as a disrupted roll-up, but as a **resilient, high-margin DACH logistics software & services platform** with:
  - Stabilized, expanding GRR/NRR and recovering NPS (+20+).
  - Demonstrable cross-sell momentum across a sticky logistics customer base.
  - Unified financial/commercial infrastructure and strong cash conversion.
  - Clear, unharvested upside for the next owner (future brand unification, deeper product-stack integration, and international expansion).
- **Pre-Diligence Preparation (Months 10–14):** Run mock CDD (customer reference readiness) and FDD (cohort ARR bridge, recurring vs. services margin split, normalized EBITDA bridge) to eliminate surprises before opening the data room.
