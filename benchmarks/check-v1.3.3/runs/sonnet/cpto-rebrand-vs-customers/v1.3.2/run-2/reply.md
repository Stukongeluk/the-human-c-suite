Don't plan Q1 as "rebrand or fix customers". Plan it as the rebrand's minimum visible layer plus a protected team on the three complaints, and take that to the CEO privately before it goes anywhere wider. NPS falling from +28 to +4 while the same three complaints sit open for months is the strongest number you have.

**Take the CEO's own line seriously.** "Any decision beats no decision" is right, so bring a decision, not an objection. Decide fast on what is reversible (the design system can roll out in phases). Go slower on what isn't (a domain move touches email deliverability, SEO, SSO and integration callbacks).

**Three options to put in front of leadership**

1. **Everything on the rebrand (current plan).** Name, design system, domain and site all land in Q1. The three complaints stay open another quarter.
2. **Split capacity (my recommendation).** Ship the part customers and the merger story need: new name in the app, domain with redirects, marketing site. Move the full design-system migration to Q2, applied screen by screen. Fix invoicing speed, the accounting integration and the top missing reports in a dedicated, named team with outcome targets, for example invoice load time and integration error rate.
3. **Fix first.** Customer fixes in Q1, rebrand in Q2. Cleanest for customers, but it only works if there is no hard date forcing the rebrand.

**Before the meeting (about a week)**
- Capacity: per team, headcount minus leave, unplanned work and on-call. The rebrand estimate must come from the people doing it, not from a date. If it only fits with overtime, say so.
- Customer evidence: churned and at-risk accounts tagged with these three issues, their ARR, and support ticket volume. This is the case, not opinion.
- Dependencies: whether the accounting integration or invoicing share code or auth with the domain and name change. If they do, sequence them together.
- One question for the CEO: is there a hard external date for the rebrand (contract, legal, event)? That decides between options 2 and 3.

**A line you can use:** "I'm committed to the rebrand. I'm asking us to also commit to one number on the three complaints this quarter, because at +4 NPS the rebrand will launch to unhappy customers. Here are three ways to fit both."

Write down what is out of Q1 and why, plan teams with some slack, and measure outcomes per team, never per person.

**Assumptions:** the three complaints are the main driver of the NPS drop, and the same teams are needed for both. If your data shows the drop comes mostly from the merger itself, or the rebrand has a legal deadline, option 1 or 3 may be better.
