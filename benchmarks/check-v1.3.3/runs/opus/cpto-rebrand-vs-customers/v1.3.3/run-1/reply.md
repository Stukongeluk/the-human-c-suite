Don't plan Q1 as a 100% rebrand. Take the CEO a sized, phased rebrand alongside fixes for the three complaints behind a 24-point NPS drop (+28 to +4), as options leadership can choose between.

The rebrand has a real case: after a merger, two names confuse customers and sales. The trap is "any decision beats no decision" applied to a whole organisation's quarter: quick to decide, slow to undo. Customers who leave over invoicing won't come back for a new website. Leadership decides, but the teams know what each option costs.

**This week**

1. **Put money on the complaints.** From support, CRM and the NPS survey: tickets and their age per issue, ARR of affected accounts, renewals due in Q1–Q2, detractor comments per issue. Revenue at risk moves a CEO more than NPS does.
2. **Split the rebrand.** Must-have: the name in the app, the new domain with redirects, the marketing site. Later: the design system everywhere, starting with new and touched screens. The domain move touches OAuth redirect URIs and webhooks, the same surface as the broken accounting integration, so plan those together.
3. **Ask the teams to estimate both** from their past throughput, leaving room for incidents. No dates before that.
4. **Write one page, three options.** A: everyone on the rebrand. B: a dedicated team on the must-have rebrand, everyone else on the three fixes. C: fixes first, rebrand in Q2. For each: capacity, revenue at risk and what slips. Recommend B.

**Then see the CEO privately, before any wider meeting** (your own manager first, if that's someone else)

Ask for the reasons first: what problem does the rebrand solve, by when, and what would change their mind? Then:

> "I agree we should decide fast, and I'm not asking to undo the rebrand, only to size it. If all of product and engineering is on it in Q1, the three issues behind our NPS drop stay open three more months, with X in renewals due in that window. Here are three options with the teams' estimates. I'd pick B. Which would you choose?"

**After the decision**

- Agree measures up front and review monthly: tickets per issue, time to invoice, NPS, renewals, rebrand milestones.
- Show your teams the options before the decision, and give customer-facing teams honest dates for the fixes.
- If the answer is still all-in, commit, but record the trade-off in a short decision record with a review date tied to renewals.
