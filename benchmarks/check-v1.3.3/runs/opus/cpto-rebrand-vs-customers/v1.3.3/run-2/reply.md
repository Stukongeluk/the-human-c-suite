Don't plan Q1 as a rebrand quarter. Plan it as a customer-recovery quarter with the must-have part of the rebrand inside it, and take that to the CEO as a choice between costed options, not as a refusal. NPS has fallen 24 points (+28 to +4) while your top three complaints stay open, and a new name won't move that number.

**This week and next**

1. **Size the complaints.** For slow invoicing, the accounting integration and the missing reports: accounts and ARR affected, and how much of it renews by mid-year. Sources: support tickets by category, CRM renewals, NPS comments.
2. **Ask the teams to estimate both pieces of work** against real capacity (leave, on-call, the unplanned work they already carry), with no plan that only fits with overtime. Ask for the smallest version of each fix that customers would notice. Estimating isn't deciding; it gives leadership better inputs.
3. **Split the rebrand.** Must-have in Q1: the name in the app, emails and legal pages, and the domain with redirects. Check now what the domain move touches: SSO, OAuth callbacks, email sending, possibly the accounting integration itself. Phase the rest: roll out the design system screen by screen, starting with invoicing and the new reports, so the fixes become the first showcase of the new brand. The marketing site can largely run outside product engineering.

**Then talk to the CEO privately, before any leadership meeting.** One page, three options, each with accounts and ARR affected, effort and dates:

- A: full rebrand in Q1; the complaints wait until April at the earliest.
- B (recommended): must-have rebrand plus the three fixes.
- C: fixes first, the whole rebrand in Q2.

The CEO has a real point: after a merger, two names confuse customers, sales and staff. Option B still delivers one name in Q1.

A line you can use: *"I'm not asking to reopen the rebrand. I'm asking you to choose between costed plans, and I'll run whichever you pick. 'Any decision beats no decision' holds for things we can undo; a domain move and parking every customer issue for a quarter are hard to undo, so give me two weeks of team estimates before we lock them."*

**Agree the measures now and review them monthly:** NPS and its comments on the three topics, tickets per topic, invoicing time, integration errors, renewals in affected accounts, and a short pulse on team load. Meanwhile tell your teams what's decided (the name changes) and what's still open (how Q1 is split).
