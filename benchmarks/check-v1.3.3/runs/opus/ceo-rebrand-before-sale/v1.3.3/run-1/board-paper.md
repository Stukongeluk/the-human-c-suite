# Board paper: Raising profit and sale value, starting with our customers

**Prepared by:** CEO · **Prepared:** 20 November 2026 · **For:** Board meeting, [date]
**Status:** Draft for discussion. Measures that affect employees are proposals, subject to consultation with the works councils.

---

## 1. Summary

**Recommendation.** We raise profit and sale value over the next 18 months by winning customers back first, taking out the costs our four companies still duplicate, and changing the organisation once, designed with employee representatives. I propose changing the three moves the leadership team agreed:

- **The rebrand** becomes a decision on the name now and a full migration later, once customers have recovered.
- **The restructure** becomes a single, designed change. We decide it by the end of June 2027, finish it by the end of October 2027, and make no further structural change before the sale.
- **Ending all contractor and junior roles** becomes a review of each contractor engagement on its merits. Junior roles stay.

**Why.**

- Our NPS fell from +31 to +6 this year and customer churn is rising. A buyer will see both in due diligence, in the retention cohorts and in the customer interviews.
- At a contribution margin of 70% (assumed; finance to confirm), €1.43m of lost recurring revenue cancels out €1m of cost savings. A saving that costs us customers lowers the sale price instead of raising it (section 7).
- Savings from dismissals in Germany arrive late. Consultation, notice periods of up to seven months and social plan costs mean a restructure started in Q1 2027 delivers most of its saving in 2028. Its costs and disruption fall in 2027, the year a buyer will look at most closely.

**Responsibility.** Customer satisfaction fell on leadership's watch. That is a leadership result, not a failure of our teams, and it is now mine to fix. Leadership pay will reflect that before anyone else's job does (section 8).

**Decisions requested today** (section 11): the direction and sequence, the revised scope of the three moves, the leadership pay measures, and the scorecard with its gates. In January 2027 I will bring back the fully sized profit bridge and the results of the customer diagnosis.

## 2. The problem

**In one sentence:** the owners need higher profit and a stronger business to sell in about 18 months (around mid-2028), and customers are leaving faster at the same time. Any plan that buys profit with customer goodwill will lower the price.

**What a buyer will examine:**

- retention cohorts and net revenue retention
- the NPS trend, plus their own customer interviews
- quality of earnings: one-off restructuring costs and savings not yet realised are discounted
- key-person risk and the depth of the bench
- employment liabilities: social plans, open disputes, the status of contractors

McKinsey analysed B2B software companies from 2019 to 2024. The top quarter by valuation had net revenue retention of 113%, against 98% for the bottom quarter, and a median enterprise value of 24 times revenue, against 5 times. This is a correlation in US software and our group is partly services, so the scale won't transfer. The direction is what buyers pay for.

**Is this a cost problem?** Partly. Four acquisitions in two years usually leave duplicated systems, vendors, offices and management, and we should remove that duplication. But the sharpest change this year is on the revenue side. If the group is profitable and cash-positive, as I expect (finance to confirm), this is a plan to raise value before a sale, not a rescue. In that case, cuts that put retention at risk work against the owners' own goal.

**Ground rules:**

- **Customers' contacts stay.** No top-50 account changes its named contact without a handover.
- **Technical identifiers stay.** In 2027 nothing changes in product names, API endpoints, EDI identifiers, single sign-on, invoicing entities, the domains invoices come from, or bank details.
- **Vacancies before exits.** At 8–12% voluntary attrition (an estimate; HR to confirm), about 72–108 people leave each year. If we don't refill one in three of those roles outside customer-facing work, that is 24–36 fewer roles a year without a single dismissal.
- **Support from day one.** If roles end, internal roles are offered first. Coaching, paid training and job-search support start on the day notice is given.

| Retention | Renewals due in 2027 × improvement in retention × contribution margin | Through 2027 | [finance] | [finance] | CCO |

| Vacancies not refilled | Leavers × share not refilled × average cost | From Q1 2027, building up | [finance] | [finance] | CHRO |
| Organisation change (if the case holds) | Roles removed, net of redeployment | From Q4 2027, in full in 2028 | [finance] | [finance] | CEO |
| **Less one-off costs** | Social plan and voluntary terms, outplacement and training, contractor conversions, brand clearance, customer recovery | 2027 | [finance] | – | CFO |

**Sale value arithmetic.** Each €1m of permanent cost saved adds €1m of EBITDA. Each €1m of recurring revenue lost removes EBITDA equal to its contribution margin (the revenue minus the costs that leave with the customer). This table shows the break-even:

| Contribution margin on lost revenue | EBITDA lost per €1m of revenue lost | Lost revenue that cancels €1m of savings |
|---|---|---|
| 50% (services-heavy) | €0.50m | €2.00m |
| 60% | €0.60m | €1.67m |
| 70% | €0.70m | €1.43m |
| 80% (software-heavy) | €0.80m | €1.25m |

Each point of gross revenue retention on recurring revenue R is worth 1% × R × the contribution margin in EBITDA. Multiply that by the buyer's multiple to get its effect on the price. This understates the effect, because buyers also tend to pay a lower multiple for weaker retention.

- **Responsibility.** The fall in customer satisfaction is a leadership result. I say so here, and I will say it the same way to our people.
- **2027 bonuses** for the leadership team, including mine:
  - at least half weighted on gross revenue retention and NPS, the rest on EBITDA
  - no payout on the retention part if retention falls below its 2026 level
- **Perks** for the leadership team are frozen from 1 January 2027.
- **Pay cut before any compulsory notice.** Before the first compulsory notice is given, the leadership team takes a 12-month cut in base pay: 20% for the CEO and 10% for the other members. This is for the remuneration committee to finalise.
- **Purpose.** These measures are a signal of who carries the cost first, not a savings line, and they don't replace the plan.

  - NPS no lower than the Q1 2027 reading

## Annex B. Law and consultation in Germany

This needs employment counsel before anything is implemented, and it is not legal advice.

- **Works councils.** We need to confirm which companies and sites have a works council, and whether there is a company-wide or group-level works council. Where they exist:
  - **Economic committee.** In companies with more than 100 employees, the economic committee must be informed in good time about rationalisation projects, changes to the organisation, mergers and a takeover involving control (§106 BetrVG). Counsel should advise on what the sale process means for this.
  - **Reconciliation of interests and social plan.** A change to the organisation of this size is likely to count as an operational change (§111 BetrVG). That requires attempting a reconciliation of interests and negotiating a social plan before implementation (§112). Implementing without the attempt exposes the company to compensation claims from the employees affected (§113).
  - **Individual dismissals.** The works council must be consulted before each dismissal (§102 BetrVG).
  - **Monitoring tools.** Tools suited to monitoring behaviour or performance need the works council's agreement (§87(1) no. 6).
- **Collective dismissals.** Above set thresholds within 30 days, we must notify the employment agency before the dismissals, for example more than 25 people (or 10% of staff) in an establishment of 60–499, or at least 30 in one of 500 or more. Termination agreements initiated by the employer count towards the thresholds, and errors can make dismissals void (§17 KSchG).
- **Social selection.** We cannot select "juniors" or "managers" as a group. Where roles go, selection among comparable employees weighs length of service, age, maintenance obligations and severe disability, not performance rankings (§1(3) KSchG). A rule aimed at "junior roles" also needs checking under the General Equal Treatment Act (AGG), because seniority often correlates with age.
- **Notice periods.** Statutory notice grows with length of service, up to seven months (§622 BGB), so savings arrive later than headcount plans suggest.
- **Apprentices and dual-study students.** After probation, the employer can end a training contract only for good cause (§22 BBiG).
- **Freelancers.** Long-running freelancers who work inside our teams may be reclassified as employees. The company then owes contributions for up to four years back, or up to 30 years where the breach was intentional, including the employee's share. About 40% of audited cases are classified as dependent employment (EY, May 2026).
- **Agency workers.** The same agency worker may be assigned to us for at most 18 consecutive months, unless a collective agreement sets a different limit. Above it, an employment contract with us is deemed to exist (AÜG).
- **Merging legal entities** comes with its own information duties towards the works councils. We are keeping it out of the 2027 brand work.

**Rules:**

- **Timing.** No announcements in the two weeks before Christmas or on a Friday afternoon.
- **Channel.** Nothing by mass email, and no login that simply stops working.

People accept decisions that go against them far better when the process was fair: they were engaged, the reasoning was explained and the expectations were clear (Kim and Mauborgne, 1997).
