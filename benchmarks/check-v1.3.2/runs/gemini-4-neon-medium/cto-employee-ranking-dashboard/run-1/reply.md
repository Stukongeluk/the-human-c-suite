### What to do next

1. **Talk to the VP privately first** (or ask your manager to raise it quietly). Keep it factual and about the system, not their motives:
   - **Why the metrics backfire:** Ranking by PRs merged and Copilot acceptance rate rewards tiny, noisy PRs and blindly accepting AI suggestions, while punishing the engineers who review code, mentor, handle incidents, and delete complexity.
   - **Why it is a legal tripwire in Rotterdam:** Using GitHub and Copilot data to monitor and rank individual performance normally requires **OR consent** (*instemmingsrecht*) under **Article 27(1)(l) WOR**, and raises GDPR proportionality issues. PIPs built on raw activity counts also sit poorly with Dutch employment law. (The EU AI Act's duty to inform worker representatives about high-risk workplace monitoring doesn't apply until **2 December 2027**, so it's Dutch WOR and GDPR that bite today. *Not legal advice—suggest they check with HR and legal.*)
2. **Bring an alternative, not a flat no.** Propose team-level metrics that answer their real question: change lead time, review wait time, change fail rate, and aggregated team-level trends before and after Copilot. Real individual performance concerns belong with managers looking at actual work and peer feedback.
3. **If they push ahead anyway, use safe channels—not Slack.** Flag the WOR risk quietly to your manager or HR (neither wants an unlawful monitoring rollout), or take it to the **OR**, whose consent right exists for exactly this. Keep only records you're allowed to keep, and weigh your own risk honestly: speaking up is brave, not an obligation to put your job on the line.
