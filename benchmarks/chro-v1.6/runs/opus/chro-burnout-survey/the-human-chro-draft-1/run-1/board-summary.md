The WHO describes burnout as the result of chronic workplace stress that has not been managed (WHO, 2019). In a study of 46,336 UK workers, people who took part in individual programmes such as resilience training were no better off than people who didn't (Fleming, *Industrial Relations Journal*, 2024). So we start with the work itself and offer individual support alongside it.
