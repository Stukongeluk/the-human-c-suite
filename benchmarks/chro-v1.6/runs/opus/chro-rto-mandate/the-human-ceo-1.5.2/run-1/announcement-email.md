Hi everyone,

As I said in today's all-hands, we want to change how much time we spend together in our offices. I'm writing it down so everyone has the same information, including what is decided and what isn't.

First, what this email does not do: nothing changes today, and nobody's employment contract changes because of this email.

**Why we want to change something**

Over the past [period] we have seen [the real evidence, for example: our engagement score on "I feel connected to colleagues outside my team" fell from X% to Y%; new colleagues tell us it takes them N months to find their way; collaboration between Utrecht and Berlin keeps getting stuck on Z].

That is on the leadership team, not on how any of you have chosen to work. We grew across two cities and many home offices without investing enough in how we onboard people, share what we know and decide together. Fixing that is our job.

**What we're proposing**

The leadership team's starting proposal is that everyone works from our Utrecht or Berlin office five days a week. I'll be open that this is where I started. Before we decide, we will look seriously at other ways to fix the same problems, for example:

- fixed team days in the office each week;
- in-office onboarding for new colleagues during their first months;
- regular on-site weeks for colleagues who live far from an office, and more time together between Utrecht and Berlin.

We'll choose what best fixes the problems above at the lowest cost to your lives, and we'll show you how we weighed it.

**What's decided and what's open**

- Decided: we will change something, and we will do it with you.
- Open: how many days, for whom, from when, and with which exceptions.

**How we'll decide, and when**

- **Works councils.** In Utrecht, a change to our home-working policy needs the consent of our ondernemingsraad. We sent it a formal request on [date]. In Berlin, we have informed the Betriebsrat and will agree the details with it. Both see the same data you do.
- **Your input.** Until [15 January 2027] through [team sessions in both offices with HR, and an anonymous form at link]. Tell us what works now, what doesn't, and what a change would mean for you.
- **Decision.** By [end of February 2027], with our reasons: what we changed because of your input, what we didn't, and why.
- **Time to adjust.** Nothing changes before [date]. Any change starts at least [three] months after the decision, and later for people for whom it is a bigger change.

**If you joined us as a remote or hybrid colleague**

About a third of you joined on that basis, and we agreed to it. Your manager will talk with you personally by [date]. Where your employment contract or a written agreement says you work remotely, that stays as it is unless you and we agree otherwise.

**If a change would cause you serious problems**

Care duties, health, a long commute: talk to your manager or HR. There will be a hardship arrangement, and you won't have to explain your private life in detail to use it.

**What the leadership team will do first**

From [January], the leadership team will be in the office at least as much as we may ask of anyone, hold our meetings there and be easy to find. We'll also fix what makes the office less useful today, starting with [desks on busy days, meeting rooms, quiet space and the video set-up between Utrecht and Berlin]. We'll look at office use per office and floor to plan space, not at who is in on which day.
