Don't take the five-year, say-nothing plan forward. Correct the same-role gap within 12 months, and tell the works councils now.

**Why the CFO's plan costs more than it saves**
- Equal pay for equal work is already law (Article 157 TFEU). A 6% gap in the same role and grade is an exposure today.
- Spreading the fix over five years doesn't remove the cost; it keeps underpaying people who can claim it back. Dutch back-pay claims reach back five years (art. 3:308 BW). In Belgium a discrimination claim can carry a six-month lump sum.
- Under the Pay Transparency Directive, an unexplained gap of 5% or more that isn't fixed within six months triggers a joint pay assessment with representatives. Yours is above that line.
- Silence may not be available. The Dutch works council has consent rights on the pay system, and Belgian employers must give the works council a pay analysis by sex every two years. Staff will soon be able to ask for pay averages by sex for their role.

**What I'd do**
1. **Confirm the figures (30 days).** Re-run the analysis in code, with group sizes, and split the 6% into what objective factors explain and what they don't. Only the unexplained part gets corrected, so it may be smaller. The 14% usually comes from who sits in which grade, part-time work and starting pay; look at it separately.
2. **Get counsel in both countries** before anything goes to staff.
3. **Brief both works councils with the real numbers** while the plan can still change.
4. **Correct in the next pay round**, or off-cycle if that is over six months away. Raise the underpaid; don't freeze anyone else or ask for waivers.
5. **Fix the causes:** published ranges per grade, written rules for starting pay and promotion.
6. **Tell everyone on the same day**, saying plainly that the gap comes from our own pay decisions and that leadership will correct it.

**What to tell the CFO**
Open with the budget: the cost is real but bounded (the unexplained differences plus charges), and a slow plan adds legal risk and attrition without reducing it. Offer what does protect cash: two steps inside 12 months, leadership bonuses adjusted first, a budget line agreed once the figures are confirmed. Be direct: confidentiality isn't ours to promise, because representatives and soon employees have a right to this. Ask for one thing: agreement to confirm the numbers in 30 days. If the unexplained part is 2%, one round clears it.

Before you talk to the CFO privately, check who has already seen the analysis, and which legal entities we have in each country, since each entity reports for itself.
