# Announcement email (draft for the CEO to sign)

Send only after the works councils have been consulted, managers have been briefed, and counsel has checked the paragraph on contracts. Items in [brackets] need a real fact before it goes out. Have a German version translated by a person, not a tool.

**To:** All employees (Utrecht and Berlin)
**From:** [CEO name]
**Subject:** Where we work from 1 March, and how we get there together

Hi all,

I want to tell you about a change to how we work, what it is for, and what is still open. You are hearing it from me first, and your manager will talk it through with your team this week.

**What we are deciding.** From 1 March, our default is that we work from the office, five days a week. We think we need to be together more than we are today.

**Why.** I know "culture" can sound vague, so here is what I mean. [Name two or three specific things, with a fact each. For example: new colleagues take longer than they should to feel part of a team; product and engineering handoffs between Utrecht and Berlin are slower than they need to be; people tell us they do not know colleagues outside their own team.] I believe being in the same room helps with these. I also know that other things help too, and that not everyone will agree with me. [One sentence on what we will measure to see whether it works.]

**What this means for you.**
- About a third of you were hired as remote or hybrid, and in some cases that is written in your contract or offer letter. This email does not change any contract. If your agreement says something different from the new default, your manager and HR will talk to you personally before 1 March, and we will not change it without your agreement or a proper basis to do so.
- If distance, care for a family member, health, or a disability makes five days hard, tell your manager or HR. We will look at each situation, one by one, and a request will get a written answer from us.
- [If true: Teams will agree with their manager which days matter most for their work, and I want to hear where five days does not make sense.]

**What we are still working out, and what you can shape.**
- Our works councils [and the Betriebsrat in Berlin, if there is one] are being consulted now, and what they tell us can change the plan. [If true: The date of 1 March and the way we handle exceptions are the parts I am most open to change.]
- Until [date], your team can tell your manager what would make this work, or not work, for you. [Name]'s team will collect this and I will share a summary with everyone by [date].
- We will review this on [date, about three months after 1 March], looking at [the measures above], how many people have left and why, and what you tell us.

**What happens next.**
- Today: your manager will tell you what this means for your team.
- This week: HR will contact everyone whose contract or agreement says remote or hybrid. If you have not heard from us by [date], write to [HR contact].
- [Date]: open Q&A with me and [HR director name], in Utrecht and Berlin, and online for anyone who cannot attend.

I understand that some of you will be disappointed, and some will be worried about your own situation. That is a reasonable reaction. Please ask us anything, including the hard questions.

[CEO name]
