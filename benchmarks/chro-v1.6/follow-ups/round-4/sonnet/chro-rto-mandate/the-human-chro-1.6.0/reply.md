Don't send this as a decision. Send it as a proposal with a date for input, after both works councils have seen it: in the Netherlands a five-day mandate announced as final risks being voided, and it cannot overrule the contracts of the people hired as remote.

The draft is in `announcement-email.md`. I changed one thing from what you asked: it says "proposal, no change until decided" instead of "from 1 March everyone is in five days a week".

**Why**
- Netherlands: changing or withdrawing home-working rules needs works council consent (art. 27 WOR). In 2025 Caterpillar ordered everyone in daily without it, and the court let the council void the order (ECLI:NL:RBOBR:2025:5758). Booking.com got substitute consent in 2026 only for a moderate plan (40%, six months' transition, hardship clause), and that is under appeal.
- Germany: ask whether you have a Betriebsrat. If so, moving people from agreed telework to the office needs its consent (§99 BetrVG).
- About 270 people were hired remote or hybrid. Where a contract or written agreement says so, an email can't change it. Have counsel check this in both countries.
- 1 March is 14 weeks away. The draft says the date may move.

**Talk to the CEO first, privately**
Ask what "culture" means in concrete terms: new joiners not settling in, teams that never meet, managers who don't know their people? Each has a better fix than five days for everyone. Evidence cuts both ways on mandates: a randomised trial of 1,612 employees found two home days a week cut quitting by a third with no hit to performance ratings (Bloom et al., Nature 2024), and 54 large firms saw turnover rise about 14% after mandates, most among women and senior staff (Ding et al., correlation only).

A line you can use: "I want this to work. Announced as decided, it risks a council challenge and losing the people we most want to keep. As a proposal, we have the same conversation without that risk."

**Options to show next to five days**
1. Fix the named problem: buddy and more office days for new joiners, funded team days.
2. Make home working good: Dutch tax-free allowance of €2.45 per home day, equipment. For scale, 270 people at three home days a week is about €91,000 a year; replacing one person costs half to two times their salary.
3. Anchor days agreed team by team.
4. A set minimum, with months of notice and individual exceptions.
5. Five days, only if the evidence supports it.
