# Company announcement: how we work together in 2027 (draft, not yet sent)

**Before sending:**
- The CEO has agreed this version.
- The Ondernemingsraad in Utrecht, and the Betriebsrat in Berlin if there is one, have been briefed and asked how they want staff told.
- Managers were briefed the day before.
- Employment counsel in both countries has read it.

**When:** a Tuesday to Thursday morning, not in the last week before the Christmas break. Suggested: Tuesday 8 or Wednesday 9 December 2026. Hold a live Q&A in both offices and online the same day. If Berlin has no Betriebsrat, delete the bracketed references to it.

---

**From:** [CEO name], with [your name], HR Director
**To:** All employees, Utrecht and Berlin
**Subject:** How we work together in 2027: a proposal, and we want your input

Hi everyone,

In the last four years we have grown to around 800 people in Utrecht and Berlin, and about a third of you joined us as remote or hybrid colleagues. That growth is a big part of why [Company] is where it is today. It has also changed how we work together, and not everything has kept up.

Here is what we see: [two or three concrete signals with the real numbers, for example "in our last engagement survey, X% said they rarely work with colleagues outside their own team", "new colleagues tell us it takes them X months to feel at home" or "Utrecht and Berlin teams increasingly work side by side rather than together"].

If our culture isn't where it should be, that is on us as leadership, not on you and not on where you work. It is ours to put right, and we want to do that with you rather than decide it for you.

**What we are proposing**

1. **Team anchor days.** Each team agrees on [two] days a week when the team is in the office together. They should be the days for the work that gains most from being in one room: planning, design sessions, mentoring and getting to know each other. The other days stay flexible.
2. **A better start for new colleagues.** Everyone who joins gets a buddy and structured first weeks with their team, and spends more time in the office with that team during their first [three] months.
3. **Funded time together across Utrecht and Berlin.** A budget for [quarterly] team days, with travel and a night's stay paid for colleagues coming from the other city or from further away.
4. **Better support for working from home.** [A home-working allowance on home days and help with a proper desk, chair and screen], plus clear team agreements on when people are reachable and when they are not.

**What is not changing**

- If your contract or a written agreement says you work remotely or hybrid, it stays as it is. We will not change it without your agreement.
- Nothing about where you work changes until the works councils have agreed and you have had at least [three] months' notice.
- Care duties, health, disability and distance will be looked at person by person, and there will be a clear route for hardship requests.
- We will not track individual attendance. We will look at how teams and the company as a whole are doing.

**What starts on 1 March 2027 anyway**

The buddy programme, the budget for team days, and training for managers in leading hybrid teams. None of these change anyone's working arrangements, so there is no reason to wait.

**How to have your say, and what happens next**

- **Today:** we have shared this proposal with the Ondernemingsraad in Utrecht [and the Betriebsrat in Berlin].
- **[Date, same day]:** live Q&A with [CEO name] and me in Utrecht, in Berlin and online. Ask anything; you can also send questions anonymously at [link].
- **Until Friday 22 January 2027:** tell your manager, use [form, with an anonymous option], or talk to your works council representatives. We have allowed extra time because of the holidays.
- **February 2027:** we adjust the proposal based on what we hear and formally ask the works councils for their agreement. We will tell you what we changed and why.
- **After agreement:** at least [three] months' notice before anything changes, and a review after the first quarter, with the results shared with all of you.

If you have a question about your own situation, talk to your manager or to [HR contact], in confidence.

We would rather take a few extra weeks and get this right with you than announce something that doesn't fix what we are trying to fix.

[CEO name], CEO
[Your name], HR Director
