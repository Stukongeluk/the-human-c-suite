**First steps**

1. **Talk to the CEO privately before anything goes out.** A line you can use: "I want to fix what you're seeing. Name the three things that worry you most, and give me two weeks to bring a plan that fixes them without losing the people we hired remote."
2. **Pull the numbers**, aggregated: contracts that say remote, where those people live, regretted attrition and engagement by team, desks per office.
3. **Take the problem to the OR, and to the Betriebsrat if Berlin has one**, before the proposal is final. In Berlin, how mobile work is organised is co-determined (§87(1) no. 14 BetrVG), and any existing works agreement binds you.
4. **Sequence it:** managers first, then people with remote agreements told by their own manager, then the email. Not in the two weeks before Christmas.

**What I'd propose instead:** team-chosen anchor days (around three), more office time for new joiners, remote hires handled one by one, and 1 March kept as the date the final policy is published.
