# CHRO follow-ups

Four rounds after the main test, each graded blind next to earlier answers to the same case. The results and what changed between drafts are in [`../benchmark.md`](../benchmark.md#follow-ups-what-the-draft-missed).

| Folder | Graded together |
|---|---|
| `round-1/` | draft 2 (the fixes), draft 1 and the CEO seat 1.5.2, on the pay gap, the return to office and the layoff list |
| `round-2/` | draft 3 (the word limit) with draft 2, draft 1 and the CEO seat, on the pay gap and forced ranking |
| `round-3/` | draft 4 (cut detail goes into a handed-over document) with drafts 3 and 2 on the pay gap, and drafts 3 and 1 on forced ranking |
| `round-4/` | the return to office on both models: version 1.6.0 (Sonnet) and draft 5 (Opus), with draft 1, the CEO seat and no skill, plus an extra check on support for home working |

An answer that appears in more than one round is the same answer, graded again by a fresh grader; its `grading.json` in each round is that round's grade. `benchmark.json` lists every graded answer with its round.
