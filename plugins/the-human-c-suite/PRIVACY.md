# Privacy

The Human C-Suite is a set of skills: Markdown instructions and reference files. The plugin and its author collect nothing. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no server of our own and no data sent anywhere by the plugin itself.

## Connectors

The plugin lists official, vendor-hosted MCP servers (see [CONNECTORS.md](CONNECTORS.md)), such as Atlassian, GitHub and BigQuery. None of them connects until you sign in to it yourself. Once you do, Claude reads data from that service with your own account and permissions, and the data passes only between Claude and that vendor. The vendor's privacy policy and your organisation's agreement with it apply, as do Anthropic's terms and privacy policy for Claude. Disconnect a server at any time from the MCP settings in Claude Code, or turn off the plugin.

The seats are read-only by default: they change nothing in your systems and send no messages without your explicit approval. For people data, they ask for aggregated, anonymised views rather than a connection to the HR system.
